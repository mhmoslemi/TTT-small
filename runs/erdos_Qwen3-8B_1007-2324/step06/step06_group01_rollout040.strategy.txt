### Approach and Rationale
We address the Erdős minimum overlap problem by employing a randomized local search over a shifted pattern space. The key insight is that highly optimal solutions often have small shifts where the overlap changes significantly. The algorithm starts from a parent solution and initializes multiple trials at shifted, randomized versions of this parent. For each trial, it performs local optimization using a directional derivative method under the constraint of integral satisfaction. 

This approach balances exploration (via broad shifts) and exploitation (via tight optimization) while respecting the problem constraints. By avoiding simplified representations (like spectral methods) and establishing rigorous correctness checks, we directly minimize the overlap integral using finite lags, maintaining equivalence to the original problem's evaluation methodology.

### Mathematical Specification
- **Domain**: h ∈ [0, 1]^{280} with ∑h = 140
- **Objective**: Minimize C₅ = max_{k} [∫h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx] over 2*n_points-1 lags
- **Constraints**: 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 for all i
- **Initialization**: Sample shifted parents with additive random noise and independent Gaussian noise
- **Optimization**: Directional derivative method to enforce convex integral constraint
- **Repair**: Jacobi projection onto the constraint hyperplane
- **Validation**: Clip to bounds then scale (if needed) to satisfy the integral constraint
