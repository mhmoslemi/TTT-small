 After careful thought, we decide to use a technique called "simulated annealing" (SA) because:
  - The problem is non-convex and high-dimensional, so local minima are common.
  - SA is effective in escaping local minima by allowing uphill moves with a decreasing probability.
  - We can adjust the cooling schedule to balance exploration and exploitation.
