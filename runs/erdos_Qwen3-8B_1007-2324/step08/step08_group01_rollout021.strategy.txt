1. Approach and rationale
The current SLSQP-based optimization from shifted parents often gets stuck in local optima. This approach combines three improvements:
   - **Adaptive random sampling**: Generates diverse random directions from the parent solution
   - **Autocorrelation-guided optimization**: Uses gradient information from autocorrelation to drive the search
   - **Multi-scale exploration**: Explores local, regional and global scales sequentially

The key bottleneck is the lack of global exploration with gradient-based methods. By combining single-variable perturbations with gradient descent along promising directions, we can escape local optima while efficiently searching the solution space. The bottleneck addresses are:
   - Improving worst-case performance using non-gradient methods
   - Maintaining solution validity 
   - Balancing exploration vs exploitation

The mathematical equivalence holds because the discrete correlation integrated over the grid exactly corresponds to the continuous form of the overlap integral at the scale of sample spacing.
