### Approach and Rationale

The provided approach minimizes the maximum overlap integral of a step function \(h(x)\) on the interval [0,2] subject to \(h(x) \in [0,1]\) and \(\int_0^2 h(x)dx = 1\). The key idea is to use multiple starting points for local optimization (SLSQP with bounds) to avoid getting stuck in local minima. The method directly optimizes the OBjective function, which computes the maximum correlation between \(h\) and \(1-h\), scaled by the grid spacing. 

The main bottleneck is the convolution calculation in `np.correlate`, which is \(O(n^2)\) per objective evaluation. To mitigate this, we:
- Use multiple resolutions (140 and 280 grid points) but limit the number of function evaluations per resolution.
- Transfer parent solutions between resolutions by up-sampling and scaling.
- Employ random and systematic restarts from various parent patterns.
- Use the provided initial solution as one of the parents and also generate random valid starting points.

The optimization tracks the best solution found and prioritizes termination by budget constraints, returning the best feasible solution within the time limit.

### Mathematical Specification

**Variables:**
- \(h \in \mathbb{R}^{n}\) with \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) for \(i=1,...,n\).
- \(n\) is the number of grid points (140 or 280).

**Objective Function:**
- Minimize \(C_5 = \max_k \left( \int h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx \right)\).
- Discretized as \(C_5 = \max_k \left( \text{correlation}(h, 1-h) \right) \cdot \Delta x\) where \(\Delta x = 2/n\).

**Constraints:**
- \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) for all \(i\).
- \(\sum_{i=1}^n h_i = n/2\) (since \(\int h(x)dx = 1\) and \(\Delta x = 2/n\)).

**Implementation:**
- The objective function computes the full cross-correlation between \(h\) and \(1-h\) and scales the maximum value by \(\Delta x\).
- The constraint is an equality: \(\sum h_i = n/2\).
- Local optimization engine: SLSQP with bounds \([0,1]\) for each parameter and equality constraint.
