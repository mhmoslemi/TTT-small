The core idea of this approach is to evolve probability distributions directly through a genetic algorithm (GA) while ensuring they are valid step functions satisfying the integral constraint. Step functions are represented as binary strings of 0/1 values (after scaling), but we'll reuse the discrete values directly. This is different from previous methods that optimized around a fixed starting point with local perturbations.  

The key rationale is that GA's population-based exploration will likely find better solutions by:  
- Simultaneously evolving a wide variety of solutions  
- Escaping local minima by perturbing samples across the entire vector  
- Exploiting crossover operations that combine sections of different solutions  

The workable GA approach does not require explicit gradient calculations, and is well-suited for non-convex spaces like this one. It also naturally incorporates the box constraints and integral constraint through repair mechanisms.
