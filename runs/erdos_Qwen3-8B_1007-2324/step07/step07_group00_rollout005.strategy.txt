### 2. Mathematical Specification  
The problem is to find \(h: [0,2] \to [0,1]\) with \(\int_{0}^{2} h(x)dx = 1\) that minimizes:  
\[\max_k \int_{0}^{2} h(x) (1-h(x+k))dx\]  
We discretize the interval \([0,2]\) into \(n\) points, representing \(h(x)\) by a vector \(h \in \mathbb{R}^n\) satisfying \(0 \leq h[i] \leq 1\) and \(\sum h[i] = n/2\) (since \(dx = 2/n\), and \(\int h = n/2\)). The correlation must be computed for all shifts \(k\), with the objective being:  
\[C_5 = \frac{1}{n} \max_k \sum_{i} h[i] (1 - h[(i+k)\mod n]) \cdot \text{rectangular window}\]  
For mode='full', the \((L1)\) relaxation is valid because the problem is linear in \(h\).
