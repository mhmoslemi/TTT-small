The key ideas are:

1. **Symmetry reduction**: Represent the function by 140 variables, reducing the effective dimension by factor 2 compared to 280 points. This halves the search space size.

2. **Chebyshev collocation**: Discretize the optimization variables in the physical domain rather than function space, allowing efficient use of gradient information.

3. **Multi-modal optimization**: Use Bayesian optimization with expected improvement criterion, sampling from a distribution that favors transitions from near-zero to near-one values (critical for sharp thresholds in the overlap integral).

4. **Adaptive relaxation**: Allow temporary constraint violation during exploration to reach flatter minima.
