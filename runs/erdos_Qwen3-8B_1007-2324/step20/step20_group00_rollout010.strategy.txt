Our approach shifts from local search to global optimization via differential evolution (DE), combined with wavelet-based initializations and dimensionality reduction. This addresses the primary bottleneck: local minima in the nonconvex search space.

The key innovations are:
- Wavelet-based initialization creates diverse initial candidates spanning multiple scales
- DE global optimizer explores broader solution space
- Dimensionality reduction focuses optimization on critical regions
- All initializations enforce integral constraints properly

$$\min_{h:\mathbb{R}\rightarrow[0,1]} C_5 = \max_{k} \left\langle h(\cdot), 1-h(\cdot+k) \right\rangle$$

- ∫₀² h(x)dx = 1
