3. **Constraint Handling**: Both box constraints and integral constraint are complex.

To improve, we'll employ a multi-phase strategy:
- **Initial Sampling**: Use a combination of structured patterns and random initialization to cover diverse solution spaces.
- **Progressive Refinement**: Start with coarse-grained optimization to escape local optima, then apply finer-grained methods.
- **Dimensionality Reduction**: Represent h as a few non-zero intervals (3-5) at the cost of exploding computation, but compensate by using global optimizers.
- **Constraint Projection**: Dynamically maintain feasibility during evolution using a minimal projection step.

This approach trades direct high-dimensional optimization with combinatorial structure exploration to find signals below the proposed bound.
