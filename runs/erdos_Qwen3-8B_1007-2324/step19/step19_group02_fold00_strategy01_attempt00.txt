### Strategy: Multi-Stage Hybrid Optimization with Adaptive Resolution

**Approach and Rationale**  
We leverage a two-stage optimization process combining a basin-hopping global search with fine-grained local refinement to navigate the high-dimensional space efficiently. The first stage uses bounds-aware random projection and gradient descent to escape local optima, while the second stage employs analytical gradient-based optimization for precision.  
**Key Innovation**: Dynamically adjusts resolution (`n_points`) during optimization to trade-off search speed and solution quality. This avoids early convergence to suboptimal solutions and allows leveraging improved structures for larger resolutions, potentially leading to structural changes not apparent in fixed-length domains.  
**Bottleneck Addressed**:  
- Limited gradient information in the provided solution
