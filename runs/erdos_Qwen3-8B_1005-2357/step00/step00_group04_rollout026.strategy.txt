### 1. Approach and Rationale

The Erdős minimum overlap problem seeks to find a step function \(h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1]\) with \(\int_0^2 h(x)  dx = 1\) that minimizes the maximum overlap integral:
\[
C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1 - h(x+k))  dx.
\]
The current record is approximately 0.38085, and the goal is to achieve a bound of 0.380876. The approach involves using a combination of **differential evolution** and **gradient-based refinement** to explore the solution space more effectively than the previous gradient descent method. 

The key insight is that differential evolution is better suited for handling the non-convexity and high dimensionality of the problem, while gradient-based refinement can polish the solution to achieve tighter bounds. This approach addresses the limitations of the previous gradient descent method, which often got stuck in local optima. 

The algorithm starts with a population of feasible solutions and uses differential evolution to explore the solution space. The best solution is then refined using gradient descent, focusing on the lag that contributes most to the maximum overlap. The resolution is fixed at 42 points for computational efficiency within the time budget, but the solution structure allows for easy extension to higher resolutions if needed.

### 2. Mathematical Specification

**Variables:**
- \(h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1]\), represented as an array of \(n_{\text{points}}\) samples with spacing \(\Delta x = 2 / n_{\text{points}}\).
- \(\mathbf{h} \in \mathbb{R}^{n_{\text{points}}}\) with \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) and \(\sum h_i \Delta x = 1\).

**Objective:**
Minimize \(C_5 = \max_k (\Delta x \cdot \text{np.correlate}(\mathbf{h}, 1 - \mathbf{h}, \text{mode}='full')[k]\).

**Constraints:**
1. \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) for all \(i\).
2. \(\sum h_i \Delta x = 1\).

**Fitness Function:**
The fitness is defined as the negative of \(C_5\) to maximize the minimization of overlap.

**Gradient Calculation:**
For the active lag \(k_0\) (the shift that maximizes the overlap), the gradient is:
\[
\nabla h_i = \Delta x \cdot (1 - h_{i - k_0})
\]
if \(i - k_0\) is within bounds, else 0.

**Feasibility Projection:**
After each update, clip \(\mathbf{h}\) to \([0, 1]\) and scale it to satisfy the integral constraint.
