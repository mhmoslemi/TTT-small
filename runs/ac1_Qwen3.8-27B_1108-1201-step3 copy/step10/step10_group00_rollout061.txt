`conv(k) = sum_{j: 0<=j<n, 0<=k-j<n} a[j] * a[k-j]`

For k = n-1 (center):
`conv(n-1) = sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a[j] * a[n-1-j]`
`= sum_{j=0}^{n-1} [(n-j) * 2/(n(n+1))] * [(n-(n-1-j)) * 2/(n(n+1))]`
`= sum_{j=0}^{n-1} [(n-j) * (j+1)] * 4/(n^2(n+1)^2)`
`= 4/(n^2(n+1)^2) * sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (n-j)(j+1)`
`= 4/(n^2(n+1)^2) * sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (nj + n - j^2 - j)`
`= 4/(n^2(n+1)^2) * (n * n(n-1)/2 + n^2 - n(n-1)(2n-1)/6 - n(n-1)/2)`
`= 4/(n^2(n+1)^2) * (n^2(n-1)/2 + n^2 - n(n-1)(2n-1)/6 - n(n-1)/2)`

Let me compute `sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (n-j)(j+1)`:
`= sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (nj + n - j^2 - j)`
`= n * sum j + n*n - sum j^2 - sum j`
`= n * n(n-1)/2 + n^2 - n(n-1)(2n-1)/6 - n(n-1)/2`
`= n^2(n-1)/2 + n^2 - n(n-1)(2n-1)/6 - n(n-1)/2`

For large n: `≈ n^3/2 + n^2 - n^3/3 - n^2/2 = n^3/6 + n^2/2`

So `conv(n-1) ≈ 4/(n^2(n+1)^2) * n^3/6 ≈ 4n/(6n^2) = 2/(3n)`.
