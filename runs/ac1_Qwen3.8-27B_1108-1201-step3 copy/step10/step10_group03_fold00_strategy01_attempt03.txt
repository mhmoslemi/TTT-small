While the previous program used a deterministic method with bidirectional LP alternation, this strategy introduces randomness during the optimization process to escape local minima. The key steps are:

1. Start with the parent sequence (if available)
2. Use multi-start points to explore different regions
3. Incorporate gradient-based updates to the sequence
4. Apply dimensionality reduction to focus optimization
5. Prioritize sequences that keep the convolution peaks flat
6. Schedule expensive evaluations only when time remains
