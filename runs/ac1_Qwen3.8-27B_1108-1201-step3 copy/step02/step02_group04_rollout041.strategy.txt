<h2>Approach and Rationale</h2>

We propose a hybrid optimization strategy combining continuous optimization and discrete structure detection. The idea is to implicitly "discover" an optimal step structure by treating sequence values as continuous variables in a simplified model, while simultaneously exploring different structural patterns that may improve the performance.

There are four key aspects:
1. We formulate a continuous optimization problem to balance extreme coefficients needed for sharp peaks against uniformly distributed coefficients for good frequency response
2. We perform a cycle of analysis where we:
   - Identify coefficients that exhibit patterns across multiple cycles
   - Perform targetted convolution value optimizations  
   - Extract core structural patterns

The rationale is to find a robust middle-ground between the two extremes, achieving superior speed and quality of optimization while avoiding both:

- Direct continuous optimization's tendency to produce local minima through overly uniform distributions
- Discrete structure optimization's difficulty in exploring candidate spaces efficiently

<h2>Mathematical Specification</h2>

We use a continuous representation of our optimization candidates while preserving the ordering constraints inherent in convolution.

Let $n$ be the sequence length (fixed to 512). We define the objective function with modifications:

\[
\text{score} = \frac{2n}{(\text{sum}(x))^2} \cdot \max_{k} (\sum_{i+j=k} x_{i} x_{j})
\]

subject to:

\[
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} x_i \geq \lambda \cdot \text{sum}(\text{prior\_candidates})
\]

and for periodicity constraints:

\[
x_i \leq c_i \cdot \text{target\_max}, \quad \text{with} \quad c_i = 1.0 \text{ if } i \text{ is a key coefficient}
\]

The steps are:

1. Parse candidate values and normalize to [0, 1000]
2. Covariance matrix to capture key coefficient patterns
3. Perform simplex projection with key coefficient preservation
