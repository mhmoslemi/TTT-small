The issue is that for p < 1/2, the objective is 2*B(1-p,1-p)*(1-p)^2 (independent of n when m=n). But if we truncate to m < n, the objective becomes 2*n/m * B(1-p,1-p)*(1-p)^2, which is WORSE (larger) because n/m > 1.

So truncation makes it worse. The optimal is always to use the full length.
