1. Approach and rationale

The discrete problem is to minimize the nonsmooth finite-support nonlinear expression over a convex feasible polytope. Each full-correlation entry is a sum of products h_i(1-h_j) over an overlapping set, so the objective changes slope whenever different row functions exchange the maximum. Generic smooth approximations lose precision on that active set. Generic local optimizers also often join active and inactive correlations, and a convex relaxation is not directly valid because the products are nonconvex.

The proposed method is based on exact small-dimensional line minimization over feasible two-coordinate mass transfers. If one coordinate is decreased by α and another by -α, the positive/sum constraint remains exactly feasible on an interval, and every finite correlation row becomes a polynomial in α, at most quadratic. The exact one-step problem along that line is therefore the minimum over a one-dimensional piecewise-quadratic function. A generic finite maximum of quadratics can be minimized globally with a finite set of active-edge candidates formed from endpoints, quadratic stationary points and pairwise row intersections. This gives a correct global line step if the line representation matches the evaluator, and nevertheless the requested solution is always accepted only after evaluating the original evaluator.

The method is different from the preceding proposals because the core update is not a global smoothed max, SLSQP-style descent, or a global tangent LP. It uses explicit feasible transfers and solves the one-dimensional problem as a finite quadratic-maximum problem. A stochastic layer instead samples world perturbations around that scheme. The main limitation is that the coordinate search is nonconvex, and the global objective is covered by a pool of searches and stochastic jumps; no mathematical guarantee of global optimality can be claimed. The improvement target is roughly 5.2e-5 below the known example for n=42, so observed activation of fractional variables is needed; the line optimizer specifically provides fractional amounts in a single screening capabilities step through exact global candidate line search on active candidates.
