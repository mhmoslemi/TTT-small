2. Main idea: polytope vertices, feasible affine arcs, and active-overlap oracles

4. Search exactly along the fine-discretization feasible segment h(alpha)= (1-alpha) h + alpha h_v, alpha in [0,1], using the original evaluator at every sampled or bracketed point.
