1. Approach and rationale

The problem is a nonconvex box-constrained nonlinear minimization

    minimize over h in [0,1]^n,   max_j dx * (np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode="full")_j)

subject to the discrete area constraint

    sum_i h_i * dx = 1   equivalently   sum_i h_i = n/2.
