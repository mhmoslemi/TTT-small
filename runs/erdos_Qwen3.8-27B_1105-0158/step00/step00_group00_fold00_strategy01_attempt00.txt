1.1 Central idea. The solver will refine a feasible construction by a trust-region sequential affine-minimax method. The current vector `y` and its exact score `C5(y)` define one nonsmooth objective:

where `A_m` is the exact finite-support correlation term for lag `m`. At each iteration, every lag term `A_m` is replaced by its first-order affine approximation around `y`, and the following convex subproblem is solved:

minimize  t + beta * sum_i |x_i - y_i|
subject to 0 <= x_i <= 1,
           sum_i x_i = n / 2,
           max_m L_m(x) <= t,
           |x_i - y_i| <= r  for all i.

The variables are `x` and `t`. The approximations `L_m` use the morning gradients of the exact correlation terms.
