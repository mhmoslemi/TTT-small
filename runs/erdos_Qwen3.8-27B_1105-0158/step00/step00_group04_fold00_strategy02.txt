1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Problem restatement and authoritative objective

The returned object is a finite array \(h=(h_0,\ldots,h_{n-1})\) with
\[
0\le h_i\le 1,\qquad \sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i=\frac n2,\qquad \Delta x=2/n.
\]
For the supplied evaluator, the only accepted score is
\[
F(h)=\max_{k=0}^{2n-2}\left(\Delta x\,\operatorname{np.correlate}(h,1-h,\text{mode="full"})_k\right).
\]
No circular correlation, no Fourier surrogate, no “one minus autocorrelation,” and no partial lag substitution acts as the official objective. Every algorithmic acceptance decision in this plan uses either the direct \(F(h)\) evaluation or a feasibility-repaired variant followed immediately by direct \(F(h)\) evaluation.

The supplied parent has \(n=70\) and a score near \(0.38085\). The requested target is \(0.3808\) or lower. The improvement required is tiny, so the primary difficulty is not global exploration but stable high-dimensional local adjustment under a nonconvex max-of-products objective and exact-feasibility pressure.

1.2 Central idea

The central method is a **trust-region factorized linear-relaxation (TR-FLR) candidate generator** combined with three auxiliary sources of valid exact-scored candidates:

1. **Factorized LP subproblems.** At a feasible parent \(h\), freeze only one side of the bilinear term: either \(q=1-h\) while optimizing \(x\) as the first factor in \(\operatorname{correlate}(x,q)\), or freeze \(h\) while optimizing \(q\) as the second factor in \(\operatorname{correlate}(h,q)\). Each subproblem is a genuine linear program in a scaling variable \(t\) and one vector of variables. It is not a smoothing approximation and not a penalty relaxation. It is a temporary penalized convex/conic-free LP heuristic that produces full-dimensional trial arrays. The exact evaluator \(F\) exclusively decides whether a trial becomes the new incumbent.

2. **Pairwise sum-preserving randomized local exploration.** Because the factorized LP can saturate boundaries and because the true nonconvex score can improve even when a frozen-factor LP does not predict a decrease, the candidate generator is embedded in a randomized trust ball using direct exact pairwise redistribution. This enables non-LP neighborhood movement while preserving the budget and sum exactly to floating-point precision.

3. **Low-dimensional global/local mode subspace search around the incumbent.** The parent is perturbed by a compact, deterministic, mean-zero discrete-sine/random basis of moderate dimension. Searches on this subspace use projected feasible candidates and exact full-evaluator scores. This provides coherent coordinated tweaks across many coordinates without spending many evaluations in \(n\)-dimensional nonsmooth gradients.

Together, these steps attack the bottleneck: a locally intractable exact nonconvex score can be improved only by carefully coordinated local moves. The factorized LP gives full-dimensional local trials based on exact finite-support correlation coefficients; local exact moves and mode subspace retries give recovery when factorized trials fail; and final quantization enforces the exact \(0/1\)-interval and sum constraints correctly at return.

1.3 What is justified vs heuristic

Justified:
- The score formula is the evaluator’s formula.
- Each generated LP is linear once one factor is frozen.
- The project/quantization steps impose \(0\le h_i\le 1\) and a practical exact sum, with exact floating design for the returned array.
- Acceptance only by direct evaluator score prevents surrogate score leakage.

Heuristic:
- A factorized LP decrease does not guarantee a decrease of the full true score because the complementary \(1-x\) changes after candidate conversion.
- Mode subspace search is not globally complete.
- Random local moves do not certify optimality.
- The method is an ensemble of valid heuristic searches, not a proof of global optimum.

1.4 Distinction from previous proposals

The plan is not primarily a log-sum-exp smoothing plus SLSQP method. It is not primarily a generic Powell line search or all-dimensional diffuse global search. The primary generative engine is the exact trust-region LP relaxation based on freezing one side of the nonconvex factorization, then verifying in the true finite-support correlation objective. The random pairwise engine and mode-search stage are auxiliary, not the primary definition. All candidates are returned only through the evaluator metric.
