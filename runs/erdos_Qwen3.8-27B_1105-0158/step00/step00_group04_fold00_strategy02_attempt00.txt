1.1 Central idea

The submitted parent construction is extremely close to the target bound, so the goal is a tiny reduction of the exact discrete max-overlap. The proposed method improves the parent by a pair-exchange, feasible-geometry, exact-objective optimization framework.

At each update, mass is transferred between two coordinates while keeping the box constraints and total sum exactly feasible. The one-dimensional trajectory induced by this transfer is evaluated using the exact evaluator `max(np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full")) * dx`. No logarithmic smoothing, convolution surrogate, penalty approximation, or linearized objective replaces the true objective for acceptance. Every accepted candidate must lower the exact evaluator score after a final dyadic quantization repair.

The method is materially different from generic log-sum-exp/SQP or arbitrary Powell line searches. It has a structured feasible direction family focused on pairwise coordinate exchange. Because feasible directions preserve the sum exactly and the candidate is always evaluated using the one-line task metric, local improvements are immediately certified.

1.2 Why this may cross the record threshold

The algorithm adds an explicit escape mechanism. Since the underlying space is nonconvex, monotone coordinate descent cannot guarantee escape.
