In increasing-q order these are precisely np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full') * Δx. Its box-feasible interval is t_low = max(−h_i, h_j−1) and t_high = min(1−h_i, h_j); it contains zero and preserves Σh=M.\n\nEach lag on that line has the exact expansion R_q(h(t)) = r_q + b_q t + c_q t², where r_q=R_q(h).
