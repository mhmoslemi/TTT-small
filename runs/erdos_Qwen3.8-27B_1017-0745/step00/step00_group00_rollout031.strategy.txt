It exploits the exact algebraic structure of the finite-support correlation along a pair-transfer direction.\n\nThe objective is nonconvex, but each pair-transfer restriction is the maximum of finitely many quadratic functions. For a transfer between coordinates a and b, only the zero lag and the two lags at separation |a-b| have quadratic curvature; all other lag terms are affine. The program can therefore partition the feasible transfer interval by the upper envelope of those affine terms and enumerate all possible locations of a one-dimensional minimum.
