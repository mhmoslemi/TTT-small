Replace only the concave part by its tangent, producing a convex quadratic upper bound for every lag that touches the exact lag value at the current vector. Minimize the epigraph of all these upper bounds over the capped-simplex constraints with a proximal penalty, using a conic solver. Recompute the exact finite-correlation score for every proposed vector and accept it only if that score improves.\n\nThis is materially different from the supplied projected-gradient/log-sum-exp continuation and final SLSQP polish, and from the earlier trust-region linear epigraph, pair-transfer line search, and differential-evolution approaches.
