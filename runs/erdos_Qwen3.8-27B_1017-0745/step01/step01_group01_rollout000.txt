- f_d(h) = sum over valid i of h[i]*(1-h[i+d])
- Score = dx * max(f_d)

The gradient of f_d with respect to h[i]:
- For pair (i, j=i+d): derivative of h[i]*(1-h[j]) w.r.t. h[i] is (1-h[j])
- For pair (k, l=k+d): derivative of h[k]*(1-h[l]) w.r.t. h[l] is -h[k]

    if not np.isfinite(fs) or fs > best_score + 1e-12:
        final = best.copy()
        fs = best_score
    if not np.isfinite(fs):
        final = project_capped(uniform, n)
        fs = exact_score(final, n)
    return final.copy(), float(fs), int(n)
