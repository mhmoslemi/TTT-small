For lag d (output index k = d + n - 1):

q_d = dx * sum_{s=0}^{m-1} h[p+s] * (1-h[r+s])
d(q_d)/d(h[p+s]) = dx * (1-h[r+s])
d(q_d)/d(h[r+s]) = -dx * h[p+s]

For d=0: p=0, r=0, m=n, so both terms affect the same coordinates:
d(q_0)/d(h[s]) = dx*(1-h[s]) - dx*h[s] = dx*(1-2*h[s])
