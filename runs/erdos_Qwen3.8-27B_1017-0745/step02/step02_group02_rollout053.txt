q_s(h) = dx * sum_{j=0}^{m_s-1} h[p_s+j] * (1-h[r_s+j])

For lag s (where s goes from -(n-1) to n-1):
- p = max(s, 0), r = max(-s, 0), m = n - |s|
- q_s = dx * sum_{j=0}^{m-1} h[p+j] * (1-h[r+j])
- dq_s/dh[p+j] = dx * (1-h[r+j])
- dq_s/dh[r+j] = -dx * h[p+j]

When p+j == r+j (which happens when s=0), both contributions apply:
dq_0/dh[j] = dx*(1-h[j]) - dx*h[j] = dx*(1-2h[j])

= qmax + tau * log(sum_k exp((q_k-qmax)/tau))

Gradient:
w_k = exp((q_k-qmax)/tau) / sum_t exp((q_t-qmax)/tau)
grad F_tau = sum_k w_k * grad q_k
