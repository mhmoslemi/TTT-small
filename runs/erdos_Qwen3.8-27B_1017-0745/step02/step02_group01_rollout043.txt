The epigraph method minimizes t subject to t >= q_d for all d. At the optimum, some subset of lags are "active" (t = q_d). The gradient is a subgradient: a convex combination of active lag gradients.

The log-sum-exp method minimizes S_tau(h) = tau * log(Σ exp(q_d/tau)). The gradient is a weighted average of ALL lag gradients, with weights exp((q_d - max)/tau) / Σ exp(...). As tau → 0, this concentrates on the maximum lag(s).

- At large tau, log-sum-exp "sees" all lags equally, which can help navigate the landscape
- At small tau, it focuses on the worst lag(s), similar to epigraph
- The transition is smooth, which can avoid the "active set changes" problem in epigraph
