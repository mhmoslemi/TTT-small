    """Compute dq_d/dh[m] for valid m in [0,n)"""
    dx = 2.0 / n

Upper hull algorithm:
1. Sort lines by slope b[d]
2. For equal slopes, keep only the one with highest intercept
3. Build upper hull: for lines in increasing slope order, the hull changes from one line to the next at their intersection. A line is on the hull if its intersection with the previous hull line is after the intersection of the previous two hull lines.

Standard upper hull (for maximum):
- Process lines in order of increasing slope
- Maintain a list of hull lines with their intersection points (where they become active)
- For a new line, compute its intersection with the last hull line
- If this intersection is before the last hull line's start, pop the last line and recompute
- Continue until the new line's intersection is after the last line's start
