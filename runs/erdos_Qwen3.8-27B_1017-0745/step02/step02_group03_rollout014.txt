The task is to find a step function h: [0,2] → [0,1] that minimizes C₅ = max_k ∫ h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx, subject to ∫₀² h(x) dx = 1.

The discretization uses n points over [0,2], with dx = 2/n. The constraint is sum(h) * dx = 1, i.e., sum(h) = n/2.

The score is computed as max(np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode="full")) * dx.

Current record: C₅ ≤ 0.38085. Previous program achieved 0.380992.

The strategy calls for a JADE-style differential evolution (DE) approach, which is fundamentally different from the SLSQP-based approach in the previous program.
