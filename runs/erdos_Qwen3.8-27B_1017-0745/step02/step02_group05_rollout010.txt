The task is to find a step function h: [0,2] → [0,1] that minimizes C₅ = max_k ∫ h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx.

The key insight from the strategy is to use a smooth log-sum-exp minimax approach instead of the tangential majorization approach used in the previous program. This is a fundamentally different algorithmic approach.

Let me implement the strategy:

1. Use log-sum-exp smoothing of the max of lag overlaps
2. Provide analytic gradient to SLSQP
3. Use continuation (decreasing temperature)
4. Multiple starting points
5. Exact scoring via np.correlate

Key implementation details:
- n=47 (from parent), dx = 2/47
- Lags d from -(n-1) to n-1, total 2n-1 = 93 lags
- Q_d(h) = dx * sum_{r=0}^{m-1} h[a+r] * (1 - h[b+r]) where a=max(d,0), b=max(-d,0), m=n-|d|
- phi_tau(h) = tau * log(sum exp(Q_d/tau)) (stabilized)
- Gradient: sum w_d * grad Q_d where w_d are softmax weights
- grad Q_d: add dx*(1-h[b:b+m]) to positions a:a+m, subtract dx*h[a:a+m] to positions b:b+m
