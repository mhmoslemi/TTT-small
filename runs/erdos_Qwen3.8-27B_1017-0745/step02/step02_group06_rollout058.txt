For the Jacobian of lag k:
L_k(h) = dx * sum_i h[j]*(1-h[i]) where j=i+k, i ranges over valid indices

dL_k/dh[q] = dx * (sum over pairs where j=q of (1-h[i]) - sum over pairs where i=q of h[j])

For the epigraph: minimize t subject to t - L_k(h) >= 0 for all k
Inequality constraint: g_k = t - L_k(h) >= 0
Jacobian of g_k: [-dL_k/dh, 1]

Equality: sum(h) - n/2 = 0
Jacobian: [1, ..., 1, 0]
