For lag d, q_d(h) = dx * Σ_{i∈I_d} h_i(1-h_{i+d})

The gradient w.r.t. h_k:
- If k ∈ I_d (i.e., 0≤k<n and 0≤k+d<n): contributes dx*(1-h_{k+d})
- If k-d ∈ I_d (i.e., 0≤k-d<n and 0≤(k-d)+d<n, which is 0≤k-d<n and 0≤k<n): contributes -dx*h_{k-d}

So for each d:
- lo = max(0, -d), hi = min(n, n-d)
- For i in [lo, hi): gradient[i] += dx*(1-h[i+d]) and gradient[i+d] -= dx*h[i]
