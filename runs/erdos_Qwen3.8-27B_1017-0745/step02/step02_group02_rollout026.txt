For each lag s, q_s(alpha) = A_s + B_s*alpha + C_s*alpha²

- Endpoints lo, hi
- Stationary points of individual quadratics: -B_s/(2*C_s)
- Intersections of pairs of quadratics: solve (A_s-A_t) + (B_s-B_t)*alpha + (C_s-C_t)*alpha² = 0

            a = min(r, 1.0 - h[i]); h[i] += a; r -= a
        else:

        B[j] = dx * (np.dot(vp, 1 - hr) - np.dot(hp, vr))
