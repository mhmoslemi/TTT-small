This is substantively different from the supplied differential-evolution and pattern-poll search: it learns a full covariance between search coordinates rather than forming coordinatewise difference mutations and polling directions. It is also different from a lag-constraint nonlinear program or smooth-max gradient descent: it forms no lag Jacobian, uses no surrogate objective, and requires no nonlinear solver.\n\nThe objective couples entries at many offsets, so coordinated changes can matter. The orthonormal cosine coordinates represent zero-sum changes; when n is at most 49 they span the entire mass-preserving subspace. CMA-ES adapts correlated moves from exact objective rankings, while projection handles the box and mass constraints jointly. This may find a better basin or balance the worst lags more effectively, but that is a heuristic, not a measured improvement. The exact score and protected fallback prevent the program from returning an accepted candidate worse than its validated starting fallback.
