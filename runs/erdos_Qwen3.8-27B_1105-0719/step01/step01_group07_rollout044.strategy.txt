1.1 Central idea

The supplied parent vector is one feasible point of a nonconvex, box-constrained, mean-value problem. The target gap from the previous reported value is small, so the main bottleneck is not generally a failure to move in a generic descent direction; it is that the maximum profile is nonsmooth and the high profile values at several nearby shifts must be lowered together. Surface-smoothed objectives or a single weighted gradient direction collapse the relevant active shifts into one scalar gradient and often move away from the true active envelope bounded state. Scalar pair moves are exact one-dimensional truncations and are subset-only.

    minimize over z, t    t
