Instead of relying only on a generic constrained semidefinite/smooth direct NLP, which can stall near a nearby local minimum, the proposed method repeatedly:

- moves the current feasible vector `h` along a feasible line segment whose endpoints are feasible;
- where each endpoint is chosen to visit/surrender an extreme box bound (a one-pair move, an LP direction endpoint, a randomized feasible perturbation endpoint, or a recombination endpoint from a good candidate);
