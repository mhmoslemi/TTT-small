Let me also think about what makes this problem hard. The constraint is ∫h = 1 over [0,2], i.e., the average value is 0.5. The objective is to minimize the maximum overlap. Intuitively, we want h to be close to 0 or 1 (to minimize h*(1-h)) but the average constraint forces some mixing.

The optimal solution likely has a structure where h is close to 0 on some intervals and close to 1 on others, with transitions arranged to minimize the worst-case overlap at any shift.

One key insight: the overlap at shift 0 is ∫h(1-h) dx, which is maximized when h is at 0 or 1 (giving 0) and minimized... wait, we're minimizing the max, so we want ALL overlaps to be small. The shift-0 overlap is ∫h(1-h) which we want small, meaning h should be close to 0 or 1 pointwise.
