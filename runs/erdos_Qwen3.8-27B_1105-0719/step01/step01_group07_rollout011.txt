For a cyclic group Z_v, a (v, k, λ) difference set has the property that for each shift k (1 ≤ k ≤ v-1), the number of pairs (d1, d2) ∈ D×D with d1-d2 = k (mod v) is exactly λ.

= k - λ (for a difference set)
