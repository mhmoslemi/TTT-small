1. Approach and rationale

1. Central scientific idea

The problem is a finite-box, exact-sum, finite-support nonconvex minimax problem. The task is to minimize the largest lagged correlation between a sequence `h` and its complement `1 - h`. The parent construction is already a strong local solution from the earlier active-set LP family. A purely local linear or smooth-maximization method is therefore expected to remain in the same basin.

The proposed material replacement is a direct geometric search on the exact feasible set using exact geometric subset transfer directions, exact one-dimensional global-style descent, structured combinatorial replications and emergency diversification/annealing around local midstate search plus occluded PD synchronization.

Primary method is two-coordinate exact simplex exchange:

For two positions `p` and `q`, set
`h_p(t) = h[p] + t`, `h_q(t) = h[q] - t`, all other entries fixed.
