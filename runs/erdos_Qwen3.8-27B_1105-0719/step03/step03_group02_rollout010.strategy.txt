1. Approach and rationale

The supplied parent operates primarily by smoothing the maximum correlation into a log-sum-exp surrogate, running gradient/SLSQP steps on that surrogate, and then polishing local “Lagrangian” or pair-transfer moves. The proposed method switches to a direct nonlinear epigraph formulation of the true peak, rather than treating the true maximum through a temperature parameter. Introduce a scalar `tau` intended to upper-bound all evaluated correlations. For variables `h` constrained to the capped simplex, minimize `tau` subject to active constraints
`tau >= dx * correlate(h, 1-h)[k]`.
For a chosen active lag set `A`, this is a genuinely different local minimizer: the solver can reduce `tau` by reasoning about each active finite-correlation constraint directly, keeping `tau` aligned exactly with the current maximum while allowing nonactive constraints to be corrected by an outer loop. The nonconvexity remains because correlation constraints include `h_i h_j` products, so this is not a convex global solver and no optimality may be claimed. Every accepted candidate must still be scored by the exact evaluator
`score(h) = max_k dx * np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full')[k]`.

The second main idea is a global “linearized-vertex jump” seed generator. At a candidate `h`, linearize the most active correlations, aggregate their gradients into a vector `G`, and solve the exact linear projection on the capped simplex:
min₂ `G^T v` s.t. `0 <= v_i <= 1`, `sum_i v_i = n/2`.
A global vertex solution is a balanced binary sequence with `n//2` ones in the positions of the smallest aggregated gradient entries. The algorithm forms a line-search family `(1-gamma)h + gamma v`, because convex combinations of feasible points are exactly feasible and preserve the sum by convexity. This gives balanced, discrete starting seeds motivated by derivative information but without a costly binary exchange search. These seeds feed a different set of basins into the epigraph solver. This is substantively different from the undisclosed proposed binary memetic algorithm and different from the parent smoothed-max + pair-transfer program: the central operator is exact nonlinear epigraph optimization with dynamically selected constraints, and the multi-start enrichment comes from gradient-LMO vertices rather than bitwise simulated annealing or threshold waterfill expansion.

The method is intended to address the bottleneck that smoothed gradients often become dispersed over many semistable correlations, whereas the active set for a true local minimum may contain a small subset of lags binding numerically equal maxima. The epigraph can follow KKT-like directions directly; the outer active set ensures赞助商 lag models are updated.

Limitations and assumptions: the epigraph subproblem is nonconvex and may converge to a local stationary point of the active-set relaxation. A candidate can satisfy active constraints and improve `tau` while increasing an omitted lag, so exact acceptance based on all lags is mandatory. The LMO seeds are heuristic models of decreasing the active sum and may not reduce the exact maximum. The reported bound is only upper-valid because it is the exact evaluator value of the final feasible array, not a global certificate.
