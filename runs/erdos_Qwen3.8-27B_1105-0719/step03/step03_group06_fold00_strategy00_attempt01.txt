1. Approach and rationale

The supplied parent solution is already close to 0.381. A new lower bound is unlikely to come from merely repeating the same SLSQP or top-active descent cycle; it should come from changing the optimization path. The proposed method is a hierarchical, multi-start “smoothed-max projected descent” search, reinforced by coordinate-pair polishing and binary-restarts.

The central idea is:

- Replace the max-over-lags objective with a smooth soft-max surface that is always comparable to the original evaluator.
- Descent on this surface under a tangent-to-feasibility projection gives smoother moves and makes it easier to move away from a local maximum constraint.
- Repeat on many resolutions. Because the dimension is part of the representation, changing resolution can change the discrete search landscape and sometimes reduces the evaluated maximum.
- Add deterministic random basin-hopping bumps, binary rounding starts, and exact pair-transfer polishing so that the method can escape the local basin of the parent.
