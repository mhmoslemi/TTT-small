The parent is already good, so the bottleneck is not global construction but escaping the first-order LP minima used by the previous method. The new algorithm keeps feasibility exact, evaluates the evaluator’s exact maximum correlation objective at every acceptance, and adds three additional mechanisms that were absent or weak in the previous program:

   - multi-direction local minmax with soft slacks and alternative linear-sum objectives, instead of only a single hard active-set LP;
   - exact one-dimensional coordinate swap refinement that captures quadratic curvature of the true objective not present in the LP linearization;
   - maintained multi-resolution restart pool using precarious grid transfers but always ending with exact evaluation on the chosen n.

   Rationale: the objective is max over many shifted products h_i(1-h_{i+d}), hence nonconvex. Soft slack objectives allow a compromise direction that lowers some active constraints while paying a bounded penalty on lags that may rise slightly. Exact line-searches guarantee every update is judged by the true evaluator objective. Exact pair moves change the current max through the actual bilinear terms, so they can find vertices that first-order LP directions cannot.
