1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

The supplied parent `initial_h_values` will be treated as one member of a small population, not as the only starting point. The search will alternate between three mutually reinforcing mechanisms:

1. Feasible local refinement in the exact discretized objective using a convex one-dimensional coordinate rule.
2. Active-set nonlinear equality/inequality optimization (SLSQP or trust-constr) that drives several near-maximum correlation lags down simultaneously.
3. Resolution search over nearby sample counts, transferring and re-projecting candidates so that a smaller or nearby discretization can expose a lower exact bound.

The exact returned value of every returned vector is re-computed with exactly

`max(np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full")) * (2 / len(h))`.

There are two main aspects:

- A key observation is that, if exactly two scalar samples `h_p` and `h_q` are changed while their sum remains fixed, every one-sided overlap `P_k(h)` along this line is a convex quadratic or linear function of the single active scalar. The maximum of these quadratic functions is therefore convex on the allowable interval, allowing a reliable, exact, restartable one-dimensional local minimizer. This is much stronger than a random coordinate line search because the line-search objective exactly matches the task metric and cannot move out of feasibility.
- The parent construction may be close to a local extremum of a nonconvex problem. Local nonconvex optimization may plateau when a barely-below-maximum lag becomes limiting but is omitted from the active-set model. Hence active constraints are continually re-expanded using the exact full profile, not just selected from the initial optimality set.

The method is not asserted to be globally optimal. It is designed as a nonconvex heuristic/multi-start framework with rigorous feasibility and exact acceptance checks.

1.2 Roles of each component

- Pairwise minimax-raising correction: an exact local operator on pairs of points. It can preserve the mass exactly, does not need repair, and is guaranteed to improve the exact score unless no proposed pair segment can improve the exact maximum.
- Active-set SQP/validation: A more global step. It can explore simultaneous movement of many variables required by the current limiting lags.
- Dimension transfer and testing: The current parent resolution is retained as a fallback, but carefully selected nearby dimensions `max(24, n-14)` to `n+16` will be tested. The exact objective accounts for different `dx` values directly, so nondimension comparison is meaningful.

1.3 Known facts versus hypotheses

- Fact: After creating any candidate with the scalar-pair exact minimization, the candidate can be accepted only after recomputing the official maximum exact shape.
- Critically, the problem is nonconvex across `h`; only one-dimensional reductions are convex, and there is no claim of global optimality in the multidimensional domain.
- Fact: Both the finite-correlation objective and the sum constraint use a closed triangular support; for a certain lag, only `n - |lag|` sample pairs cannot be replaced without subdivision or extension of dominating terms / generalized claims.
