1.1 Central idea

``C_k(h) = dx * sum_i h_i(1-h_{i+k})`` under exact box and mass constraints. The plan attacks this bottleneck by optimizing a smooth approximation of the maximum, but never making that approximation the final answer. At every current feasible vector, a log-sum-exp (LSE) smoothing assigns dual-like weights to all lag correlations; these weights form an additive objective whose gradient indicates a zero-sum feasible direction that reduces the smoothed maximum while preserving the exact mass constraint.
