\[

A pure smoothing or random-promotion stage can become inefficient because it does not explicitly seek a feasible direction that decreases the entire active set of near-maximal lags.

The morally different primary method proposed here is an exact-active linearized descent method. At a feasible point, compute exact correlation gradients for the lags that are currently at or very close to the maximum. Then take an exact line search along d, modify a candidate with the original evaluator, and accept only if the true non-smooth C5 value strictly decreases. If no strict linearized common descent exists, perturb the point within the feasible box through bounded zero-sum directions and continue from the best feasible vector.
