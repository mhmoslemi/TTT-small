1. Approach and rationale

The central observation is that the discrete objective is a black-box exact min-max quadratic problem

    F(h) = max_{k=0,...,2n-2} dx * corr(h, 1-h, "full")[k],   dx = 2/n,

with only box and integral constraints. The supplied parent exists on an exact symmetric feasible manifold that can be represented by a half-length vector q. If this q representation is used throughout optimization, every point is automatically feasible by construction; constraint repair and projection are not needed. The new method treats the exact evaluator as the sole acceptance criterion. This directly addresses the largest weakness of a local smoothing or active-set program run from one parent: once the program becomes entrained in one continuous basin, local descent cannot escape structural changes such as rearranging q blocks, changing phase or choosing a different resolution grid.

The primary approach is therefore a two-layer search:

  A. Exact-fitness global search in reduced feasible q-space.
     Use a controlled population/statistical-cloud search whose proposals are converted to q, built into h, and evaluated by the exact np.correlate objective. No surrogate value is ever justified as an improvement. This layer is responsible anbined representation-level moves and multiple starting seeds/resolutions.

  B. Exact-accepting active-lag refiner.
     Use log-sum-exp soft-max descent, exact coordinate-rounders in active directions, a small violation-driven epigraph correction and a final fast cooling plateau. All专场 locally modifies q and calls exact acceptance. The q-representation automatically preserves feasibility.

The solution is materially different from the supplied parent refinement because its main improvement narrative is "global exact q-cloud search (at multiple resolutions) + exact/active-lag q-refine" rather than only smoothing or parent local homotopy SLSQP. It is also made substantially more resolution-aware: it tries many smaller and moderately larger even grids, transfers q structurally and compares all candidates through exact real objective. This can exploit meaningful grid effects and is also attractive because the task explicitly encourages smaller sample sizes when they are competitive.

Justified facts:
- The q anti-symmetric construction exactly enforces bounds and integral constraint in exact math.
- The hard objective is max over all full correlation lags; all accept rules call it directly.
- Q-space reduces every even-length problem to 0<=q_i<=1 with no equality constraints.
- Smooth surrogate gradients can provide useful descent but cannot be trusted as objective. Every stored bound comes from the exact evaluator.

Hypotheses and limits:
- Optimal or near-optimal discretized step functions frequently appear near anti-symmetric/structuring in central difference overlap. The q manifold is searched for the primary wire, but not claimed to contain all optima.
- The stochastic/global phase is a heuristic diversity generator and does not prove global optimum.
- Local solvers are nonconvex-manifold experts and may fail due to kinks.
- A smaller n may be chosen only if its exact score is better than current record or within a tiny tolerance. This does not prove smaller bound.
