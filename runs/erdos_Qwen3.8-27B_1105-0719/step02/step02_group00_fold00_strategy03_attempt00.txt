1.1 Central idea

We will optimize the exact discrete evaluator from the supplied feasible parent, but rather than continuing only deterministic parent-neighborhood local epigraph SOP searches, the proposed program is a memetic/random-repair search over the entire finite feasible set. The central primitive is exact diagonal repair followed by differential-evolution-style population updates on the repaired exact-score landscape. Candidate proposals are repaired to satisfy exactly both `0 <= h[i] <= 1` and `sum(h) = n/2`, and every accepted move is validated by the same evaluator that defines the problem. The good candidates discovered by the stochastic phase are then compressed with several exact local methods: active-subgradient descent with sum-zero line searches, a convexized first-order linear program over a trust region, and local epigraph optimization with exact acceptance. The final candidate projected from the supplied parent is evaluated by the task formula and returned only if it is absolutely valid and gives a competitive bound.

This approach is deliberately different from the parent program, which predominantly repeats perturbations of the current continuous parent. The bottleneck it targets is not just unreliable line-search directions but entrapment in one finite sample sequence motif.
