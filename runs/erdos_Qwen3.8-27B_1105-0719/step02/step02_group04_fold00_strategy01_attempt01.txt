1.1 Central idea

The supplied parent uses, internally, smooth minimax surrogates and quasi-Newton BFGS-style updates. That is a legitimate strategy, but it replaces the exact maximum by a smoothed objective and then accepts candidates by checking the exact maximum. The search therefore spends much effort fitting a surrogate, and the exact active constraints can move unpredictably after that surrogate is changed. The algorithm specified here avoids replacing the objective in the optimization loop. It keeps the exact task evaluator at the center and makes progress along directions on which that exact objective, restricted to a one-dimensional feasible segment, is provably convex. Consequently the search is not a smooth-max surrogate, not CVXOPT/BFGS multivariate descent, and not a stochastic heuristic-only patch; it is exact objective-aware pattern/coordinate descent using a special family of feasible line searches, wrapped by a randomized diversification stage and by resolution/work-pattern variations.

The key feasible directions are pairwise balanced transfers: increase h at one sample, and decrease h at another by the same amount.
