1. Approach and rationale

The authoritative objective is the exact finite-support evaluator:
for a length \(n\) vector \(h\), with \(dx=2/n\),
\[
\mathrm{score}(h)=dx\max_j \operatorname{Corr}(h,1-h)_j,
\]
where \(\operatorname{Corr}(a,b)=\texttt{np.correlate(a,b,mode="full")}\). The previous parent score is near a local plateau. Classical smooth-max SLSQP and random binary swaps move in the same local neighborhood; the plan below changes the search geometry.

Core algorithm: exact stochastic zero-sum line minimax descent (ESZS-DR).

For any zero-sum direction vector \(d\) and scalar line parameter \(\theta\),
\[
h(\theta)=h+\theta d
\]
remains feasible if the box constraints are respected. The full correlation vector along such a line is not merely approximated: because correlation is bilinear,
\[
\operatorname{Corr}(h+\theta d,1-h-\theta d)
= c_0+\theta L-\theta^2 Q
\]
with
\[
c_0=\operatorname{Corr}(h,1-h),\quad
L=\operatorname{Corr}(d,1-h)-\operatorname{Corr}(h,d),\quad
Q=\operatorname{Corr}(d,d).
\]
Thus each line trial is an exact evaluation of all original evaluator outputs as a one-dimensional minimax of quadratic components. For a given direction, the proposed step is the best scalar \(\theta\) found among a rich critical and sampled candidate set: bounds, grid samples, individual quadratic minima, and pairwise branch intersections. Candidate proposals are then applied, restored for numerical invalidity, and independently validated with the true evaluator. Only exactly validated candidates update the best structure.

This is materially different from earlier reported plans:
- It does not globally minimize the whole vector via smooth maximum substitution or SLSQP smoothing.
- It does not restrict mountain crossing to independent binary swaps (one coordinate from 1 to 0 and another from 0 to 1). Directions may be blocks, profiles, randomized patterns, historical successful changes, and multisite redistribution patterns, with an exact amplitude chosen along the entire path.
- The objective along the chosen line is treated exactly through the original correlation operators; candidate decisions are refined by re-evaluation with the precise evaluator.
