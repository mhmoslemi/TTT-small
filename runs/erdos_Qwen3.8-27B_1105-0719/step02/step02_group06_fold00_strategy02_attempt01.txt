1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central direction

The previous workflow primarily minimized a smoothed maximum approximation of the correlation objective and solved nonlinear epigraph subproblems. That is a valid local-search strategy, but the remaining improvement from roughly 0.380938 toward the target 0.3808 is very small, and in a highly constrained nonconvex problem a smoothed surrogate can replace the actual objective's exact tie-breaking behavior.

The proposed materially different approach is:

1. Search directly on the symmetric half-vector representation, which preserves feasibility exactly by construction.
2. Use an exact accept/reject loop based only on the original evaluator's maximum over all full-correlation lags.
3. Improve vectors by repeated one-coordinate direct improvement sweeps. For each coordinate and a local trust interval, evaluate the complete objective at several carefully selected probe points, accept only improvements on the exact objective, and use an intermediate affine-model subproblem only to generate promising probe locations; the affine model is never used for acceptance.
4. Run the coordinate driver on a pool constructed from:
   - the supplied parent construction,
   - deterministic/structured symmetric binary and run-length balanced vectors,
   - random symmetric vectors,
   - transfers of the current best vector to other even resolutions, especially progressively shorter sequences, because the returned evaluator accepts any feasible discrete length and smaller length is preferred when tied.
5. At late stages shrink coordinate trust intervals and increase the number of active lags inspected, enabling convergence behavior close to exact local minimization of the max-over-lags objective.

The bottleneck targeted is the mismatch between a smoothed or penalized surrogate and the exact tie-sensitive C5 objective. At the parent construction, the observed bound is special because binary discrete sequences at half-length about 63 naturally produce values near 24/63 = 0.380952. The target is only roughly 0.00015 below this. To get below it, the final vector likely requires carefully tuned continuous mixing between near-integer active-lag violations and exact ties. A coordinate direct exact driver is adversarial to such exact-tie structure: it attempts to reduce the maximum correlation at the current selected lags by moving one coordinate or combinations of coordinates and then verifies on the complete 2n-1 lags.

1.2 Why it is different, not cosmetic

The earlier strategy heavily used:

- exponential smoothing beta continuation,
- nonlinear epigraph constraints in SciPy SLSQP,
- a reduced epigraph symmetric objective,
- full-variable epigraph full constraints,
- multi-resolution transfers only as secondary moves.

The new core is not a renamed epigraph approach. It instead:

- minimizes the complete exact objective rather than a scalar epi-variable plus smooth approximation;
- treats coordinate improvements as exact value-search problems with safety by full re-evaluation;
- drives the candidate pool away from a single polished parent and into structured binary families and shorter resolutions;
- handles each active lag through independent exact trials rather than relying on nonlinear KKT-style convergence alone.

1.3 Important hypotheses and limitations

- The objective is nonconvex. This is a heuristic result-producer, not an exact optimizer. No global optimality claim is made.
- Coordinate-only descent can stall. The strategy mitigates this through multiple starting families, resolutions, rank-order randomization, shrinking and occasional widened intervals, and acceptance tied to the exact objective only.
- One-lag violation can become an inactive-lag hidden violation after accept; the algorithm explicitly guards against that because every accepted candidate is evaluated on all 2n-1 correlations.
- At some lengths, a better exact number of levels is discrete, so the continuous tuning in an adjacent larger length may be easier. Therefore cross-resolution feeds include shorter vectors, but they are accepted only through the value measured by the same exact evaluator.

Fact checks used here:

- The authoritative objective is exactly `np.max(np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full")) * dx` with `dx = 2 / len(h)`, over all 2n-1 lags, including both positive and negative offsets.
- We do not circular-wrap or replace the objective by autocorrelation alone.
- All final candidates are re-evaluated using this metric.
