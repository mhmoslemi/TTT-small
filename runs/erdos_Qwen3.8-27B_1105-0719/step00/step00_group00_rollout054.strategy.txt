1.1 Central idea  
Because the authoritative evaluation is a small-scale, exact, finite-support correlation maximum, any valid discrete vector `h` with a lower score immediately proves an upper bound for the requested discrete model. The broader mathematical optimum is not certified. The main search must therefore be: preserve exact feasibility, evaluate only the task objective for acceptance, and use a scalar surrogate only as a guide for expensive local optimization. The proposed primary mechanism is a constrained reduced-coordinate smooth-minimax optimizer. It optimizes the entire left/right correlation profile jointly through a Log-Sum-Exponential smooth-mass surrogate and gradient information, while the final objective remains the original `max(np.correlate(h,1-h,mode="full"))*dx`. With an equality-preserving parameterization, variables are removed analytically and one remaining dependent coordinate is written by formula, so clipping and rescaling are not used as a joint feasibility mechanism.
