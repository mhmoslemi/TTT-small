### 2. Mathematical specification

$$C_5 = \max_{k} \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n - |k|} h[i] (1 - h[i + |k|]) \right) \times \frac{2}{n}$$
where `k` is the shift index ranging from `-(n-1)` to `n-1`.

The objective is evaluated using `np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode='full')` multiplied by `dx = 2.0 / n`.

#### Constraints
1. Element-wise bounds: `h[i] ∈ [0, 1]`
2. Integral constraint: `∑ h[i] = n / 2`

#### Surrogate model

- Mean function: `μ(h) = constant`
- Kernel: Squared Exponential (SE) kernel with automatic relevance determination (ARD):
  $$k(h_i, h_j) = \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2} (h_i - h_j)^T \Lambda^{-1} (h_i - h_j)\right)$$
  
The GP is trained on pairs `(h, C5)` from evaluated candidates to predict `C5` for new candidates.
