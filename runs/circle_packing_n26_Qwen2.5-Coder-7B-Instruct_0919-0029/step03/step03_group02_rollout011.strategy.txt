### 2. Problem encoding

**Decision vector**  
\(z = (x_1,…,x_{26}, y_1,…,y_{26}, r_1,…,r_{26}) ∈ ℝ^{78}\).

**Objective** – Maximise the sum of radii → Minimise `-Σ r_i`.

**Constraints** – All expressed in the form `c(z) ≤ 0` for the `trust-constr` solver.

| Type | Equation | Formulation in `c(z)` |
|------|----------|------------------------|
| Non‑negative radii | `r_i ≥ 0` | `-r_i ≤ 0` |
