Unlike the parent program's softmax-based gradient estimation, this approach uses polynomial approximations to create differentiable surrogate functions. The core innovation is replacing heuristic coefficient updates with mathematically grounded methods like Tikhonov regularization and recursive polynomial filters. This addresses gradient estimation bottlenecks by providing explicit control over optimization paths, rather than relying on data-parallel exploration.
