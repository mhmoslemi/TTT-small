 Simulation Annealing (SA) is a probabilistic technique for approximating the global minimum of a given cost function. It's particularly useful for large state spaces and complex energy landscapes.

    - Start with a high temperature and gradually reduce it until the process cools down.

    - Metropolis criterion: accept a new state if the new objective is lower than the current one.
