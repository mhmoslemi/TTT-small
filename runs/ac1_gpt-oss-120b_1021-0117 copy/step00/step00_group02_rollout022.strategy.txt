Approach and rationale
The central idea is to exploit the observation that distributing non-zero coefficients in a parent sequence reduces its AC1 upper bound. The previous implementation used local perturbations, but this approach requires initialization with a highly effective sequence. The ratio 2*n*MK / S² must be minimized, where MK is the maximum convolution coefficient and S is the coefficient sum. We refocus on second-order perturbations and piecewise linear interpolation, using local minima in sparse matrix-free convex & concave separators.
