1.1. Approach and Rationale
We propose a novel variant of stochastic gradient descent (SGD) tailored for high-dimensional constrained optimization on the simplex. Unlike the previous approach which used temperature-based softmax weighting, this method incorporates normalized gradient weighting with automatic gradient sparsification. The core idea is to:
- Project candidates onto the simplex via Euclidean projection to ensure sum=1
- Compute exact gradients using FFT to avoid stochastic components
- Apply normalized gradient descent with adaptive learning rates
- Maintain two distinct simplex states (parent and child) to ensure exploration
- Employ rigorous early termination conditions
- Use exact score evaluation only for candidates exceeding a confidence threshold

The primary advantages are:
- Computational efficiency through FFT-based operations
- Guaranteed simplex preservation via explicit projection
- Enhanced geometric progression targeting discrete extrema
- Robust escape from saddle points through coordinate restarts
- Exact score-based selection rather than surrogate optimization

- $a \in ℝ^{n}$ nonnegative vector with $\sum a_i = \√{2n}$
