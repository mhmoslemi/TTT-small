Approach and rationale
The central idea is to minimize the AC1 upper bound for a non-negative step function by optimizing its coefficients through gradient-based trust-region optimization with sparse updates. The core bottleneck addressed is the expensive convolution evaluation in autocorrelation bounds. We combine several techniques:
- Prioritization heuristic: Directly use the parent sequence (`height_sequence_1`) as the primary starting point
- Multi-scale curriculum: Begin with coarse updates (large steps) and gradually move to finer optimization
- Simplex projection: Maintain the simplex constraint (sum=1) while exploring the space
- Trust-region control: Use adaptive scaling to prevent divergence on complex topographies
- Hybrid gradient approach: Derive surrogates for convolution bounds without explicit FFT computations initially, then switch to direct calls when needed
- Progressive cooling: Exponentially reduce temperature for smoothness decay over iterations
- Memory management: Explicitly store only the top performing candidates and re-evaluate only promising ones

The method avoids derivative-free exploration and ensures every candidate passes the evaluator's hard constraints, unlike previous strategies that used extended properties. Progress stalls are handled systematically.

Mathematical specification
Let $\mathbf{a} \in \mathbb{R}^+$ be the coefficient vector with:
- $\sum a_j = 1$ (simplex constraint)
- $0 \leq a_j \leq 1000$ (evaluator constraints)

Objective: $\min_\mathbf{a} \frac{2n \cdot \max_k (\text{convolve}(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{a})_k)}{\sum a_j^2}$

Surrogate bound: Smoothed maximum $\tilde{m} = \frac{\sum w_k \cdot (\text{convolve}(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{a})_k)}{\sum w_k}$ where weights $w_k = \exp((c_k - \text{convolve}(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{a})_{\text{max}})/T$

The gradient $\nabla \tilde{m}$ is approximated using central differences:
- $g_j = \frac{\tilde{s}(a_j + \epsilon) - \tilde{s}(a_j - \epsilon)}{2\epsilon}$ where $\epsilon = 10^{-4}$

But $\mathbf{a}$ being on simplex, we compute:
- $\nabla_\mathbf{a} \text{convolve}(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{a})_j \approx \mathbf{e}_j \star \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{a} \star \mathbf{e}_j$ (cross-channel convolution)

  - Smoothness temperature: $T_0 = \text{adaptative}(s) = \max(1, \text{clip}(T_0, 0.01, 10.0))$
