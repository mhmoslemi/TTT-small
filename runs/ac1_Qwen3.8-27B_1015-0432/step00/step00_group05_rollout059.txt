Actually, I just realized: for a peak at position k, the contribution a[k-j] for j=0..n-1 is just a reversed slice of a. Specifically:

- If k >= n: h[k-n+1:n] += a[n-1:k-n] (a[n-1], a[n-2], ..., a[k-n+1])

Let me verify: for k < n, j ranges from 0 to k (since k-j >= 0 requires j <= k, and k-j < n is always true).
