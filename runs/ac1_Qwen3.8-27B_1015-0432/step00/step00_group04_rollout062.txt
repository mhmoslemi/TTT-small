Actually, the steepest descent direction on the simplex for minimizing `f(a)` is the projection of `-∇f(a)` onto the tangent space `{v: sum v = 0}`, then we move `a ← a - lr * d` and reproject onto the simplex (clip negatives, renormalize).

4. Clip: `a_new = max(a_new, 0)`
5. Renormalize: `a_new = a_new / sum(a_new)`
