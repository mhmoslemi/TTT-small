Let me think about what the "state of the art" LP approach does. It:
1. Normalizes f so sum(f) = sqrt(2n)
2. Sets M = max(f*f)
3. Solves: max sum(g) s.t. g ≥ 0, (f*g)[k] ≤ M for all k
4. Rescales g to have sum = sqrt(2n)
5. Updates f ← (1-t)f + tg
