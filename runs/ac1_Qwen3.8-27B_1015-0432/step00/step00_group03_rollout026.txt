Let me try a different approach: instead of gradient, use a "repulsion" from the current peak:
- At each step, identify the peak lag k*.
- Reduce f at positions that contribute most to (f*f)_{k*}.
- The contribution of f[j] to (f*f)_{k*} is f[j]*f[k*-j].
- To reduce (f*f)_{k*}, decrease f where f[j]*f[k*-j] is large.
