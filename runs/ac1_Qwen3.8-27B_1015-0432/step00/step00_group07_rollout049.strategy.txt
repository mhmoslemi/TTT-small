Given the objective's scale-invariance, we can fix the sum of coefficients to a constant (e.g., 1) to decorrelate the normalization effect, using the evaluator's actual sum if necessary to avoid bias in gradients. This approach avoids perfectly uniform sequences by using gradient information to guide the search, promoting more frequent changes to coefficients that reduce the max autocorrelation.

The solution involves defining a normalized version of the objective by subtracting the mean and scaling to control the distribution dynamically during the perturbation phase. Initial steps use uniform perturbations to escape local minima, and later switch to gradient-based moves for efficient, targeted improvement.
