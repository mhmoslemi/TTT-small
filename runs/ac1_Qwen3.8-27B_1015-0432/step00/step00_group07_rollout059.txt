**Smoothing approach:**
Instead of max C[k], use a log-sum-exp approximation:

So ∂f/∂a[j] = (2/t) * sum_k p[k] * a[k-j] * I(valid) = (2/t) * (p * a_shifted)[j]

This is a convolution of p (the softmax weights) with a (shifted)! We can compute it with FFT!

Specifically: ∂f/∂a[j] = (2/t) * sum_{k: 0<=k<2n-1, 0<=k-j<n} p[k] * a[k-j]

= (2/t) * correlation(p, a)[j]

This can be computed with FFT in O(n log n)!
