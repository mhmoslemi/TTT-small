Let me also think about: what if I use the subgradient of the "smoothed max" instead of the hard max? The smoothed max is:
M_ε = (1/ε) * log(sum_k exp(ε * conv_k))

This is smooth and its gradient is a convex combination of the subgradients, weighted by softmax. This might give a better search direction.

The gradient of M_ε:
dM_ε/da[j] = (1/ε) * sum_k [exp(ε*conv_k) / sum_l exp(ε*conv_l)] * d(conv_k)/da[j]

This is a weighted average of the per-lag gradients, with weights proportional to exp(ε*conv_k). For small ε, this concentrates on the max lags.
