`sum f(j)*f(n-1-j) = sum cos(2*pi*j/n)*cos(2*pi*(1+j)/n)`.

`cos(A)*cos(B) = (cos(A-B)+cos(A+B))/2`.
`A-B = 2*pi*j/n - 2*pi*(1+j)/n = -2*pi/n`.
`A+B = 2*pi*j/n + 2*pi*(1+j)/n = 2*pi*(2j+1)/n`.

`sum cos(2*pi*(2j+1)/n)` over `j=0..n-1`: this is a geometric series of complex exponentials. If `2` is coprime to `n`, this sums to 0. If not, it might not.
