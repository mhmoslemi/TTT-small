Actually, for coordinate i:
Σ_k p_k · 2·a(k-i)·1_{0≤k-i≤n-1}

This is like a weighted sum of a, where the weights depend on k. For each i, we sum over k where 0≤k-i≤n-1, i.e., i≤k≤i+n-1.
