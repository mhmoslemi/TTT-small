   - **Variables**: \(a \in \mathbb{R}^n\), non-negative vector with element-wise bounds \([0, 1000]\).
   - **Objective**: \(f(a) = \frac{2n \cdot \max_t |\sum_{i} a_i a_{t-i}|}{(\sum a_i)^2}\), minimized over \(a \in \mathbb{R}^n\):
     - \(t\) ranges over \([0, 2n-2]\) for full convolution.
     - Defensiveness: clipping elements to \([0, 1000]\) and ensuring \(\sum a_i \geq 0.01\) via projection.
   - **Convolution Definition**: Linear convolution via zero-padding (\(2n-1\) points), not circular.
   - **Smoothed Objective**: 
     \[
     f_\epsilon(a) = \frac{2n}{(\sum a_i)^{2}} \max_{\tau} \sum_i |a_i \cdot a_{\tau-i}|^{\epsilon}
     \]
     with \(\epsilon \in (0, 1)\) controlling smoothness.
   - **Optimization Type**: Projected gradient descent with RPROP momentum.

3. **Implementation Procedure**

               score_val = evaluate(candidate)
               if score_val < best_score:
