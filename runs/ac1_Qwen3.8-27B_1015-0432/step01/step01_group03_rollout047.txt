3. Use the LP-inspired update: at each step, find the direction g that reduces the peak while maintaining nonnegativity.

For the peeling step: if c_k is at the max, we want to reduce it. The gradient of c_k w.r.t. a_i is a_{k-i} (for valid indices). So to reduce c_k, we should decrease a in the direction of the gradient of c_k.

A practical approach: 
- Compute c = convolve(a, a)
- Find indices where c_k > (1-eps) * max(c)
- For each such k, compute the gradient of c_k w.r.t. a
- Take a small step in the negative gradient direction (projected to maintain a >= 0)

This is essentially a subgradient method for the max function.
