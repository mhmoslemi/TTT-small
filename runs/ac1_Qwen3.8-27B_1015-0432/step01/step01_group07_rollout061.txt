A better approach might be to use a "smoothed" objective. Instead of max, use a soft-max:
`soft_max(T, conv) = (1/T) * log(sum_k exp(T * conv[k]))`

As T → ∞, this approaches max. The gradient is:
`d soft_max / d a_i = sum_k [exp(T*conv[k]) / sum_j exp(T*conv[j])] * a[k-i]`
`= (W * a)[i]` where W is the softmax weights and * denotes cross-correlation

This can be computed with FFT: `W_reversed = W[::-1]`, then `cross_corr = ifft(fft(a) * conj(fft(W_reversed_padded)))`... actually it's just a convolution.
