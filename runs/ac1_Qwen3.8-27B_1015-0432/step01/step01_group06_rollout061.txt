- Given f, find the g that maximizes sum(g) subject to ||f*g||_inf <= M where M = ||f*f||_inf.

But implementing this LP efficiently for n in the thousands is challenging. The constraint ||f*g||_inf <= M means (f*g)[k] <= M for all k=0..2n-2, which is a system of O(n) linear inequalities in O(n) variables (g).
