The central idea is to optimize a non-negative step-function sequence to minimize the AC1 upper bound, which is proportional to the maximum value of the autocorrelation divided by the square of the sequence sum. We use a projected gradient method initialized with a high-quality starting sequence (`height_sequence_1`). The key innovations are:

This approach addresses the previous implementation's bottlenecks by avoiding dense matrices and expensive direct convolutions. The algorithm is guaranteed to remain within the storage and time constraints, operating on fixed-size vectors with controller budget optimization.
