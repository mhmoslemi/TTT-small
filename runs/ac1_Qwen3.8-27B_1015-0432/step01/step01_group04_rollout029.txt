Another idea: **subspace methods**. The max of c is at some lag k*. The gradient at that lag is `a[k*-j] + a[j-k*]`. This is a "shifted" version of a. If I only decrease the components that contribute to the max lag, I reduce the max while minimally affecting other lags.
