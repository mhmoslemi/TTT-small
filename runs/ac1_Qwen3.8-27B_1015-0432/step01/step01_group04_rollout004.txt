d score/d a[j] = 2n * (g[j]*S - 2*M*a[j]) / S^3

where g[j] = dM/da[j] = 2*a[k-j] (with bounds).

Now for the y-space gradient (where a = softmax(y)):
da[j]/dy[i] = a[j]*(δ_{ji} - a[i])

grad_y[i] = sum_j d score/d a[j] * da[j]/dy[i]
= sum_j d score/d a[j] * a[j]*(δ_{ji} - a[i])
= d score/d a[i]*a[i] - a[i]*sum_j d score/d a[j]*a[j]

So: grad_y = (grad_a * a) - a * dot(grad_a, a)

This is O(n).
