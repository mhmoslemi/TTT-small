Now, the question is whether `g = 2.0 * cv[n-1:2n-1][::-1]` correctly computes ∂m/∂a.

So g[i] = 2 * cv[2n-2-i] for i=0,...,n-1

cv[2n-2-i] = sum_j a[j] * p[2n-2-(2n-2-i)+j] = sum_j a[j] * p[i+j]

So g[i] = 2 * sum_j a[j] * p[i+j]
