The objective is to minimize `max(b)` subject to `sum(f) = 1, f >= 0`.

where project is: clip to [0, ∞) and normalize to sum 1.

But the multiplicative update (mirror descent with entropy) is often better:
f_{t+1} = f_t * exp(-eta_t * g_t) / (f_t * exp(-eta_t * g_t)).sum()

This automatically handles the nonnegativity constraint.

For the step size, we can use:
- eta_t = c / sqrt(t) (standard)
- eta_t = c / t (faster but may oscillate)
- Backtracking: reduce eta until the score decreases
