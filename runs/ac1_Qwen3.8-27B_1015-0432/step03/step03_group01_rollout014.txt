The condition max(a*a) = M means ||A||_∞^2 = M (in the continuous frequency domain, for the appropriate normalization).

Actually, the full convolution a*a corresponds to the product A(ω)^2 in the frequency domain (not |A(ω)|^2). So max(a*a) is related to the L∞ norm of A(ω)^2.
