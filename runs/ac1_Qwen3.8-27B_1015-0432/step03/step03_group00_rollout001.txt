Let me think about this problem from a different angle. The key quantity is:
`R = 2n * max_j b_j / S²`

We want to minimize R. Since `sum_j b_j = S²` (over all 2n-1 lags), the average of b_j is `S²/(2n-1)`. If the b_j were all equal, `max_b = S²/(2n-1)` and `R = 2n/(2n-1) ≈ 1`. But achieving equal b_j is likely impossible with nonnegative a.

The minimum of `max_j b_j` subject to `sum_j b_j = S²`, `a >= 0` is at least `S²/(2n-1)` (by the pigeonhole principle applied to the sum). But the constraint `a >= 0` and the specific structure of the convolution makes this tighter.
