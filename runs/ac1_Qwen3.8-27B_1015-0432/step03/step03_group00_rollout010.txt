The constant shift is uniform, so it doesn't matter when projected to the sum constraint. The "useful" gradient is 2 * a[k*-i], which means we should decrease a[i] proportional to a[k*-i].

A simple projected gradient step:
1. Compute k* = argmax(conv(a,a))
2. Compute g[i] = a[k*-i] for valid i, 0 otherwise
3. Subtract the mean: g[i] -= mean(g) (to preserve sum)
4. a[i] <- max(0, a[i] - eta * g[i])
5. Renormalize to sum = sc
