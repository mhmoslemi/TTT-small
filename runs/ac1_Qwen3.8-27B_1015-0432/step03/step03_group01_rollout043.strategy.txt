Approach and rationale
We develop a parameterized step-function using block-pulse functions, where the optimization process systematically adjusts the block positions and widths to minimize the AC1 upper bound. This approach is inspired by the fact that minimizers often exhibit bimodal or block-like structures. The method starts with an initial guess from a provided parent sequence and then iteratively refines candidate sequences through a convex optimization phase. This approach aims to directly search for near-optimal structures by leveraging mathematical insights about minimizers.

Mathematical specification
The sequence is represented as a step-function with fixed amplitude and varying block positions/widths. Let $a_i$ be the sequence elements. The objective is:
$$ \text{AC1} = \frac{2n \cdot \|a * a\|_{\infty}}{\ (\sum a_i)^2} $$
where $\|a * a\|_{\infty}$ is the maximum value in the full convolution of $a$ with itself.

Our representation uses piecewise constant blocks. The parameters are:
- Block values $A = [A_1, A_2, \dots, A_m]$

- Block end positions $E = [E_1, E_2, \dots, E_m]$
