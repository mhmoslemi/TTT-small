- At lag n-1: essentially 0 (if σ << n)
- The max is at lag 0

To minimize the peak, we want sum(a_j^2) to be as small as possible, which means a should be as flat as possible. But if a is too flat, the autocorrelation at intermediate lags becomes large.

The optimal trade-off is achieved when the autocorrelation is as flat as possible. For a "flat-top" window (like the previous program's `make_flat_top`), the sidelobes of the autocorrelation are low, so the peak at lag 0 dominates.
