For the "soft max" approximation `G_β(a) = (1/β) log sum exp(β*b[k])`, the gradient is `sum_k softmax(β*b[k]) * ∇b[k]`.

The previous code uses `w = exp(β*(b/M - 1))` which is proportional to `softmax(β*b)` (since `softmax(β*b)[k] = exp(β*b[k]) / sum exp(β*b[j])` and dividing by `exp(β*M)` gives `exp(β*(b[k]-M))`).

So the gradient is a weighted average of `∇b[k]` over all lags k, with weights concentrated on the max lag(s) for large β.

This is correct. The question is whether the step size and β schedule are optimal.
