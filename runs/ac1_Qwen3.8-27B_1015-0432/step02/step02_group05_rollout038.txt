In continuous space, a uniform distribution on [0, 1] has triangular autocorrelation. To get flat autocorrelation, we need something like a "uniform on a group" distribution, but our space is discrete.

For the discrete case, the optimal is related to "perfect difference sets" or "Singer difference sets". A Singer difference set in $\mathbb{Z}_v$ (v prime power) has the property that every nonzero element is a difference of exactly $\lambda$ pairs. This gives perfectly flat autocorrelation.
