The approach focuses on optimizing the sequence coefficients to minimize the AC1 upper bound using a combination of gradient-based search and stochastic perturbations. The key idea is to leverage the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) for efficient convolution calculations and gradient estimation, ensuring the search remains computationally feasible for large sequences. The method includes restarts with stochastic perturbations to explore new regions and avoid local minima, while the gradient descent updates locally refine the solution within promising areas.

- **Objective**: Minimize \( \text{AC1}(a) = 2n \cdot \max_{\text{convolve}(a, a) } / \sum_{i} a_i^2 \).
- **Convolution**: Computed using FFT to ensure \( O(n \log n) \) complexity per operation.
- **Gradient estimation**: Uses a smooth surrogate gradient or spiking gradient to navigate the optimization landscape.
- **Constraints**: Each \( a_i \in [0, 1000] \).
