### **1. Understanding the Problem and Algorithm**
The problem is to minimize the overlap integral:
$$
C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1 - h(x+k)) dx

The function $ h $ is represented as a step function over $ n $ points evenly spaced on [0, 2], with $ dx = 2 / n $, and the integral constraint is maintained via normalization.
