### **1. Mathematical Perspective**

The problem is fundamentally a **minimax optimization problem**, where we seek a function h(x) ∈ [0, 1] with ∫₀² h(x) dx = 1 that **minimizes** the maximum overlap across shifts k in [0, 2]. This is equivalent to:

$$
C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1 - h(x + k)) dx
$$

This can be rewritten using the convolution of h with (1 - h), as we have for the overlap integral.

Key observations:
- The functional depends on overlaps between h at different lags.
- This is a **nonlinear** and **non-convex** optimization problem due to the integral and the constraint on ∫h(x) dx.
- We're trying to **minimize the worst-case overlap over known lags**. This is a **robust optimization** problem.
