### 1. Approach and Rationale

The goal is to find a step function h that minimizes the maximum overlap with its shifted complement over all shifts. We are given a parent solution (n=70 points) achieving C=0.38085. Our approach involves:

- **Piecewise Constant Approximation**: Represent h as a piecewise constant function with efficient knot selection to reduce verification time.
- **Conservative Step Function**: Limit the number of active intervals to ensure quick validation of 0 <= h(x) <= 1 and sum constraints.
- **Enhanced Random Initialization**: Use strategic seeding to explore diverse solutions while maintaining structural integrity.
- **Binary-Int Linear Encoding**: Translate the piecewise nature of h into a compact linear encoding suitable for integer programming.

This approach prioritizes mathematical robustness and optimality checks to ensure delivered solutions meet problem constraints and outperform the parent.

### 2. Mathematical Specification

**Objective Function**:
Minimize \(C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1-h(x+k)) \,dx\)  
discretized over [0,2] with samples:

- \(dx = 2.0 / n\_points\)
- \(h = (h_0, h_1, ..., h_{n_points-1})\) where \(0 ≤ h_i ≤ 1\) and \(\sum h_i \cdot dx = 1\)

**Constraints**:
- \(0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1\) for all \(i\)
- \(\sum h[i] \cdot dx = 1\)

**Variable Transformation**:
- Transform active intervals (knots) from a binary integer vector \(s = (s_0, s_1, ..., s_{m-1})\) where each \(s_j\) is a sample interval indicator.
- Solve for \(h\) using dot products ensuring each interval has a constant value.

**Optimality Check**:
- Validate \(0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1\) via projection onto constraints.
- Sum constraint enforced through normalization.
