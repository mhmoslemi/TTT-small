1. Approach and Rationale
Convex Underestimation: The core insight is to transform the non-convex optimization problem into a sequence of convex subproblems. The Erdős minimum overlap constant problem is inherently non-convex due to the product terms in the correlation objective and box constraints. However, we can use a piecewise-linear convex underestimator based on the perspective formulation. This approach leverages the fact that the maximum correlation can be bounded by a convex function, and employs a branch-and-bound algorithm combined with active learning to search the domain efficiently.

The rationale is to overcome the limitations of local optimization methods and symmetrization by providing a rigorous global search framework. We treat the non-negative step function as a convex transformation of its active set, which allows us to decompose the problem into convex subproblems and apply efficient constraint projection. This multi-start method ensures exploration across different realizations of the step function spectrum.
