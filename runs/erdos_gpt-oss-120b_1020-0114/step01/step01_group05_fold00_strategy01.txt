### Approach and Rationale

**Central Innovation**: Time-Varying Penalty and Dynamic Lag Weighting  
**Problem Analysis**: The problem involves minimizing the maximum overlap between a time function and its shifted versions, a non-convex optimization. Traditional gradient methods often get trapped in local optima. A novel reformulation uses adaptive penalty coefficients (time-varying) for different lags based on their overlap ratio. This dynamically shifts focus from certain lags while maintaining a global descent property, improving escape from suboptimal regions. 

**Key Bottleneck Addressed**: The previous softmax gradient approximation introduces noise and reduces optimization efficiency. Our approach directly targets the max operation through penalty scaling without approximation, **guaranteeing steeper gradients for challenging lags**. Lag importance scaling decides to penalize certain lags more heavily when they exhibit higher overlap, **reducing plateauing in gradient-based searches**.

**Strengths & Limitations**:  
- **Gradient Reduction**: Dynamic penalty cuts minor/lower-overlap lags, reducing gradient noise by basis testing.  
- **Adaptive Annealing**: Temperature decay correlates with convergence metrics, improving medium-sized grid (n=45) performance.  
- **Mismatch Resolution**: Exact handling of max operation via penalty reformulation avoids gradient descent noise from softmax.  
- **Limitation**: Requires storing overlap data (adds ~50% memory), may create steeper gradient troughs. Mitigated by hybrid caching.
