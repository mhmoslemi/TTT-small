1. **Approach and Rationale**  
   The Erdős minimum overlap problem aims to minimize the maximum overlap between a step function and its complement over all translations (lags). The challenge is non-convex and high-dimensional, and traditional local search methods may get stuck in suboptimal regions.  
   This strategy employs a **Burer-Monteiro approach** to transform the problem into a low-dimensional space by finding correlations between variables, then uses **subgradient descent** in this space to minimize the overlap.  
   Critical bottlenecks addressed:  
   - **Explicit subgradient calculation**: Computes directional derivatives efficiently to drive the search toward optimal candidates.  
   - **Constraint projection**: Uses linear programming (via CVXPY + Gurobi) to enforce sum and bound constraints without heuristic rounding.  
   - **Adaptive Lag selection**: Focuses on key lags by identifying the worst-case overlap with minimal computation.  

2. **Mathematical Specification**  
   - **Objective**: Minimize \( \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1-h(x+k))  dx \)  
     Discrete proxy: \( \max_{k} \max_{lag} \left( \text{dx} \times \max \left\{ \text{cross\_corr}_\text{mode=full} \right\} \right) \)  
   - **Variables**: \( h_i \in [0,1] \) for \( N \) samples, with \( \sum_{i=1}^N h_i = \frac{N}{2} \).  
   - **Low-Dimensional Embedding**: Tracks dominant correlations across lags.  
   - **Algorithm**: Subgradient descent in projected residual space autoregressive objective (RAR) form.  

3. **Implementation Procedure**  
   - **Input/Output**:  
     - `run(seed, budget_s, **kwargs)` returns `(h_values, c5_bound, n_points)`.  
     - `n_points`: Chosen adaptively (fixed initial value 200, limited evaluation).  
   - **Initialization**:  
     - If `initial_h_values` exists, project it into low-dimensional space via cross-correlation analysis. Otherwise, start from sparse uniform random hits with integral enforcement.  
   - **Core Loop**:  
     - Compute subgradient using cross-correlation entropy.  
     - Update solution with projected gradient steps.  
   - **Repair**: Projection onto feasible set via [CVXPY + Gurobi (LP)] to maintain \( \sum h_i = \frac{N}{2} \) and bounds.  
   - **Termination**: Budget exhaustion, improvement stall, or direct evaluation score < 0.38.
