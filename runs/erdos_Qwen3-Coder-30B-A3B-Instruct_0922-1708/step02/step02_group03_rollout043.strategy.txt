$$ 0 \leq h[i] \leq 1 \quad \forall i $$
$$ \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i] = \frac{n}{2} $$

Where $n$ is the number of points (n_points=100).

$$ \mathcal{L}(h, \lambda, \mu) = C_5 + \lambda \left( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i] - \frac{n}{2} \right) + \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \mu_i h[i] + \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \nu_i (h[i] - 1) $$

We solve the KKT conditions for optimality:
$$ \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial h[j]} = 0 \quad \forall j $$
