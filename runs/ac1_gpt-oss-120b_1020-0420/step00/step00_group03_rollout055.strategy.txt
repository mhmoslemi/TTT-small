Since the AC1 bound is scale-invariant, we can normalize the signal and separate signal identification from structure. We use a spectral unmixing technique to resolve the signal into positive and negative frequency components, then optimize each component separately. The signal is represented as a complex exponential series, and we optimize amplitude and phase to minimize the AC1 bound. Unlike the previous block perturbation approach, this method focuses on global optimization through frequency-based unmixing.

- Input: parent signal of length 2535
- Output: sequence of nonnegative real numbers
- Variables:
  - k: frequency index (1 to 2534)

- Computational steps: 
  1. FFT of parent signal to identify frequency components
