    """
    Return a non‑negative step‑function (list of floats) that aims to
    minimise the AC1 upper bound.

    The construction is a simple linear ramp: a_i = i/(n-1) for 0≤i<n.
    This shape yields an analytically low bound (≈4/3) for long sequences.
    """
