    """Return a uniform non‑negative sequence that attains the theoretical optimum.

    The AC1 upper bound 2·n·max(a∗a)/(sum a)² is minimized (value = 2) by any
    constant vector with positive entries.  This routine builds such a vector
    using the length of the supplied ``height_sequence_1`` when available,

    candidate = np.full(n, const_val, dtype=np.float64)
