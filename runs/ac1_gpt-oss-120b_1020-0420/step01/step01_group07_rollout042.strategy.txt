The central idea is to minimize the AC1 upper bound by optimizing a sequence representation using variational optimization combined with projection-gradient steps. Unlike the parent program's binary mask approach, we directly search in the space of real-valued sequences while leveraging the observation that optimal sequences exhibit logarithmic scaling in their concentrated energy regions. This approach reduces the optimization burden by focusing on energy distribution while automatically handling non-negativity and the coefficient bound (<=1000). The previous best bound (1.574) arises from local-optima trapping and inefficient energy distribution. Our method addresses these by:
- Using log-coordinates for more stable updates
- Selectively updating subsets of coefficients (those affecting active constraints)
- Incorporating multi-scale energy matching
- Adaptively centering updates based on gradient information

The primary limitation is scalability to very large n (thousands) but our method maintains O(n) operations via sparse updates.
