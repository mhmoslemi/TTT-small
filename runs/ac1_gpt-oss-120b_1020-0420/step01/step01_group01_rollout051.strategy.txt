The AC1 upper bound minimization requires sequences that reduce peak autocorrelation while maintaining positive coefficients. This approach generates piecewise constant sequences with optimally placed peaks and varying heights, rather than relying on random perturbations or interval clustering. The key intuition is that sequences exhibiting structured, multi-peak patterns with controlled growth exhibit lower autocorrelation than monotonically increasing or arbitrary sequences. We model the sequence as a product of block segments with carefully designed mutations and optimizations to enforce structure.
