We approach the problem of minimizing the AC1 upper bound by focusing on sequences that are sparse in the convolution domain. Our central idea is that sequences with minimal autocorrelation energy will exhibit a lower AC1 bound. We specifically target sequences that can be represented as a combination of few intervals of constant height. 

The key insight is that maximizing the number of zero-valued intervals while ensuring the convolution maxima are minimized leads to improved performance. We use a parameterized form of a piecewise constant sequence (discrete triangular distribution) which inherently concentrates energy away from the center.
