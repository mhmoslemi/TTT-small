### Completed Strategy

We are to find a step function `h` that minimizes the maximum overlap integral `C_5`. This problem is highly non-convex and requires global optimization, which is challenging given the dimensionality (n_points can be up to 320 or more). 

We use a genetic algorithm (GA) for global search, coupled with repair operators toensure the constraints (0 <= h[i] <= 1 and integral `h`) are satisfied. We also leverage resolution doubling: by increasing the resolution (using linear interpolation) and reating a solution pool for higher resolutions. The GA is designed to explore the search space effectively while maintaining feasible solutions.

### Approach and Rationale

The rans that to achieve a better upper bound for the Erdős constant, we need to move beyond the initial solution and explore higher resolutions. Resolution doubling helps escape local optima by and complex problem. re how to four given the initial parent (at n=80 samples) for optimization.

We follow these folds:
1. **Constraint Handling**: We repair feasibility for each candidate by clipping to [0,1] and then scaling to unit integral.
2. **Ojective Evaluation**: Using `numpy.correlate` with mode "full" and multiplying by the sample spacing (dx) to get the overlap integral.
3. **Global Search**: GA with uniform crossover and mutation for diversity.
4. **Resolution Handling**: After each GA run, double the resolution by and the best solution from the lower resolution, and repeat for higher resolutions within the budget.
5. the best solution found across all resolutions.

- GA provides powerval susceptible to local optima.
