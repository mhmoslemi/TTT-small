In coordinate descent with the sum constraint:
- We fix h[0:i-1], h[i+1:n-1], h[n-1] is determined by sum constraint
- Wait, this means only TWO variables are free at each step (h[i] and h[n-1])

- Need h[n-1] ∈ [0,1], so h[i] ∈ [C-1, C] ∩ [0,1]

For each choice of h[i], we can compute C5 and minimize.
