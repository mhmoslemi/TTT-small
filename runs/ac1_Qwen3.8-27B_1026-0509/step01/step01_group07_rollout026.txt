2. A = FFT(a, n=2n) (zero-pad to length 2n for linear convolution)
3. R = |A|^2 (this is the "periodogram" or "power spectrum")
4. r = IFFT(R) (this gives the circular autocorrelation of the zero-padded sequence, which equals the linear autocorrelation)
5. t = max(r)
6. Modify R to reduce the peak: R_new = R - β * peak_structure

8. a_new = real(IFFT(A_new, n=2n))[:n] (take the first n elements)
9. a_new = max(0, a_new) (project onto nonnegative)
10. a_new = a_new / sum(a_new) (renormalize)
11. a = a_new, go to 2

3. Reconstruct: we need A such that |A|^2 = FFT(r_clipped). But FFT(r_clipped) might have negative values.
4. Use the "phase retrieval" approach: A_new = sqrt(max(0, FFT(r_clipped))) * exp(i*angle(FFT(a, 2n)))
5. a_new = real(IFFT(A_new, 2n))[:n]
6. Project and renormalize.

This is essentially the "FISTA" or "projected gradient" approach in the Fourier domain.
