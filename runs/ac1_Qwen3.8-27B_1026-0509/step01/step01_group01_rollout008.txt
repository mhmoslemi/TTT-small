The full convolution `b = conv(a, a)` where `b[m] = sum_j a[j] * a[m-j]` for valid indices.

The gradient `db[m]/da[i]`:
- `a[i]` appears in `b[m]` as:

  - `a[j] * a[m-j=a[i]]` where `m-j = i`, i.e., `j = m-i` → contribution `a[m-i]` (if `0 <= m-i < n`)
