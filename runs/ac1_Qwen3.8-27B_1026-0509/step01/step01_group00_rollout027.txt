- This is a quadratic form: a^T W a
- Subject to a >= 0, sum a = 1
- Can be solved by projected gradient or by using the KKT conditions

- 2 * W a = mu + nu (where mu >= 0 is the dual of a >= 0, mu_j = 0 when a_j > 0)
- sum a = 1

1. Given w (dual weights on lags), find a such that (W a)_j = const on support(a)
2. Update w to be concentrated on the argmax of (a*a)
