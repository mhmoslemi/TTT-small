The Frank-Wolfe method for min f(x) s.t. x in simplex:
1. Compute subgradient g of f at current x
2. Solve: min_{y in simplex} <g, y> (linear minimization over simplex)
3. Update: x <- x + t*(y - x) for appropriate t

Step 2: min <g, y> s.t. y >= 0, sum y = 1. The solution is y = e_{argmin g} (put all mass on the coordinate with smallest g).

So Frank-Wolfe would be:
1. Compute subgradient g of max(a*a) at a
2. Set y = e_{argmin g}
3. Blend: a <- (1-t)*a + t*e_{argmin g}

This is a very simple update! It just moves mass from high-gradient coordinates to the lowest-gradient coordinate.
