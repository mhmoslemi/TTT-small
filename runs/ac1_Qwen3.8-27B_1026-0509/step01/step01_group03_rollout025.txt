- $\max(b*b)$ could be anything
- $\max(a*b) \leq M = \max(a*a)$

The blend $a' = (1-t)a + tb$ has:
$a'*a' = (1-t)^2 a*a + 2t(1-t) a*b + t^2 b*b$

For small $t$, the change is $\approx 2t(1-t)(a*b - a*a)$ (lower order). Since $\max(a*b) \leq \max(a*a)$, the new max should be $\leq$ the old max (for small $t$). So the blend always improves (or doesn't worsen) for small $t$.

For large $t$, the $b*b$ term dominates, and we need $\max(b*b) \leq M$ for the improvement. This is not guaranteed.

So a safe strategy is to use small $t$ (like 0.1-0.3) and many iterations.
