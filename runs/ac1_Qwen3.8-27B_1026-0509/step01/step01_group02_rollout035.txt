**Alternative: use the convex inner problem**

For a fixed weight vector w (on the simplex over the 2n-1 autocorrelation lags), the problem:
$\min_{x \in \Delta} x^T B x$ where $B = \sum_m w_m A_m$

If B is PSD, this is a convex quadratic program on the simplex, which can be solved exactly. The solution gives a "stationary point" for the minimax problem.

The dual problem is:
$\max_w \min_{x \in \Delta} x^T B(x,w) x$

By alternating:
1. Given w, solve the inner QP to get x*
2. Update w based on the autocorrelation at x* (e.g., w_m ∝ c_m(x*))
