$(a*a)_k = \sum_j a_j \cdot a_{k-j}$ where the valid range of $j$ is $\max(0, k-n+1) \leq j \leq \min(n-1, k)$.

$\frac{\partial (a*a)_k}{\partial a_i}$:

Actually for the full convolution, both $i$ and $k-i$ need to be in $[0, n-1]$ for the term to exist. So the derivative is $2a_{k-i}$ when both are valid, 0 otherwise.

$g_i = 2 a_{k^* - i}$ (if $0 \leq k^* - i < n$), else 0.

This is just a shifted and scaled version of $a$. So the subgradient descent step is:
$a \leftarrow a - \eta \cdot 2 \cdot a[k^* - \cdot]$ (shifted)

Then project to the simplex (sum = 1, $a_j \geq 0$).
