max sum(b) s.t. (a*b)_k ≤ M for all k, b ≥ 0

where M = max(a*a). The optimal b is the "best response" to a.

After the update `a_new = (1-t)*a + t*(b/sum(b))`, the new a should have a lower peak autocorrelation (by the properties of the LP dual).

The convergence of this LP-based method is sublinear (like O(1/sqrt(T)) for multiplicative weights). So it might take many iterations to converge. The L-BFGS should be faster for local optimization.
