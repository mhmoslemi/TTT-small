**1. Approach and rationale**  
We propose a **gradient-based optimization with smooth max approximation**. The goal is to minimize the AC1 upper bound by iteratively updating a sequence using projected gradients. The key insight is to approximate the non-smooth max operation in the autocorrelation by a smooth maximum over a small set of points. This allows gradient descent with efficient updates. The method balances between ensuring non-negativity and the coefficient cap, leveraging the smoothness to avoid expensive subgradient methods.

The previous state-of-the-art used a linear programming approach, but we differentiate by using analytic gradients with smoothing. This reduces computational overhead and avoids the need for solving auxiliary LPs at each step.  

**2. Mathematical specification**  
We minimize the objective function:  
\[ \text{score}(a) = \frac{2n \max_k R_k}{(\sum a_i)^2} \]
