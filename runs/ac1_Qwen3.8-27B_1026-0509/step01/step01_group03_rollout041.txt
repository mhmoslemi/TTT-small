For nonnegative sequences, the best we can do is related to the "additive energy" of the support. If the sequence is an indicator of a set $S \subseteq \{0, ..., n-1\}$, then $(a*a)_k = |S \cap (k-S)|$ (the number of representations of $k$ as a sum of two elements of $S$).
