For the hard max case (k* = argmax):

`= 4n*(a[k*-i]*S - c[k*])/S³` (for valid i)
`= 0` (for invalid i)

`direction[i] = c[k*] - mean` (for invalid i, after centering)

After centering: `direction[i] -= mean(direction)`.
