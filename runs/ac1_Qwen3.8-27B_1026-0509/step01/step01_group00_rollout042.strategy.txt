1. The objective function is non-convex and the space is finite (though high-dimensional and continuous) but bounded.
2. The constraint is non-negativity and boundedness (each element in [0,1000]).
3. The objective function is smooth in the interior.

2. Mathematical Specification
For a sequence `a` of length `n`:
- Compute the full convolution `c` of `a` with itself
- The objective is `score = 2*n * max(c) / (sum(a))^2`
- The gradient for component `i` is approximated by considering the top `p` coefficients (default 100) and computing the subgradient of the max function
- Constraints: `0 <= a_i <= 1000` and `sum(a) >= 0.01`

The optimization uses projected gradients onto the feasible set, with backtracking line search for step size determination.
