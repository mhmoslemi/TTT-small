**Approach: "Anti-correlation" direction.**
Instead of solving the LP, compute the direction d = -∂max_b/∂a (the negative subgradient of max_b). Then project d onto the tangent space of {a >= 0, sum a = 1} and take a step.

(∂/∂a_j) b[k*] = a[j+k*] (if j+k* < n) + a[j-k*] (if j-k* >= 0)

Then: a_new = a - lr * d, project onto {a >= 0, sum a = 1}.

This is the subgradient method, which I discussed earlier. It's O(n) per step (plus O(n log n) for the FFT to compute b).
