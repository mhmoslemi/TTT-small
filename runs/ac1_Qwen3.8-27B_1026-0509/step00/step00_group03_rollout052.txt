A² = 2AB + (n-2)*B²

B*(n + sqrt(n-1)) = 1
B = 1/(n + sqrt(n-1))
A = (1+sqrt(n-1))/(n+sqrt(n-1))

For large n: B ≈ 1/(n + sqrt(n)) ≈ 1/n * (1-1/sqrt(n)), A ≈ 1/sqrt(n) * 1/(1+1/sqrt(n)) ≈ 1/sqrt(n).
