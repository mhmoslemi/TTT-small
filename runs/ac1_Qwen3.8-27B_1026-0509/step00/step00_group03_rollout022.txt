Now, the gradient of F(a) = 2n * max(b) / S^2:
Using the chain rule and the fact that d(max(b))/da is a subgradient:
∂F/∂a[i] = 2n * [(∂max(b)/∂a[i]) * S^2 - max(b) * 2*S * 1] / S^4
