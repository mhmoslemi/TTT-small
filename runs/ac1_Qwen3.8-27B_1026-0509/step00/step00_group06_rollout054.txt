For a sequence with uniformly distributed autocorrelation, the DFT must have constant magnitude (flat power spectrum). For nonnegative sequences, this is very restrictive.

This requires $|\hat{a}(\omega)|$ to be constant, which for a real sequence means specific phase relationships. For a nonnegative real sequence, the DFT has specific properties (the minimum phase constraint is not required, but the sequence being nonnegative is a strong constraint).
