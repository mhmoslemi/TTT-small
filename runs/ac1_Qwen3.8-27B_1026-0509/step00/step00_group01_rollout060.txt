By Parseval: sum_k c[k]² = (1/2π) integral |A(e^{iω})|⁴ dω = (1/2π) integral |A|⁴ dω

And sum_k c[k] = S² (sum of convolution = (sum a)(sum a) = S²)

So the mean of c is S²/(2n-1). We want the peak to be close to the mean, i.e., c to be as flat as possible.

The flat c corresponds to |A|² being a delta (in frequency), which means A is a complex exponential, i.e., a is a complex sinusoid. But we need a to be REAL and NONNEGATIVE.

For a real nonnegative a, A(0) = S > 0, and |A(ω)|² is the periodogram. We want the periodogram to produce a flat inverse-FFT.

This is essentially the problem of designing a signal with a flat power spectrum constrained to be nonnegative in time domain. It's a well-studied problem in signal processing (minimum entropy spectral estimation, all-pass filtering, etc.).
