The problem `min max_k b_k subject to sum a = 1, a >= 0` can be solved by mirror descent with entropy regularization. The update is:
`a_t[i] = a_{t-1}[i] * exp(-eta * grad_i) / Z`

where `grad` is the subgradient of `max_k b_k` (i.e., the gradient of the active `b_{k*}`), and `Z` is a normalizing constant.

This is equivalent to multiplying each `a[i]` by `exp(-eta * 2*a[k*-i])` (with bounds) and renormalizing.

This is a simple O(n) update per step! Let me try this.
