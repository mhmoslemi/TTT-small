2. Use Adam or SGD with the subgradient to optimize θ.

With the softmax parameterization, the gradient is:
∂a_i/∂θ_j = a_i(δ_{ij} - a_j)

And the chain rule gives:
∂f/∂θ_j = sum_i (∂f/∂a_i) * (∂a_i/∂θ_j) = sum_i sg_i * a_i(δ_{ij} - a_j)
= a_j * sg_j - a_j * sum_i sg_i * a_i

Where sg is the subgradient of f w.r.t. a.

This ensures the update preserves the simplex constraint automatically.
