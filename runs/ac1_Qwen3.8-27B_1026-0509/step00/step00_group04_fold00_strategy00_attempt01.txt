Approach and rationale:
We aim to find a nonnegative step-function sequence with length 3809 that minimizes the AC1 upper bound (defined by the evaluated function) by improving upon the parent sequence's score of 2.0. The central idea is to use an alternating optimization method: starting from the given parent sequence, iteratively mix it with specially constructed sequences derived from solving a linear program (LP).
