Simple update:
a[i] = a[i] * (1 - λ * contribution_i)

where contribution_i measures how much a[i] contributes to c[k*].

contribution_i = ∂c[k*]/∂a[i] / c[k*] = (a[k*-i] + a[i]) / c[k*] (approximately)

Then: a[i] ← a[i] * (1 - λ * (a[k*-i] + a[i]) / c[k*])

This is a multiplicative update that reduces the elements contributing most to the peak.
