To improve the upper bound for the Erdős minimum overlap problem constant $ C_5 $, we aim to minimize the overlap integral:

$$
C_5 = \max_k \int h(x)(1 - h(x+k)) dx
$$

under the constraint $ \int_0^2 h(x) dx = 1 $.

The strategy involves:
1. **Starting with a known good initial guess**: We begin with the provided `initial_h_values` which might already be close to a good solution.
2. **Local optimization**: Using a local optimization method like SLSQP with constraints to refine the solution.
3. **Randomized perturbations**: Introduce controlled random perturbations to escape local minima and explore the solution space.
4. **Efficient normalization**: Ensure the integral constraint is maintained at each step.
5. **Adaptive refinement**: Iteratively improve the solution by perturbing and optimizing, ensuring the process stops in time.
6. **Early stopping**: Stop if the time budget is reached to ensure the solution is returned before the deadline.

This approach combines a structured optimization with perturbations to escape local minima and improve the bound effectively.

    def objective(h):
        h_array = np.array(h)

        overlap = np.correlate(h_norm, 1 - h_norm, mode="full") * (2.0 / n_points)
