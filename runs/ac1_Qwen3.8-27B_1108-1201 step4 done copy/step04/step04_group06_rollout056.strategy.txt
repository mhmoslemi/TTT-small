# Approach and Rationale
We are to minimize the evaluation function for a sequence of nonnegative floats: 2*n*max_b/(sum_a)^2, where max_b is the maximum of the linear convolution of the sequence with itself. The previous approach was a hybrid algorithm combining gradient descent and linear programming for sequences of length 768.
