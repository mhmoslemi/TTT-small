### Approach and Rationale
The evaluation function is a ratio:  
- Numerator: `2 * n * max(convolve(a, a))`—high values can be achieved by peaked sequences.  
- Denominator: `sum(a)**2`—this is minimized when the sequence is normalized to sum=1, but peaks can be magnified by reducing the number of peaks (making them sparser).  

We want to balance the number of peaks (creating attack surface) and their positions to lower the objective. The previous work used a uniform fallback and fixed length, but we introduce:  
- **Dynamic peak selection:** We vary the number of peaks (from 8 to 32) and leverage the evaluators normalization to ensure sum>=0.01.  
- **Three-phase approach:**  
  Phase1: Sparse optimization (8-16 peaks)  
  Phase2: Intermediate (16-24 peaks)  
  Phase3: Full-featured (24-32 peaks)  
- **Time budgeting:** Spend ≈600 seconds on sparse peaks, ≈900 on intermediates, and ≈300 on full optimization.  

### Mathematical Specification
- Sequence length: `n = 768` (fixed)  
- Variables: `a_i >= 0` as fractions of total norm (sum=1).  
- Peaks are positioned at: `positions_k = [round(k * 767 /  num_peaks)]` for `k=0,1,...,num_peaks-1`.
