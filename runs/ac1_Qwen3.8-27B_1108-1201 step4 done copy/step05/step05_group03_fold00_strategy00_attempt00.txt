2. **Objective Function**: Minimize \( J = \frac{2 \times n \times \max(\text{convolve}(a, a))}{(\sum a)^2} \) with normalized sequence \( a \).

3. **Convolution**: Full linear convolution of length \(2n-1\).

4. **Optimization Phases**:
   - Adam-SG: Smooth, memetic optimization using temperature-scheduled softmax.
