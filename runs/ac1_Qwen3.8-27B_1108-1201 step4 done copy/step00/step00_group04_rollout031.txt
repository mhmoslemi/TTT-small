**Approach: Subgradient descent on the max convolution**

**Alternative: Smooth approximation**

Instead of the max, use a smooth approximation like the log-sum-exp:
$f(a) = \frac{1}{t} \log \sum_k \exp(t \cdot c[k])$

For large $t$, this approximates the max. The gradient is:
