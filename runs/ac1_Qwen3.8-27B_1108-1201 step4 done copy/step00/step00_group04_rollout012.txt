    cols = []

**Alternative: Uses the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions**

At the optimum of the LP, the KKT conditions give:
- $g_j \geq 0$
- $(f*g)[k] \leq M$ for all $k$
- Complementary slackness: $g_j > 0 \implies$ the dual constraint for $j$ is tight
- Dual feasibility

The dual variables $y_k \geq 0$ satisfy:

$\sum_k y_k f_{k-j} = 1$ for $j$ where $g_j > 0$
