     s_{ij} = \frac{d_{ij}}{r_i + r_j},\quad

     - First 52 entries: flattened `x_i`, `y_i` (positions).

   \[
   f(x) = -\sum_{i=1}^{26} r_i
   \]

3. **Penalty terms**

     \[
     p_{ij} = \max\bigl(0,\; (r_i + r_j) - \|c_i - c_j\|_2 \bigr)^2

     \[
     p_{i,\text{left}}   = \max\bigl(0,\; r_i - x_i \bigr)^2,  \quad

   \[
   F(x) = f(x) \;+\; \alpha \sum_{i<j} p_{ij} \;+\; \alpha \sum_{i,\,s} p_{i,s}
   \]

     \frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}\|c_i-c_j\|_2 = \frac{x_i-x_j}{\|c_i-c_j\|}
