1. **Problem reformulation**
   - The objective is the L∞ norm of a linear map: `A h = correlate(h, 1-h)`. Since `1-h` appears, the map is quadratic, but we can rewrite the integrand for each shift `k` as a bilinear form:
     \[
     I_k(h)=\sum_i h_i(1-h_{i+k})dx
          = dx\big(\sum_i h_i - \sum_i h_i h_{i+k}\big)
          = dx\big(1 - \sum_i h_i h_{i+k}\big)
     \]
     because ∑h_i dx = 1. Hence
     \[
