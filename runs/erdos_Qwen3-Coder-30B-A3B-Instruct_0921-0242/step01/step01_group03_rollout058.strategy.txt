### 1. SDP‑style relaxation with a low‑rank factor (Burer–Monteiro)

Introduce a symmetric PSD matrix \(X\succeq0\) with entries \(X_{ij}=h_i h_j\).  
The constraints become  

* \(X_{ii}=h_i\) (diagonal equals the vector),  
* \(\operatorname{tr}(A_k X)=c_k(h)\ge t\) for all k, where \(A_k\) has a 1 at \((i,i+k)\) for every admissible pair,
