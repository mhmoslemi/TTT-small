### 1. Discretisation and notation

* Index points \(i=0,\dots,N-1\).  Let \(x_i\in\{0,1\}\) denote the step value at point i (so \(h_i = x_i\)).  The integral constraint becomes  

\[
\sum_{i=0}^{N-1} x_i = S\;,\qquad S:=N/2 .

* For a forward shift k (non‑negative integer) the overlap term is  

\[

### 2. Linearisation of the quadratic terms
For each shift k and each admissible pair \((i,i+k)\) introduce an auxiliary binary variable  

\[
w_{i,k}\in\{0,1\},\qquad w_{i,k}=x_i\wedge x_{i+k}.
\]

The exact AND relationship for binary variables is captured by the three McCormick constraints  

\[
\begin{aligned}
w_{i,k} &\le x_i,\\
w_{i,k} &\le x_{i+k},\\
w_{i,k} &\ge x_i + x_{i+k} - 1 .
\end{aligned}

c_k = S - \sum_{i=0}^{N-1-k} w_{i,k}.

\[
\sum_{i=0}^{N-1-k} w_{i,k} \;\ge\; S - t .
\]

All constraints are now linear in the decision variables \(\{x_i\},\{w_{i,k}\},t\).

### 3. MILP model
**Variables**
* \(x_i\) – binary, \(i=0,\dots,N-1\).  
* \(w_{i,k}\) – binary, for every shift k (0≤k≤N‑1) and every i with \(i+k<N\).

**Constraints**

4. **Trivial bound**: \(t \ge 0\) (optional, improves solver performance).

**Objective**  
\(\min\; t.\)

The model is a pure mixed‑integer linear program (MILP).  Its optimal value \(t^\*\) gives a rigorous upper bound

\[
C_5 \le t^\* \,dx .
