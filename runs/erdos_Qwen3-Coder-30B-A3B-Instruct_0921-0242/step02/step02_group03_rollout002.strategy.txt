**Key Insight – binary formulation**  
Because the integrand contains the product \(h_i\,h_{i+k}\), the worst‑case value is attained when each sample is either 0 or 1.  
If \(h_i\in\{0,1\}\) and \(\sum_i h_i=n/2\) then

\[
\sum_i h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+k}\bigr)=\frac{n}{2}-\underbrace{\sum_i h_i h_{i+k}}_{\text{autocorrelation of }h}.
\]

Thus minimising the maximum of the left‑hand side is equivalent to **maximising the minimum non‑zero aperiodic autocorrelation** of a binary sequence with exactly half its entries equal to 1.
