### 3. Fast swap‑based local improvement (hill climbing + simulated annealing)

Given a balanced binary vector `b`, we improve its worst autocorrelation via elementary *pair swaps*:

1. **Identify worst lag** – Compute `A = autocorr(b)`.  Let `k* = argmin_{k≠0} A_k` and `m = A_{k*}`.

   \[
   \Delta A_k = -b_{i+k} + b_{j+k}\quad(\text{mod }n).

   In particular, the change at the worst lag `k*` is `Δ = -b_{i+k*}+b_{j+k*}`.  If `Δ>0`, the worst autocorrelation improves (its value becomes `m+Δ`).
