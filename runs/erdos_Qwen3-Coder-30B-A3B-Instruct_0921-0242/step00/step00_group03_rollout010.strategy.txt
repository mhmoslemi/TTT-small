**Goal**: Produce a discrete step‑function h on [0,2] (length n ≤ 1000) with values in [0,1] and average 0.5 that minimizes  
\(C_5=\max_k \int h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx\), evaluated by full correlation of the sampled vector with its complement.

**Key Insight**  
The objective is the ℓ∞‑norm of a linear map \(A h = h * (1-h)\) (discrete convolution). This is a convex‑in‑h problem? Not globally convex because of the product h·h inside the convolution, but for fixed shift k the map is quadratic. We can treat the problem as a **quadratically constrained quadratic program (QCQP)** with a single ℓ∞‑type objective. By lifting to a matrix variable \(X = h h^{\top}\) the objective becomes linear in X, the constraints become linear plus a rank‑1 requirement. Dropping the rank constraint yields a **semidefinite relaxation (SDR)** that can be solved quickly for moderate n (≤300). The relaxed solution provides a lower bound; we can then extract a feasible binary‑like vector via randomized rounding, followed by local polishing. This approach differs fundamentally from gradient‑based SLSQP.
