With N >= len(w) + n - 1 = (2n-1) + n - 1 = 3n-2, this gives the correct linear cross-correlation.

So: grad[j] = 2 * (w ⋆ a)[j] = 2 * ifft(conj(W) * A)[j]

where W = fft(w, N) and A = fft(a, N), N >= 3n-2.
