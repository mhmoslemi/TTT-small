3. Maintenance of structural constraints enhances search quality while bounding the search space

## Mathematical Specification
Our candidate sequences are defined by four-dimensional parameters governing four distinct random processes. Each sequence is constructed by:
1. **Base**: A matrix-valued random field `M(u,v)` with determinant ensuring constrained positivity
2. **Normalization**: Projection onto a simplex with element-sum `S = √(2n)`
3. **Convolution evaluation**: Uses standard linear convolution in `numpy` to compute self-correlation

- `A` controls sum normalization and base field stretching

- `D` governs the overall scale and smoothing dynamics
