The property is: ifft(X * conj(Y)) gives the cross-correlation of the ifft's.

Let x = ifft(fft(w, N)) = w (zero-padded), y = ifft(fft(a_pad, N)) = a_pad (zero-padded).

Then ifft(fft(w) * conj(fft(a_pad))) = correlation of w and a_pad.

The standard formula: if x and y are length N (zero-padded), then:
c[m] = ifft(X[m] * conj(Y[m]))[m] = sum_n x[n] * y[(n-m) mod N]

For the linear case (N large enough to avoid wrap-around):
c[m] = sum_n x[n] * y[n-m] (where y is zero outside its support)
