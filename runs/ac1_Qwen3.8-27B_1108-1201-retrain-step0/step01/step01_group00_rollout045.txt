And `c[n-1] = n(n+1)(n+2)/6 ≈ n^3/6`.

So both c[n-1] and c[n] are ≈ n^3/6 for large n. The peak is max(c[n-1], c[n], c[n+1], ...).

For n=64: c[n-1] = c[63] = 64*65*66/6 = 45,760. c[n] = c[64] = 67
