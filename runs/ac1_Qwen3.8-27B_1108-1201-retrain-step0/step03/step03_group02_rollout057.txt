def fft_convolve_self(a):
    n = len(a)
    size = 2 * n - 1
    fsize = 1 << (size - 1).bit_length()
    A = fft(a, fsize)

So to first order, the peak of a_new*a_new is at most M (it doesn't increase). And the sum increases. So the objective decreases! This is the key insight.

More precisely:
max(a_new * a_new) ≤ M + O(α) (the O(α) term is ≤ 0 at the previous peak, but might be positive at other locations)
sum(a_new) = sum(a) + α(sum(g*) - sum(a))

For small α:
objective(a_new) ≈ 2n * M / (sum(a) + α ΔS)² ≈ 2n * M / sum(a)² * (1 - 2α ΔS / sum(a))

where ΔS = sum(g*) - sum(a) > 0.

So the objective decreases by a factor of (1 - 2α ΔS / sum(a)) to first order. This confirms the LP direction is a descent direction.

Now, the question is: how large can α be before the O(α²) terms (involving g**g*) dominate? This is what the line search handles.
