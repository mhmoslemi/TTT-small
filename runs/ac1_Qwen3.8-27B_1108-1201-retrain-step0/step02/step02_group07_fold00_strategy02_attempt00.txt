## Phase 2: Optimization Steps

Our optimization process combines three methods:

1. **Mirrordescent**: Explores the feature space by low-cost multiplicative updates.
2. **Projected Gradient Descent**: Refines the sequence by lowering the features that negatively correlate with the current peak or pushing features that positively correlate above zero.
3. **Simulated Annealing**: Allows occasional uphill moves to escape local minima, guided by a probabilistic model based on gradient information.

However, unlike the previous program, we will order these methods differently and introduce a systematic dimension exploration strategy.

The rationale for this change:
- By systematically exploring dimensions (lengths) and applying tailored optimization methods, we increase the chances of escaping local minima.
- Combining gradient descent with exploratory annealing creates a balance between local refinement and global optimization.
