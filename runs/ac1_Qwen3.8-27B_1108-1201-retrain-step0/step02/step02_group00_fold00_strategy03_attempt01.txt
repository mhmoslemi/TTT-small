**1. Approach and Rationale**  
The goal is to minimize the evaluation function `2*n*max(convolve(sequence, sequence)) / sum(a)**2`. The core challenge is dealing with the non-differentiability of the max operation in the convolution. Instead of relying solely on gradient-based methods (like FISTA or Nesterov), we use a hierarchical strategy:  
- **Exploitation Phase**: Optimize a small candidate sequence (length 32) where gradients are manageable.  
- **Exploration Phase**: Use the learned structure to generate longer sequences, optimizing marginally.  
This leverages the observation that optimizations in small dimensions yield better gradients, and the structure helps control the convolution peak in larger sequences. The approach avoids local minima by conditioning the problem on optimal substructures.

**2. Mathematical Specification**

  - `a = [x0, x1, ..., x31]` with `sum(a) = sqrt(64) ≈ 8.0`.
