The key point is: `sum_k c[k] = (sum a)^2` and `c[k] >= 0` for non-negative `a`. The lower bound `max(c) >= (sum a)^2/(2n-1)` is valid, giving `score >= 2n/(2n-1)`.

So the strategy is: find a non-negative sequence whose convolution with itself is as flat as possible (all lags close to `(sum a)^2/(2n-1)`).

The uniform sequence gives a triangular convolution (peaked at center). To flatten it, we need to reduce the center peak while increasing the edge values.

One approach: use a sequence that is NOT symmetric. For example, a sequence that increases and then decreases asymmetrically, or a sequence with a specific periodic structure.

Another approach: think of it as an optimization problem. We want to minimize `max(c)` subject to `sum(a) = 1`, `a >= 0`. This is a non-convex problem, but we can use various optimization techniques.
