    ```
    - Or: swap subsets of elements
    
    Phase 4: Fine polishing (10 min)
    - K=1, very small steps
    - Focus solely on the maximum peak
    ```

35. **Key hyperparameters to tune:**
    - K (number of top peaks to consider): Start small (5-10), increase when stuck
    - Step size: Use backtracking line search
    - When to diversify: After X iterations without improvement
    - Beta (if using LSE as alternative to top-K)

36. **One more insight: The "dual" problem.**
    
    We want to minimize max_k conv[k] subject to a ≥ 0, sum(a) = S.
    
    The Lagrange dual: maximize S*λ - sum_k max(0, μ_k - (something))...
    
    Actually, the dual of "minimize max_k f_k(a) subject to a ≥ 0, 1^T a = S" is:
    maximize S*λ
    subject to: sum_k μ_k * ∇f_k(a) + λ*1 ≥ 0 (for all a ≥ 0 direction)
    μ_k ≥ 0, sum_k μ_k = 1
    
    This isn't super helpful directly, but it tells us that at the optimum, the active constraints (peaks) should be "balanced" in some sense.
