8. **Practical approach: Mirror descent / Exponentiated gradient**

   Given the min-max formulation, we can use the following algorithm:

**Algorithm: Alternating (Mirror) Descent**

1. Initialize $a$ (e.g., constant or from previous best).
2. Initialize $w_k = (a*a)_k / \sum_k (a*a)_k$ (proportional to current convolution).
3. Inner loop: Minimize $\sum_k w_k (a*a)_k$ subject to $a \geq 0, \sum a = S$ using FISTA/projected gradient.
4. Update $w_k \propto \exp(\beta \cdot (a*a)_k)$ (softmax with temperature).
5. Repeat.

This is essentially the "mirror descent" or "exponentiated gradient" approach to the min-max game.
