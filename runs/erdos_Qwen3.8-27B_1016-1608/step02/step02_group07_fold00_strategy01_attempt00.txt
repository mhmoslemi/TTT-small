### 1. Approach and Rational
We are addressing the Eŕds discrepancy problem, which aims to minimize the maximum overlap between a step function and its time-shifted version over the interval [0,2]. The current solution achieves a bound of 0.380969, and our goal is to find a function achieving 0.380859. The optimization is nonconvex and high-dimensional (n=70 points), so we need a global optimization strategy that balances exploration and refinement. The differential evolution (DE) algorithm is chosen for its ability to escape local optima while efficiently exploring the search space. We repair constraint violations (sum and bounds) using linear projection, and we stage the search: (1) global exploration via DE with repair, (2) local refinement with SLSQP, and (3) resolution adaptation to neighboring n-values for diversity.

The key improvement here is the explicit repair function for sum and bounds, separate from the optimization loop. This ensures stricter feasibility enforcement. We also use adaptive strategies for mutation and crossover in DE to enhance convergence.

### 2. Mathematical Specification
**Variables**: `h ∈ [0,1]^{n_points}` with `sum(h) * (2/n_points) ≈ 1` (i.e., `sum(h) ≈ n_points/2`).  
**Objective**: Minimize \(\max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx\).  
**Discrete Objective**: `compute_c5(h) = max(np.correlate(h,1-h,'full'))*(2/n_points)`.

**Constraints**:  
- Box constraints: \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) for all \(i\).  
- Integral constraint: \(\sum_{i=1}^{n\_points} h_i \cdot dx = 1\) \(\rightarrow \sum h_i = n\_points/2\) (with \(dx=2/n\_points\)).

**Repair**:  
- **Bounds**: `clip_to_bounds(h) = np.clip(h, 0, 1)`.  
- **Sum**: Adjust via gradient projection:  
  \(\min_{h^{\prime} \in \mathbb{R}^{n}} \sum h_j^{\prime} \to 0\) subject to \(h^{\prime} \perp \sum h_j - \text{target}\), with `project_sum(h) = h + (\text{target} - \sum h) \cdot w`, where \(w\) is a vector of weights optimized for minimal perturbation.

**Algorithm**:  
- DE with fitness-proportional selection and adaptive mutation/crossover.  
- Constraints enforced via repair, not central to DE parameters.
