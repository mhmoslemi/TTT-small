**Objective and Constraints:**

- Constraints: \( h[i] \in [0,1] \) for all i, \( \sum h[i] \Delta x = 1 \).

We work in the space of discrete vectors. To allow gradient-based methods, use a smooth surrogate of \( C_5 \). Define the surrogate via the negative reciprocal of a linear regression fit of the correlation subproblems, but dynamically smooth it using Gaussian convolution. This surrogate is used to compute gradients for local optimization.

- After any update, enforce:
  1. Clip \( h[i] \in [0,1] \).
  2. Apply a one-step iterative project to \( \sum h[i] \Delta x = 1 \): that is, flip one point and scale another to balance the sum, repeating a few times.
