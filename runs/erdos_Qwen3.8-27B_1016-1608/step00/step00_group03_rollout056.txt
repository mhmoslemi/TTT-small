The objective is:

But wait, the integral is over [0,2], and h is defined on [0,2]. What happens when x+k > 2? The function h(x+k) is not defined there.

Looking at the evaluation: np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode="full") * dx

For arrays of length n, np.correlate gives output of length 2n-1. The lags range from -(n-1) to (n-1). For lag k, the computation is:
c[k+n-1] = sum_{i} h[i] * (1-h)[i+k]

where the sum is over valid i (0 ≤ i < n and 0 ≤ i+k < n).

So the "integral" at lag k is: sum_{valid i} h[i]*(1-h[i+k]) * dx

This is NOT the same as ∫₀² h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx in the continuous sense, because it only sums over the intersection of [0,2] and [-k, 2-k]. But that's how the problem is defined in the discretization.
