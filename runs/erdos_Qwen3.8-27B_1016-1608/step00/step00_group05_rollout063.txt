For the local search, I need to efficiently compute the change in objective when swapping positions i and j.

- For each lag k, c_k changes by:
  - Removing contribution of position i having a 1: affects c_k where h[i+k] or h[i] was involved
  - Adding contribution of position j having a 1

This is O(n) per swap evaluation. With n=70, that's fast.
