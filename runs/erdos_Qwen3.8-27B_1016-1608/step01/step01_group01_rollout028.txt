In simulated annealing:
- State: the vector h (feasible)
- Move: pick a random coordinate and change it (or pick two coordinates and swap/adjust)
- Accept: if improvement, always accept. If worsening, accept with probability exp(-delta/T).
- Cooling: T decreases over time.

The advantage is that it can escape local minima by accepting worse solutions.

For the move, I'll use:
- Single coordinate move: change h[i] to a random value in [0,1]
- Pair move: change h[i] and h[j] such that the sum is preserved (h[i] += δ, h[j] -= δ)
- Swap: exchange h[i] and h[j]
