c_k (k>0) = sum_{i=0}^{n-k-1} h[i+k] * (1-h[i])  -- h shifted by k, multiplied by 1-h
c_{-k} (k>0) = sum_{i=0}^{n-k-1} h[i] * (1-h[i+k])  -- h multiplied by 1-h shifted by k

These are not the same in general! c_k ≠ c_{-k} unless h has some symmetry.
