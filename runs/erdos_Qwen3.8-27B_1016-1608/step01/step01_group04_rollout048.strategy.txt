1. Approach and rationale:
The current implementation uses SLSQP optimization with multiple restarts, but it tends to get stuck in local optima. This approach replaces SLSQP with Differential Evolution (DE), a population-based stochastic optimization method that's better for global exploration. DE maintains a population of candidate solutions and iteratively improves them through mutation, crossover, and selection. By using a higher resolution (n=400) and more diverse initialization strategies than the parent solution, we aim to find a lower C5 value. The key improvements are: (1) switching from gradient-based SLSQP to gradient-free DE, (2) using n=400 resolution for better precision, and (3) implementing bounds and constraints explicitly through repair steps.

2. Mathematical specification:
We minimize C5⁺ = dx * maxₖ ∫h(x) * (1-h(x+k)) dx subject to:
- 0 ≤ h(x) ≤ 1 (boundedness)
- ∫h(x)dx = 1 (integral constraint)
h is represented as a real-valued vector of length n_points (default 400), and the objective is evaluated using:
C5⁺ = max(dx * np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full'))
Constraints are enforced via:
- Bounds: 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 for all i
- Integral constraint: sum(h) * dx = 1 (i.e., sum(h) = n_points/2)
