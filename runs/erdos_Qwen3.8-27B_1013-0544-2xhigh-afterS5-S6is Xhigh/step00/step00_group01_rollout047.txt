For arrays a (length n) and v (length n), the full correlation is:
c[k] = ∑_m a[m+k] * conj(v[m])   for k = -(n-1), ..., 0, ..., n-1

In numpy's indexing, c_full[j] corresponds to k = j - (n-1).

The sum is over m such that 0 ≤ m < n and 0 ≤ m+k < n.

Now, ∂c_full[j]/∂h[m0]:
- h[m0] appears as h[m+k] when m+k = m0, i.e., m = m0-k. Contribution: (1-h[m]) = (1-h[m0-k]) if m0-k is valid.
