### Approach and Rationale
The current approach uses differential evolution and SLSQP with polynomial estimation. We aim to replace this with a Simulated Annealing (SA) based optimization since SA is more effective at escaping shallow local minima due to its temperature-scheduled global search. SA allows exploring a broader solution space by occasionally accepting worse solutions, facilitating convergence to deeper global minima. We'll combine SA with SLSQP refinement at multiple grid sizes and subgradient polishing on the finest grid. This approach directly targets the global structure of the Erds problem while using local optimization for detailed refinement. Key differences from the previous strategy are:
1. Complete replacement of DE with SA for exploration phase
2. Integration of SLSQP refinement directly into SA cooling schedule
3. Subgradient polishing as final optimization step
4. Working directly with grid point distributions while maintaining sum constraint

            h += adjust * (diffs / np.std(diffs) if np.std(diffs) > 0 else np.ones(len(h)))
