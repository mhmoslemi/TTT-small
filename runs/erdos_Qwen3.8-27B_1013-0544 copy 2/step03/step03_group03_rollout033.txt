But wait, we also need to maintain the sum constraint sum(h) = n/2. If we change h[i] from its current value to t*, the sum changes. We need to either:

- Pair the change: increase h[i] by δ and decrease some h[j] by δ

The simplest approach: after each coordinate update, project to feasibility. This might undo some of the improvement, but over many iterations it should converge.

Actually, a better approach: do the coordinate update, then fix the sum by adjusting the coordinate that has the most "room" (closest to 0 or 1 without being at the boundary).
