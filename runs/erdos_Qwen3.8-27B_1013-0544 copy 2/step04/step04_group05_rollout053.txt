The soft-max with parameter beta:
SM(c, beta) = (1/beta) * log(sum exp(beta * c_i))

As beta -> infinity, SM -> max(c). For finite beta, SM is smooth and we can compute its gradient exactly.

The gradient of SM w.r.t. h[i] is:

where w_k = exp(beta * c_k) / sum_j exp(beta * c_j)

This is a weighted average of the subgradients at each lag, weighted by the soft-max weights.

The advantage: this gives a smooth gradient that points in the "average" direction of all the worst lags, not just the single worst one. This can help with convergence.
