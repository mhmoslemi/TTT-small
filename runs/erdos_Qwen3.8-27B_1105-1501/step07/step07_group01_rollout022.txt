When h[b] changes from 0 to 1: we gain the contribution of h[b]=1, i.e., we add q[b - offset(m)] for each m.

OK this is the issue: the correlation is a sum of products, and changing one element affects multiple products (both as the h-factor and as the (1-h)-factor).
