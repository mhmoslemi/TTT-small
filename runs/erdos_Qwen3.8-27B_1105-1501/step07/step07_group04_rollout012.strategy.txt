1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

Use a two-pronged evaluation-driven scheme that improves the supplied parent by means different from the previous subgradient/annealing mix.

Primary new method:

- Solve the exact minimax problem locally with a nonlinear epigraph formulation:
  variables `h` and an auxiliary scalar `t`, objective `minimize t`, constraints
  `t >= max_lag C5_lag(h)` in exact discrete form, plus the true discrete box and
  sum constraints.
- Maintain every returned candidate with the authoritative discrete evaluator
  `max(np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full")) * 2 / len(h)`.
  No surrogate score is accepted as final.
- Use several legitimate parents/transformed parents to avoid being trapped in the exact parent corner.

Secondary new method:

- Run a target-specific binary local/annealing search using exact incremental
  correlation updates. This is different from recomputing the full correlation
  for every swap and from the earlier generic simulated annealing. It can
  explore balanced binary patterns much more thoroughly and extract a strong
  starting point for the continuous epigraph solver.

The previous construction was already near `0.380889`. The gap to `0.3808` is tiny, so the bottleneck is likely: (a) a flat greedy direction, (b) coarse binary feedback, and (c) not using curvature/active-constraint information. The epigraph solver directly deals with curvature and active constraints; the binary incremental search supplies balanced corner starts that may lie in a lower basin.

What is justified and what is heuristic:

- The epigraph formulation is an exact re-expression of the discrete objective;
  solving it well can only change the local basin the objective is explored.
  The formulation is nonconvex because products `h_i h_j` enter the constraints.
  Thus, a solver can provide a local improvement, not a certificate.
- The binary incremental exact updates are algebraically exact for binary flips,
  because for a shifted lag source and target are distinct. The center lag can be
  handled exactly as zero for binary vectors.
- Which new basin gives the best `0.3808` range is empirical.

Main limitations:

- `scipy` nonlinear solvers may stop early or temporarily be infeasible. Every
  final accepted h must still be projected and evaluated exactly.
- Binary exact updates cover single bit flips but not arbitrary block moves; they
  are local.
- Resolution transfer changes the exact evaluator and may move the parent to a
