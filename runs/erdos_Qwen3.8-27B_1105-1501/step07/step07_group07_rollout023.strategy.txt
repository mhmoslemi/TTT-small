1.1 Central idea

   Precision. The plan is a hybrid exact-objective global search whose
   primary engine is combinatorial graph-cut optimization. For a set of
   nonnegative weights on the exactly computed finite-support lag scores,
   the weighted sum is a binary quadratic pseudo-Boolean function whose pairwise terms are `x_i x_j` with nonnegative unary kernels after extracting binary structure or after coordinated cardinality penalties. This submodular structure can be transformed exactly into an s-t min-cut, hence any weighted-lag metric can be minimized at the maximum level by a polynomial-time min-cut, rather than by a locally linearized trust-region direction or a smooth maximum. Feasibility is invariant to the target mean via flow. Feasibility repair via water-fill. Every accepted final candidate is checked against the original evaluator using `np.correlate(h, 1.0 - h, mode="full") * dx`, and the current parent is retained as fallback.

   Material difference from earlier plans. Prior methods approximate minimax
   local descent by including the maximum as a scalar variable and the worst
   constraints; their local directions remain constrained linear models
   and do not encounter a structure favorable to submodularity. In this
   plan, the search engine operates in a different representation space,
   binary variables and cardinality-constrained submodels. The surrogate
   is a weighted correlation metric, not a smoothing or partial
   constraint. The smooth maximum may only be used as an optional final polish,
   explicitly acknowledged in acceptance criteria; non-minimu
   improvement acceptance requires actual evaluator results.

   The exact parent resembles a locally optimal vector. Its active
   objective value contains several sub-lag scores and fractional boundary
   structures. Min-cut candidates search a globally different combinatorial
   basin because they solve a subproblem exactly rather than giving a
   local descent moment. For many binary combinations and intervals that a
   blended gradient / constraint/QP method is unlikely to encounter, the
   cut on a strong weighting process can drive the most harmful lag
   scores sharply. In contrast, the global-smoothing continuous plan may become
   weaker for the discrete component. The plan also samples lengths and
   target weights intervals, favors shorter resolutions, and terminates with
