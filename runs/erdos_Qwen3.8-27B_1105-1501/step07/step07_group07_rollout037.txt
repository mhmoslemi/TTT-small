The soft-max: S(h; β) = (1/β) * log(sum_k exp(β * R_k(h) * dx))

As β → ∞, this approaches max_k R_k(h) * dx.

The gradient is:
∂S/∂h[i] = dx * sum_k p_k * ∂R_k/∂h[i]

where p_k = exp(β * R_k * dx) / sum_j exp(β * R_j * dx) are the softmax probabilities.

This is a convex combination of the gradients of individual lag scores, weighted by how "active" each lag is.
