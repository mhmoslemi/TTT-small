1. Approach and rationale

Central idea. Treat the task as an exact finite-dimensional constrained minimax problem, rather than only as a smoothed surrogate optimization. The supplied parent construction is already a very good feasible point: an n=99 discretization with about C5≈0.38085, and the target is only lower by roughly 5e-5. A broad random search or only a log-sum-exp smoothed gradient is inefficient because most iterations try to improve a soft approximation, not the exact max overlap. The proposed method attacks the exact constraints that are active at the maximum overlap lag(s).

Primary local mechanism. At a feasible point h, compute the full correlation vector c_k. Let τ be an active-set tolerance close to the final improvement scale, e.g. τ=5e-5 to 1e-4 while close to target and larger earlier. The active or near-active set A = {k : c_k ≥ max_j c_j − τ} describes which lags determine the present bound. Since each c_k is smooth quadratic, use exact scaled gradients ∇c_k. Then solve a small linearized tangent subproblem: find a perturbation δ with sum_i δ_i=0 and box-compatible bounds such that the tangent increase of every active correlation is as small as possible. If the solved tangent maximum is negative, a competent step along δ should decrease the exact min-max objective. But because the correlations are nonconvex, every step is accepted for the feasible, interpolation, and globally stored only if the actual evaluator gives a better value than the current point or saved best.

Secondary mechanisms.
1. A smooth log-sum-exp projected gradient/Adam method is used as an escape mechanism from linearized stagnation and for strong initial descent. Its exact-candidate tracking is still used for best storage.
2. A direct SLSQP/NLP polish is used on selected high-quality points. The SLSQP formulation promotes the bound t and imposes all full correlations as explicit inequalities plus the linear mass constraint. This gives an exact-bound local descent method that naturally equalizes many near-maximal constraints.
3. Dimension-transformed search near the parent is included. This helps find local minima missed by bottleneck in the parent resolution; however, valid outputs from larger n are accepted so accuracy is not peurk detector.

Bottleneck it solves. The linear-tangent/ SQP active set directly moves mass along directions that reduce the exact overlapping constraints causing the maximum.
