**Fast minimax line search:**

Given current $h$ and direction $d$ (with $\sum d = 0$):

For each lag $k$ (0 to 2n-2), the score is:

- $b_k = \text{correlate}(d, 1-h)[k] \cdot dx - \text{correlate}(h, d)[k] \cdot dx$
  = $(\text{correlate}(d, 1-h) - \text{correlate}(h, d))[k] \cdot dx$
  
  Wait, let me redo. $f_k(h + td) = \sum_i (h_i + td_i)(1 - h_{i+s} - td_{i+s}) dx$
  where $s = k - (n-1)$ is the shift for lag index $k$.
  
  $= \sum_i [h_i(1-h_{i+s}) + td_i(1-h_{i+s}) - th_i d_{i+s} - t^2 d_i d_{i+s}] dx$
  
  So:
  $a_k = \sum_i h_i(1-h_{i+s}) dx = \text{correlate}(h, 1-h)[k] \cdot dx$
  $b_k = \sum_i [d_i(1-h_{i+s}) - h_i d_{i+s}] dx = (\text{correlate}(d, 1-h) - \text{correlate}(h, d))[k] \cdot dx$
  $c_k = -\sum_i d_i d_{i+s} dx = -\text{correlate}(d, d)[k] \cdot dx$

The box constraints on $h_i + td_i \in [0,1]$ give:
- If $d_i > 0$: $t \leq (1-h_i)/d_i$
- If $d_i < 0$: $t \geq -h_i/d_i$ (i.e., $t \geq h_i/(-d_i) = -h_i/d_i$)
- If $d_i = 0$: no constraint

$t_{min} = \max(0, \max_{i: d_i < 0} (-h_i/d_i))$
