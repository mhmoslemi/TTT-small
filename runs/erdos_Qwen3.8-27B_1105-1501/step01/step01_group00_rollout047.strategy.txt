1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea and why it is materially different

The previous best programs centered on minimizing a smoothed/maxified surrogate with SLSQP-type constrained optimizers and then polishing. That approach can be fragile for the present objective because the true quantity is the maximum of 2n-1 directed overlap functionals at once. Surrogate smoothing tends to concentrate on the currently largest constraints and may miss the exact corner where a nearby lag becomes the maximum after a step. It also carries smoothing bias into accepted candidate proposals.

The method proposed here optimizes the original, exact objective throughout. The main search engine is an exact-score local/global hybrid:

- Strict feasibility is maintained by constructive moves whenever possible: two-index line moves preserve sum exactly; active gradient moves are projected to zero mean; proposed vectors that are not exactly feasible enter a robust joint feasibility repair.
- The proposed directions are only proposals. Every proposal gets a full exact score with the authoritative evaluator before it is accepted.
- The deterministic improvement engine evaluates maximal objective values exactly over a neighborhood of single-pair coordinate moves, because these moves are low-dimensional, bound-preserving, sum-preserving, and can exploit quadratic cross terms that linear smoothing gradients ignore.
- If single-pair greedy moves are stuck, exact objective simulated annealing over the same feasible pair algebra explores directions without smooth surrogate bias.
- Active constrained directions and active-linearized polynomials/directions are retained as complementary proposal generators, but again no proposal is accepted unless its full objective improves or, in annealing, it is explicitly accepted by a chance subroutine and only the exact global best is ever recorded.
- Multiple candidate resolutions and the exact parent construction feed a small population. Interpolation between resolutions is always followed by feasibility repair and exact rescoring. Small resolutions are preferred only when exact objective value is effectively tied.

This is different from smoothing-based local optimization because the outer loop never optimizes a surrogate and then trusts its value. The objective itself is evaluated at every proposed move. The search neighborhood is concentrated precisely where one can construct nontrivial nonconvex moves with exact feasibility: two-coordinate line segments for h-values, bounded line segments for mean-zero directions from active gradients, repaired random directions, and transfer between resolutions with exact objective evaluation. The method should therefore preserve a safe bound at all times and can locate improvements that are invisible to smoothed filtering.

1.2 What it addresses

The bottleneck that this plan attacks is that the previous smooth method often moves all h_i simultaneously using a misleading averaged gradient, while the active lag points in the true objective impose conflicting quadratic constraints. Two-coordinate line searches can tune a pair well enough to lower two active lag values because terms h_i(1-h_j) change quadratically when both variables are changed with equal opposite steps. Smooth gradient descent cannot distinguish those pair-wise curvature effects.

At the same time, exact random pair annealing, interpolated parents, and direction moves explore beyond the local two-variable subspace. The plan is a global structured local search over the true function, not a high-dimensional global symbolic solver and not a convex relaxation.

1.3 Honest limitations and hypotheses

Facts used:

- The evaluator is finite-support discrete cross-correlation, exact and available for every h.
- The two-variable transformation h_a += δ, h_b -= δ preserves the average constraint exactly if h_b <= 1 and h_a+δ <= 1 and h_a+δ >=0, h_b-δ >=0.
- Along a fixed pair line, the exact objective values are piecewise quadratic in δ in any case where the constraints and feasible box are unchanged, but exact methods do not need that form; we can evaluate full correlations for rated δ’s.
- A mean-zero direction plus clipping after small repair can satisfy constraints by direct exact evaluation, not by rescaling and clipping separately.

Hypotheses:

- Improvements to C5 are likely to be found with combinations of exact resisted pair moves and slight non-local directions, rather than convex relaxations of the full correlation constraints.
- Ideas with `h_i + h_rev_i = 1` and power-level symmetric averaging may help due prefix/suffix symmetry but are not assumed necessary for the optimum; map to pool and retained only if exact score improves.
- Simulated annealing helps escape local minima at small nonconvex curvature, but acceptance must not cause global best to degrade.
- Higher resolution may not be better and should be balanced with lower resolutions at exact rescoring.

No global optimality or convexity insight corresponding to the global problem is claimed. The returned value is always an exact computed upper bound for that concrete h and n.
