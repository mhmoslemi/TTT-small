1.1 Central construction

The current parent construction is already a high-quality local optimum reached by LP-style descent with stochastic restarts. The remaining gap to the target, approximately 1.15e-4, is too small for crude randomized restarts and too small for a method that only follows one fixed descent direction. - First, run continuation-constrained responses to a smooth approximation of the maximum, specifically a log-sum-exp smooth maximum solved with scipy SLSQP. - Then, use a strict trust-region LP descent that accepts candidates only through the actual evaluator. It reduces the maximum in the linearized space but refuses any candidate whose true Numpy correlation objective does not improve.
- Then, perform an active-coordinate/active-pair polynomial-style exact search along mass-preserving rays between high-gradient and low-gradient coordinates.
