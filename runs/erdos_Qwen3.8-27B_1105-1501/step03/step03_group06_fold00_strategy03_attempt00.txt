1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

The supplied parent construction is already close to the current discrete optimizer landscape, but the prior successful code mainly performed a smoothed-maximization / linearized-trust-region continuation. It evaluates structures that are related to, but not identical with, the bottleneck `max corr_lag`. The proposed algorithm instead performs a constrained, exact-objective, population-and-line-search hybrid on the feasible polytope/box. It has three distinct components that are not present in the previous continuous smoothing pipeline:

1. A compact population of fully feasible step-function candidates is maintained. Candidates are created by structural mutations that change the shape of `h` without replacing the official objective by a smoothed surrogate.

2. Each promising candidate is improved by exact, constraint-preserving line searches along deterministic and randomized zero-sum directions, including complement/reflection/roll/swap/binary-like structural moves and block perturbations.

3. A pairwise and block coordinate polish pass is used after a candidate becomes locally competitive. This pass uses exact one-dimensional maximization by dense scanning/bracketing on directions that are often missing when a surrogate is optimized.

4. Resolution is not fixed to one `n`: the same procedure is applied to transformed copies of the best-found candidate at nearby reasonable resolutions, whenever the candidate is stored with a different dimension. Since the evaluator and feasibility constraint are both explicit functions of `n`, the population and polish stages can be applied independently at each dimension.

The method is materially different from the previously supplied continuous optimization code because:

- The maximum correlation is always evaluated exactly, not replaced by a log-sum-exp smoothed surrogate.
- Local moves make the exact discrete/directional feasible line and its official exact score the candidate-selection criterion.
- Structural “mutations” and a global population topology are used to generate starting points and side structures, while more memory-efficient local geometric/progression moves are used inside computed margin.
- The parent normalization is held as fallback that cannot be lost; additional proposals are final-evaluated only with the official array operation.
- Candidate acceptance/evaluation is exactly what the final evaluator uses.

1.2 Justified facts vs hypotheses
