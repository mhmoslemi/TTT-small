1. Approach and rationale

The inherited construction starts from the supplied `initial_h_values` (not necessarily revisited numerically until runtime; all processing will be algorithmic) and a smooth soft-max/max descent followed by LP-style refinements reached `C5 ≈ 0.380917`. The dominant bottleneck is not evaluation cost: the array length is small and the exactcorrelation can be evaluated rapidly. The bottleneck is premature termination at one local plateau of a nonconvex max-over-integer-lag problem. A single proximal soft-descent run can be trapped because:
- the soft objective emphasizes only the currently highest lags, and may lose visibility of secondary lags that will become active after a small move;
- projection to the box-plus-sum constraint can cause decreases in the smooth surrogate to disappear or reverse in the exact discrete score;
- the local neighborhood has a nearly flat active-set manifold and may require coordinated changes across many coordinates, including occasional temporary increases in some correlations, before a lower maximum appears;
- discrete resolution itself may matter, so the best coefficient boundary may not lie at the initial `n_points`.

The proposed primary approach is therefore a hybrid, deadline-driven, exact-acceptance basin-hopping search based on a mathematically valid discrete objective representation. It keeps a `run()` top-level fallback to the parent projection or an algorithmically constructed fallback at all times, and it targets provable bounds by always accepting a candidate only after an evaluation by the original `np.correlate(...), 1 - h(...)...` metric, not merely by a surrogate.

The architecture is:
1. Validate and project the supplied parent, and compute its exact C5.
2. Build an internal model whose max value equals the original objective for every feasible vector, with an analytically correct gradient for the smooth soft-max of that same max. Use this internal model for search directions while final comparisons use the original evaluator.
3. Improve the parent using a strengthened soft descent that uses many-lag weighting, robust projection, exact directional line search, and a memory/diversity archive.
4. Add global escape mechanisms: simulated reheating, random feasible chain moves, roll and reorder perturbations, parent resampling, plus multi-resolution transfers.
5. Relax the `n_points` constraint without changing the exact definition by testing a discretized ladder of resolutions, transferring candidates by piecewise-linear resampling followed by a common feasibility projection, and retaining only exact C5 improvements.
6. Polish the best exact candidate again with a short restream line and optional deterministic SLSQP smooth minimizations.
7. Return the smallest accepted right during the best approved smallest `n_points` measure.

This is not a global optimizer. I am not claiming a global minimum, only that the requested search spaces are explored robustly under exact re-evaluation and that the returned construction is valid. The mathematical proposition used here is a set-equality statement for the discrete objective representation; everything else — temperature schedules, basin-hopping probabilities, and resolution-ladde— are heuristic. I intentionally keep the acceptance step exact so that any improvement of the returned value is genuinely proven for that returned sequence.

2. Mathematical specification

Notation and discrete feasibility: for `n := n_points`, define `dx = 2.0 / n`,

\[
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i = n/2 \qquad 0 \le h_i \le 1,
\]

and let `q_i = 1 - h_i`. The evaluated objective is

\[
C(h)=dx\,\max_{r=0}^{2n-2}\operatorname{corr}_r(h,q),
\]

where `corr_r` denotes the `r`-th coefficient of `np.correlate(h, q, mode="full")`.

For every positive lag index:

- set `s_d` centered as indices `n-1+d` and `n-1-d`;
- the two possible valid point contributions for absolute lag `d` are
  \[
  V_d^{(a)}(h)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-d-1} h_i q_{i+d},

  V_d^{(b)}(h)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-d-1} h_{i+d} q_i.
  \]
- at `d=0`
  \[
  V_0(h)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_iq_i=\sum_i h_i(1-h_i).

\[
\mathcal{S}(h)=\left(
\{V_d^{(a)}(h),V_d^{(b)}(h):1\le d<n\}

\right)

\[
\max_r corr_r(h,q) = \max_{x\in\mathcal S(h)}x.
