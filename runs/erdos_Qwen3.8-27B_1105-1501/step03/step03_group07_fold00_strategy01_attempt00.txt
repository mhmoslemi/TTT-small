1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea
The parent construction has already entered a delicate continuous local basin: a purely sequential line-search, pairwise-flip, or smooth-gradient approach can make tiny changes but may not exploit the full 2k−5 different lag constraints that define the boundary of the minimax objective. The planned program is built around a different central method: solve a high-fidelity finite-dimensional epigraph NLP with exactly the evaluator’s lag functions using SLSQP, and combine it with two complementary supports: a smooth log-sum-exp/log-exp surrogate and an exact binary branch-and-local-reciprocity exploration. The exact objective is always used for acceptance and return; the solvers search with a mathematically specified relaxed or surrogate objective, never as a purported parallelsolution. Concretely, we will:

- Use an epigraph representation where variables are the sampled values plus a scalar upper-bound variable. Every signed correlation lag becomes a finite-support bilinear inequality. The optimization direction minimizes the upper bound.
