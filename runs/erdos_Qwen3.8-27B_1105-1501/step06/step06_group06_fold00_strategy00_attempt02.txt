1. Approach and rationale

2. The central idea is a feasible-stochastic-minimax pipeline. The exact objective is the maximum of about 2n-1 quadratic correlation values. Instead of treating the maximum as nonsmooth throughout, the planner replaces it temporarily by a smooth log-sum-exp surrogate and optimizes that surrogate with equality and box constraints. As the smoothing parameter is increased, the surrogate approaches the true maximum from above. Exact correlation values are always recomputed from the task evaluator, so all acceptance decisions and the returned bound are true C5 bounds. This addresses the bottleneck of the current construction: smooth active set local solvers can stall on the piecewise-linear/quadratic “maximal-envelope” surfaces where several lags are active and descent directions are poorly characterized. A smooth surrogate, followed by exact pair-coordinate descent and global dimension transfers, gives a mechanism to leave plateaus and touch previously inactive lags.

3. The method is not claimed to find a global optimum. It is a structured search for a strictly better feasible step vector. It differs from the previous program by adding: (i) a continuation from soft to hard log-sum-exp objectives, (ii) exact pair-move descent, which preserves the sum constraint exactly without projection, (iii) stochastic multi-resolution transfers in and near the parent length, and (iv) constrained polishing and strict final reevaluation. The parent supplied by `initial_h_values` is always retained as a valid fallback after it is validated; without it, algorithmic deterministic/random starts are used.

4. Why the log-sum-exp surrogate can help: The surrogate \(\Phi_\alpha(D(h))=(1/\alpha)\log\sum_k\exp(\alpha D_k(h))\) is smooth and satisfies \(\max_k D_k(h)\le \Phi_\alpha(h)\). Its gradient is a soft convex combination \(\sum_k p_k\nabla D_k(h)\) with \(p_k\) increasing sharply in \(D_k\). Thus it approximately follows active-lag gradients at high \(\alpha\), but at low \(\alpha\) it does not ignore nearly-active lags. A low-to-high continuation gives more damping than a hard active-set method and can move mass away from “latent” peaks.

5. Why exact pair moves help: The constraint sum(h)=n/2 is preserved exactly by pairwise mass shifts \(h_i \mapsto h_i+t, h_j \mapsto h_j-t\). Since all domain bounds are enforced by an interval field-search, these moves never require a destructive projection to remain feasible. Each pair direction gives a one-dimensional exact maximum of quadratic functions. This mechanism can improve an objective after smooth solvers stop, because it searches a large collection of constrained two-coordinate directions and accepts only exact decreases.

6. The final returned candidate undergoes the authoritative evaluator. If this fails, the known parent may still be returned after validation if it is feasible and within tolerance. If neither reformulation provides a valid candidate, the best valid parent or an indispensable evaluated fallback from stochastic search is used.
