1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea. The parent construction is already a strong local point, but the remaining gap to 0.3808 is very close to the resolution of a smooth local minimax model. Instead of extending another active-set nonlinear solver, use the following primary search: softened maximum annealing plus exact feasible one-dimensional minimization. The objective is treated jointly by all lag constraints through a soft-max gradient, while each step is an exact one-dimensional descent along a feasible transport direction and is accepted only by the authoritative discrete maximum-correlation evaluator. This separates exploration from robustness: the soft-max direction gives a smooth, global view of many active and near-active lags; the one-dimensional line search and exact evaluation avoid accepting surrogate-dependent moves.

1.2 Why this can improve the supplied parent. The problem becomes nonsmooth and nearly stationary when dozens of correlations sit within a small tolerance of their maximum. A fixed active subset or a non-exact trust-region model can fail along shallow valleys because the quadratic interaction terms are not globally convex. The soft-weighted gradient is dominated by a smooth probability vector over all lags and therefore can suggest collective reductions instead of reacting to only a handful of active lags. The line search itself is exact in the true discrete objective; step lengths and directions can remain feasible while correcting roundoff and preserving the mass constraint. Additionally, binary and thresholded variants of the parent are inserted as diversity: the objective contains negative pairwise products, so moving from nearly-fractional values to compressed 0/1 values can remove center-lag mass and expose different local basins. Exact parent validity and best-save offer a guaranteed no-worse fallback.

1.3 Substantive differences from existing local active-constraint/SLSQP searches. 
- The direction generation is soft-max with exponentially increasing emphasis, rather than a small set of active constraints at a fixed model.
- Updates follow exact one-dimensional feasible line minimizations with random/transport directions, not multi-dimensional SLSQP solves or full projected SLSQP steps.
- Candidate diversity is generated by exact-threshold parent data and low-memory balanced binary/balanced pattern starts, rather than smoothing multistart alone.
- Feasibility is always preserved through the proposed transport line, not repaired by clipping plus renormalization.
- Objective acceptance is always the exact evaluator; any soft surrogate is only a directional heuristic.
