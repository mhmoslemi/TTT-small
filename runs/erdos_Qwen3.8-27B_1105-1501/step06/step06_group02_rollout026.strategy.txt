1.1 Central method

The proposed improvement is to make local search actions directly represent many-coordinate directions rather than only pairs or random four-coordinate moves. The core idea is:

1. Keep the evaluator’s exact nonconvex objective, including all linear-lag boundary terms, as the only accepted criterion.
2. At the current feasible `h`, read the current peak values `R_s(h)` for every lag `s` in `[-(n-1), n-1]`.
3. Convert selected near-best peaks into a small linear direction problem: find a zero-sum displacement direction `p` that, according to a first-order model, lowers a chosen set of currently high correlation rows.
