1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

Use the supplied parent only as a valid fallback and a reservoir seed. The primary optimization mechanism is a feasible-population, global-stochastic reservoir search. At every candidate generation step we produce one or more proposed coefficient vectors, enforce the two original constraints immediately by an exact projected waterfilling routine, and judge the candidate by the task evaluator itself:
\[
C(h)=\max_q dx\,[\operatorname{np.correlate}(h,1-h,\text{full})]_q,
\qquad dx=2/\operatorname{len}(h).
\]
The search explores the feasible simplex using exact evaluations of many constructed step-functions: recombination of existing feasible vectors, weighted convex mixing, block mass transfers, sparse pairwise mass pumping, block interval smoothing, and covariance-scaled stochastic diffusion. A secondary refinement stage performs a few exact one-dimensional line searches along feasible balanced directions. This differs substantively from a pure trust-region, LP-directed descent, p-norm surrogate descent, or relaxed partnerสะพาน algorithm because the reservoir contains many distinct feasible points and the update rules explicitly move mass globally rather than committing to a single deterministic linearized descent path.

1.2 Why this addresses the previous bottleneck

The parent has likely reached a strong local optimum of the deterministic pipeline. Linearmized edge moves and smooth surrogate rules can follow one active-set topology and become cyclic. By maintaining multiple feasible tips and allowing non-monotone exploration in the objective landscape through population selection, the search can move from one parent basin, recombine two nearly improved configurations, and sample structural transformations that do not appear as feasible continuous first-order line segments. The exact objective is not optimized by allowing infeasible surrogate minima; every returned candidate has been directly projected back into
\[
\mathcal H_n=\{h\in\mathbb R^n:0\le h_i\le1,\ \sum_i h_i=n/2\}
\]
and recomputed with the official correlation metric.

1.3 Fallback policy considered and limitations

The supplied `initial_h_values` array, if present, is copied once, projected, evaluated by the exact formula, and retained as a safe answer. If `initial_h_values` already scores below the requested target, the search may continue only to improve it or exit early after validation.

No global optimality is claimed. The problem is nonconvex due to the bilinear correlation. The reservoir search is a serious heuristic, not a proof. The algorithm can diagnose numerical invalidity, step failure, and stagnation, but can still stagnate in a basin not discovered by the random initialization pool. The exact scoring mechanism being strict tolerance monotone merely for the portfolio best value; the population samples themselves are not monotone accepted. The plan’s robustness comes from parallel candidate pools, constraint projection, and exhaustive generation control, not a mathematical performance guarantee.
