1.1 Central idea

The optimizer proposed here is deliberately different from the parent program. The central method is a Sequential Linearized Minimax (SLM) outer loop:

1. At the current feasible array h, linearize every finite overlap constraint exactly to first order.
2. Solve a small linear program (LP) that minimizes the maximum of these linearized overlaps under exact sum feasibility, box constraints, and a trust-region ball around h.
3. Instead of accepting the LP point cally, use it only as a proposed descent direction. Perform exact constrained one-dimensional line searches along that direction, including both positive and negative multipliers.
4. Use the original nonconvex evaluator as the only acceptance rule. Any step that increases the true objective is rejected, not accepted.
