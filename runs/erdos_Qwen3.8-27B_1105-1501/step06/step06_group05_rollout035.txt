The objective is: min_δ max_l (c[l] + δ·d[l]) where d[l] = profile[src][lag] - profile[dst][lag] = Δc(lag)/δ.

This is a piecewise linear function of δ, and the minimum is at a breakpoint. The breakpoints occur where c[l] + δ·d[l] = c[l'] + δ·d[l'] for some l, l', or at the bounds.
