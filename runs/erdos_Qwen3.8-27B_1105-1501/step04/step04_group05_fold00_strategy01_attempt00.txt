The task is global nonconvex minimax optimization. The nonconvexity comes from bilinear terms `h[i] * h[j]`, not from the max alone, and from the discontinuous dependency of individual overlap channels on the support lengths at the boundaries.

1.2 Bottleneck in the supplied parent / prior approach

The previous parent obtained by SLSQP-style local active constraint optimization reaches a local score around `0.380915` with `n = 64`. The logical floor for a further program is that the local SLSQP basin is too narrow. A purely re-parametrized active SLSQP continuation is not likely to gain the desired `1e-4` gap.

The dominant remaining gap is likely caused by the following combination:

- The local optimizer is trapped near one continuous basin.
- The exact evaluator is sensitive to discrete resolution: a better feasible construction may lie on another sample length or a nearby low-dimensional interpolation grid.
- Small constrained local moves require several correlated changes; targeted one-dimensional gradient projections in a single basis are insufficient.

The primary substantive change is therefore not “another SLSQP run on the parent”. Instead:

- Search in the lower-dimensional direct construction space: the decision vector is an array of values for the same construction grid already represented, treated as a free vector rather than the solution of a finite bound-constrained smooth problem.
- Evaluate those optimizations through a smooth epigraph-style maximum surrogate and keep only those smoothed sequences via exact evaluation.
- Apply in parallel a custom, direct evaluator-driven multiple restart, hidden optimization verification pipeline.
- Express h in principal resolutions instead of maximum, causing natural discrete minimax plateau candidates.
- Recombine good candidates; use global line searches in exact-objective induced good-loop directions depending on each lag; and exchange/shift-all variant/basis-tilting transformations.
