1. Approach and rationale

The target improvement from about 0.380894 to below 0.38085 is small, so a single local method that merely follows a hard active set is unlikely to be enough. The essential difficulty is that, at and near the current best parent, several shifted-overlap inequalities are nearly tied at the maximum. A hard LP/active-subgradient method can arbitrarily exclude or overweight these tied rows, while a hard-pair or SA method ignores the differentiability structure. The proposed method attacks this tied-active bottleneck with a differentiable but fully annealed soft-max model, then keeps a strictly exact-objective proof gate for every candidate actually returned.

Central algorithm: sequentially reduce a temperature/scale tau in
    F_tau(h) = tau log(sum_r exp(S_r(h)/tau)),
where S_r(h) are the exact shifted overlap scores with the evaluator’s dx scaling. As tau decreases, F_tau tends from a smooth average/mixture objective to the exact maximum C5. For each tau, perform a constrained, feasible, nonlinear line-search/annealing search on F_tau. At every provisional accepted step or during grid scanning, compute the exact C5 of the candidate vector and update the global exact best. Acceptance of an actual move is governed by either improvement of F_tau with controlled slack, or by exact C5 improvement; the sharp exact objective and exact feasibility of the final vector are the only final authorities.

Why it may improve the supplied parent:
- The smoothed model uses all shifted-overlap constraints together. When constraints are nearly tied, its gradient is essentially a convex combination of tied gradients; it can move them all down together instead of selecting one lag and harming others.
- The annealing schedule gives a natural continuation path from coarse basin exploration (large tau) to sharp minimax refinement (small tau), which is not a mere linear LP local step and not a random SA. It is a smooth objective continuation / trust-line method for a nonsmooth minimax problem.
- Exact line checks against the actual unknown nonconvex correlation function can recover improvements that a surrogate method alone would not confidently admit; surrogate evaluated vectors are only exact candidates.
- Resampling, perturbations, and comparison across compact sizes around the parent size exploit the fact that step-function optimal constructions may be better resolved or represented at nearby discretizations. Parent h is used whenever feasible and improved is only replaced by full strict validation.

New and substantively different elements from earlier proposals:
1. The external model is not a hard active set, coordinate/LP, pair, Fourier population, or random SA strategy by itself. It is an annealed differentiable smooth-max over the full correlation vector.
2. For all time during an annealed search, candidate exact overlap values are retained even when they are moved by smooth descent or random line checks; exact C5 is the global gate.
3. Constrained steps are generated by projected smooth-minus scalar gradient descent with transversality normalization and exact feasibility checking through the step polygon, in line search evaluated both in F_tau and C5.
4. Optional equality/active residual subsections may be used for a final “tie breaking” direction to safeguard if the annealed Softmax path stalls, but it is secondary. This method itself is executed independently for polynomials sizes with optional directions generated either by annealing, random exact lines, or exact active equality least squares.
5. A second generic exploration class (not global optimum): a hidden SLSQP run can also be requested if SciPy is available. If not, rejected gracefully; the method must still operate independently. Do not rely on a global solver.

Bottleneck addressed: tied inequalities. The soft maximizer proposal relaxes and forces tied constraints to reduce their logit-sum to a common level for improving. Also the annealing schedule makes it possible to move in a broad basin then refine the hard upper minmax level without explicitly declaring things. Maps from parent constructions immediately republish initial constraints. They derive "pro bono" by enforcing exact feasibility point, repeated acceptance collects consistent overlap.

Important limitations:
- The objective S_r(h) values remain nonconvex because contain bilinear h_i h_j terms. This is not global optimality. loops exact sanitizer returns strict feasible best.
- Smooth objectives do not guarantee exact bounds relaxing. Many trials lower `F` discontinuous but raise degrees. Nonlinear continuation base alleviates reconciliation change. Terms are measured independently, not reversed. Multiparning search levels minimization preserving estimates.
