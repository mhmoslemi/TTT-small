1. Approach and rationale

The parent construction used by the official evaluator is already a high-accuracy point for one fixed grid resolution. The remaining task is therefore not to generate another first-order direction; it is to escape a narrow local plateau of the discrete minimax objective and then close a very small gap of order 1e-4 or 1e-5. The method proposed below is materially different from the earlier deterministic active-LP, SLSQP epigraph, and soft-max/Levenberg proposals. Its primary search subprocess is an exact-objective stochastic annealing process over mass-preserving lattice configurations. The lattice construction is not a surrogate: every move is converted to a valid continuous vector and is judged only by the official objective `np.correlate(h, 1-h, "full")) * 2/n`. The lattice/annealing part is used to generate diverse nonient basins near the parent basins and at other resolutions. Deterministic continuous line-play and pair exchanges are inserted as accelerators from those annealed basins.

The key opportunities are:

1. The evaluator is exact and simple: `np.correlate` over arrays of length up to about 180 is cheap in optimized C. A large number of exact objective validations is feasible.

2. Mass constraint `sum(h)=n/2` can be enforced exactly by exchange moves: increase one coordinate and decrease another while preserving box bounds. This permits a Markov search that never leaves the feasible manifold before candidate acceptance.

3. The finite objective is a max over many positive terms. In an exact local maximum plateau, pure randomized moves that strictly decrease the max are scarce. Annealing with a temporary always-new-current objective can still accept small uphill moves, thereby exploring other nearby plateaus. Best-only acceptance is retained separately for the returned construction.

4. The lattice representation with levels `q_i ∈ {0,1,...,m}` where `h_i=q_i/m` gives a controllable discretization of feasible probability-like functions. A given lattice can have lattice steps `1/m` corresponding to physical diagonal objective changes of roughly `(2/n)/m` or `(2/n)/m^2` after product terms, and multiple `m` values plus multiple `n` values let the search cross both the parent smooth fractional range and the integer/binary discretization traps.

5. Parent transformations provide a known valid fallback. No result is ever accepted unless the exact evaluator confirms feasibility and a better or tie-preferred smaller sequence. All nonaccepted trial states become possible annealing starting points and elite feeders.
