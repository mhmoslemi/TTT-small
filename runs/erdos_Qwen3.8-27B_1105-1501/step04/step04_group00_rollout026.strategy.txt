1.1 Central idea

The supplied repeatedly-optimized continuous vector appears to sit near a discretized minimum of a finite-support minimax problem. A pure continuous local method often has difficulty moving microscopically because a large set of shifted correlation constraints has become nearly tied. This proposal does not open by staying in that basin. Its first substantive move is a combinatorial *sequence-design search*: solve finite binary/integer template problems formulated directly for the exact evaluator values. A binary optimum for a structured shift pattern can reveal orderings, boundary transitions, and orientations that are not reachable from the parent by short gradients. Each template is then projected to a feasible continuous representation and passed to a small, exact-aware refinement chain. The final value returned is always the value of a fully validated feasible vector.
