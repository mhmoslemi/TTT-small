Let me think about the most efficient implementation:

For a binary sequence h, the overlap at lag d (d ≥ 0) is:

The C₅ = max_d overlap(d) * dx, where d ranges over -(n-1), ..., n-1.
But by symmetry of the "full" correlation, we need to check both positive and negative lags.

Actually, for the "full" correlation:
corr[n-1+d] for d ≥ 0: sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i] * (1-h[i+d])
corr[n-1-d] for d ≥ 0: sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i+d] * (1-h[i])

These are different values in general! So I need to compute both.

corr[n-1+d] = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i] - sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i]*h[i+d]
corr[n-1-d] = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i+d] - sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i]*h[i+d]

Note that:
corr[n-1+d] - corr[n-1-d] = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i] - sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i+d]

So if I know one and the difference, I know both.

Let me define:
A(d) = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i]*h[i+d] (the "autocorrelation" at lag d)
B(d) = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i] (prefix sum of h up to n-1-d)
C(d) = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-d} h[i+d] (prefix sum shifted)

Then:
corr[n-1+d] = B(d) - A(d)
corr[n-1-d] = C(d) - A(d)

And C₅ = dx * max(max_d(B(d)-A(d)), max_d(C(d)-A(d))) for d=0,...,n-1
(where d=0 gives corr[n-1] = sum h[i](1-h[i]) = 0 for binary)
