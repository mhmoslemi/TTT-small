Equivalently, for lag `s`:

`F_beta(h) = (1/beta) * log(sum_p exp(beta * V[p]))`.

- Compute `M = max(beta * V[p])`.
- Compute `w[p] = exp(beta * V[p] - M)`, normalized by `W = sum_p w[p]`.
- `F_beta(h) = M/beta + log(W)/beta`.
