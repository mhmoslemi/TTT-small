1. Approach and rationale
-------------------------------

1.1 Central idea
The supplied parent is a strong 60-sample construction produced mainly by smoothed/projected descent and active-set linear programming. Those methods are excellent in a basin once the overlap structure is locally stationary, but they can become trapped because the full objective is a maximum of bilinear overlap terms. The main geometric bottleneck is therefore global basin discovery, not raw evaluation precision.

The proposed algorithm separates the two tasks explicitly:

- A genuinely new globalizer searches a small family of smooth single-period functions with a Fourier/DCT coefficient model. The search is over low-dimensional coefficient vectors, not over the full 60-dimensional vector by a smoothed subgradient method. Every coefficient vector is mapped to a truly feasible sampled vector, and the accepted quality is the exact official finite-support correlation score. This explores shapes that are difficult to reach by iterative smoothing of the parent.
- After a basin is discovered, the full vector is polished with exact accepted improvements using two mechanisms: (a) constrained two-coordinate exchange line minimization, where a pair of coordinates is updated as `h[i] = x`, `h[j] = S - x`, preserving the measure constraint up to a final joint feasibility correction; and (b) a small linearized active-overlap linear program, which is solved locally, mapped back through the same exact feasibility repair, and accepted only if the exact official score improves.
- The same best shape is periodically transferred to nearby resolution targets. The exact objective is recomputed for every target resolution using the official evaluator for that resolution. Thus a physically better vector at the same resolution remains the true best, and another feasible discrete vector at a different allowed resolution can be returned if its exact score is better. It is much larger in scope than parametric sweep alone and it will ensure 알고 any parent is retained as a fallback.

1.2 What makes this materially different
The primary method is no longer a projected coordinate descent over the current 60-sample vector. It is a coefficient-space stochastic global search producing diverse admissible continuous shapes. It is also different from the earlier proposed “active-set linearization first” programs because linear programming is only local and exact-validated polishing.

The new search is:
- DCT/Fourier: low-dimensional smooth global structure.
- Pairwise exchange: effective discrete steepest direction moves.
- Pair-lefst LP: locally flattens top overlaps within particular basin shapes.
- Multi-resolution families: better resolution can be found and preserved.

1.3 Limitations and assumptions
There is no guarantee of solving a global NLP in coefficient space; the stochastic evolution is a heuristic. Low-order bases cannot represent all sharp single-particle boundaries, but the feasibility map may replicate saturated thresholds as an approximation to h shapes. Resolution changes use official score as exact physical comparison; the exact objective functional actually imposes different discretizations, okay as task setup. We clearly label low-dim search as a proposal; acceptance/return is always exact official score.
