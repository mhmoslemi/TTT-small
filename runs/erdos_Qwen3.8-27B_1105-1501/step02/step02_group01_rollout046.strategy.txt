1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

The authoritative objective is the exact finite-support correlation

  C5(h) = dx * max_{ell=0,...,2n-2} np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode="full")[ell], dx = 2/n.

Every accepted candidate must therefore be judged by recomputing this exact quantity after any continuous or combinatorial update. No slider or autocorrelation-only surrogate is allowed as the final accept/reject rule.

The previous approach relied heavily on simulated annealing and general non-smooth minimization. The new approach is materially different: it treats the nonconvex minimax objective as a correlated combinatorial-energy optimization over balanced binary or half-binary representative strings and performs exact multi-coordinate local improvements inside contiguous blocks. For a fixed block, it exhaustively minimizes the exact objective over all feasible binary permutations inside that block while fixing all outside coordinates and preserving the global sum exactly. This is a true multi-swap coordinate minimization, not a pair move, simulated-annealing stochastic move, or SLSQP step.

The algorithm has two complementary search layers:

1. Discrete exact-block layer:
   - Convert feasible vectors to balanced discrete forms:
     * even length: 0/1 values with exactly n/2 ones,
     * odd length: floor(n/2) ones, one 0.5, and the rest zeros when using the optional odd discrete layer.
   - Run local exact block coordinate minimization over all feasible assignments inside powers of blocks. This can move many boundaries at once and escape barriers that overcome pair swapping.

2. Continuous smoothing polish:
   - Apply smooth log-sum-exp approximation around the exact correlation and solve constrained SLSQP problems to allow small fractional improvements.
   - Every SLSQP result is rebuilt feasible and then exactly rescored; it is accepted only if exact C5 decreases.

The goal is to preserve the supplied parent construction as a correct baseline, generate special balanced discrete solutions from it, improve them with exact block moves, and recover the outline flexibility by scores.

1.2 Why this might improve on the parent

For binary strings, the correlation values are exact region counts computed using np.correlate and the objective is a relatively sharp boundary estimate. The worst objective-lag structure usually comes from mismatch boundaries and thus multiline swap patterns: flipping several isolated coordinates or repositioning several balanced ones simultaneously improves.
