1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Primary idea
Use a new unconstrained, exactly-feasible, smooth-minimax reformulation in a reparametrized search space and use the exact finite-support evaluator to accept or reject every candidate. The central idea is not to implement another constrained local solve in the original h-space, but to replace the constraint system by a low-dimensional map that preserves sum and box constraints while leaving the objective smooth enough for gradient-based descent.

Represent every interior feasible vector by a raw logistic vector q plus a scalar λ, selected so that

   h_i(q, λ) = σ(q_i - λ),   sum_i h_i = n/2,

where σ is an overflow-safe logistic function. The scalar λ changes dynamically during evaluation so the equality constraint remains satisfied after unconstrained changes to q. This removes projection and multiplier correction from the core descent loop. Then smooth the maximum over all finite-lag correlation terms by a log-sum-exp surrogate.
