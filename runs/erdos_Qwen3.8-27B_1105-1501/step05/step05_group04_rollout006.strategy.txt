1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

The evaluator is a nonconvex minimax optimization problem

C5(h) = max_r dx * [np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode="full")]_r,

over vectors h in [0,1]^n with sum(h)=n/2. The supplied parent near C5 ≈ 0.380892 is already close to a local optimum in a conventional single-direction minimax direction framework. The main bottleneck is therefore not the objective evaluation itself, but that many near-active lags must move together and can leave a flat first-order plateau while still admitting tiny nonlinear improvements.

The proposed method is a "certified target descent" method. Instead of repeatedly choosing a feasible direction and performing a line search along that direction, the algorithm repeatedly forms an epigraph target problem with a candidate objective level B and variable t:

B = current_realizable_value - Δ,
