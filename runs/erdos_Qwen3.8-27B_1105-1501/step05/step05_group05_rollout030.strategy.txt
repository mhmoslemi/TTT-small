1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea
The supplied parent is not treated as the sole search domain. Instead, the algorithm searches a different feasible coordinate system that makes the box and mass constraints automatic, while still judging every proposed candidate with the exact evaluator. The central representation is:

\[
h_i(\theta)=\sigma(v_i+\theta),\qquad \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}},
\]

with a scalar \(\theta\) chosen by one-dimensional root solving so that

\[
\sum_i \sigma(v_i+\theta)=n/2.
\]

Every finite \(v\in\mathbb{R}^n\) therefore produces an exactly mass-balanced candidate satisfying \(0\le h_i\le 1\) (up to numerical roots of tolerance), without clipping followed by rescaling. The scalar \theta either absorbs the mass change or, if needed, a final joint water-level projection is used. This is materially different from the parent LP, trust-region, linearized active-set, or direct h-space transfer methods because integer-like boundary patterns, soft fractional patterns, and their rescalings are all connected through smooth root-preserving moves, and every new shape can be judged exactly.

The primary search mechanism is an exact-evaluator Logit Evolution Strategy (LES). A population of \(v\)-vectors is mutated and recombined, each vector is transformed to an exact feasible \(h\) by the root rule, and the official score is computed by the actual correlation objective. The population contains diverse initializations and repeated operators: coordinate mutations, value exchanges/swaps, interval cells, crosses, cyclic shifts, reflections, soft-gradient directions, direct pair transfer lines in h-space, and resolution transforms. The cumulative LES is intentionally not claiming optimality; acceptance is exact and global.

1.2 Why this can improve the parent
The parent value is close to \(0.380893\). The desired value is slightly below \(0.3808\), i.e. it needs a precise improvement of less than \(10^{-4}\). This kind of improvement is difficult for one local linearization because at the parent many lag scores are near-maximal, and boundary variables may stall because \(h_i(1-h_i)\) is tiny or zero. LES addresses two bottlenecks:

- Boundary/locality: In h-space, moving mass between near-boundary variables is mechanically awkward and can over-relax in clipped projection; the logistic vector encodes the same change in an unconstrained coordinate system without clipping/rescaling and reduces artificial boundary attraction.
- Plateaus: Randomized exchanges and mutation around near-binary levels can traverse flat silent regions where tiny local h modifications cannot move the max active score. Population crossover also captures beneficial spatial patterns from different modest-threshold candidates. The exact pair/line polish afterward can return a clean boundary-respecting descent direction.

This is a heuristic optimization, not proof of optimality. Every returned candidate is the best candidate that passes exact feasibility and exact score evaluation. No surrogate is ever allowed to store an answer based on surrogate miss; all returns are verified against the actual evaluator.

1.3 Scope and factual limits
Facts governing the specification:
- The executable evaluator is finite-support linear correlation, not circular/cyclic correlation. The objective is
  \[
  C_5(h)=\max_k\,(np.correlate(h,1-h,mode="full"))\cdot(2/n),
  \]
  including all \(2n-1\) lag rows and both sign directions. If a circular shift is used, it is a direct array shift of the discrete function under evaluator scoring, no wrap-around implication in evaluation.
- Feasibility requires \(0\le h_i\le 1\) and sum exactly about \(n/2\). Simple clip/rescale is rejected because it trivially destroys the exact mass; use root mass solving for \(v\)-vectors and an exact joint projection for arbitrary interpolated or repaired arrays.

Hypotheses:
- A better point may be present in a boundary/Lobular parameter neighborhood or at another resolution \(n\).
- Exact validators score critical structures collectively as candidates at that vector resolution. There is no continuity across different \(n\) values as this discrete mathematical partition may yield a slightly smaller C for smaller \(n\). The algorithm evaluates all lengths explicitly and accepts only where exact score is lower or equal with smaller length.
- The LES is not globally guaranteed, and no nonconvex convex or MILP relaxation is claimed to produce reliable global bounds directly. Any proposal that fails feasibility or exact evaluation is discarded.
