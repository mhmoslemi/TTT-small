1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea
The principal method is an exchange-atom exact minimax descent. At every iteration a candidate is judged only by the authoritative evaluator
`raw = np.correlate(h, 1.0-h, mode="full")`, multiplied by `dx=2.0/n`, and no surrogate is ever accepted as a final score. The search improvements are produced on exact feasible zero-sum faces of the box-simplex `0 <= h_i <= 1`, `sum(h)=n/2`. The most important exact face used here is a two-coordinate transfer
`h_i -> h_i - t`, `h_j -> h_j + t`, and, because the raw objective is a bilinear function of `h`, the full curve of every raw lag value along this face is exactly quadratic in the transfer variable. Therefore the current best possible decrease of the evaluated objective by any one exchange pair can be found by a one-dimensional global minimization of an exact upper envelope of quadratics. This is a materially different local operation from a linearized LP, a smooth-log-sum-exp descent, a proximal gASTEP-down projection, or a purely random pair transfer; it directly explores whether any feasible finite transfer of one coordinate to another can reduce the true evaluated maximum on the current grid. The same model-line line search is augmented with low-relaxed three-coordinate integer transfer atoms so the method cannot be confined to simple binary exchange directions.

1.2 Why this may improve the supplied parent
The parent is at a relatively low finite resolution (`n` around 120), and a family of continuous and linearized local stages are stopped close to stationarity in those derivative/LP neighborhoods. Three objects that this plan specifically attacks:

- Resolution mismatch: the parent grid may not be the grid that the exact evaluator operation rewards. We evaluate final candidates only in their own evaluator, but the search deliberately migrates between compatible resolutions and compares only exactly the same thing. The search still evaluates each candidate by the authoritative evaluator; it does not make an invalid lower-dimensional claim.

- Coordinate-pair facethickness停滞: a coordinate descent that only checks a first-order tangent on a line may conclude that no adjacent move helps, especially when the location of several active maxima slips as soon as the transfer begins. The 1D exact minimax curve sees the whole feasible interval, so it can accept a finite batch that moves around a nonlocal peak exactly.

- Structural local stickiness: smooth surrogate or subgradient directions do not exploit zeros of the exact raw objective. A massed deterministic pool plus random coarse movers plus low-file/fs巅 seeds provoke fresh basin starts while each tangible update is exact.

1.3 Coherent primary approach and limitations
The board is a hybrid:
- **primary local:** deterministic pool of two-coordinate transfer faces, exact object-model golden/grid minimization, followed by three-coordinate exact line hits leading to a 2D coordinate schema;
- **global/resolution board:** resolution sweep from reconstructed parent and from self-generated seeds; simulated/kicked broadcasting from improved histories;
- **hard accept rule:** every committed move is rechecked by the exact evaluator and feasible solver/check, with no reliance on the model or projection to prove a bound.

This is still an ab initio local/non-lnodd metaheuristic.
