- Increase h[i+k*] where h[i] = 1 (reduces (1-h[i+k*]))

The key insight: if we decrease h[i] and increase h[i+k*], we reduce c[k*] directly (both terms help). But we might increase other lags.

- new_h[i] = h[i] - delta
