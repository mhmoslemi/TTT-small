The hard part is that the continuum of quadratic functions C_d has an indefinite bilinear curvature; direct convex programming cannot be used without changing the problem.

The proposed method is a hybrid direct-hit minimax optimizer whose primary search engine is not single-coordinate random sampling or an unverified convex relaxation. It is a non-smooth active-plane trust procedure:

- dynamically identify only the constraints that make up the current maximum;
- solve a small linear program (LP) to find a feasible descent direction that lowers all active linearized constraints by a common margin, while respecting feasibility;
- validate every proposal under the exact evaluator;
- exploit this fast local descent for multiple starting points;
- escape unproductive local orientations with a homotoped smooth-max local nonlinear optimization (analytic gradient, nonconvex, stored only after exact validation);
- fortifique candidate pools with a short stochastic anneal using the same exact objective.

The mechanism is materially different from generic pairwise line/random operators or a vaguely named solver call: because it works directly on the true nonsmooth epigraph structure of the exact finite-support overlap, and because the directions are produced from active-gradient Gram rows rather than from arbitrary geometric sampling, it can efficiently reduce an objective if its active functions shift gracefully in the polyline. Since the accepted score is always computed from the official expression and candidate feasibility is rechecked, this remains a rigorous safe-check rather than a claimed lower-bound algorithm.

1.2 Why this could help with C₅ around 0.38085
The parent construction is already very near a local optimum: local neighboring pair moves tend to rebalance the max, and the numerical lemma to be gone from 0.38085 to 0.3808 is extremely small. A useful search should not depend on prolonged random sampling (whose marginal improvements become noisy) nor a large multi-dimensional nonlinear system (which is unconstrained and expensive to secure). Active-plane descent directly optimizes the binding terms and ignores harmless inactive constraints; line search accounts for curvature and invalid model points; diversified starts and a short anneal maintain global reliability.

1.3 Errors and limitations
Because the objective is nonconvex, no activity is guaranteed to discover a greater-improvement-to (C≤0.3808). It implies no evidence from partial branch assumptions. Hence concurrency is qualitative heuristic; global optimum is neither claimed nor assumed. Every returned bound is a corruption proof bound: the vector is submitted after validation for exact maximum and feasibility constraints. Relaxed nonlinear optimizers assist only pursue diverse points; they do not claim proof, and any finite-arithmetic infeasible hidden quality is removed by projection.
