sum s[i+j] over i=0 to n-1-j = sum s[i'] for i'=j to n-1 = 0 - sum s[i'] for i'=0 to j-1

So c_j = [(0 - sum_{i=n-j}^{n-1} s[i]) - R_s[j] + (n-|j|) - (0 - sum_{i=0}^{j-1} s[i])] / 4
where R_s[j] = sum_{valid i} s[i]*s[i+j]

The peak of c_j * dx = (n-|j| - R_s[j])/4 * 2/n ≈ (1 - |j|/n - R_s[j]/n)/2 ≈ 1/2 - R_s[j]/(2n) for small |j|.
