1. Approach and rationale

The target is a tiny reduction of an already very strong upper bound, from about `0.38085` to `C5 <= 0.3808`. The difficulty is that the objective is the maximum of many nonconvex finite-support overlap correlations. A naive ”global” optimizer or a cvxpy epigraph program with arbitrary quadratic overlap terms is not usable: the overlap entries are bilinear and not DCP-compatible, and a claim of global convexity would be false.

The proposed method is an exact-objective active-set local descent framework:

- Every candidate is scored with the true formula
  `dx * max(np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full"))`.
- Feasibility is handled with a joint projection/integer-grid mechanism that preserves `0 <= h_i <= 1` and `sum(h) = N/2`, rather than successful clipping and rescaling in succession.
- At each point, the constraints that are close to the current maximum are identified. For these active/near-active constraints, an exact derivative of each finite-support correlation is computed analytically.
- A small linear program proposes a feasible-perturbation direction that simultaneously lowers all selected active overlap values in a first-order model while preserving total area. - The predicted descent direction is tested with exact line search and dyadic feasibility repair. Only candidates whose exact overlap objective strictly improves are accepted.
- A secondary exact epigraph SQP/SLSQP stage uses the same analytic gradients on the selected active constraints. This helps when the linearized descent subproblem has degeneracy but a nearby variable change in the nonconvex constraints still exists.
- Pair transfer and randomized dyadic searches are used as exact certificate-driven escape mechanisms: every move is feasible and every change is accepted only by exact evaluation.
- The method retreats to the parent, or a feasibility-repaired parent, if no verified improvement is found. No global optimality is claimed. This is a local refinement procedure plus controlled diversification, specialized for the tiny active-plateau bottleneck of the current construction.

Material differences from vague ”use a solver” or ”random search” plans:
