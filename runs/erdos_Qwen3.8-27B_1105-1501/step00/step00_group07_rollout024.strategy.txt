1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

The plan optimizes the true evaluator objective directly, but moves the search from the constrained Cartesian set
    {h : 0 <= h_i <= 1, sum_i h_i = n/2}
to a smooth unconstrained parameter manifold that makes the box and sum constraints constructive:
    h_i(theta) = phi(z_i + lambda)
where phi is a strictly increasing map into (0,1) and lambda is the unique root of
    sum_i phi(z_i + lambda) = n/2.
The parameter z is restricted to a low-to-medium-dimensional deterministic basis tree, with stages that grow the basis until the full available residual size is allowed. The optimizer is a population-memetic algorithm whose outer search is in the smooth parameter manifold and whose final acceptance is always performed using the exact evaluator formula
    C(h) = max_k correlate(h,1-h,"full")_k * (2/n).

This is materially different from exhaustive pair-transfer descent or pairwise stochastic exchange: mass is never moved from one Cartesian coordinate to another. Instead, the candidate is implicitly normalized to the required integral by the monotone map phi and then globally adapted through its unconstrained trend coefficients. Exact scoring is only used for fitness/acceptance and final repair, not for replacing the objective by circular or reduced autocorrelation formulas.

1.2 Why this can improve a supplied 95-sample parent

The 95-sample parent is probably very close to a flat local region. Random pairwise moves can stagnate because the score is a maximum over many correlation lags and many nearby single coordinate perturbations are worthless in the maximum. The smooth manifold changes the local geometry. Optimizing the nonconstrained latent trend z can move many coordinates in a shaped, mass-preserving way determined by a global basis. The family provides low-trend versions for secure bracketing, and near-full basis versions for local fine shaping. A seed obtained by a least-squares inverse-logit fit of the parent gives a nontrivial representative of the parent-derived manifold; subsequent moving of the coordinates coefficients cannot simply return the fixed un-debiased for exactly reshaping over large trajectories while objective balancing remains exact in the generated feasible histogram.

The multistage basis evolves many feasible output states rather than estimating raw vector gradient at a single plateau. Exact evaluation of the finite-window objective at each generated trajectory prevents surrogate, individual parameterization from dominating the final bounds.
