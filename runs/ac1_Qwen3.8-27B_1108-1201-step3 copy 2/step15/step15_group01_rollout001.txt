Actually, wait. I just realized something. Let me re-examine the formula.

score = 2 * n * max_b / (sum_a)^2

For the uniform sequence: max_b = n * (S/n)^2 = S^2/n. So score = 2*n*(S^2/n)/S^2 = 2.

For ANY sequence, by the arithmetic mean - geometric mean inequality or just basic algebra:
conv[n-1] = sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a[j] * a[n-1-j]

If a is symmetric (a[j] = a[n-1-j]), then conv[n-1] = sum a[j]^2 >= (sum a)^2 / n by Cauchy-Schwarz.

But if a is NOT symmetric, conv[n-1] = sum a[j] * a[n-1-j] can be less than (sum a)^2 / n!

By the rearrangement inequality, sum a[j] * a[n-1-j] is minimized when one sequence is sorted in ascending order and the other in descending order. Since a[n-1-j] is the reverse of a, the sum is minimized when a is "anti-sorted" relative to its reverse, i.e., when a is monotonically increasing (or decreasing).

So for a monotonically increasing sequence, conv[n-1] < (sum a)^2 / n.
