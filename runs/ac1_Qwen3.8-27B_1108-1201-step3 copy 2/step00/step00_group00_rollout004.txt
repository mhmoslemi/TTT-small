For a distribution concentrated near its mean (small variance), like a uniform on [1-ε, 1+ε]:
μ ≈ 1, σ^2 ≈ ε^2/3, score ≈ 2(1+ε^2/3) ≈ 2.

So random sequences with low variance are close to uniform and give score ≈ 2.
