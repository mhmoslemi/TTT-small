### 1. Approach and Rationale
We will use a coordinate-wise optimization algorithm with large step acceptance (CS-CO) to systematically improve our candidate sequence. CS-CO balances exploration and exploitation by accepting large steps when they yield demonstrable improvement, while gradually tightening the search window to avoid premature convergence. This approach starts by initializing with the parent sequence (if available) and then iteratively updates coordinates based on their individual impact, stopping when no improvement is being made.

### 2. Mathematical Specification
We represent our sequences as elements of the probability simplex (non-negative entries summing to 1). The objective is defined as:
$$ f(a) = 2n \cdot \max_{k \in \{0, \dots, 2n-2\}} (a * a)[k] $$
where $(a * a)[k]$ denotes the full convolution at lag $k$. We denote the objective at a given sequence $a$ as $f(a)$.

Our update rule presents at each iteration:
1. Select a coordinate $j$ and consider updating it to $x^+\exp\left(-\frac{\beta}{2}\right) for various $\beta$ (which we understand as the boosting degree).
2. For each candidate $x^+$, compute the new full convolution $c^+ = \text{evaluate\_convolve}(a \text{ with } x^+ \text{ at position } j)$
3. Evaluate $f(x^+)$ and compare it against the current $f(a)$.
