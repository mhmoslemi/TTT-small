    # Math background for the vector generation:

    Given vectors x in [0, 1]^{n}, the L-infinity norm of their autocorrelation is \(\max_k x^{\star} x = \| \text{autocorrelation} \|_{\infty}\), where the notation is \( x[k] = \sum_{j} x[j] x[k-j] \).

    The score we want to minimize is \( S(x) = 2 n \|x^{\star}\|_{\infty} / (\sum x)^2 \).

    We wish to \( \min_x S(x) \).

    How to relate the wavelet analysis to the optimization?

    We can consider a wavelet threshold approach (similar to soft-threshold). In the wavelet domain, the \(\ell_2\) norm might be easier to control.

    However, we are constrained by the L-infinity norm of the autocorrelation.

    Let's consider the dual formulation:
