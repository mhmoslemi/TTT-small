Wait, conv_full(a, w)[k] = sum_j a[j] * w[k-j]
Setting k = i: sum_j a[j] * w[i-j] = term2[i] ✓ (with the understanding that w[k-j] = 0 for k-j < 0 or k-j >= 2n-1)
