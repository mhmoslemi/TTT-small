   Now for the LSE optimization in log-space:

   The log-space approach: let $a_i = e^{x_i}$. The objective is:
   $J(x) = 2n \cdot \max_k C_k(x) / S(x)^2$
   
   where $C_k(x) = \sum_i e^{x_i} e^{x_{k-i}}$ and $S(x) = \sum_i e^{x_i}$.

   Since $J$ is scale-invariant (adding a constant to all $x_i$ doesn't change $J$), we can fix $\sum x_i = 0$ (or $x_0 = 0$).

   For the LSE approximation:
   $\text{LSE}_\tau(C) = \frac{1}{\tau} \log \sum_k e^{\tau C_k}$

   Gradient: $\nabla_x \text{LSE}_\tau(C) = \sum_k w_k \nabla_x C_k$ where $w_k = e^{\tau C_k} / \sum_j e^{\tau C_j}$.

   $\nabla_{x_i} C_k = e^{x_i} e^{x_{k-i}} + e^{x_{k-i}} e^{x_i}$ if $i \neq k-i$ (and both valid)
   $= e^{2x_i}$ if $2i = k$

   More precisely: $C_k = \sum_j e^{x_j} e^{x_{k-j}}$ (valid indices only).
   $\partial C_k / \partial x_i = e^{x_i} e^{x_{k-i}} \cdot \mathbf{1}[0 \leq k-i < n] + e^{x_{k-i}} e^{x_i} \cdot \mathbf{1}[0 \leq k-i < n]$
   $= 2 e^{x_i} e^{x_{k-i}} \cdot \mathbf{1}[0 \leq k-i < n]$ (if $i \neq k-i$)
   $= e^{2x_i} \cdot \mathbf{1}[k = 2i]$ (if $i = k-i$, i.e., $k = 2i$)

   This can be computed efficiently using FFT:
   $\sum_k w_k \partial C_k / \partial x_i = 2 \sum_k w_k e^{x_{k-i}} \cdot \mathbf{1}[0 \leq k-i < n] \cdot e^{x_i}$
   $= 2 e^{x_i} \sum_k w_k e^{x_{k-i}} \cdot \mathbf{1}[0 \leq k-i < n]$

   The inner sum is a cross-correlation of $w$ and $a$ (where $a_i = e^{x_i}$):
   $\sum_k w_k a_{k-i} \cdot \mathbf{1}[0 \leq k-i < n]$

   This is the convolution of $w$ and $a$ at a specific index!

   $(w * a)[m] = \sum_k w_k a_{m-k}$

   So $\sum_k w_k a_{k-i} = (w * a)_{2... }$ hmm, let me be more careful.

   $\sum_k w_k a_{k-i}$ where $0 \leq k-i < n$ and $0 \leq k < 2n-1$.

   Let $j = k - i$. Then $j$ ranges over valid indices of $a$, and $k = j + i$.
   $\sum_j w_{j+i} a_j \cdot \mathbf{1}[0 \leq j+i < 2n-1]$

   This is the cross-correlation $\sum_j w_{j+i} a_j = (w \circ a)_i$ where $\circ$ denotes cross-correlation.

   Cross-correlation can be computed via FFT: $\text{IFFT}(\text{FFT}(w) \cdot \overline{\text{FFT}(a)})$.

   So the gradient is:
   $\nabla_{x_i} \text{LSE} = 2 e^{x_i} \cdot (\text{cross-correlation of } w \text{ and } a)_i$

   And the gradient of the log-sum: $\nabla_{x_i} \log S = e^{x_i} / S$.
