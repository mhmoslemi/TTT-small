### Approach and Rationale

The strategy starts with a randomized initialization, then applies a cycle of optimizations. Spectral shaping is enhanced by targeting the first 5% of the spectrum, which allows quicker identification of frequency-based weaknesses. In each optimization cycle, Poland is updated with an injection of LP-optimized responses. This balances global and local search, leveraging the Adam optimizer for gradient-driven steps and the LP solver for maximizing spectral flatness.

The algorithm:
1. **Initialization**: Use a set of four distinct initialization patterns to cover more dense solution spaces.
2. **Optimization cycle**: For each length from 1000 to 5000:
   - Apply adaptive spectral shaping to aggressively reduce low-frequency autocorrelation peaks.
   - Run Adam optimization (with high learning rate) but also perform an LP step in every iteration to introduce more aggressive activeset-driven updates.
   - This combination allows faster improvement than the previous phase-by-phase optimization.
3. **Phase wrapping**: After optimizing at a given length, interpolate to a longer version for further improvement if time allows.
4. **Post-optimization polish**: Apply peak shaving on the best candidate found (sorted) to reduce unnecessary peaks, then renormalize.
5. **Termination check**: Before termination, verify arrangements through the adaptive Adam optimization cycle and post-optimal interpolation.
