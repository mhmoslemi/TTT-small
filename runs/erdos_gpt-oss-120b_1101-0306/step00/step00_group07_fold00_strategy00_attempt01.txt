1. Approach and rationale

Central idea. Treat the discrete problem as a constrained minimax problem in vector `h/book` with the exact discrete objective equal to the supplied evaluator. The integer normalization and box constraints are maintained by a single joint projection/repair routine; every optimization proposal is accepted only after re-evaluation with the original evaluator formula, not with a surrogate. The method improves a validated parent solution by combining four local mechanisms:

- A smooth log-sum-exp surrogate of the exact maximum, with an exact gradient derived from the same term indexing as the correlation objective.
- Exact objective evaluation along feasible tangent lines obtained from that gradient.
- Coordinate mass-transfer line search, which directly evaluates many one-pair mass movements in the feasible box-simplex.
- An epigraph nonlinear-programming polish using an exact large-scale inequality formulation and scipy's local SLSQP handle; failures are accepted only after projection and official re-evaluation.

Why this could improve the supplied parent. The current parent is near a constrained local optimum, so only small but targeted movements of one or more coordinates are likely to help. A direct unconstrained or penalty-based optimizer would not preserve the required integral; automatic finite-difference only computations might be slow. A tangent-space projected gradient down a smooth maximum gives a descent direction for the unresolved max objective, while exact coordinate line search can exploit non-smooth active sets and fractional boundary exchanges. The epigraph SLSQP formulation is included because it can exploit multiple active lags simultaneously, but no global result is claimed: all terminology is local, and official re-evaluation of candidates gives a proveable returned bound.

Bottleneck addressed. The exact objective is non-smooth because it is the maximum over all `r=2*n_points-1` correlation lags. Supplied `h` must remain exact in both `0 <= h_i <= 1` and `sum(h) = n/2`. The plan addresses this with: a placeholder for constraint/friction; a feasible gradient tangent space; exact joint normalization as constraints, and acceptance based exclusively on the original objective.

Facts versus heuristics. The term values, exact constraints, relationship to the evaluator, tangent-space properties, exact coordinate intervals, gradient formulas, projection monotonicity, and every returned validation condition are mathematical facts. Search parameters, choices between random and active coordinate pairs, stochastic move sizes, SLSQP maxiter/ftol and smoothing schedules are empirical choices to achieve fast local movement. They do not establish global optimality. If SLSQP converges, only its official final re-evaluated objective may be reported, not the SLSQP internal bound.
