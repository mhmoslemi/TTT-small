1. Approach and rationale
-------------------------

The supplied parent is a finite-dimensional sample of a step function on [0,2] with integral 1 under `dx=2/n`. Its current evaluator is approximately 0.38085. The goal is to return a fully feasible sampled vector `h` whose exact evaluator value is smaller, preferably below 0.3808.

The central plan is a deterministic hybrid local optimization scheme:

1. Preserve and exactly validate the parent using the evaluator formula. The parent is retained as a guaranteed valid fallback.
2. Optimize the true mini-max objective using an epigraph nonlinear program in a solver that accepts explicit equality constraints and analytic gradients. The epigraph minimizes a scalar t subject to `t` being at least each correlation value.
3. Run many cheap and medium-cost variants around the parent:
   - cyclically translated copies,
   - slightly perturbed continuous copies,
   - threshold/binarized copies,
   - two-sided integer randomizations from the parent scores,
   - coarse/fine transferred parent constructions,
   - special two-value feasible constructions.
4. Finish by exact evaluation, feasibility checking, local repair, and selection. Accept only candidates that pass the exact evaluator and constraints.

This addresses the task because the problem is bilinear and non-convex and a single initial point can be stuck in a local optimum near 0.38085. The epigraph method can move in objective space while respecting the finite-support correlation in all directions. The multiple randomized variants provide escape directions and can discover cheaper states that are then polished.

Important limitations:

- `scipy.optimize.minimize` SLSQP is a local continuous optimizer for a non-convex problem. It does not prove a global optimum. The objective is improved only by explicit re-evaluation after each candidate.
- The solver may return candidates that slightly violate bounds or the sum constraint after its internal tolerance. They are accepted only after exact feasibility validation and after their exact objective is recomputed.
- The construction must not represent circular correlation and must not replace the correlation maximum by a periodic autocorrelation. Boundary lags correspond to finite-support overlap pieces and are built into all derivative formulas.
