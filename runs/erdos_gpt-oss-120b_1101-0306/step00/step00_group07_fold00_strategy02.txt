1. Approach and rationale

Central idea. Treat the exact evaluation as a finite-support discrete problem whose objective is the maximum, over all integer lags, of one shifted bilinear expression. Maintain feasibility exactly during the core update step. Instead of searching only in single-coordinate-mass-transfer directions, or using a log-sum-exp surrogate, or throwing a raw nonlinear epigraph solver at the problem, use a trust-region convexified epigraph method: at the current vector h, approximate the finite number of objective terms by tangent affine planes, include only the currently threatening lags and a dynamic set of near-threatening lags, and solve a small linear program for a simultaneous decrement of the whole local maximum. After obtaining a proposed vector or direction, exact one-dimensional line search along that direction is used to recover high-quality feasible search points. Every candidate is accepted only by re-evaluating the actual task evaluator and after a valid feasible projection or repair.
