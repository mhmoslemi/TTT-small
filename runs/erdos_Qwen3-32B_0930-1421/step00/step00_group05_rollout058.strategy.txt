C₅ = max_k ( np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full") * dx )

Subject to:

0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1  
sum(h) * dx = 1

The method iteratively adjusts h to reduce the maximum overlap, enforcing constraints through clipping and normalization.

3. **Termination**:
