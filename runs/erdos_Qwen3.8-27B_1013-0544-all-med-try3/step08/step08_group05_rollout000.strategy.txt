**Approach and Rationale**

The goal is to minimize the Eard\Hős minimum overlap constant by optimizing a step function h: [0,2] → [0,1] with integral 1. The central idea is to represent h using piecewise constant segments and apply gradient-based optimization to minimize the maximum overlap with its shifts.

The initialization uses the provided parent solution, and the optimization leverages the gradient of the worst-case shift to efficiently guide the search. This approach balances between:

1. **Exploration**: Using multiple resolutions and restarts to escape local optima
2. **Exploitation**: Guided by the gradient calculated for the worst-case shift
3. **Constraint Handling**: Ensuring the solution maintains integral 1 and bounds [0,1] through projection

The key bottleneck addressed is the high dimensionality (number of grid points) which typically hinders optimization. This is mitigated by:

1. Progressive resolution scaling
2. Efficient gradient calculation using the worst-case shift
3. Restart mechanisms to escape local optima
