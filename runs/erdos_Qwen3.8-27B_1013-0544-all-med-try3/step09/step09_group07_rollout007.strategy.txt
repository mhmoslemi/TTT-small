   The idea is that the coarse resolution will find a good, but possibly not optimal, candidate that is still general. Then, as we increase resolution, we refine the candidate with the hope of improving the objective value.

   The main advantages are:

   - Coarse resolution optimization is faster.

   - Progressive refment allows us to leverage function evaluations from previous resolutions.

   - Fewer local optima in each resolution.
