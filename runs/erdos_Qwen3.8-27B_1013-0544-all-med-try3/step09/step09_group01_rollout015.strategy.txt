Instead of optimizing over all continuous shifts, we focus on shifts within [0,1] (or [0,2] with symmetry). 1. Reduces dimensionality by one (through per-period constraint)

3. May reveal previously missed symmetries in optimal solutions

The limitation is that we assume periodicity holds, which may sometimes constrain optimal solutions. But considering the problem structure, we'll validate any resulting solution with a non-periodic check.
