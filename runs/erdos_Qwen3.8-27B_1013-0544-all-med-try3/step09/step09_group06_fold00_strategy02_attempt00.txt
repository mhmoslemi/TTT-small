        C5 = -np.max(c_val)  (minimize max overlap)

   Gradients derived by:

        δC5/δβ_j = 2·1.0·(1-coeff)*offset·(-(β_j-i)·exp(-(β_j-i)²))·γ^(-(β_j-i)^2)

   where coeff are correlation coefficients, offset depends on lag.

   This derivation only applies locally but suffices for Newton-like search.
