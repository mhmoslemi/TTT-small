**Variables and Constraints**:  
- Let \( h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1] \) be piecewise constant, approximated by \( h \) on \( n \) points.  
- Constraint: \( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i] \cdot dx = 1 \) (where \( dx = 2/n \)).  
- Objective: \(\min \max_{k} \int h(x)(1-h(x+k)) \, dx\).  

**Representation**: Use sample-based \( h \) vectors of arbitrary length \( n \), interpolating between resolution steps.  

**Objective Function**:  
The overlap at shift \( k \):  
\[
C_k = dx \cdot \sum_{i} h[i] \cdot (1 - h[(i + k + \text{shift\_center}) \mod n])
\]  
where shift_center ensures centered correlation (here, by \( -(n-1) \)).  

**Optimization Steps**:  
- Compute gradients at each point using finite differences:  
  \[
  \nabla h[i] = \frac{\partial}{\partial h[i]} \left( \sum_{k} dx \cdot h[i] \cdot (1 - h[(i + k + \text{shift\_center}) \mod n]) \right)
  \]
- Update \( h \) proportionally to \(-\nabla h\) (gradient descent) but scale by a learning rate.  
- After updating, clip the vector to \([0, 1]\) and adjust globally if the sum \( \sum h[i] \cdot dx = 1 \).
