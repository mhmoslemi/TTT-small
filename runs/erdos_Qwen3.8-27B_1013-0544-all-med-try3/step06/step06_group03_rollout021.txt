**Algorithm: Projected Gradient Descent with Log-Sum-Exp**

1. Initialize h
2. For each iteration:
   a. Compute f_k for all k
   b. Compute softmax weights w_k = exp(beta*f_k) / sum exp(beta*f_k)
   c. Compute gradient: grad_j = sum_k w_k * ∂f_k/∂h_j
   d. Take step: h_new = h - eta * grad
   e. Project to feasible set: clip to [0,1] and adjust to meet sum constraint
