**2. Mathematical Specification**

- **Representation**: A step function \( h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1] \) is discretized as a vector \( \mathbf{h} = (h_0, h_1, \dots, h_{n-1}) \) at n points, with \( x_i = i \cdot \frac{2}{n} \) for \( i=0,1,\dots,n-1 \), goal: \( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i \frac{2}{n} = 1 \).
- **Objective**: Minimize \( C_5 = \max_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} \int_{0}^{2} h(x)(1 - h(x+k)) dx \). Since the interval is finite, k ranges such that \( x+k \in [0,4] \), but discretization approximates this max. For a \emph{valid} \(\mathbf{h}\), \( C_5 = dx \cdot \max_k \text{cross-correlation} \), with \( dx = \frac{2}{n} \). In discrete terms, \( C_5 \) is the maximum (scaled) cross-correlation value obtained from \( \text{np.correlate}(\mathbf{h}, 1 - \mathbf{h}, \text{mode}='full') \).

- **Constraints**:
  - Sum constraint: \( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i = \frac{n}{2} \).
  - Box constraints: \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \) for all i.

- **Feasibility**: The function vector must satisfy both the sum and the box constraints after optimization. Any updates should apply the projection described in `project_feasible` to maintain feasibility.

- **Key Formulas**:
  - **Overlap computation**: For each lag k, the discretized correlation is \( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k} h_i (1 - h_{i+k}) \) if \( k \) is valid (non-wrap, mode 'full' handles lags without circularity). The max is evaluated over all lags, then scaled by \( dx = \frac{2}{n} \).
  - **Formulation for COBYLA**: Define the objective as \( C_5(\mathbf{h}) \), add the constraint \( g(\mathbf{h}) = \sum h_i - \frac{n}{2} = 0 \), and use bounds for the box constraints. COBYLA minimizes a nonlinear objective with linear constraints and bounds.

DFO does not assume convexity; the sum constraint is global, and \( C_5 \) is non-convex. COBYLA finds feasible points without derivatives, ensuring the solver can evaluate \( C_5 \) at any candidate vector.
