Σ_{k=0}^{n-1} w_k^+ * (1-h[j-k]) * 1[k≤j] where w_k^+ = w_{n-1+k}
= Σ_{k=0}^{j} w_k^+ * (1-h[j-k])
= (w^+ * (1-h))[j] (cross-correlation, truncated)
