    - Smooth binary representation: Instead of treating h as continuous, we use a sigmoid function to map a continuous variable to [0,1]. This helps in having a smoother landscape.
    - Multi-resolution optimization: Start with a low-resolution version (fewer points) and gradually refine to higher resolutions. This is because high resolutions are computationally expensive and may trap in local optima.
    - Randomized restarts: After optimizing at one resolution, we restart the optimization at that resolution to avoid local minima.
    - Hybrid methods: Combine local search (with gradient descent) and global search (with simulated annealing or SLSQP) to balance exploration and exploitation.

    We hope that by using a smooth binary representation, we can escape the strict binary restriction and find better solutions. Also, by using multi-resolution, we can escape local minima found at lower resolutions.
