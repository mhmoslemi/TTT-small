## Central Idea

The key challenge is to arrange the "1"s and "0"s (since h is a step function) on the interval [0,2] such that the integral of h(x)(1-h(x+k)) over all shifts k is minimized. This is equivalent to minimizing the maximum overlap when shifting the function by k.

A common approach in such problems is to use a periodic structure. Notice that the domain is [0,2] and the function is periodic in the sense that we are shifting by k (which can go from -n+1 to n-1 for an n-point grid). 

We can think of the function h as a binary function (also called a thresholding or wavelet-like sparse representation) with exactly n_points/2 ones (for n_points even) and the rest zeros. The overlap integral for a shift k is the integral (or in discrete setting, the sum) of h(x+k) * (1 - h(x)).

Alternatively, note that the total overlap for all shifts is connected to the L2 norm of the autocorrelation, but here the objective is the maximum shift that causes the maximum overlap.
