### 2. Mathematical Specification

#### Variables
- $h \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times 1}, h_i \in [0, 1]$  
- $n = n_{\text{points}}$  
- $\text{sum}(h) = n / 2$ (feasibility constraint)

#### Objective
Minimize:
$$C_5 = \max_k \left( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i (1 - h_{(i + k) \mod n}) \right) \cdot \Delta x$$
where $\Delta x = 2.0 / n$.

#### Convex Relaxation
Approximate the nonconvex objective by replacing $h_i (1 - h_j)$ with a linear term $a_{ij}$, leading to a linear program:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min_{a, h} \quad & \max_k \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a_{i, (i + k) \mod n} \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & a_{ij} \leq h_i \\
& a_{ij} \leq 1 - h_j \\
& 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \\
& \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i = n / 2
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Genetic Operations
- **Mutation**: Randomly adjust $h_i \pm \epsilon$ and project onto feasible region.  
  $$h_i' = \max(0, \min(1, h_i + \epsilon))$$  
  $$h' = h' \cdot \frac{n / 2}{\sum h'} \quad \text{(rescale)}$$
