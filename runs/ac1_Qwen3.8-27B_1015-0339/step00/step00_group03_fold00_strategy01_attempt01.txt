## Approach and rationale  
The central idea is to iteratively minimize the peak autocorrelation (AC1) by combining frequency-domain analysis with sparse optimization and gradient-based refinement. Unlike the previous approach's hybrid block-edge perturbations, this method leverages the asymptotic properties of sequences designed to minimize autocorrelation. The key strengths are:  
1. Efficient exploration via spectral decomposition to identify candidate peaks,  
2. Scalable gradient projection to refine subsequences while respecting bounds,  
3. Adaptive recalibration to handle multiple local optima, and  
4. Integration with the definitive evaluator for objective-based validation.  
The limitation is potential slow convergence near the global minima, but the domain constraints (n < 10,000) ensure feasible computations.  

## Mathematical specification
