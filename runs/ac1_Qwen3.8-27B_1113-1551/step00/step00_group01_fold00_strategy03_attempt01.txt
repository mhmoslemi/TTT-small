Approach and rationale:
The central goal is to minimize the ratio given by the evaluator function:
    score = 2 * n * max(convolve(a, a, mode='full')) / (sum(a))^2

We recognize that the key challenge is reducing the maximum convolution value while maintaining a high enough total sum. This requires sequences that avoid sharp peaks in the convolution.

We'll use a three-phase strategy:
1. Initialization: Start from a structure-aware sequence, possibly better than the provided parent.
2.Iterative refinement: Perform gradient-based updates in a structured way.
3.Finalization: Optionally swap with an entirely different optimized sequence.

The search will focus on sequences with period-4 block patterns, as these can reduce the maximum convolution value without significantly altering the total sum.

3.Iterative strategy:
