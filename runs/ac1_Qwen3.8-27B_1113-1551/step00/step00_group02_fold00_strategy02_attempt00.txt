Proposed Algorithm: Multi-scale Search with Convex Relaxation

The core idea is to search for an optimal sequence by operating across different structural scales. This method avoids the trial-and-error nature of local searches and the high computational cost of full sequential optimization.

Instead of working with the raw sequence, we define its structure in terms of a sparse pattern with elements occasionally clustered. The search space dimensional reduction allows solving a convex relaxation problem with fewer variables.

The mathematical formulation:

    Score(p) = 2*n*max(convolve(p, mode='full')) / (sum(p))^2

We will approximate this with a sequence q defined by:

The objective is to find a scaling vector r such that Score(q) is minimized.

This approach allows us to reduce the dimensionality of the optimization problem from n to the number of structural positions k (which is derived from the pattern repetition).
