# Approach and Rationale

The goal is to minimize the objective function: `2*n*max(convolve(a,a))/sum(a)**2`, where `n` is the sequence length and `a` is a sequence of non-negative floats. We have established via experiments that explicitly zero parts of the sequence is not beneficial, and better results come from focusing on increasing the sum of `a` while carefully managing the maximum value of the convolution `convolve(a,a)`. The central idea is to find periods of small convolution values and amplify them within these regions, while keeping other areas minimal but positive.

We will use a gradient-based optimization approach that searches for three key characteristics in the sequence:
1. **Bowels**: Short sequences of positive coefficients with many zeros around them to minimize the convolution's energy in that region.
2. **Gaps**: Zero-value regions between non-zero segments.
3. **Normalization**: After optimization, we will normalize the sequence to maintain a sum of √2n, matching the previous program.
