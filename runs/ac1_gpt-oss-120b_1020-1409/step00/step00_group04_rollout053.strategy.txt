This differs from the parent's local perturbation-based approach by:  (a) actively tracking multiple solutions simultaneously (b) inheriting traits from both the highest-performing solution and newly generated ones. We use the parent solution `height_sequence_1` as our initial and perturb candidate blocks using local noise. The rationale is that local perturbations from diverse starting points can more effectively discover global minima than pure local refinement.
