Let me think about the Nesterov momentum in more detail.

Standard Nesterov:
v_{t+1} = β * v_t + (1-β) * (x_t - x_{t-1})  (momentum)
x_{t+1} = x_t - α * ∇f(x_t + v_{t+1})  (lookahead)

Or more commonly:
v_{t+1} = β * v_t - α * ∇f(x_t)
x_{t+1} = x_t + (1+β)/α * v_{t+1} - β/α * v_t
= x_t - α * ∇f(x_t) + β * (x_t - x_{t-1})

Let me use the simpler form:
m_t = β * m_{t-1} + (1-β) * g_t  (averaged gradient)
x_{t+1} = project(x_t - α * m_t)

This is like a "Polyak averaging" variant. Actually, for our purposes, a simple momentum should suffice:

v_t = β * v_{t-1} + (1-β) * g_t
x_{t+1} = project(x_t - α * v_t)

where g_t is the LSE gradient and project is the simplex projection.
