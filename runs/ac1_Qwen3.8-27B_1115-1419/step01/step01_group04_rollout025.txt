Let me think about this more carefully. The convolution at position k is:
`(a*a)[k] = sum_{i=max(0,k-n+1)}^{min(k,n-1)} a[i] * a[k-i]`

The number of terms is `min(k+1, n, 2n-1-k)`. The maximum number of terms is n, achieved at k = n-1.

At k = n-1: `(a*a)[n-1] = sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a[i] * a[n-1-i]`

This is the "reversal" pairing. To minimize this, we want a[i] and a[n-1-i] to be "anti-correlated" - one large, one small, for each pair.

But we also need to keep other positions low. The position k=0 is just a[0]^2, and k=2n-2 is a[n-1]^2. So we want a[0] and a[n-1] to be small.

This suggests a structure like: small at the extreme edges, large in the first quarter and last quarter (but not the very last), creating an asymmetric pattern.

Actually, let me think about this from a different angle. The problem is equivalent to finding a non-negative vector a that minimizes ||a*a||_inf / (sum(a))^2 * 2n.

Since the objective is scale-invariant, let's fix sum(a) = 1 and minimize 2n * ||a*a||_inf.

The sum of all convolution coefficients is (sum(a))^2 = 1. There are 2n-1 coefficients, so the average is 1/(2n-1). The minimum possible maximum is 1/(2n-1), giving a lower bound of 2n/(2n-1) ≈ 1.
