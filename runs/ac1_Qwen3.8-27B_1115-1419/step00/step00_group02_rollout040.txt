**Algorithm: Active-set QCQP**

1. Initialize a (e.g., from the given sequence or a periodic pattern).
2. Compute b = convolve(a, a). Find the top-K lags (by b value).
3. Solve QCQP: minimize t subject to sum(a)=1, a>=0, b_k(a) <= t for k in top-K.
4. Recompute b. If any lag not in top-K now has b_k > t, add it to the active set and go to step 3.
5. If the active set stabilizes, we're at a (local) optimum.
