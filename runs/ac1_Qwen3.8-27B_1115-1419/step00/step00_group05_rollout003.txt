Let me think about a specific construction. Consider a sequence that is periodic with period p, repeated n/p times. If p divides n, then:
a(i) = b(i mod p) for i = 0, ..., n-1.

The convolution of such a sequence with itself:
(a*a)(k) = sum_i a(i)*a(k-i)

For a periodic sequence, this is related to the circular convolution of the period, plus edge effects.

If n is large and p is small, the edge effects are negligible, and:
(a*a)(k) ≈ (n/p) * (b*b_circ)(k mod p)

where b*b_circ is the circular convolution of b with itself, period p.

The sum of a is (n/p)*sum(b).

= 2*p*max(b*b_circ) / sum(b)^2
