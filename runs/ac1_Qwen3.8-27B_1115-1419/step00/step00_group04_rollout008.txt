c[2j] was over-added by 2*delta*a[j] (from the vectorized step), but should have been new^2 - old^2.

c[2j] += 2*delta*old_x (from vectorized step)
c[2j] += (new^2 - old^2) - 2*delta*old_x (correction)
Net: c[2j] += new^2 - old^2 ✓
