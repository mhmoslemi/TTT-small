Actually, for a monotonically increasing sequence, the peak of the convolution is always at k = n (or k = n-1). This is because c[k] = sum_j a[j]*a[k-j], and for an increasing a, the product a[j]*a[k-j] is maximized when both j and k-j are as large as possible, which happens at k = n (where j can range from 1 to n-1, and both j and n-j are in the "large" range).

So the peak should be at k = n for all power ramps with p > 0.
