For a sequence with entries 0 and c (binary scaled), the convolution at lag k is `c² * (number of pairs (i, k-i) where both a[i] and a[k-i] are nonzero)`. So it's `c² * r(k)` where `r(k)` is the "autocorrelation" of the binary pattern.

The sum of all `r(k)` is `w²` (each pair of ones contributes to exactly one lag). There are `2n-1` lags. So the average `r(k)` is `w²/(2n-1)`.

The minimum possible `max(r)` is at least `ceil(w²/(2n-1))`.
