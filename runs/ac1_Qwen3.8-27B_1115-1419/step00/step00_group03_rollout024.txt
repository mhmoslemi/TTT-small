Can we do better? The question is whether there exists a non-negative sequence whose self-convolution is "flatter" than the triangle.

I believe the answer is yes, but it's hard to construct explicitly. The LP approach should find such sequences.

Let me also consider: what if I use a sequence with some zeros? Like a "comb" pattern?

What about a = [1, 1, 0, 0, 1, 1, 0, 0, ...] (periodic with period 4, two 1s and two 0s)?
The convolution will have a periodic structure. The peak will be at the center.

c[n-1] = sum_i a[i]*a[n-1-i]. Since a is periodic with period 4, and n-1 = 4M-1:
a[i]*a[4M-1-i]: if i mod 4 is 0 or 1, then (4M-1-i) mod 4 is 3 or 2, so a[4M-1-i] = 0.
If i mod 4 is 2 or 3, then (4M-1-i) mod 4 is 1 or 0, so a[i] = 0.
So c[n-1] = 0!
