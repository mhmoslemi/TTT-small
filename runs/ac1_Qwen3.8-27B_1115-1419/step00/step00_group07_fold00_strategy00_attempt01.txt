### Approach and Rationale

The goal is to minimize `F(a) = 2 * n * max_k (conv_k) / (sum(a))^2`, where `conv_k` is the convolution of the sequence `a` with itself in full mode. The previous solution achieved `F(a) = 2.0` but we aim for a lower value, specifically `< 1.5027`.

While tabu search can explore improvements, its time complexity is O(n²) per evaluation, which may be too slow for sequences of 6733 elements. Instead, we adopt a more efficient approach based on a block spectral factorization method:

1. The method begins with a normalized sequence (sum of elements equals √(2n)), calculates its autocorrelation, and uses an iterative process to find a sequence that further reduces the objective.
2. By projecting the accumulated gradient onto eigenvectors of the convolution, we achieve faster convergence and better exploration of the sequence space.
3. The optimization leverages linear algebra operations (matrix multiplications) within the constraints to push the solution toward the desirable value.

This method is known for applications in adaptive signal processing and filtering, and it provides a structured way to optimize step sequences—ensuring that each neighborhood is fully characterized and decisions are made systematically.

### Mathematical Specification

**Sequence Representation:** We consider a sequence `a` of length `n`. Each value must be non-negative and bounded between 0 and 1000, inclusive.

**Objective Function:** We require minimizing `F(a) = 2 * n * max_k (conv_k) / (sum(a))^2`, where:
- `conv_k = full_convolution(a, a)` for `k ∈ [0, 2*n - 1]`
- `full_convolution(a, a)` computes the linear convolution of `a` with itself.

**Constraints:** For every candidate sequence `a`, the sum must be ≥ 0.01 after normalization. Each element must remain within [0, 1000].

**Optimization Variables:** The normalized sequence `f` (to reduce numerical issues) and the integer `τ` (tau-parameter) determine the convolution structure through a block spectral factorization.

The gradient approach will consider the structure of the normalized sequence:

- **Monotonic Decrease:** Initially, the sequence is normalized to have sum √(2n), and the autocorrelation is bounded. Then, updates are designed to reduce the peak of autocorrelation monotonically.
