For every valid input, the evaluator post-processes the sequence to a
nonnegative finite vector a of length n.  Its objective is

    2 * n * max_k (a * a)[k] / (sum_i a_i)^2.

The central autocorrelation coefficient is

    (a * a)[n-1] = sum_i a_i^2.

By Cauchy-Schwarz,

    (sum_i a_i)^2 <= n * sum_i a_i^2,

so for any admissible nonnegative sequence,

    2 * n * (a * a)[n-1] / (sum_i a_i)^2 >= 2.

Therefore the global minimum is exactly 2, and equality is attained by any
positive constant sequence (within the evaluator's clipping bounds).  No
