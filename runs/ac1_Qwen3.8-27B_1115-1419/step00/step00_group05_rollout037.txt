Let me think about a different approach. What about using the "majorize-minimize" (MM) algorithm?

The MM algorithm works as follows:
1. At the current point $p^{(t)}$, find a "majorizer" $Q(p, p^{(t)})$ such that $Q(p, p^{(t)}) \geq F(p)$ for all $p$, and $Q(p^{(t)}, p^{(t)}) = F(p^{(t)})$.
2. Minimize $Q(p, p^{(t)})$ to get $p^{(t+1)}$.
3. Repeat.

For $F(p) = \max_k (p*p)_k$, a natural majorizer at $p^{(t)}$ is:
$Q(p, p^{(t)}) = F(p^{(t)}) + \nabla F(p^{(t)})^T (p - p^{(t)}) + \frac{\alpha}{2}\|p - p^{(t)}\|^2$

The minimizer is: $p^{(t+1)} = \text{proj}_{\mathcal{C}}(p^{(t)} - \frac{1}{\alpha} \nabla F(p^{(t)}))$
