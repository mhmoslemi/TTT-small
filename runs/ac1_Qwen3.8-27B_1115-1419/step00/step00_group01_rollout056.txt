This is a well-studied problem in probability! The self-convolution of a distribution is the distribution of the sum of two independent copies. We want to minimize the maximum probability mass.

For continuous distributions, the uniform distribution on [0,1] gives a triangular density for the sum, with peak 1 (at the center). The "flatness" ratio is 1/(1/2) = 2 (peak is twice the average, since the sum has support [0,2] with density that goes from 0 to 1 to 0).
