The key realization is: for a non-negative sequence, the convolution c[k] = sum_j a[j]*a[k-j] is a sum of non-negative terms. The "most constrained" k is typically near the center (n-1) where all n terms overlap.

For a long sequence (n >> 1), the interior constraints dominate. Specifically, for k in {1, 2, ..., 2n-3} (excluding the very edges), c[k] involves approximately n terms (for interior k) or fewer (near edges).

Actually, for k in the range [n-1, n-1] (just the center), c[n-1] has exactly n terms. For k = n-1±1, it has n-1 terms, etc.
