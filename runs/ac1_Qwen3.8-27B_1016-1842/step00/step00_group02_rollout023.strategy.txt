The central idea is to decompose the 6962-length sequence into 10 contiguous blocks (step functions) with constant values within each block. This drastically reduces the dimensionality from 6962 to 20 parameters (10 block values and 10 block lengths). We then optimize this compressed representation using gradient-based search, leveraging random finite-differences for gradient estimation to avoid expensive exact convolution computations during search. The 1D search ensures minimal memory usage and computational overhead. The strategy aggressively uses the worst-case time budget to retire a potentially optimal candidate sequence.
