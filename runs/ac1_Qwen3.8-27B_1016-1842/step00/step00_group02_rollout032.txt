The issue is: for smooth, slowly-varying sequences, the convolution peak is approximately sum a[j]^2 (since a[j] ≈ a[n-1-j] for large n), and by Cauchy-Schwarz, sum a[j]^2 >= (sum a[j])^2/n, giving score >= 2.
