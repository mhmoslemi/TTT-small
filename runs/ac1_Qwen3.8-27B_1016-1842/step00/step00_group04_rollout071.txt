OK so my initial excitement about the linear ramp was wrong. Let me reconsider.

The key issue: for a non-symmetric sequence, the peak of the convolution can be much larger than the center value.

For SYMMETRIC sequences (a_i = a_{n-1-i}), the convolution IS symmetric, and the peak is at the center (or near it). For symmetric nonnegative sequences, the center value is sum a_i^2, and by Cauchy-Schwarz, sum a_i^2 >= (sum a_i)^2/n, giving score >= 2.

So for symmetric sequences, score >= 2, period.
