Specifically: (a_flip * y) has length n + (2n-1) - 1 = 3n-2 elements. And (A^T y)_j = (a_flip * y)[j + n - 1] for j = 0, ..., n-1.

So we can compute A^T y by computing the full convolution of a_flip (length n) and y (length 2n-1) via FFT, and taking elements [n-1 : 2n-2].
