- convolve at lag k: the alternating parts partially cancel
- b[k] ≈ (k+1) + ε^2 * (alternating correlation)

Actually for a[i] = 1 + ε*(-1)^i:
b[k] = sum_i (1+ε*(-1)^i)(1+ε*(-1)^{k-i})
= sum_i [1 + ε*(-1)^i + ε*(-1)^{k-i} + ε^2*(-1)^{k}]
= (number of terms) + ε*sum_i (-1)^i + ε*sum_i (-1)^{k-i} + ε^2*(-1)^k*(number of terms)

For k even: b[k] ≈ m + 0 + 0 + ε^2*m = m(1+ε^2)
For k odd: b[k] ≈ m + 0 + 0 - ε^2*m = m(1-ε^2)

where m is the number of terms (overlap count).

At the center (k=n-1), if n is even, k is odd: b[n-1] ≈ n(1-ε^2).
At k=n-2 (even): b[n-2] ≈ (n-1)(1+ε^2).

For small ε: center peak ≈ n - nε^2, adjacent peak ≈ (n-1) + (n-1)ε^2.
The max is at the center if n - nε^2 > (n-1) + (n-1)ε^2, i.e., 1 > ε^2*(2n-1), i.e., ε < 1/sqrt(2n-1).

For n = 6733, ε < 1/sqrt(13465) ≈ 0.0086.

So with ε = 0.005: center peak ≈ n(1-0.000025) ≈ n. The reduction is tiny.
