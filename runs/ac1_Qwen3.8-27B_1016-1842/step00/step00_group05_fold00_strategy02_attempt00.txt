Approach and Rationale:

The evaluator penalizes sequences with high autocorrelation via the max-value of the convolution over the input sequence. We use a fast Fourier transform (FFT) to approximate the expensive full convolution evaluations, allowing us to make many function evaluations within the time budget. The search is entirely random and depends on a perturbation step that we hope will yield lower objective values.

- The exact objective is computed by `evaluate_sequence`, which uses `np.convolve`. We approximate it with an FFT-based convolution. The candidate sequences must be nonnegative and bounded between 0 and 1000, and the sum must be at least 0.01 after clamping.
