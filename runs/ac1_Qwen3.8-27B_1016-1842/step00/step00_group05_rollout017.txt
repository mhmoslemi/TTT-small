So: -ε² * sum cos(πi/n) * (-cos(πi/n + π/n)) = ε² * sum cos(πi/n) * cos(πi/n + π/n)
= ε²/2 * sum [cos(π/n) + cos(2πi/n + π/n)]
= ε²/2 * (n*cos(π/n) + 0)
= ε²*n*cos(π/n)/2 ≈ ε²*n/2.

Still positive! So this perturbation also increases c[n-1].
