Let's analyze a_i = r^i for i=0,...,n-1.
S = (1-r^n)/(1-r)
result[k] = sum_{i=max(0,k-n+1)}^{min(n-1,k)} r^i * r^{k-i} = sum ... r^k = r^k * (number of terms)

Wait! result[k] = sum valid i of a[i]*a[k-i] = sum r^i * r^{k-i} = sum r^k = r^k * (number of valid i's).

Number of valid i's for lag k:
- For k = 0, ..., n-1: number = k+1
- For k = n, ..., 2n-2: number = 2n-1-k

So result[k] = r^k * (k+1) for k < n, and r^k * (2n-1-k) for k >= n.

The maximum of k*r^k for k in [0, n-1] is at k ≈ 1/ln(1/r) = -1/ln(r).

For r = 0.9: peak at k ≈ 9.5. value ≈ 9.5 * 0.9^9.5 ≈ 9.5 * 0.376 ≈ 3.57
S = (1-0.9^n)/(0.1) ≈ 10 for large n.
