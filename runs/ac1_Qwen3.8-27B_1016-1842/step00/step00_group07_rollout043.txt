Actually, I realize I should think about this differently. The score is:
score = 2n * max_k (a*a)(k) / (sum a)^2

For this to be < 2, we need max_k (a*a)(k) < (sum a)^2 / n.

Now, (a*a)(n-1) = sum_i a_i a_{n-1-i}. By the rearrangement inequality, this is maximized when a and its reverse are similarly sorted, and minimized when they're oppositely sorted. But we can choose a to make this small.

The sum over ALL k of (a*a)(k) = (sum a)^2 = S^2. There are 2n-1 values. So the average is S^2/(2n-1). The max is at least this. For the score to be < 2:

So we need: S^2/(2n-1) < max < S^2/n, i.e., the max is less than about twice the average. This is achievable if the convolution is fairly flat.
