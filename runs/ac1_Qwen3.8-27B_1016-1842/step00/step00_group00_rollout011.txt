For uniform a, c has a triangular shape: c[k] ≈ c^2 * min(k+1, n, 2n-1-k). The average is S^2/(2n-1) ≈ S^2/(2n), and the max is n*c^2 = S^2/n. Ratio of max to average: n/(2n-1) * (2n-1)/1 ≈ 1/2... wait.

Max/mean = (S^2/n) / (S^2/(2n-1)) = (2n-1)/n ≈ 2.

So the peak is about twice the mean. To minimize the score, we want to flatten the convolution, i.e., reduce the peak-to-mean ratio.
