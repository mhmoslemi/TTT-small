For larger n, can we do better? The idea is to have the sequence decay so that the convolution peak is away from the center.

For a geometric sequence a[i] = r^i (i=0..n-1) with 0 < r < 1:
conv[n-1] = sum r^i * r^{n-1-i} = sum r^{n-1} = n*r^{n-1}
conv[0] = 1
sum = (1-r^n)/(1-r)

For large n and small r, conv[0] = 1 dominates, sum ≈ 1/(1-r), and score ≈ 2n/(1/(1-r))^2 = 2n(1-r)^2.

For this to be < 2: n(1-r)^2 < 1, so 1-r < 1/sqrt(n), r > 1 - 1/sqrt(n).
