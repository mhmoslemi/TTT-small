In FFT terms: $p \star a = \text{IFFT}(\text{FFT}(p) \cdot \text{conj}(\text{FFT}(a)))$.

So $g_i = \text{IFFT}(\text{FFT}(p) \cdot \text{conj}(\text{FFT}(a)))_{-i}$.

For $i = 0$: $g_0 = (p \star a)_0 = \sum_k p_k a_k$ (but $p$ has length $2n-1$ and $a$ has length $n$, so this is $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} p_k a_k$).

For $i > 0$: $g_i = (p \star a)_{-i}$. In the circular FFT of length $N \geq 3n-2$, the index $-i$ corresponds to $N - i$.
