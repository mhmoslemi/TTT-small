Actually, I think the key insight is:

For a non-decreasing non-negative sequence, the convolution has a specific structure. The peak tends to be around index n-1 or n (the "center" of the convolution), and the value there is `sum_j a[j]*a[n-1-j]` which, for a non-decreasing sequence, pairs small values with large values, giving a smaller product sum than if the sequence were symmetric.

Wait, for a non-decreasing sequence a, the sequence a[n-1-j] is non-increasing. So we're pairing small a[j] with large a[n-1-j] and vice versa. By the "rearrangement inequality", this gives the MINIMUM of `sum a[j]*b[j]` when a is sorted one way and b the other. So for a non-decreasing a, `sum a[j]*a[n-1-j]` is minimized among all permutations!

But we also need to check the other lags. The maximum might not be at the center.
