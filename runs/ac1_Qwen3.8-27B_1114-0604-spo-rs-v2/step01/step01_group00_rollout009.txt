The key insight is: we want to minimize max(conv(a,a)) subject to sum(a) = 1, a >= 0.

This is equivalent to: find a probability distribution on {0, ..., n-1} that minimizes the maximum of its self-convolution.

The self-convolution at position k is the "probability" that two independent random variables (distributed according to a) sum to k. So we're looking for a distribution that minimizes the maximum point probability of the sum of two i.i.d. copies.
