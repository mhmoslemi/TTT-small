Let me redo the lower bound.

We want to minimize: 2n * max_k convolve(f,f)[k] / (sum f)^2

Now, is there a lower bound on max_k convolve(f,f)[k]?

Note that sum_k convolve(f,f)[k] = sum_k sum_i f[i] * f[k-i] = sum_i sum_j f[i] * f[j] = (sum f)^2

So the AVERAGE of the convolution values is (sum f)^2 / (2n-1).

By the pigeonhole principle, max_k convolve(f,f)[k] >= (sum f)^2 / (2n-1).

This gives: objective >= 2n * (sum f)^2 / (2n-1) / (sum f)^2 = 2n/(2n-1) ≈ 1 for large n.
