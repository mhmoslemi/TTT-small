Wait, no. If we take b = c*f for c > 1, then (f*b)_k = c*(f*f)_k. For active lags where (f*f)_k = M, this becomes c*M > M. So b = c*f is infeasible for c > 1.

But the LP might still find a b* with sum(b*) > 1, by redistributing mass from the "peak" positions of f to other positions.

In any case, after solving the LP, we normalize b* to sum 1 and use it as a direction.
