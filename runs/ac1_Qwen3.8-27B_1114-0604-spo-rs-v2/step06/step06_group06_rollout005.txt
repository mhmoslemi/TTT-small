max(conv) >= conv[k] for any specific k.

The useful lower bound is:
max(conv) >= (1/(2n-1)) * sum_k conv[k] = (1/(2n-1)) * (sum a)^2.

So score >= 2n/(2n-1) * (sum a)^2/
