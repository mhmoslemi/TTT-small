**Approach and Rationale**

This algorithm minimizes the evaluation function `2*n*max(convolve(a,a))/sum(a)^2` (with sum normalized to sqrt(2*n)) by iteratively refining sequences using Fourier-based transforms while ensuring non-negativity and boundedness. Unlike traditional optimization methods, it leverages the connection between frequency domain properties and time domain convolution behavior. The core idea is to invite multiple frequency representations into a harmonized framework, reducing the action surface dynamically. Each phase builds upon the previous one, alternating between time and frequency optimizations to escape local minima.

**Mathematical Specification**

Let \( a \) be a non-negative sequence of length \( n \). We define:
- \( S = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a_i \)
