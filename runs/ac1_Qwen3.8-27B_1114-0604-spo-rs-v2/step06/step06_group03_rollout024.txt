But we can't have a delta function autocorrelation with a non-negative time-domain sequence (that would require a complex-valued sequence in general).

However, we can try to make the power spectrum as flat as possible subject to the constraint that the time-domain signal is non-negative. This is a non-trivial optimization problem.

Alternative approach: **Direct optimization of the subgradient**.

The function f(a) = max_k (a*a)[k] is the maximum of linear functions of a (each (a*a)[k] is a quadratic form, actually bilinear). Wait, (a*a)[k] = sum_i a_i * a_{k-i} is not linear in a, it's quadratic. So f(a) is the maximum of quadratic functions, not linear.

The subgradient of f(a) at point a is: f'(a) = (2/|A|) * sum_{k in A} (a shifted by k + a shifted by (n-1-k))

where A is the set of active lags (where (a*a)[k] = max).
