1. **Approach and Rationale**  
   The central idea is to represent the step function \( h(x) \) as a periodic extension of a triangle wave, optimized over its width and amplitude parameters. This approach leverages the **periodicity** and **analytical structure** of triangular functions, which often exhibit optimal properties in minimization problems like the Erds minimum overlap. Unlike previous methods that rely on high-dimensional spatial discretization and local optimization, this strategy reduces complexity by parameterizing the function with two variables:  
   - **Width** (\( w \)) of the central peak,  
   - **Amplitude** (\( a \)) scaling the height.  

   The rationale is that triangular functions are **smooth**, **analytic**, and can model localized structures efficiently.
