Approach and rationale
We are tackling the discrete step function minimization problem for the Erds constant. The goal is to find a function h:[0,2] → [0,1] with ∫h(x)dx=1 that minimizes:
    C_5 = sup_{x} ∫h(y) · (1-h(y+x)) dy

The current record is 0.3808, and our previous program achieved 0.381432. We need to find a better upper bound.

We design a genetic algorithm combined with local search (CMA-ES) to explore the solution space. Key reasons for this approach:

1. Genetic algorithm will explore the solution space globally, avoiding local optima
2. CMA-ES provides efficient local optimization in hyperellipsoidal regions
3. The problem is non-convex with many degrees of freedom

Critical bottlenecks addressed:
- The optimization problem is high-dimensional (n_points variables)
- The objective calculation is O(n_points^2) making exhaustive search infeasible
- We need distributed search to escape local minima

The algorithm balances exploration (genetic crossover/mutation) and exploitation (local refinement with CMA-ES). We also include repair mechanisms to maintain feasibility.
