Unlike previous methods that relied on greedy binary constructions or hill climbing, this approach uses a novel combination of wavelet decompositions at increasing frequency resolutions to generate candidates with targeted structural properties. The key innovation is the multi-resolution wavelet transformation that explicitly controls the smoothness of transitions between high and low values, which is hypothesized to reduce peak overlaps. This approach addressess the limitation of being easily trapped in local optima by using wavelet coefficients as structural blueprints rather than discrete patterns.
