**Objective**: Minimize \(C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x) (1-h(x+k)) \, dx\) over all shifts \(k \in \mathbb{Z}\).

**Optimization**: Use projected gradient descent on the Fourier coefficients, projecting onto the constraint set to maintain \(0 \leq h(x) \leq 1\).
