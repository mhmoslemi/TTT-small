The central idea is to optimize the maximum overlap integral \(C_5\) of a step function \(h(x)\) on \([0,2]\) with integral 1. The key insight is to reduce the autocorrelation within the function, as \(C_5 = \max_k \int h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx\), which is equivalent to maximizing the minimum autocorrelation. A lower \(C_5\) is achieved by distributing the function's values evenly across the domain to minimize self-overlap at any shift. This approach avoids local optima by combining local search (GREEDY) with global exploration (PENALTY), leveraging exact sum and box constraints through repair mechanisms.
