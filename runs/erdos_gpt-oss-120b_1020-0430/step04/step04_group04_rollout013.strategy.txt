<h2>5. Rosebrock-like Correlation Barrier Construction (RBCC)</h2>

<dt>5.1 Approach and rationale</dt>
<dd>
<p>Rather than hill climbing from known points, we'll construct a function whose autocorrelation properties are analyzed analytically and optimized. This method follows the principle of parameterized function forms that can be both visualized and globally optimized. We will use:</p>

<ol>
<li>A family of piecewise functions defined on [-1,1] (mapped to [0,2])</li>
<li>Parameterized by two key parameters: a shape factor α and a transition width β</li>
<li>An analysis of the maximum correlation that must occur due to symmetry properties</li>
</ol>

<p>This approach explicitly incorporates the knowledge of the problem's integer optimal solutions and their correlation properties. By working with slices and derivatives, we avoid purely numerical methods and use mathematical relationships to accelerate optimization.</p>
</dd>

<dt>5.2 Mathematical specification</dt>
<dd>
<dt>2.1 Representation</dt>
<dd>
The function is represented as a piecewise corresponds to samples we'll denote as:

$$f(t) = \begin{cases}
g_1(-t) + g_1(t) & -1 \leq t \leq 1 \\
g_1(-t) + g_1(t-2) & 1 < t \leq 2
\end{cases}$$

Where $g_1(x) = \exp(-αx^2)$. This defines a function that is symmetric about t=0 and has two humps.

We sample at n equally spaced points: $x_i = (i - 0.5) \cdot Δx$, $Δx = 2/n$

The condition ∫h dx=1 is naturally satisfied at a critical value of α.

</dd>

<dt>2.2 Objective</dt>
<dd>
The Erdős minimum overlap problem requires:

$$C_5 = \max_k \int_0^{2-k} h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx$$

After appropriate analysis and substitution, we convert this into:

$$\min_{α,β} \left\{ C = \max_{|k|≤2} \left[ A(α,β,k) + B(α,β,k) + R(α,β,k) \right] \right\}$$

Where A, B, R are analytical expressions derived from the correlation function using symmetry properties.

</dd>

<dt>2.3 Formulation</dt>
<dd>
We parameterize the function by two parameters:
$$parameters = [α, γ]$$

Where:
- $α$ controls the global scaling and heaviness of the hump
- $γ$ controls the transition width between the two humps

The optimization problem is:
$$\min_{α,γ} \quad f(α,γ) = \max_k \left\{ \text{Conv}_k(α,γ) \right\} \cdot \Delta x$$

Where the individual correlation terms are expressed in terms of complete elliptic integrals and connected to the parameters.

</dd>
