### 1. Approach and Rationale

We aim to improve the Erdős minimum overlap problem constant by minimizing the maximum shift-overlap integral of a step function over a periodic interval. The key challenge is the non-convex nature of the problem. The provided initial parent solution (n=360) offers a baseline, but may be trapped in a local optimum. Our approach explores multiple scales (n=360 and n=720) and leverages a combination of local optimizers and smooth surrogate functions to escape local optima. Three distinct algorithms are employed: Hill Climbing with Simulated Annealing (HC+SA), and a series of SLSQP optimizations using a surrogate function. The surrogate smoothens the non-differentiable max operation to enable efficient optimization.

The main bottlenecks are:
1. The high-dimensional space (dimensions = 360 or 720)
2. The slow evaluation of the correlation integral (O(n log n))

Improvements include:
- Using a geometric progression of β parameters to balance between smoothing and fidelity to the original objective
- Upsampling to higher resolution (n=720) for potentially better function smoother representation
- Tight integration between local optimizers and global exploration

### 2. Mathematical Specification

**Representation**: The function is represented as samples \(h = [h_0, h_1, \dots, h_{N-1}]\) on \([0, 2]\) with \(\Delta x = 2.0 / N\) (N=360 initial, then 720). The function satisfies \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) and \(\sum_{i} h_i \Delta x = 1\).

**Objective**: Minimize \(C = \max_{k \geq 0} \int_0^{2-k} h(x) (1 - h(x+k))  dx\), discretized as:
\[
C_5 = \max_{k} \left[ \text{np.correlate}(h, 1-h, \text{mode}=\text{"full"}) \cdot \Delta x \right]
\]
where \(\Delta x = 2.0 / N\).

**Surrogate Objective** (optimized):
\[
\tilde{C}_{\beta}(h) = \max_{k} \text{conv}[k] + \frac{1}{\beta} \log \left( \sum_{k} e^{\beta (\text{conv}[k] - \max_k \text{conv}[k])} \right)
\]
where \(\text{conv}[k]\) is the discrete convolution via FFT, and \(\beta\) controls the smoothness.

**Constraints**: \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) for all \(i\), and \(\sum_{i} h \Delta x = 1\).

**Optimizers**:
- **Hill Climbing** with stochastic transfers
- **Simulated Annealing** with exponential cooling
- **SLSQP** (Sequential Least Squares Programming) constrained optimizer
