1. Approach and Rationale

   - **Pattern Design**: Create periodic base patterns that are expected to minimize the maximum overlap by distributing active/desired states (h=1) sufficiently over multiple regions.
   - **Local Optimization**: Refine these patterns using efficient local search to reduce the maximum overlap.

The core idea is that well-designed patterns with limited local concentration of high-values and integrated to total area 1 will yield lower maximum overlaps. We use Latin Hypercube Sampling to generate diverse initial candidates, then apply local optimization methods like stochastic rounding and simulated annealing.
