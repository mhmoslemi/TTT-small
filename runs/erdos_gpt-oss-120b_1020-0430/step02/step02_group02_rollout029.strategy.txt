Approach and Rationale:

The current method uses SLSQP with stochastic restarts. However, global optimization via SLSQP might be too slow and might get stuck in local optima. We propose using a genetic algorithm (GA) with a different representation and mutation strategy. The GA will keep track of the best individual found and allow for local refinement via gradient-based methods. The key idea is to use GA to explore a wide range of solutions and then use local optimization to fine-tune the best candidate.

We choose GA because it is a population-based method that can escape local optima, and we can incorporate problem-specific mutations (e.g., inverting parts of the function, swapping blocks) that might be more effective than random mutations. Additionally, we can integrate a local search step (like gradient-based) after GA has found a candidate solution.

The GA approach:
