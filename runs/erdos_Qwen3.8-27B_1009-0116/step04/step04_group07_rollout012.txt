- For k=1 (shift by 1): corr = 32 (all 1s are followed by 0s) → C₅ contribution = 32/32 = 1.0
That's terrible!

What about [1,1,0,0,1,1,0,0,...]? (period 4, two 1s then two 0s)
- 16 ones and 16 zeros per period of 4, so 32 ones total. ✓
- For shift 1: positions where h[i]=1 and h[i+1]=0: i=2,6,10,... (16 positions) → 16/32 = 0.5
- For shift 2: positions where h[i]=1 and h[i+2]=0: i=0,1,4,5,... (32 positions) → 32/32 = 1.0
That's also bad.
