## 1. Approach and Rationale

The solution involves a population-based optimization using differential evolution (DE) to explore the solution space more thoroughly, followed by local refinement to escape local optima found by the DE phase. This strategy addresses the key bottleneck of the previous approaches—overreliance on local optimization that can easily get stuck in suboptimal regions. By employing DE with a repair mechanism to enforce constraints, and then applying targeted local searches, this method ensures better global exploration while maintaining constraint satisfaction. The approach leverages the current record solution as a starting point but adapts it for a more robust search.

The central idea is to treat the problem as a constrained optimization over a high-dimensional discrete function space. DE is chosen for its ability to handle large numbers, with repair and tactical local searches incorporating the integral constraint. This is expected to yield a lower C5 value by avoiding early convergence and explicitly satisfying constraints.

Limitations include computational cost, which may limit resolution explored, and potential overfitting if the repair is too aggressive. However, the trade-off is managed by budgeting computational time.

## 2. Mathematical Specification

The objective is to minimize:
\[
C_5 = \max_{k} \int_0^2 h(x) \cdot (1 - h(x + k)) \, dx
\]
discretized as:
\[
C_5 = \frac{1}{n} \times \max_{0 \leq k \leq 2n-2} \left( \sum_{i=1}^{2n-1} \text{shifted correlation values} \right) \times n
\]
adjusted for numerical stability (as per the evaluator).

Constraints:
- \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \) for all \( i \)
- \( \sum_{i=1}^n h_i \cdot dx = 1 \), with \( dx = 2/N \), so \( \sum_{i=1}^N h_i = N/2 \)

Variables: \( \mathbf{h} \in \mathbb{R}^N \), where \( h_i \in [0,1] \) for all \( i \).

Repair Mechanism:
- Input: h, N (number of points)
- Output: H, feasible h with sum \( \approx N/2 \)
  1. Clip all values: \( h_i = \max(\min(h_i, 1), 0) \)
  2. Compute s = sum(h). Let \( t = N/2 \)
  3. If \( |s - t| < \epsilon \), return h (with no adjustment).
  4. Else, sort h in increasing order. Compute difference ds = t - s.
  5. Adjust extreme values: for ds > 0, decrease largest h or increase smallest; for ds < 0, increase smallest h or decrease largest. Use a binary search for adjustments, incrementally changing two indices worst and best sorted to meet tolerance.

The objective function for DE is: \( \text{objective}(h) = C_5(\text{repair}(h, N)) \)

DE Objective: Minimize C5.

Constraint Handling: Integral constraint enforced via repair. Bounds enforced by DE bounds.

Solver Support: DE (scipy.optimize.differential_evolution) handles box constraints. Integral constraint handled by objective repair.
