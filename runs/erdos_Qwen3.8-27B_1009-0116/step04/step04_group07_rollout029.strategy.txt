Approach and rationale
The core idea is to use a competitive coevolution approach combining local search (gradient-based optimization) and global exploration (evolutionary strategies) to minimize the maximum overlap integral. The non-convex nature of the problem is addressed by maintaining multiple competing solutions and optimizing under resource constraints. Unlike local methods, this approach ensures broader exploration and helps escape shallow minima by periodically comparing solutions at different resolutions and applying cross-resolution transference. The key limitation of previous methods was their susceptibility to local optima; this strategy dynamically balances exploration and exploitation using cooperative learning mechanisms.

Mathematical Specification
We minimize:
    C₅ = dx * max_{k=0}^{2*n-3} ∫[0,2] h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx
Subject to:
    0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 ∀i∈[0, n-1]
    ∑h[i] * dx = 1

Represented as a vector h ∈ ℝⁿ with dx = 2.0 / n.

The objective is evaluated via:
    autocorr = np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full') * dx
    C₅ = max(autocorr)
