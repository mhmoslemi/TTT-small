The Hessian of sum_i h[i]*h[i+k] with respect to h is a symmetric matrix with 1's at positions (i, i+k) and (i+k, i) for valid i. This matrix is NOT positive semidefinite in general (it can have negative eigenvalues). So c[k] is not necessarily convex or concave in h.
