**1. Approach and Rationale**

We address the Erdős minimum overlap problem by employing a multi-stage optimization strategy using **binary particle swarm optimization (BPSO)** combined with **local search refinement**. The central idea is twofold:

**a) Global Search with BPSO**: We use binary PSO to explore candidate solutions in the high-dimensional space of binary functions (h: step functions). **b) Local Search Refinement**: After finding promising candidates via PSO, we perform **gradient-based local optimization** with careful handling of the discrete nature of the problem using a **clamped-$\ell_0$ penalty term**. **2. Mathematical Specification**

**Variables and Domains**:
- Let $\Omega_{n} = \{0,1\}^{n}$ be the configuration space for n-point grid samples
- $h \in \Omega_{n}$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} h_i = n/2$ (note: $n$ must be even)
- The cost function to minimize is:
   $$
   C(h) = \frac{2}{n} \max_{k=-n+1,...,n-1} \left| \sum_{t=0}^{n-1-k} h[t] \cdot (1 - h[t+k]) \right|
   $$
   (using mode='full' correlation)

**Objective Function**:
- Minimize the maximum cross-correlation (overlap)
- Discretized version maintains the continuous integral property

**Constraints**:
1. $h \in [0,1]^n$ (actual binary values enforced)
2. $\sum_{i} h_i = n/2$ exactly

**Optimization Formulation**:

**Stages**:
**A) Global Search (BPSO)**

Minimize $C(h)$
Subject to:
- $h \in \{0,1\}^n$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{n} h_i = n/2$

**B) Local Refinement**

Minimize $C(h)$
Subject to:
- $h \in \{0,1\}^n$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{n} h_i = n/2$

**Nonlinear Transformation**:
$$
\min_{h} \max_{k} \left| \left(h \star (1-h)\right)[k]\right| + \rho \|h - h_{proj}\|_2^2
$$

where $h_{proj}$ is a soft projection toward feasible solutions.

**Representation**:
- $n$ is the grid size (default 70)
- $h$ is represented as a binary vector in $\mathbb{R}^n$

**Feasibility**:
- Both stages enforce:
   - Binary values $h_i \in \{0,1\}$
   - Exact sum $n/2$
- Handling is done by:
   - Penalty terms in objective
   - Reparameterization as indicator function
