### Approach and Rationale
The Erds minimum overlap problem is to find a step function \( h: [0,2] \to [0,1] \) with \( \int h \,dx = 1 \) that minimizes the maximum overlap integral \( C_5 = \max_k \int h(x)(1-h(x+k)) \,dx \). This is a non-convex, discontinuous optimization problem due to the cross-correlation structure. The approach combines multi-resolution optimization, machine learning-based initialization, and derivative-free local search to systematically explore the solution space. The key steps are:
1. Transfer the given base solution to higher resolutions while preserving integral constraints
2. Use transformer-based embeddings to initialize high-resolution solutions
3. Apply a monotonic gradient-free optimizer (BOBYA) to refine solutions
4. Combine candidates from multiple resolutions to find the global optimum

The multi-resolution strategy leverages the fact that higher resolution allows finer control over the function shape, while the transformer-based initialization provides improved starting points through functional approximation. The optimization focuses on feasible regions using a reformulation of the non-convex objective.

### Mathematical Specification
**Variables:**  
- Input resolution \( n \)-point tensor \( \mathbf{h} \in \mathbb{R}^{n} \) with \( 0 \leq h[i] \leq 1 \)
- Constraint: \( \sum h[i] = n/2 \)

**Objective:**  
Minimize \( C_5(\mathbf{h}) = \max_{k} \left( (\text{np.correlate}(h, 1-h, \text{mode='full'}) \right) \times \frac{2}{n} \)

**Constraints:**  
- Box bounds: \( \mathbf{H}_{\text{low}} \preceq \mathbf{h} \preceq \mathbf{H}_{\text{high}} \)
- Integral constraint \( \mathbf{s}_{\text{ref}} = \mathbf{h} - \mathbf{h}_{\text{ref}} = 0 \)

**Notation:**  
- \( \mathbf{h}_{\text{ref}} \): Reference low-resolution solution
- \( \mathbf{S}_{\text{res}} \): Candidate solution set at resolution `res`

**Feasibility Restoration:**  
Adjust \( \mathbf{h} \) by projecting onto feasible set via:  
\[
\mathbf{h}_{\text{clip}} = \max\left(0, \min\left(1, \mathbf{h} + \varepsilon \cdot \mathbf{s}_{\text{ref}}\right)\right)
\]

**Objective Transformation:**  
Reduce dimensionality by projecting onto low-res space, then:
\[
C_5(\mathbf{h}) = \max\left(C_5(\mathbf{h}_{\text{clip}}), 1 - C_{\text{autocorr}}(\mathbf{h})\right)
\]
where \( C_{\text{autocorr}} \) enforces minimal negative correlation at lag shifts.
