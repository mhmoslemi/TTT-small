This mixed-integer solver aims to bypass local minima by maintaining a diverse population of candidate functions simultaneously. The key idea is:

2. Apply mutation operations (combining parent functions) to create new offspring

4. Periodically inject novel candidate structures from uninitialized random generation

6. Minimize $C_5=\max_k \int h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx$ using the provided correlation evaluator

This method specifically addresses the inadequacy of gradient-based optimization by providing global exploration in concert with local refinement in the form of repair and pattern generation. The algorithm avoids analytic gradients and relies solely on function evaluations.
