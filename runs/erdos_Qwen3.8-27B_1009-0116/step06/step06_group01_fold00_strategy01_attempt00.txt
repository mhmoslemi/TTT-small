**Stepwise Regression**  
- **Goal**: Model the sales as a function of time with seasonal and trend components using L1 or L2 penalized regression.
- **Implementation**:  
  1. Normalize historical sales data and split into training and testing sets.
  2. Use recursive least squares with early stopping to fit the model on training data.
  3. Regularize using L2 penalty to handle multicollinearity (time, time², seasonality).
  4. Tune hyperparameters (regularization strength, rolling window size) via cross-validation.
- **Dimensions**: Features include temporals time lags (lags from 1 to 12) plus seasonal dummies.

**Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)**  
- **Approach**:  
  - Input: Historical sales (time series), daily seasonality indicator.
  - Output: Predicted sales for the next period.
  - Training: Use BCE with smmothed targets (label smoothing) to avoid sharp peaks.
  - Hyperparameters: 4 hidden layers (16, 32, 16 nodes), ReLU activation.
  - Data prep: Pad with leading zeros or use time distributed layers.

**Rolling Forecast Combination**  
- **Method**:  
  - Collect forecasts from 2-3 independent models (ARIMA, Prophet, Bi-LSTM).
  - Compute weighted average based on recent performance (PBIL or Bayesian weights).
  - Confidence intervals: Gaussian Process quantile regression on historical forecast errors.

**Time Series Feature Engineering**  
- **Features**:  
  - `rolling_mean_7days`: 7-day rolling mean→L1.
  - `weekday_diff`: Difference from average weekday value→L2.
  - `moving_std`: 14-day moving standard deviation→L2.
  - `event_encoding`: Encode holidays or promotions→Binary.

  - Output: Fully connected layer with 24-hour period encoding.
