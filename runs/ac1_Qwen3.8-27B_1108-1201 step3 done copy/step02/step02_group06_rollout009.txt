Let me formalize this:
- Objective: minimize 2*n*max(c)/(sum a)^2
- Since it's scale invariant, fix sum(a) = sqrt(2n) (or any constant)
- Minimize max(c)
- Gradient: g[j] = sum_k w[k] * a[k-j] where w = normalized exp(c/tau)
- Update: a = max(0, a - lr * g), then renormalize

The key parameters are tau (smoothing) and lr (learning rate). We can anneal tau from large (smooth) to small (sharp max).
