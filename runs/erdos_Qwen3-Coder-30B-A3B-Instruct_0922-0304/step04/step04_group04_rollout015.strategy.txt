#### Function Representation

$$ h(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} c_k \psi_k(x) $$

- $\psi_k(x)$ are Haar wavelet basis functions
- $c_k$ are coefficients to be determined

#### Haar Wavelet Basis

$$ \psi(x) = \begin{cases}
1 & 0 \le x < 0.5 \\
-1 & 0.5 \le x < 1 \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} $$

$$ \phi(x) = \begin{cases}
1 & 0 \le x < 1 \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} $$

Scaled and translated versions form the basis:
$$ \psi_j(x) = \psi(2^j x - k) \quad \text{for } j \ge 0, k = 0,1,...,2^j $$

#### Objective Function
The overlap integral becomes:
$$ C_5 = \max_{k} \left| \int_0^2 h(x)(1 - h(x+k)) dx \right| $$
Which in wavelet space:
$$ C_5 = \max_{k} \left| \sum_{i,j} c_i c_j \int_0^2 \psi_i(x)\psi_j(x+k) dx \right| $$

#### Constraints
1. Integral constraint:
   $$ \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} c_k \int_0^2 \psi_k(x) dx = 1 $$
2. Bounds constraint:
   $$ 0 \le \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} c_k \psi_k(x) \le 1 \quad \forall x \in [0,2] $$
3. McCormick envelopes for $c_i c_j$:
   $$ z_{ij} \ge c_i + c_j - 1 $$
   $$ z_{ij} \le c_i, z_{ij} \le c_j $$
