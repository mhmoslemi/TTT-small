### 2. Mathematical Specification

**Variables**:
- \( h \in \mathbb{R}^n \): Step function values with \( n = n\_points \).
- \( v \in \mathbb{R}^n \): Particle velocities.
- \( pbest, gbest \in \mathbb{R}^n \): Personal and global best positions.
- \( \omega, c1, c2 \): PSO parameters (inertia, cognitive, social).

**Objective**:
Minimize \( C_5 = \max_k \sum_{i=0}^{n-k-1} h[i](1 - h[i+k]) \cdot dx \), where \( dx = 2/n \).

**Constraints**:
1. \( 0 \leq h[i] \leq 1 \).
2. \( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i] = n/2 \).

**PSO Update Rule**:
\[
v_{t+1}[i] = \omega v_t[i] + c1 r1 (pbest[i] - h_t[i]) + c2 r2 (gbest[i] - h_t[i])
\]
\[
h_{t+1}[i] = h_t[i] + v_{t+1}[i]
\]

**Gradient Descent Refinement**:
\[
\Delta h[i] = -\eta \frac{\partial C_5}{\partial h[i]}
\]
\[
h_{refined}[i] = h[i] + \Delta h[i]
\]
