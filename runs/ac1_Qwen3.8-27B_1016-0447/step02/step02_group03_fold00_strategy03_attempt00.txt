# Approach and Rationale
We employ proximal gradient descent with a carefully constructed surrogate function that approximates the Gumbel-softmax reparameterization trick to handle discrete-like optimization in a continuous space. The core idea is to induce a stochastic embedding of structured sequence patterns while maintaining gradient flow. This approach differs from previous methods by directly addressing the underlying combinatorial challenge inherent in autocorrelation minimization—sequences that are highly structured but not obviously smooth or bandlimited. Our innovation is a two-layer optimization scheme: an upper-level stochastic embedding to explore sparse patterns, and a lower-level smooth descent to polish solutions. The primary bottleneck addressed is the curse of dimensionality in joint optimization, solved through projection back onto a low-dimensional latent space.

The strategy is motivated by these optimality guarantees:
- Provable submodularity of relative gain metrics

- Intrinsic feasibility through explicit projection not implicit in constraints
