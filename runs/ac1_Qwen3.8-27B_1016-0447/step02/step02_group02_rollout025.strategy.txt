The central idea is to minimize the AC1 upper bound by leveraging a projection-based gradient descent approach within the probability simplex (i.e., sequences with nonnegative entries summing to 1). The justification is that the objective is scale-invariant, meaning we can fix the sequence norm and minimize its autocorrelation spectrum. The key differences from the parent are: (1) explicit use of projection to maintain the simplex constraint, (2) efficient gradient computation using FFT-based convolution, and (3) smarter line search and adaptive step control to escape local minima. Limitations include the need for precise gradient/magnitude estimates and potential initialization bias.
