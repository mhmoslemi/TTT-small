1. Approach and Rationale

The proposed approach leverages iterative refinement combined with physically-inspired regularization to minimize the AC1 upper bound. We begin with a sequence containing large amplitude pulses spaced periodically (achieved via a gap sequence) and iteratively adjust pulse widths, spacings, and amplitudes based on gradients informed by FFT convolutions. This sparse representation minimizes the maximum signal strength by concentrating energy and reducing cross-correlations. The core idea is that sequences with well-defined periodicity and controlled magnitude variation have compact energy distributions that failures to use high density sampling would ensure.

The expected improvement stems from focusing search not only on reducing the peak convolution value but also on maintaining a narrow bandwidth through spectral control. The approach balances several aspects:

- Energy concentration: Controls sum² in the denominator without artificially normalizing
- Dynamic sparsity: Insures that convolution peaks are statistically isolated
- Gradient steering: Uses FFT-derived cues about convolution peak locations to impact pulse separations
- Iterative reweighted sparsity: Each cycle adjusts the influence of different convolution indices on optimization

This multi-faceted approach stands in contrast to gradient descent by incorporating physical structure into candidate generation. The outcome is more targeted energy management and potentially deeper reduction in convolution peak than possible through random or gradient-only searches.

2. Mathematical Specification

Representation:
- A sequence consists of $N = 4498$ non-negative real numbers in [0, 1000]
- Sequences optimized are characterized by a periodic pattern of a few large amplitude regions interspersed with silence, and a retained constant energetic floor even after any variation

Objective:
$$
\min_{a \in \mathbb{R}^N_{\geq 0}} \quad f(a) = \frac{2 \times 4498 \times \max_{\text{lags}} (\text{linear\_convolve}(a, a)[k] }{(\sum_n a_n)^2}
$$

Constraints:
- Non-negativity: $0 \leq a_i \leq 1000 \ \forall i$
- Finite energy: $\sum a_i \geq 0.01$

Special Variables:
