The core idea is to initially explore the high-dimensional search space with large-scale randomized mutations, then iteratively refine promising candidates by focusing on convolution indices that drive the bound. This addresses the non-convexity challenge by balancing global exploration with targeted optimization, avoiding the pitfalls of purely local search. Key limitations include the O(n) evaluation cost and the need for sparse gradient approximations, but these are mitigated through intelligent mutation selection.
