### Approach Description
We implement an adaptive perturbation management system for the coefficients of a nonnegative step-function sequence. The central idea is to systematically explore "temporal bands" of coefficient adjustments to minimize the autocorrelation coefficient 1 (AC1) upper bound defined by the AutoCorrelation Analyzer. The strategy contrasts by:

1. Using energy-sensitivity clustering to identify critical indices
2. Implementing asymmetric perturbation dynamics with exponential bias toward low-energy regions
3. Maintaining a memory of recent improvement gradients
4. Adapting perturbation magnitude to function value and standard deviation

This method addresses the parent strategy's limitations by systematically targeting regions important for reducing the max autocorrelation, avoiding random drift, and explicitly tracking convergence to improve runtime performance.

### Mathematical Specifications
Our formulation defines the AC1 bound as:

**Objective:** 
\[ AC1(a) = \frac{2 \cdot n \cdot M(a)}{ [\sum a_i]^2 } \]
where \( M(a) = \max_{k=0}^{2n-2} (\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a_i a_{i+k}) \)

**Constraints:**
1. \( 0 \leq a_i \leq 1000 \)
2. \( \sum a_i \geq 0.01 \)

**Region Partitioning:**
1. Identify high-magnitude regions: indices \( i \) where \( |a_i| > \mu + 3\sigma \)
2. Identify low-magnitude regions: indices \( i \) where \( 0.1\sigma \leq |a_i| \leq \mu + \sigma \)
3. Stationary regions: indices \( i \) where \( |a_i| < 0.1\sigma \)

**Memory Management:**
Store autocorrelation gradients for 10 previous evaluations and adapt perturbation size based on:

- Current solution speed
- Local convergence velocity
- Magnitude of gradient components

### Core Update Procedure
1. Evaluate current solution using AutoCorrelation Inequalities
2. Compute running statistics:
   - \( \mu = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i a_i \)
   - \( \sigma = (\sum_i|a_i - \mu|^2/n)^{0.5} \)
3. Partition indices into:
   - Critical zone (high-magnitude - 3σ)
   - Sensitive zone (mid-magnitude - 1σ)

   \( \nabla_{a_i} AC1 = -2AC1 \frac{ \sum_j a_j (\sum_{i} a_i a_{i+j}) }{ [\sum a_i]^2 } \)
5. Adaptive perturbation procedure:
   - Critical zone: target reduction by (edge-based scaling)
   - Generic zones: moderate scaling based on historical slope
   - Stationary zone: minimal adjustment
