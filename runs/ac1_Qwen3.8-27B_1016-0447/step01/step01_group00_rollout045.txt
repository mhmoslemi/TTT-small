sum_k (x*x)[k]^2 = sum_{i,j,k,l} x[i]x[k-i]x[j]x[k-j] (very complex)

I don't think there's a closed-form solution. Gradient-based methods are the way to go.

One more thought: the problem has a symmetry: if x ispalindrome (x[i] = x[n-1-i]), then (x*x) is also palindrome. The maximum might be at the center. By breaking this symmetry, we might be able to reduce the max.

The original program adds a small linear tilt to break mirror symmetry. I'll do something similar but more principled.
