For a random sequence with i.i.d. entries, the convolution values are sums of products, and by CLT they're approximately Gaussian with mean ≈ S^2/(2n) and variance depending on the fourth moment. The peak of (2n-1) such values would be about mean + std * sqrt(2*ln(2n)).
