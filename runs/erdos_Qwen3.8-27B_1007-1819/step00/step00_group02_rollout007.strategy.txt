This differs from the previous approach in two key ways:
1. The problem is approached with a probabilistic interpretation, framing h as a stochastic process with fixed mean and variance, leading to a score-based objective that scales more efficiently for large n.
2. Rather than focusing on local gradients with SLSQP and global simulated annealing, this method uses a scoring-reweighting mechanism inspired by reinforcement learning to guide the optimization, ensuring broader exploration without requiring explicit derivations for specific constraints.
