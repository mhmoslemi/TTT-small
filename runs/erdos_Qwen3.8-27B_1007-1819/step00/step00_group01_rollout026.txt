    k_lag is the lag index in the 'full' correlation output (0 to 2n-2).
    The actual shift is k_shift = k_lag - (n-1).

    # ∂/∂h[j] of -h[i]*h[i+k] contributes:

    #   -h[j-k] when i+k=j i.e. i=j-k
