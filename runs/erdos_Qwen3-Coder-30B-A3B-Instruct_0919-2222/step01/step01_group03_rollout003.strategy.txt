**1. Problem insight**

The objective  

\[
C_5(h)=\max_{k}\int_{0}^{2}h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)\,dx
\]

is the maximum over all shifts of the *overlap* between the function \(h\) and its complement.  
Because  

\[
\int h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx
   =\int_{I_k}h(x)\,dx-\int_{I_k}h(x)h(x+k)\,dx,
\]

where \(I_k\) is the interval where both \(h(x)\) and \(h(x+k)\) are defined, the problem is to **keep the product \(h(x)h(x+k)\) large for every shift**.  
In the discrete setting with samples \(h_i\) (spacing \(dx=2/n\))

\[
c_k=\sum_{i\in I_k}h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+k}\bigr)  \qquad
C_5=\max_{k}c_k\;dx .
\]

Thus the design variable must satisfy  

* box constraints \(0\le h_i\le1\)  
* an equality constraint \(\sum_i h_i = n/2\) (the integral equals 1).

* add \(w_s\,(1-h_j)\) to \(\nabla_i\);
