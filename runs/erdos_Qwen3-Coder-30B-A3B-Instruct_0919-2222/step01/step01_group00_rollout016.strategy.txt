**1.  Mathematical Re‑formulation**

The objective used in the evaluator is  

\[
F(h)=\max_{k}\;C_k(h),\qquad 
C_k(h)=\int_0^2 h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)\,dx .
\]

With a uniform grid of `n` points (`dx=2/n`) the discrete analogue is  

\[
C_k(h)=dx\;\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i\;\bigl(1-h_{i+k}\bigr)\,,\qquad 
k\in\{-n+1,\dots ,n-1\},
\]

where out‑of‑range indices are treated as zero (exactly what `np.correlate(...,mode='full')` computes).  
The feasible set is the *capped simplex*

\[
\mathcal{S}=\Bigl\{h\in[0,1]^n \;|\;dx\sum_i h_i =1\Bigr\}
\;\Longleftrightarrow\;
\Bigl\{h\in[0,1]^n \;|\;\sum_i h_i =\tfrac{n}{2}\Bigr\}.
\]

Thus we must **minimise** a non‑smooth convex‑concave functional over a convex polytope.

\[

\[

All indicator masks are pre‑computed once per `n`, so a full gradient evaluation costs only a few vector operations.

*Rationale*
