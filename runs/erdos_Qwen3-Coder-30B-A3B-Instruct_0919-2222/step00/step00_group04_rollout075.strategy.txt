### Detailed recipe

1. **Pick a symmetric template.**  
   A convenient family is the *three‑level* shape:
   - outer zones of total length `2·a` (`a∈[0.5,1]`), each having height `v∈[0,1]`;
   - a central plateau of length `2‑2a` with height `1`.  

   The area constraint yields  
   \[
   2av + (2-2a)\cdot1 = 1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
   v = 1 - \frac{1}{2a}.
   \]  
   Thus the whole family is parameterised by a single scalar `a` (the outer‑zone length).  For `a=0.5` the outer height is `0` (pure “flat‑top” interval); for `a=1` the whole interval is constant `0.5`.

   - outer zone length `a`, height `0`;

   \[
   2a·0 + 2b·u + (2-2a-2b)·1 = 1,
