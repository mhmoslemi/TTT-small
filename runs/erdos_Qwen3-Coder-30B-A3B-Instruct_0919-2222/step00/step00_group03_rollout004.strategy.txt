### 2. Exact MILP Formulation (binary `h`)

The product `h[i]·h[j]` is linearised with an auxiliary continuous variable
`y_{i,j}` that satisfies the McCormick envelope **exactly** because `h` will be binary:

```
y_{i,j} ≤ h[i]
y_{i,j} ≤ h[j]
y_{i,j} ≥ h[i] + h[j] − 1            (2)
```

With binary `h[i] ∈ {0,1}` these constraints force `y_{i,j}=h[i]·h[j]`.

**Variables**

| Symbol | Type | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|
| `h[i]` | binary | indicator of the step‑function at sample `i` |
| `y[i,d]` | continuous, 0 ≤ ≤ 1 | product `h[i]·h[i+d]` for shift `d ≥ 1` |
| `T`   | continuous, ≥0 | upper bound on the *scaled* overlap `Δ = n/2 − Σ y[i,d]` (see below) |

**Constraints**

1. **Mass constraint** – exactly half the points are 1:  

   `Σ_i h[i] = n/2`.

2. **Product constraints** – (2) for every pair `(i,i+d)` with `d = 1,…,n‑1` and `i = 0,…,n‑d‑1`.

3. **Shift‑wise overlap constraints** – using (1), define

**Objective**
