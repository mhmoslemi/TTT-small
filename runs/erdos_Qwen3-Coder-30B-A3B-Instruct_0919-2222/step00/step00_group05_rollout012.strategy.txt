### 1.  Mathematical reformulation
For a discretised step‑function `h` (length `n`, spacing `dx = 2/n`) we have  

\[
C(h)=\max_{k}\; \int_{0}^{2} h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)\,dx
      =\max_{s}\;dx\;\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+s}\bigr)
\]

where the sum is taken only over indices that stay inside the array
(`np.correlate` with `mode='full'` implements exactly this zero‑padded correlation).

Because the total mass is fixed,  

\[
\int_{0}^{2}h(x)\,dx = \sum_i h_i\,dx = 1,
\]

the objective can be written as  

\[
C(h)=1-\min_{s}\;\sum_i h_i h_{i+s}\,dx .
\]

Thus **minimising** `C(h)` is equivalent to **maximising** the minimal auto‑correlation
\(\displaystyle R_{\min}(h)=\min_{s}\sum_i h_i h_{i+s}\,dx\).
The problem is a *min–max* quadratic program with linear box constraints
\(0\le h_i\le 1\) and a linear equality \(\sum_i h_i=n/2\).
