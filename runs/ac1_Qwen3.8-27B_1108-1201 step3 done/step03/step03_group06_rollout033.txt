Let me also think about a completely different approach: **direct optimization of the peak location**.

The idea: if we know which lag k* achieves the maximum, we can formulate a convex subproblem:
minimize (conv(a,a))[k*] subject to sum(a)=1, a>=0, and (conv(a,a))[k] <= (conv(a,a))[k*] for all k.

But this is still non-convex because of the second constraint.
