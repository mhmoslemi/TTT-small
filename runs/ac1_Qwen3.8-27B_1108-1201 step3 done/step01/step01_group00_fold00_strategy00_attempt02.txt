The key challenge is to find a step function sequence \(a \in \mathbb{R}^n\) that minimizes the peak of the linear auto-correlation function \(\|a * a\|_\infty\), normalized by \(\frac{\|a\|^2}{(\sum a)^2}\). This problem is highly nonconvex, and our strategy leverages gradient-based optimization with tempered constraints to escape shallow local minima.

1. **Gradient-based Optimization (Phase 1):**

   - The sequence is parameterized in log-space to ensure nonnegativity, normalized via softmax.

\[
\theta \rightarrow s = \sigma(\theta) \quad (\text{supported constraint space})
