1. **Approach and rationale**
The Erdos minimum overlap problem seeks to find a step function `h` on [0,2] with integral 1 that minimizes the maximum overlap integral `C5` with its shifts. The current record is 0.3808, and our goal is to find a lower bound for the constant.
