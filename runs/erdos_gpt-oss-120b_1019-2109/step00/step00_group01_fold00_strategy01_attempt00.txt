Fundamental Problem Analysis:

We are to find a step function h(x) on [0,2] with values in [0,1] and integral over [0,2] equal to 1, that minimizes the maximum overlap integral defined as:

C_5 = max_k \int_0^2 h(x) (1 - h(x+k))  dx

converted discretely to:

C_5 = max_k np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full') * dx

with dx=2/n_points, and feasibility conditions: 0<=h[i]<=1 and sum(h)*dx=1 (i.e., sum(h)=n_points/2).

The initial solution is provided with n_points=47, C_5≈0.3808.

Our goal is to find a better (lower) upper bound for the Erds constant.

Given the time budget and lack of global optimization methods to solve the problem directly, we will use a heuristic: population-based search (like genetic algorithms) to explore the vast solution space.

The problem is nonconvex and the search space is discrete with constraints. We choose evolutionary algorithms because:

2. They perform well around numerous optima in hill-valley-like landscapes
3. They are adaptable to the cost function (overlap integral via convolution)

Key implementation details:

Optimization Approach:

We will use a (mu, lambda) evolution strategy with a repair mechanism. However, given the computational cost of each evaluation (O(n_points^2) due to convolution), we need efficiency.

In each generation:

- Initialize / select mu (usually taken from parent and crossings)
- For each offspring, perform mutation and apply repair (if needed)
- Compute fitness via the correlation and dx multiplication
