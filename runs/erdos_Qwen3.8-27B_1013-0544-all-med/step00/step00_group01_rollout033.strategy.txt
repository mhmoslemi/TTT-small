### 1. Approach and Rationale
We use a hybrid optimization approach combining simulated annealing (SA) with adaptive grid refinement to minimize the Erdős minimum overlap integral \(C\). Simulated annealing navigates the complex non-convex landscape of the objective function, while adaptive grid refinement balances exploration and exploitation across resolutions. The key bottlenecks addressed are:
1. **Non-convexity**: The objective's dependence on correlations makes it non-convex; SA handles this through probabilistic uphill moves
2. **Grid size limitations**: Higher resolution improves accuracy but at the cost of computational expense; adaptive doubling optimizes resource allocation
3. **Local optima avoidance**: Memory-guided search and restart mechanisms prevent premature convergence

The approach leverages empirical observations from the literature that optimal solutions often exhibit symmetry and harmonic variation, guiding the search structure.
