2. Mathematical specification:

Minimization Problem:
   minimize_{h \in \mathbb{R}^{n}} \max_{k} \int_0^2 h(x)(1-h(x+k)) \, dx \\
   subject to: 0 \leq h_i \leq 1, \sum_{i} h_i \cdot dx = 1

Variable Representation:
   \use{n_{act}} (active dimension) as the grid size with $dx=2.0/n_{act}$

Objective Function:
   \use{C_5} = \max_{k} \left( \text{conv}[h, 1-h](k) \right) \cdot dx

where conv is the full cross-correlation. This is discretized to yield a smooth real-valued function to minimize.

Constraint Transformation:
   Integral constraint is enforced numerically via projection. Bounds are maintained by clipping followed by balancing.
