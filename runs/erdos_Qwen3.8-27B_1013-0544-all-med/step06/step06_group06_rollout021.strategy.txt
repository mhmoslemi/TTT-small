The proposed solution departs from standard optimization methods by employing a **deterministic piecewise polynomial approximation** that adapts its complexity to satisfy both the integral and continuity constraints with minimal bracketing of Fourier modes. Inspired by the Erdős discrepancy problem's connection to harmonic analysis, we decompose the function h(x) into a basis of orthogonal functions that naturally minimize correlation across shifts. The algorithm iteratively refines the polynomial coefficients to approach the minimal C_5 value.

The piecewise polynomial approach offers a structured, convex-requiring framework initially, transitioning to nonlinear optimization when needed.
