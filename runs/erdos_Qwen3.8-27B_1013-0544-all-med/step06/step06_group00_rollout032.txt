For a given h of length n, the gradient of the smooth max of correlate(h, 1-h, full) w.r.t. h is:

grad[j] = dx * sum_k w_k * [(1-h[j-(k-n+1)]) * 1[0<=j-(k-n+1)<n] - h[j+(k-n+1)] * 1[0<=j+(k-n+1)<n]]

Let me define:
A[j] = sum_k w_k * (1-h[j-(k-n+1)]) * 1[0<=j-(k-n+1)<n]
B[j] = sum_k w_k * h[j+(k-n+1)] * 1[0<=j+(k-n+1)<n]

For A: let i = j-(k-n+1), so k = j-n+1+i
A[j] = sum_i (1-h[i]) * w[j-n+1+i] * 1[0<=i<n] * 1[0<=j-n+1+i<2n-1]
     = sum_{i=0}^{n-1} (1-h[i]) * w[j-n+1+i] * 1[0<=j-n+1+i<2n-1]

This is like a cross-correlation of (1-h) with w, but with a window.

Similarly for B: let i = j+(k-n+1), so k = n-1+i-j
B[j] = sum_i w[n-1+i-j] * h[i] * 1[0<=i<n] * 1[0<=n-1+i-j<2n-1]
