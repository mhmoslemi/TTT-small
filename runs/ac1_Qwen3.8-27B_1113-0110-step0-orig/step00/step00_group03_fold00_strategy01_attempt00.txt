The goal is to minimize the score defined as:
- **Score** = \((2 \times n \times \text{max}(c)) / (\text{sum}(a))^2\)
where \(c\) is the full convolution of the sequence \(a\) and \(n\) is the sequence length (2535).

### Approach
The key idea is to use a gradient-based method that minimizes the maximum autocorrelation value. Start with the initial uniform sequence and iteratively update it by:
1. **Clipping**: Ensure all values remain between 0 and 1000.
2. **Scaling**: Adjust the sequence such that its sum is \(\sqrt{2n}\) (same as the initial sequence).
3. **Gradient Calculation**: Compute the gradient to minimize the maximum convolution value, even though the sequence is non-negative.
4. **Iterative Update**: Apply the gradient to the sequence and clamp values to avoid negatives while keeping imitations.
