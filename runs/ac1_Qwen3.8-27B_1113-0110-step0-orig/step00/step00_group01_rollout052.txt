Actually, the issue is that for p near the crossover, both the center and edge contributions are comparable, and the true max is the larger of the two. At the exact crossover, they're equal, and J = 2n * (edge value) / sum^2.

Let me compute J at the crossover more carefully.
At crossover: c[n-1] = c[2n-2] = C^2 * n^{2p}.
sum = C * n^{p+1}/(p+1) (using the integral approximation).
sum^2 = C^2 * n^{2p+2}/(p+1)^2.
J = 2n * C^2 * n^{2p} / (C^2 * n^{2p+2}/(p+1)^2) = 2n * (p+1)^2 / n^2 = 2(p+1)^2/n.
