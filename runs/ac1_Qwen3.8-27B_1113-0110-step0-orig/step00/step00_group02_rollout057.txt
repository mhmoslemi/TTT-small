Let me also think about the "direction" of perturbation. For a near-constant sequence:
- The gradient of the max constraint is uniform (as I showed earlier)
- So gradient descent alone won't help
- We need to use the softmax gradient, which weights all constraints
- For a near-constant sequence, conv is approximately triangular
- The softmax gradient will weight the center more heavily
- The subgradient at the center is 2*a (symmetric), so it's still uniform
- But the subgradient at other positions is 2*shift_k(a), which is NOT uniform
- So the softmax-weighted gradient will have some non-uniformity, even for a near-constant sequence!
