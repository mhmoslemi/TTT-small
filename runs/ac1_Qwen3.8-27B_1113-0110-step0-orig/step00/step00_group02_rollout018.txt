    # (f * b)_k = sum_i f[i] * b[k-i] (with appropriate bounds)

                cols.append(j)

Let me think about this from the Fourier perspective. The linear convolution of a with a (length n) can be computed via:

2. Compute FFT of padded a.

4. Inverse FFT.
5. Take the first 2n-1 elements.
