### Approach and Rationale

We aim to minimize the AC1 upper bound, which is defined as `2 * n * max(|convolution(a, a, mode="full")|) / (sum(a))^2`, by refining the provided parent sequence. We reduce the risk of abrupt oscillations that might increase the bound by replacing sharp transients with smoother, exponentially decaying profiles. Every coefficient should be replaced by a weighted component of `exp(-|distance_from_center|^p_k)` terms, normalized across the feature space. This ensures the sequence maintains minimal AC1 by reducing autocorrelation peaks.

\text{AC1}(a) = \frac{2 \cdot n \cdot \max|\text{convolve}(a, a, \text{mode}=\text{"full"})[k]|}{(\sum a)^2}
\]

#### Representation
Each sequence coefficient \( a_i \) is derived from a basis of Laplacian-spread features:
\[
a_i = \sum_{k=1}^{K} w_k \cdot \text{exp}\left(- \frac{|i/c_k - j_k|^p}{\sigma_k}\right)
\]
where \( w_k, \sigma_k \) are learned weights and positions.

Constraints ensure non-negativity and reasonable scaling:
\[
0 \leq a_i \leq 1000 \, \forall i
\]
The sum constraint is handled via normalization after optimization, but the AC1 score is invariant under scalar multiplication, so we enforce \( \sum a \geq 0.01 \).
