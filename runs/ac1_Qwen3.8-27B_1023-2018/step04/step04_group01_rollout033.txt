Actually, I want to add one more optimization: **block coordinate descent**. Instead of updating one coordinate at a time, update a block of adjacent coordinates. This can help with the "plateau" problem where individual coordinates can't improve because the max is determined by the collective effect.

For a block of size B (say B=5 or 10), the optimization is:
- The affected convolution positions are the union of the individual affected positions.
- The objective is 2n * max of (quadratic functions of the B variables) / (S + sum of changes)^2.
- This is a small-scale optimization (B variables) that can be solved with a local method (e.g., Nelder-Mead or gradient descent).
