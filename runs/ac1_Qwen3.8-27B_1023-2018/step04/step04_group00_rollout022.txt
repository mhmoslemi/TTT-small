The LSE objective:
$f_T(a) = T \cdot \text{logsumexp}(c(a) / T)$

where $c(a) = a * a$ (full convolution).

Gradient:
$\nabla_a f_T = 2 \cdot \text{cross\_corr}(w, a)$

where $w_j = \text{softmax}(c_j / T) = \exp(c_j/T) / \sum_k \exp(c_k/T)$.

For the log-softmax parameterization $a = \text{softmax}(u)$:
$\nabla_u f_T = a \odot (\nabla_a f_T - a^T \nabla_a f_T)$
