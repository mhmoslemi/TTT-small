- The change is: c[k] += 2w*a[k-i] for k in [i, i+n-1], and c[2i] += w^2

This is an O(n) update (the affected range has n elements). I can track the max by maintaining a max-heap or by recomputing the max over the affected range plus the unchanged max.

For the 1D subproblem, I need to find the w that minimizes:
f(w) = 2n * max(max_{k in [i, i+n-1]} (c[k] + 2w*a[k-i] + w^2*δ_{k,2i}), max_const) / (1+w)^2

where max_const = max(c[k] for k outside [i, i+n-1]).

This is a piecewise function. The "max" over the affected range is the upper envelope of n linear (or quadratic at one point) functions. Finding the minimum of f(w) over w >= 0 (or w > -v to keep a[i] >= 0) is a 1D optimization.

For efficiency:
- Precompute the affected c segment: c_seg = c[i:i+n] (length n)
- Precompute the influence: infl = 2*a (length n) -- wait, a[k-i] for k in [i, i+n-1] is a[0], a[1], ..., a[n-1]
- The w^2 term is at position 2i-i = i in the segment (0-indexed)

For a grid of w values:
affected = c_seg[None, :] + ws[:, None] * infl[None, :]  (G x n)
affected[:, i] += ws**2  (add w^2 to column i)
max_affected = affected.max(axis=1)  (G values)
total_max = np.maximum(max_affected, max_const)  (G values)
scores = 2*n*total_max / (1+ws)**2  (G values)

Then golden section refine in the best interval.
