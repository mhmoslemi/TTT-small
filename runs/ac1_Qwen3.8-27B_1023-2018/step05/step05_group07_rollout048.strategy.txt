Approach and Rationale
The central idea is to minimize the AC1 upper bound by iteratively improving a candidate sequence. Unlike previous methods that use linear programming and gradient searches, this approach employs a hybrid strategy of trust-region optimization combined with spectral gradient methods. The rationale is that the AC1 upper bound is a difficult non-linear optimization problem with many local minima. By combining trust-region (which ensures meaningful steps) and spectral gradient (which is efficient by avoiding explicit Hessian computation), we achieve both faster convergence and robustness near flat regions. This addresses the bottleneck of manually crafting indices versus solving large LPs by automating the search through a more sophisticated gradient methodology.

Mathematical Specification
We represent the sequence as a vector \(x \in \mathbb{R}^n\), initialized to satisfy \(x_i \geq 0\) and \(x_i \leq 1000\) for all \(i\). The objective is to minimize:
\[
f(x) = \frac{2n \cdot \max_{k=0}^{2n-1} (x \star x)_k}{(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i)^2}
\]
where \(\star\) denotes linear convolution. The sequence length \(n\) is varied across {100, 200, 350, 500} during the search. Constraints are handled via penalty functions: the degree of constraint violation \(c(x) = \max(\max(x_i - 1000) \), 0\) and projection ensures all \(x_i \geq 0\) and \(\sum x_i \geq 0.01\).

Implementation Procedure
1. Initialization:
   - Read parent sequence if available, interpolate to n=350 if needed, else use uniform sequence.
   - Project sequence to satisfy constraints and compute initial objective.

2. Hybrid Optimization:
   - Use spectral gradient step (with finite differencing for gradient) to determine a descent direction.
   - Optionally apply globalization via trust regions to avoid failures near minima.
   - Project updated sequence to maintain constraints.

3. Length Exploration:
   - After optimization, try additional sequence lengths using interpolation.
   - For each new length, initialize a candidate and optimize.

4. Termination:
   - Track best scoring sequence: minimal \(f(x)\)
   - Stop if no improvement in last 100 evaluations or total time exceeds budget.

Search Choices
- **Lengths**: {100, 200, 350, 500}. Large steps between 200 and 350 balance evaluation cost and diversity.
- **Trust Region**: Enabled for sequences at risk of boundary violation (\(|\nabla f| < 1e-1\)).
- **Spectral Gradient**: Always used, with finite differencing to 5 points (central difference for better accuracy).

Computebudget and Robustness
- Each optimization: 200 function evaluations, each involving a \(O(n^2)\) convolution.
- Total budget: Cap at 1200 seconds for evaluation.
- Fallback: Maintains best scored, non-empty sequence.

Validation and Return Contract
