   score = (2 * n * max(convolve(a, a)) / (sum(a))^2

   - Each element of `a` (a nonnegative step function) is in [0, 1000]
   - The sum of `a` is at least 0.01 after clipping (though the evaluator first clips to [0,1000] and then requires sum>=0.01)

The goal is to find a sequence `a` (of length 3072) that minimizes `score` (since lower is better).

However, note that the problem is ill-conditioned due to the ratio form and the convolution. We are going to use the following approach:

1. **Initialization**: Start from the provided parent sequence (if available, else a uniform sequence of length 3072). The parent sequence is of length 3072. We are allowed to change the length by resampling.

2. **Optimization**: We will use a combination of local and global search methods. Since the objective function is non-convex and has many local minima, we need an approach that can escape local minima. We consider:

   - **Gradient-based optimization** (L-BFGS-B) for fine-tuning around good candidates.
   - **Random resampling** and **coordinate descent** for exploration.

3. **Surrogate and exact evaluation**: We will use a surrogate (stochastic/gradient) optimization for most of the time, because the exact evaluation is expensive.
