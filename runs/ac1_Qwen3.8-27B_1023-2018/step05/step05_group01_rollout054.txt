For u=1: integral_0^0 ... = 0.

Is conv(1+u) < B for all u in (0,1)?
conv(1+u) = integral_0^{1-u} (y+u)^alpha (1-y)^alpha dy.
The integrand (y+u)^alpha (1-y)^alpha for u > 0: at y=0, it's u^alpha. At y=1, it's 0.
Compared to u=0: y^alpha (1-y)^alpha.

For u > 0, the integrand is shifted: (y+u)^alpha > y^alpha for u > 0, but the integration range is shorter [0, 1-u] vs [0, 1].
