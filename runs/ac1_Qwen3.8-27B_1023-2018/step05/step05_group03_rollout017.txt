∂f/∂u[m] = Σ_j (∂f/∂x[j]) * ∂x[j]/∂u[m]
= Σ_j g[j] * x[j] * (δ[j,m] - x[m])
= x[m]*g[m] - x[m]*Σ_j g[j]*x[j]
= x[m] * (g[m] - x·g)

So the gradient in u-space is: (softmax(u)) * (g - x·g)

This is clean. Let me use this parameterization.
