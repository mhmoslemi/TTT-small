For a "two-peaked" sequence (high at both ends), the convolution would have high values at c[0], c[2n-2], and potentially c[n-1] (if the two peaks "reinforce" at the center).

Hmm, this is getting complicated analytically. Let me just trust the numeric optimization.

Let me focus on writing clean, efficient code that:
1. Uses the parent as initialization.
2. Runs LSE optimization with a good temperature schedule.
3. Does coordinate descent polishing.
4. Tries a few perturbations and re-optimizes.
5. Returns the best result.
