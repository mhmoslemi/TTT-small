u = log(a), a = exp(u) / sum(exp(u))

Then:
∂a[i]/∂u[j] = a[i] * (δ_ij - a[j])

The gradient in u-space:
∂f/∂u[j] = sum_i (∂f/∂a[i]) * (∂a[i]/∂u[j])
= sum_i g[i] * a[i] * (δ_ij - a[j])
= g[j]*a[j] - a[j]*sum_i g[i]*a[i]
= a[j] * (g[j] - sum_i g[i]*a[i])

So the gradient in u-space is:

This ensures sum(g_u * a) = 0, which is the tangent condition.
