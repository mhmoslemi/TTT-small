2. Mathematical Specification
Let $a$ be a sequence of length $n$ with $0 \leq a_i \leq 1000$. The objective is to minimize:
\[
\rho(a) = \frac{2n \max(\text{convolve}(a))}{(\sum a_i)^2}
\]

where $\text{convolve}(a)$ is the full-mode self-convolution of length $2n-1$. The following components are mathematically defined:
