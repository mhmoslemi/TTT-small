**Approach 2: Frank-Wolfe (conditional gradient) method**

The problem is: minimize max_k (a*a)[k] subject to a >= 0, sum(a) = 1.

This can be written as: minimize t subject to (a*a)[k] <= t for all k, a >= 0, sum(a) = 1.

Frank-Wolfe would:
1. At current a, compute the gradient of the smooth approximation or the subgradient of the max.
2. Solve the linearization: find s that minimizes <grad, s> subject to s >= 0, sum(s) = 1.
3. Update a <- (1-t)*a + t*s.

The linearization step: if we use the subgradient at the active constraint k*, the gradient w.r.t. a is 2*a[k*-j] (for the constraint at lag k*). The minimizer of a linear function over the simplex is a vertex (one-hot vector).

This is essentially what the LP approach does, but in a simpler form.
