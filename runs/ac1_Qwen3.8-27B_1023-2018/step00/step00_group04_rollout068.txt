To reduce this peak:

2. But this increases other peaks (like f(2n-2) = a[n-1]²)

The optimal trade-off is where all peaks are equal. For the linear ramp, the peak at k=n is ≈ n³/6 and the peak at k=2n-2 is a[n-1]² = n². The threshold is S²/n ≈ n³/4. So the k=n peak is 2/3 of the threshold, and the k=2n-2 peak is 4/n which is tiny. The binding constraint is the k=n peak.

To reduce the k=n peak further, I need to make the "cross-correlation" even smaller. This means making the sequence more concentrated on one end.
