Let me try a different approach: use the fact that the problem is equivalent to minimizing the L-infinity norm of p*p in the space of nonnegative probability vectors. This is a "minimax" problem.

One effective approach for minimax problems is the "multiplicative weights" or "follow-the-regularized-leader" method:

At each iteration:
1. Compute C = p*p
2. Find the worst lag k* (where C is maximized)
3. Update: p[j] ← p[j] * exp(-η * dC_{k*}/dp_j) = p[j] * exp(-η * 2*p[k*-j])
4. Normalize

This is a "mirror descent" step with the negative entropy regularizer. It has good convergence properties for constrained problems.
