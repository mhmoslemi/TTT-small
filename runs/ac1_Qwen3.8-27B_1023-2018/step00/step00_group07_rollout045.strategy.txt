   - A magnitude-based constraint in the spectral domain
   - A time-domain nonnegativity enforcer 
   - A trust-region gradient project 

   The central idea is to minimize the AC1 bound by shaping the sequence's spectral properties while adhering to the coefficient magnitude constraints. This differs from previous iterations that either only used time-domain gradient-based exploration or complete spectral decomposition without coordinated optimization between domains.

   We first reformulate the problem as a quadratic minimization with linear constraints that has a sparse structure. The solution involves projecting iteratively onto the feasible set while respecting the coefficient bounds and sum requirement. While the global minimum remains elusive, this approach offers improved local explore with less risk of premature convergence.

   - Time-frequency domain balance for global-local optimization tradeoff
   - Spectral envelope control to prioritize high-energy low-correlation sequences
   - Dynamic time-to-completion assessment of gradient descent iterations

   Drawbacks: 
   - Requires explicit handling of just convex constraints (not their relaxations)
   - Does not ensure maintaining positive and bounded coefficients
   - May require fallback if early iterations stall
