So the LP doesn't improve from a uniform start! The update $f_{new} = (1-t)f + tg$ with $g$ having the same sum as $f$ and $\|f*g\|_\infty \leq M$ would give:
$\|f_{new} * f_{new}\|_\infty \leq (1-t)^2 M + 2t(1-t) M + t^2 \|g*g\|_\infty = M - t^2 M + t^2 \|g*g\|_\infty$.

For this to decrease, we need $\|g*g\|_\infty < M = \|f*f\|_\infty$. So the LP helps only if the "best response" $g$ has a smaller self-convolution peak than $f$.

This is the same as the "alternating minimization" or "best response dynamics" for a zero-sum game. The convergence depends on the structure of the problem.
