**Subgradient method for: min 2n * max_k (a*a)_k / (sum a)^2 subject to a >= 0.**

By homogeneity, fix sum(a) = 1. Then minimize 2n * max_k (a*a)_k.

The subgradient of max_k (a*a)_k at point a is: the subdifferential of max is the convex hull of gradients of the active functions. If (a*a)_k is uniquely maximized at k*, then the gradient is ∇(a*a)_{k*} = 2 * P_{k*} a (where P_{k*} is the appropriate permutation/shift).

If multiple k's are tied at the max, the subgradient is the average of their gradients.

Algorithm:
1. Initialize a (normalized to sum 1).
2. Compute (a*a)_k for all k.
3. Find k* = argmax (a*a)_k (or average over ties).
4. Compute gradient: g = 2 * shift(a, k*) (or average over active k's).
5. Update: a = a - η * g, then project to {a >= 0, sum(a) = 1}.
6. Repeat.

The projection onto {a >= 0, sum(a) = 1} is the "Euclidean projection onto the simplex", which can be done in O(n log n) time.

Step size: η = α / t (diminishing) or use backtracking.
