### Mathematical Specification
1. **Representation:**  
   - Input: Initial sequence from `height_sequence_1` (2536 coefficients).  
   - Output: A vector \(\mathbf{a} = (a_0, a_1, \dots, a_{2535})\) with \(0 \leq a_i \leq 1000\) and \(\sum_{i} a_i \geq 0.01\).

2. **Objective Function:**  
   \[
   AC1(\mathbf{a}) = \frac{2 \cdot n \cdot \max_{k} \text{convolve}(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{a})[k]}{\left(\sum_{i} a_i\right)^2}
   \]  
   - Minimize \(AC1(\mathbf{a})\) via exact evaluation with the provided `evaluate_sequence`.

3. **Optimization:**  
   - Gradient-based optimization steps with analytical gradients:  
     \[
     g_h = 2 \cdot \nabla_{a_h} \text{convolve}(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{a}) - \frac{2 \cdot AC1(\mathbf{a}) \cdot \sum_{i} a_i}{\left(\sum_{i} a_i\right)^2} \cdot \nabla_{a_h} \sum_{i} a_i
     \]
   - Constraints handled via projected gradient projection.

4. **Notation:**  
   - \(\text{convolve}(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{a})[k]\): Linear convolution (mode='full') at lag \(k\).  
   - \(n = 2536\).
