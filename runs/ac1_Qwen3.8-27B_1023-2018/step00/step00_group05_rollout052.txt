What kind of sequence has a flat convolution? Let me think about this from the frequency domain.

$a * a$ has DFT equal to $|A(f)|^2$ where $A(f) = \text{DFT}(a)$. For the linear convolution, it's a bit different (involves zero-padding), but the idea is similar.

If $|A(f)|^2$ is constant, then $a * a$ is a delta (in frequency), which means $a * a$ is constant in time. But for real, nonnegative $a$, having constant $|A(f)|$ is very restrictive (it's the "flat spectrum" condition).
