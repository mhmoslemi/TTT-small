Let me think about what the optimal structure looks like for large n.

For a "smooth" sequence, the convolution is approximately the "blur" of the sequence with itself. If a is a smooth function, then conv(a,a) is like a wider, smoother bump. The peak of the convolution is roughly at the center of the "support" of a.

For a symmetric smooth bump (like a Gaussian), the convolution is a wider Gaussian, and the peak is at the center. The peak value is proportional to 1/sqrt(width).

For an asymmetric sequence (like a step or ramp), the convolution peak is off-center, and the peak value can be lower.

I think the optimal sequence for this problem is some kind of "quarter-wave" shape - high at one end, low at the other, with a smooth transition.
