**General approach for large n**:

The key question is: what non-negative sequence of length n minimizes `2*n*max(conv(a,a))/(sum(a))^2`?

This is equivalent to minimizing `max(conv(a,a))/(sum(a))^2` (the factor 2n is constant).

Let S = sum(a). We want to minimize max(conv(a,a))/S^2.

Note that conv(a,a) has 2n-1 entries, and sum of all entries = S^2 (since sum of conv = sum(a)*sum(a)).

So the average of conv entries is S^2/(2n-1). If we could make all entries equal to S^2/(2n-1), the max would be S^2/(2n-1), giving score = 2n*S^2/(2n-1)/S^2 = 2n/(2n-1) ≈ 1.
