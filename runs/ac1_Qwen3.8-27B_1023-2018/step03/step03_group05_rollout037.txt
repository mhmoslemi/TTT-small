- Dual update: `lambda <- project_simplex(lambda + eta_d * (A*a - nu*1))` (or solve the LP exactly)

If I solve the dual LP exactly at each inner iteration, and take a primal step, this is like a **bundle method** or **cutting plane method**.

Actually, this is exactly the **subgradient method** for the convex problem:
min `f(a) = max_k (A*a)[k]` s.t. `a ∈ simplex`.

The subgradient of `f` at `a` is `A^T lambda` where `lambda` is concentrated on the argmax set. The optimal subgradient is the one that minimizes the norm, which is what the dual LP finds.

2. Take a step: `a_new = project_simplex(a - eta * A^T lambda*)`.
3. Repeat.
