# 2. Mathematical Specification

## Representation and Variables

We represent sequences as n-dimensional positive real vectors $f \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with entries $0 \leq f_i \leq 1000$. The AC1 bound is defined as:
$$
AC1(f) = \frac{2n \max_{k} |(f * f)[k]|}{|\sum_{i} f_i|^2}
$$
where $(f * f)[k]$ denotes the linear convolution at position k, and $|\cdot|$ denotes the absolute value.

## Objective and Constraints

The optimization objective is to minimize the unconstrained AC1 value. The only constraint is non-negativity of coefficients, though the going constraint (implicitly $|\sum f_i| \geq 0.01$) is handled by the evaluator.

## LP-based Direction Finding

1. Formulate LP problem for a given sequence $f$:
   - Variables: $g \in \mathbb{R}^n$
   - Maximize: $\sum g_j$
   - Subject to: $|\sum_{i} f_i f_{i+j-k}| \leq M_k$ for each $k$, where $M_k$ are the bounded convolution values (from the current evaluation of $f * f$)
   - Bounds: $0 \leq g_j \leq 1000$ for all $j$

2. The dual from this LP provides a Newton-like step direction $g$ to move the current sequence

## Canonical Gradient Calculation

The gradient for AC1 with respect to a coefficient $f_i$ is:
$$
\frac{\partial AC1}{\partial f_i} = \frac{2}{|\sum f_j|^2} \sum_{k,k'} \frac{\partial |(f * f)[k]|}{\partial f_i} |(f * f)[k]|^{-1} (f * f)_{k'} + \text{terms from the denominator}
$$

However, due to the $max$ operation and $s^2$ denominator, we compute finite-differences or use simulated gradients from directional derivatives.

## Matrix Formulation

The constraint matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{(2n-1) \times n}$ where:
$$
A_{k,j} = 
\begin{cases} 
f[k-j] & \text{if } 0 \leq k-j < n-1 \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
