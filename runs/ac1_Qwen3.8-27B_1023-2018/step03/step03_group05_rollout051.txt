Actually, $(b*b)_k = \sum_i b_i b_{k-i}$ is a quadratic in $b$. The sum $\sum_k e^{\lambda(k)} (b*b)_k$ is a sum of exponentials of quadratic forms, which is convex in $b$ (exponential of a convex function is convex, and the sum is convex). So the soft-max QP is convex!

But the dimension is $n = 512$, and the QP has 1023 constraints (one for each $k$) plus the equality constraint. This is solvable but expensive.

For now, let me stick with the projected gradient method, which is simple and effective for this type of problem.
