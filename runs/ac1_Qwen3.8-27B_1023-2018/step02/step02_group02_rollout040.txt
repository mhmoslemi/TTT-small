We want to minimize $\max_k (a*a)[k]$ subject to $a \geq 0$, $\sum a = 1$.

The subgradient of $\max_k (a*a)[k]$ at point $a$ is the convex hull of the gradients of the active constraints. If only one constraint is active (the max is achieved at a unique $k^*$), the gradient is $\nabla (a*a)[k^*] = 2 P_{k^*} a$ where $(P_k a)_j = a[k-j]$ if $0 \leq k-j < n$.

For the smooth approximation with weights $p$, the gradient is $\sum_k p[k] \nabla (a*a)[k] = 2 \sum_k p[k] P_k a$.

$(\sum_k p[k] P_k a)_j = \sum_k p[k] a[k-j] = \sum_{l} p[l+j] a[l]$ (substituting $l = k-j$)

So $\nabla_j = 2 \sum_{l=0}^{n-1} p[l+j] a[l]$. ✓
