But this counts each pair twice (once as `(i,j)` and once as `(j,i)`) unless `i=j`. So:
`a^T Q_k a = 2 * sum_{i < j, i+j=k} a_i a_j + sum_{i: 2i=k} a_i^2`

While `q(a) = sum_i a_i a_{k-i} = 2 * sum_{i < j, i+j=k} a_i a_j + sum_{i: 2i=k} a_i^2`
