**Alternative: Semidefinite programming (SDP) relaxation**

The problem $\min \max_k (a*a)_k / (\sum a)^2$ can be related to SDP. But SDP solvers are typically slower than LP solvers for problems of this size.

**Alternative: Frank-Wolfe / Mirror Descent**

Since the feasible set is the simplex (after normalization), we could use Frank-Wolfe:
- At each step, find the vertex (standard basis vector) that minimizes the linearization of the objective
- Take a step toward that vertex

But the objective is not convex, so this might not work well.

**Alternative: Direct approach - minimize max_k (a*a)_k subject to sum(a)=1, a>=0**

This is a non-convex QP. We can solve it with:
- Successive convex approximation (SCA)
- Penalty methods
- Interior point methods
