$\nabla_j \text{score} \approx \sum_k \lambda_k^* \cdot \partial_k (a*a)_j$

where $\partial_k (a*a)_j = 2 a_{j-k}$ (for valid indices).

This gives: $\nabla_j = 2 \sum_k \lambda_k^* a_{j-k} = 2 (\lambda^* * a)_j$ (over valid range).

So the gradient direction is proportional to $\lambda^* * a$!

This is a clean, interpretable gradient: the "dual weights" $\lambda^*$ tell us which lags are "binding," and we move in the direction that reduces the binding lags.
