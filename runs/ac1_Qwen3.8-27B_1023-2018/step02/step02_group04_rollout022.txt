The matrix M_k is NOT positive semidefinite in general. For example, for k=1 (n>=2): M_1 = [[0,1],[1,0],[0,0],...] which has eigenvalues 1, -1, 0, ... So it's indefinite.

Therefore, Q_k(a) = a^T M_k a is NOT convex in general, and the problem is non-convex.

This matches the fact that the LP power iteration (which is like a multiplicative weights / mirror descent method) is the natural approach.
