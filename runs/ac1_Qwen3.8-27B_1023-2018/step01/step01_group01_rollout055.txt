The smooth max is `M_s = Cmax + (1/T) * log(Σ_k exp(T*(C_k - Cmax)))`.

The weights are `w_k = exp(T*(C_k - Cmax)) / Σ_j exp(T*(C_j - Cmax))`.

Wait, is that true? `Σ w_k * C_k = Cmax + (1/Σ exp(T*(C_k-Cmax))) * Σ exp(T*(C_k-Cmax)) * (C_k - Cmax)`.

Let `u_k = T*(C_k - Cmax)`. Then `w_k = exp(u_k) / Σ exp(u_j)`.
