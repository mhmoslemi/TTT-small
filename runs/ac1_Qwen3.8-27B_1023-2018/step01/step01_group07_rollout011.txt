**Phase 2: LP-based refinement**

The LP from the literature:
- Given f = a (normalized), M = max(f*f)
- Solve: max sum(g) s.t. (f*g)[k] <= M for all k, g >= 0
- Normalize g: g = g / sum(g)
- Update: a = (1-t)*a + t*g

The key insight: the LP finds the "largest" g (in sum) that, when convolved with f, stays below M. This means g has more "mass" spread out, reducing the peak of f*f when we move towards g.

For the LP, I'll use a subset of constraints (the active ones) to keep it tractable.
