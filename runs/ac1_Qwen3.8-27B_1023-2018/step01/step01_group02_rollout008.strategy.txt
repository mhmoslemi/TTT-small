#### 1. Approach and rationale
The core idea is to minimize the AC1 upper bound by iteratively refining a sequence through constrained optimization. Unlike the parent method that relied on solving linear programs, we use gradient-based optimization with projected gradients. This approach allows us to leverage the frequency domain properties of autocorrelation while maintaining computational feasibility. The gradient computation involves convolving the sequence and identifying the maximum values in the convolution, from which we compute gradients. By combining Adam optimization with box constraints (non-negativity and bounded values), and incorporating phase rotations and random perturbations, we ensure exploration and fast convergence. This method directly targets the minimization objective rather than constraint-based updates.

The key advantages are:
- **Gradient mastery**: Uses exact analytical gradients for each iteration.
- **Phase shaping**: Rotates the sequence in frequency domain to escape local minima.
- **Dimension coverage**: Works for sequences of varying lengths while maintaining normalized sums.
- **Dynamic acceleration**: Adjusts optimization parameters based on performance and runtime.
- **Component decoupling**: Decomposes the sequence through eigenvalues to allow for efficient gradient projection.

The rationale is that by combining smooth gradient steps with periodic disruptive perturbations (phase rotation, value mutagenesis), the algorithm can navigate the high-dimensional space more effectively than the parent method's linear programming updates, which remain trapped in suboptimal solutions.

#### 2. Mathematical specification
**Variables**:  
- $a \in \mathbb{R}^n$: Sequence coefficients of length $n$.
- $s = \sum_{k=1}^n a_k$: Normalized sum (handled in gradient projections).
- $M = \max_{i}\left(\sum_{k=1}^n a_k a_{k+i}\right)$ for $i<n$: Maximum autocorrelation value.

**Objective to minimize**:  
$f(a) = \frac{2nM}{s^2}$

**Constraints**:  
- $a_k \geq 0 \quad \forall k$: Non-negativity.
- $a_k \leq 1000 \quad \forall k$: Bounded coefficients.
- $s \geq 0.01$: Minimal signal strength.

**Gradient component**:  
For element-wise gradient:
$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial a_j} = \frac{2n}{s^2} \sum_{i \in I} a_{i-j} - \frac{4nM}{s^3}
$$
where $I$ is the index set of autocorrelation lags achieving the maximum value $M$.

**Projection onto feasible set**:
$$
\textrm{proj}_{\mathbb{R}_{\geq0}}(a) = \max(a, 0) \\
\textrm{proj}_{[0,1000]}(a) = \min(\max(a,0),1000)
$$

**Rotation matrices**:
For phase shifts by angle $\phi$:
$$
R(\phi) = \begin{pmatrix}
\cos \phi & -\sin \phi \\
\sin \phi & \cos \phi
\end{pmatrix}
$$
applied to coefficients.
