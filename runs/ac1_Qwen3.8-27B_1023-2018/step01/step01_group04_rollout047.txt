This follows because (sum a)² = sum_j a_j * sum_k a_k = sum_{j,k} a_j * a_k. And each term a_j * a_k appears in exactly one b_{j+k} (when both indices are valid). The total number of valid (j,k) pairs is n². By pigeonhole, some b_k must be ≥ n²/n * (average over all b_k's)...
