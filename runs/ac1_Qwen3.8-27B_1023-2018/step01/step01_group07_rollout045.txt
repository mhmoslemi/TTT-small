- Each term a[i]*a[k-i] ≤ (a[i]+a[k-i])²/4
- The peak is at most n (when all a are 1, uniform)
- We want the peak to be as low as possible

For a "random" sequence with mean 1 and variance σ², the expected b[k] at the center is n*(1+σ²) and the variance is about n*σ⁴ (for large n). The max over 2n-1 values would be roughly n*(1+σ²) + σ²*sqrt(2n*log(2n)).
