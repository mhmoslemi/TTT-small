For the self-convolution (a*a)[k] = sum_i a[i]a[k-i], the "energy" at each lag k is a sum of products. To minimize the maximum, we want these sums to be as equal as possible across all k.

For a uniform sequence, all "interior" lags have the same value (n terms each contributing equally), but the edges have fewer terms. The peak is at the center.

For a geometric sequence, the "mass" is concentrated at one end, so the convolution is skewed. The peak is either at the center or near one end.

The truly optimal sequence might be something like a "raised cosine" or a specific polynomial, but without more analysis, let's just use the iterative methods.
