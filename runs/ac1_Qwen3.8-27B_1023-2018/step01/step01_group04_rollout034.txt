The convolution has 2n-1 entries. So the average value is 1/(2n-1). The maximum is at least the average: max_k (a*a)[k] >= 1/(2n-1).

This gives Score >= 2n/(2n-1), which approaches 1 for large n! But this is a very weak bound.

A better bound: by Cauchy-Schwarz, (a*a)[k] = sum_j a[j]*a[k-j] <= (sum_j a[j]^2)^{1/2} * (sum_j a[k-j]^2)^{1/2} = ||a||_2^2.
