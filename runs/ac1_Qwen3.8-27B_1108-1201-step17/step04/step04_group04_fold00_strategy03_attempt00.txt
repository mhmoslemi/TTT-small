    conv = np.fft.irfft(A * A, fft_size)[:size]

def compute_soft_gradient(a, conv, top_k=20, temp=0.005):

1. **Gradient Calculation**:

2. **Sequence Optimization**:

3. **Anti-concentration**:
   - Resonant frequency pattern matching (`sin` function) provides separation-of-concerns by focusing on particular stabilities.
