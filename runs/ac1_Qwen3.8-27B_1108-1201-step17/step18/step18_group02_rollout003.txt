Actually, I think the key insight is: we want the autocorrelation to be as FLAT as possible. The uniform sequence gives a triangular autocorrelation (bad). We need to "flatten" it.

The way to flatten the autocorrelation is to make the sequence more "random-like" in its structure. A truly random sequence with independent elements would have autocorrelation close to flat (by the law of large numbers, each b[k] for k away from 0 and 2n-2 would be approximately S^2/(2n-1) with small fluctuations).

1. Be as "flat" as possible (all elements similar) to keep edge values low
2. Have structure that makes the CENTER autocorrelation values flat (not peaked)
