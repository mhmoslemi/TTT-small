**Key insight: The problem is equivalent to minimizing the "peak" of the autocorrelation relative to the "DC" component.**

In signal processing terms:
- sum(a) is the DC component (up to a factor)
- max(conv(a,a)) is related to the peak of the autocorrelation
- The ratio is like a "peak-to-average power ratio" (PAPR) of the autocorrelation

Low PAPR is desirable (it's a well-studied problem in communications!). The standard approach for PAPR reduction is tone reservation, iterative clipping, and adaptive search.

**Tone Reservation analogy:**
- In PAPR reduction, you reserve some "tones" (frequency bins) that you can modify freely to reduce peaks in the time domain.
- In our problem, we can think of it as: we want to modify a to reduce the peak of conv(a,a). The "reserved" degrees of freedom are the coordinates of a.
