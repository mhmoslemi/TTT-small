The best we can do is approach the "random" level of autocorrelation sidelobes. For a random non-negative sequence, the autocorrelation sidelobes are O(1/sqrt(n)) in magnitude while the peak is O(1/n) (wait, no, for sum=1, the values are O(1/n), so autocorrelation sidelobes are O(1/n^2 * n) = O(1/n)).
