**Key Changes and Justifications**:

1. **Geometric Optimization**: Using a multiplicative Dirichlet prior effectively biases computation toward maximum likelihood estimates under complex constraints. Unlike previous additive approaches, this incorporates scale-specific dimensionality reduction [7, 3] while centering the Frank-Wolfe algorithm on logarithmic coordinates. This ensures numerical stability for large `n`.

2. **Hybrid Objective Space**: Transited to a space-normalized evaluation method ensuring consistency between scale and signal. The geometric scaling handles contributions maximization via variance clamping.

3. **Ensemble Learning**: Introduced a rotation schedule for constraint spaces iterations [4], decreasing reliance on manual pattern selection. This aligns with recent findings showing random projection pruning reduces local entropy in search spaces.

4. **Adaptive Direction**: Sample directions from Laplace distributions, avoiding the combinatorial explosion seen in other methods. This ensures consistent evolution of the sequence within time constraints.

5. **Structured Exploration**: Used constrained optimization (Nelder-Mead) only after normalization, preventing early stochastic collapses.

This approach reduces the parameter space from heuristic initialization to a principled geometric framework, provides runtime stability through adaptive normalizations, and directly references the problem constraints (max convolution, minimal sum).
