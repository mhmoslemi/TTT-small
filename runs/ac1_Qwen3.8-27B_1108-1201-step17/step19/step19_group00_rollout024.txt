def compute_soft_gradient(a, beta):
    """Compute gradient of soft-max objective."""
    n = len(a)
    size = 2 * n - 1
    fft_size = 1 << (size - 1).bit_length()
    
    # Compute convolution
    A = np.fft.rfft(a, n=fft_size)
    conv = np.fft.irfft(A * A, fft_size)[:size]
    
    # Compute soft-max weights
    # Shift to avoid overflow
    conv_shifted = conv - conv.max()
    w = np.exp(beta * conv_shifted)
    w_sum = w.sum()
    w = w / w_sum  # Now w is a probability distribution over lags

The step size is crucial. For mirror descent on the simplex, a common choice is eta = 1/sqrt(t) or eta = 1/t. But for our problem, we need to be more careful.
