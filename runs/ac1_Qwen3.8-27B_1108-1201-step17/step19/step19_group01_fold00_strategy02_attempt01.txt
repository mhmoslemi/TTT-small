## Mathematical Specification

Our formulation:

For a sequence `a` of length `n`, let `A(f)` be its Fourier transform with frequency `f` ranging from 0 to `n-1`.

The autocorrelation is then given by:
`b(tau) = (1/n) * sum_{f=0}^{n-1} A(f) * A(-tau) * exp(2πi f tau/n)`

To restrict the autocorrelation, we:
1. Force spectral cancellations by requiring "consistent phases" at opposing frequencies
2. Impose maximum energy constraints at frequencies closest to problematic autocorrelation peaks
3. Use normalized exponential smoothing to ensure strict convexity

Through algebraic manipulation, the optimization can be reformulated as:

`minimize L1 = (2n * max_{tau} |A(tau)|^2) / (sum |a|)^2`
subject to
`sum_{t=0}^{n-1} a(t) = 1` and `a(t) >= 0 for all t`

The Fourier transfer of the sequence satisfies:
`sum_{f} A(f) * A(-f) <= M_{limit}`

We can transform these frequency constraints into LP conditions via:

`A(f) * A(-f) <= M_{limit}`
