**Approach: Direct optimization of the max via the "soft" formulation**

Instead of directly minimizing max_k C[k], we can minimize a soft version:
F_β(a) = (1/β) * log(sum_k exp(β * C[k]))

As β → ∞, this approaches max_k C[k]. For finite β, it's smooth and we can use gradient methods.

The gradient of F_β with respect to a[i] is:
∂F/∂a[i] = (1/β) * sum_k exp(β*C[k]) * (∂C[k]/∂a[i]) / sum_k exp(β*C[k])

where ∂C[k]/∂a[i] = a[k-i] (if k-i is valid, i.e., 0 ≤ k-i ≤ n-1).

This is a "soft" subgradient that weights the lags by their exponential of their values. Lags with high C[k] get more weight.
