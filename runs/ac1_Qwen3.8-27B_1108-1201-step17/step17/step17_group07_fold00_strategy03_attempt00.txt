Mathematical specification
Define a candidate sequence a of length n with element-wise gradient information g(k) = 2 * a[k] for specific k's. This gradient stems from the derivative of the max convolution value with respect to the sequence coefficients. The target is to minimize J = 2*n*max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a))^2.

The approach:
1. For each position k, we consider the contribution a[k]*a[k+j] to the convolution values at lags j.
2. Build a candidate sequence a and its gradient g simultaneously.
3. Use gradient-based exploration to identify promising directions.

Key operations:
- Maintain candidate sequences with normalized sums (≥0.01)
- Use top-k gradient subgradients to focus updates
- Combine with Remez method constraints by prioritizing convolution control points

3. **Search**:
