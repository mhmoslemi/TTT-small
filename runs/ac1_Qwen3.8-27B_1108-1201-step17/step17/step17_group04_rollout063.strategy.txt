Bayesian optimization will select candidates based on an expected improvement metric derived from a Gaussian process model, while local search (via the previous program's methods) will be applied to the best candidate to refine it.

   Why Bayesian optimization:
   - It handles expensive black-box evaluations well.
   - It formally accounts for both exploration and exploitation.
   - It builds a model of the previous program's (parent's) structure and finds optimal coefficients using this knowledge.

   Why Bayesian plus local:
   - Global Bayesian will identify better structures.
   - Local refinement will polish these structures to get very low J.

      J(a) = 2 * n * max(convolve(a, a, mode="full")) / (sum(a))²
