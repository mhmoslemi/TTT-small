I propose a novel approach called "Adaptive Subfunction Isolation and Progressive Optimization" (ASIPO) to minimize the autocorrelation peak. The core idea is to:

- Treat the sequence as a composition of fundamental wavelets (with known autocorrelation properties)
- Quantify how energy concentrates in high-peak regions
- Dynamically decompose the optimization problem into subfunctions whose maximal convolution is monotonic decreasing with respect to the original metric

This approach directly leverages Fourier duality and energy preservation properties, which were implicit improvements in previous programs. ASIPO can be viewed as a continuous version of the coordinate descent strategy embedded in coord_polish(), but with adaptive transformations.

**2. Mathematical Specification**
