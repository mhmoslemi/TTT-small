Approach and rationale:

We use a comb-style optimization with adjusted peak frequencies to minimize the self-convolution-to-sum² ratio. The key insight is that repeatedly oscillating between adjusting peak positions and optimizing amplitudes can break the problem into more manageable subcomponents. This method limits the time spent by optimizing each peak independently, then resolving conflicts.

Compared to previous strategies:
- Instead of tracking bespoke `active_peak_sets`, we use a fixed comb with variable peaks per subset
- The filtering and training process is more systematic, iterating by gradually reducing peak counts
- We integrate frequency optimization within each comb construction phase
- The fallback candidate regeneration is done by simply scaling the initial sine-generated reference, avoiding complex normalization issues
