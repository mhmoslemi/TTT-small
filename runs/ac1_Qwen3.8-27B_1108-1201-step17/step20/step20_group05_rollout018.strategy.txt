1. Identifying significant "modes" for coefficient peaks
2. Optimizing the positions and widths of these modes while ensuring even sampling (overlap requirement)

- Scan input sequence for potentially useful peaks

Phase 2: Mode Optimization
- Using identified peaks, optimize their positions and widths
- Ensure they do not conflict and each has minimal overlap with others
- Return the best sequence found within mission time
