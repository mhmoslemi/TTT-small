Now, for the subgradient method on the outer problem (minimizing `max_k (a*a)[k]` over `a` in the simplex):

1. Compute `b = a * a` (autocorrelation via FFT)
2. Find active set: `k` where `b[k]` is near `max(b)`
3. Compute `p` (probability distribution over active set, e.g., softmax or uniform)
4. Compute subgradient: `sg = compute_subgradient_fast(a, p)`
5. Update: `a_new = project_to_simplex(a - eta * sg)`
6. Accept if `max(a_new * a_new) < max(a * a)`

This should be fast! Each iteration is `O(n log n)`.
