📐 **Approach and Rationale**  
The goal is to minimize the autocorrelation score `2*n*max(convolve(a, a))/sum(a)^2` for a sequence `a` of nonnegative floats. We need to find a sequence that balances length (to minimize the attack surface) and structure (to reduce autocorrelation peaks). A multi-length grid search combined with gradient-based refinement will escape local optima.  

We'll:  
1. Evaluate sequences across lengths from 100 to 5000 (step 500) to find the length with the lowest score.  
2. Then, for that length, use exponential averaging to refine the sequence.  
3. The grid search ensures we find potent solutions, while gradient-based updates optimize within a length.  

**Mathematical Specification**  
We search for sequences `a` of length `n` (in `[100, 600, 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500, 3000, 3500,  4000, 4500, 5000]`):  
- `sum(a) = sqrt(2*n)`  
- `a_i ∈ [0, 1000]`  
- Minimize `max(convolve(a, a, mode="full"))`
