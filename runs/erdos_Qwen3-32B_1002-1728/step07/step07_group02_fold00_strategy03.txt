The key insight is that the overlap integral can be expressed as a cross-correlation, which has a well-known Fourier transform relationship.

By casting the problem in terms of Fourier coefficients, we can:
1. Exploit convex duality to find a bounded solution
2. Leverage advanced spectral methods to handle the correlation structure
3. Use frequency domain constraints to simplify the optimization

The primary limitation is that the Fourier representation introduces additional variables and constraints, but the tradeoff is worth it for the improved convexity properties.

#### Objective

$$ C_5 = \max_k \left| \mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[ \mathcal{F}[h] \cdot (1 - \mathcal{F}[h]) \right]_k \right| $$

Where $\mathcal{F}$ denotes the Fourier transform.

#### Key relationships
Using the convolution theorem:
$$ \text{Corr}(h, 1-h) = \mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[ \mathcal{F}[h] \cdot \overline{\mathcal{F}[1-h]} \right] $$
Since $1 - h$ is simply an invertible linear transformation of $h$, we can express the problem in terms of magnitude squared constraints.
