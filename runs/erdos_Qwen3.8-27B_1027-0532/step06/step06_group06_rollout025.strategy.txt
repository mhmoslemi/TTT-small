1. Approach and Rationale
The proposed approach combines a hierarchical optimization strategy with randomized rounding and function interpolation. Unlike previous methods, it first optimizes the function representation in low-dimensional grids to reduce computational complexity, then employs a coarse-to-fine interpolation strategy to maintain solution quality. Key improvements include:
   - Using grid hierarchy from small resolutions (2, 4...) to full size, preserving optimal features
   - Adaptive projection to desired grid size
   - Randomized rounding during grid transition
   - Progressive temperature annealing in continuous-to-binary transitions
