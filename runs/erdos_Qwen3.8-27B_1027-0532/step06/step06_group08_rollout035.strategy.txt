Rather than optimizing over step functions directly, we formulate the problem as a minimax optimization in continuous space with periodic boundary conditions. This allows us to use Fourier analysis and moments to efficiently explore the worst-case shift behavior. The rationale is that the underlying continuous structure of periodic step functions can be captured via their Fourier coefficients, enabling a more structured search than typical local heuristics. The method systematically handles the multi-objective trade-off between minimizing overlaps at various shift distances by incorporating these periodic integral constraints through dual variables.
