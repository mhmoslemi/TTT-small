- No repeated opponents
- Minute alignment $\mathcal{M}_i(t) \in \{0,1\}$

- Discriminative minute availability: $\sum_i S_{t,i} \leq c_{\text{max}}$
- Non-stop play: all minutes must be filled
- Timely completion: $\sum_t S_{t,?} = \text{rounds} \times (\text{teams}-1)$

The mixed-integer program minimizes the maximum overlap in match outcomes while maintaining balanced scheduling.
