### Approach and Rationale  
We address the Erdős minimum overlap problem by iteratively minimizing the maximum overlap \(C_5\) for a step function \(h\) constrained to \([0, 1]\) with unit integral. The key challenge is escaping local optima, which the algorithm achieves through multi-start local search and primal-dual projection. The core idea is to perform gradient-based updates directed by overlapping intensity, combine this with a rigorous projection step into the feasible set, and ensure numerical stability.  

### Mathematical Specification  
**Variables**:  
- \(n\) discrete points (initially \(n=600\) from `initial_h_values`)  
- \(\mathbb{R}^n\) with box constraints \(h_i \in [0, 1]\)  
- Linear balancing constraint \(\sum_i h_i = n/2\)  

**Objective**: Minimize                
\[\max_{k \in \{-n+1, n-1\}} \left(2/n\right) \cdot \sum_{i} h_i (1-h_{i+k})\]  

**Projection**: Uses explicit zeroing of all nearby violating entries for enforcement.  
**Gradient**: Skyline-difference style directional derivative estimates.
