&\quad \sum_{\mathrm{aligned}\,i,j} h_i(1-h_j) \leq t \cdot \text{scale}
\end{align*}
\]

The SDP relaxation augments variables via 
\[
z_{ij} = [\begin{array}{c|c} h_i & \sqrt{h_i(1-h_j)} \\ 1-h_i & \sqrt{(1-h_i)(1-h_j)} \end{array}]
\]
for each relevant $(i,j)$  
and uses the fact that this structure preserves key integrality.

The objective value in the SDP is minimized by an interior-point method guaranteeing solution to a specific precision.
