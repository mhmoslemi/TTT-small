The Erdős minimum overlap problem asks us to find a step function h: [0,2] → [0,1] that minimizes:
C₅ = max_k ∫ h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx

with constraints h(x) ∈ [0,1] and ∫₀² h(x) dx = 1.

The previous program achieved C₅ = 0.380872 and we need to beat 0.38085 (ideally get below 0.38085).

Key observations:
1. The problem is essentially: minimize the maximum cross-correlation between h and 1-h.
2. The previous approach used L-BFGS-B with log-sum-exp (LSE) approximation, pair coordinate descent, and subgradient methods.
