#### 1. Approach and Rationale
- **Population-based:** Maintain a population of functions h(x) represented on a grid.
- **Multi-scale:** Start with coarse grids (e.g., 40 points) and then refine (e.g., use interpolation to 80, then 160, etc.). This helps escape local optima and finds better solutions.
- **Adaptive restarts:** When the population becomes stuck, we restart from a different random projection (but keeping the integral constraint).
- **Objective focus:** We track the best solution (lowest C5) and also maintain diversity in the population to avoid premature convergence.

#### 2. Mathematical Specification
We will represent each candidate solution by a vector h of length n, with:
  - 0 <= h[i] <= 1
  - sum(h) = n/2 (i.e., integral constraint)

The fitness function is:
  score(h) = max_k ( np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full') * dx )
where dx = 2.0/n

We consider a population P of m candidates. Each candidate is updated via a local search operator. The global best is kept.

The multi-scale aspect is implemented by periodically increasing the grid size (number of points) of the best candidate and of the population, and interpolating (or up-sampling) other candidates.
