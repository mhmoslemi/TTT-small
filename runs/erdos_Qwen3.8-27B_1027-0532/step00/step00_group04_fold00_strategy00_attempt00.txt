### Strategy for Erdős Minimum Overlap Problem

#### 1. Approach and Rationale
The Erdős minimum overlap problem seeks to find a step function \( h: [0,2] \to [0,1] \) with integral 1 that minimizes the maximum overlap integral \( C_5 = \max_k \int h(x) (1 - h(x+k))  dx \). The challenge is non-convex, involving both integer-like constraints (sum of h must equal n_points/2) and continuous constraints (each h[i] in [0,1]). 

Current best solution achieves a bound of \( C \approx 0.3808 \). We will use stochastic optimization with repair mechanisms because:
- The objective is a max over many lags, making gradient-based methods ineffective.
- The large grid size (n_points=70) limits practical exact methods.

**Key Components:**
- **Repair Functions:** These adjust solutions to satisfy sum constraints while minimizing step changes for better local search.
- **Initialization:** Uses provided parent solution (for n_points=70) or uniform initialization adapted to grid size.
- **Mutation:** Random step changes to explore the state space, with repair ensuring feasibility.

#### 2. Mathematical Specification
**Variables:**
- \( h \in [0,1]^{n_{\text{points}}} \): Represents function samples on [0,2] uniformly spaced.
- \( dx = 2 / n_{\text{points}} \): Grid spacing.

**Objective:**
- Minimize \( C_5 = \max_k \text{overlap}(k) = \max_k \left( dx \cdot \text{np.correlate}(h, 1-h, \text{mode}=\text{"full"})_{\text{max}} \right) \).
- Here, \( k \) ranges over lag indices where the shift is feasible (\( |k| \leq n_{\text{points}} - 1 \)).

**Constraints:**
- \( \sum h = n_{\text{points}} / 2 \): Integral of \( h \) is 1.
- \( 0 \leq h[i] \leq 1 \): Each grid sample is a step.

**Representation:**
- FeasiblePoint: \( h \in [0,1]^{n_{\text{points}}} \) with \( \sum h = n_{\text{points}} / 2 \).

**Repair:**
- Adjustments keep \( h[i] \) from boundaries unless necessary, minimizing changes.

2. **Sum Repair:** Minimize \( \sum |h[i] - h_0[i]| \) subject to \( \sum h = n_{\text{points}}/2 \), \( 0 \leq h[i] \leq 1 \).
