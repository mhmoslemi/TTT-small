The projection of y onto {h: 0≤h[i]≤1, sum(h)=t} is:
h[i] = clip(y[i] - λ, 0, 1) where λ is chosen so that sum(h) = t.

Since sum(clip(y[i]-λ, 0, 1)) is a continuous, non-increasing function of λ, we can find λ by binary search.
