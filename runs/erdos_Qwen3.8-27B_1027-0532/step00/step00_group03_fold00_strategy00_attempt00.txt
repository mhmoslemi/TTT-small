## Approach and Rationale

**Central Idea**: We use a deterministic two-level strategy to iteratively compress the search space for minimax functionals. The approach transforms a high-dimensional quadratic minimax optimization into a sequence of lower-dimensional quadratic programs, each preserving a portion of the previous solution and incorporating safety margins to escape local minima.

**Key Motivation**: This method combines: 1) A reduction strategy that transforms solutions across scales by compressing near-units, 2) A curvature minimization phase that smooths small-scale oscillations, and 3) Automatic parking of low-energy zones that effectively zeros out regions where the function contributes minimal overlap.

**Bottleneck Addressed**: 
1. Current constructions suffer from unavoidable local features (phase plateaus) that drive the minimum overlap upwards
2. The discretized space's high dimension (n=45) makes well-sampled optimization intractable
3. Each alternative approach must be systematically integrated to escape plateaus

**Structure**:
- 1st level: Reduce/Transform problem using piecewise compression
- 2nd level: Minimize curvature penalization in active zones
- 3rd level: Park inactive zones to clear space
- 4th level: Scale solution to higher resolutions

**Limitations/Heuristics**:
- Requires candidate projection (never 100% optimal)
- Works systematically away from 0 until parked
- Park-to-zero ensures only truly active points remain
- Transformation maintains extremal points

    # Compute all autocorrelations and scale by dx
    corr = np.correlate(h_vec, 1-h_vec, mode="full")
    over_c = (corr * dx).max()
    return over_c
