1. **Approach and Rationale**

The central idea is to minimize the Earth Mover's Distance (EMD) between the histogram of $h(x)$ and a target distribution that maximizes the essential minimum overlap. This is inspired by the concept that the minimum overlap occurs when the two functions are as "spread out" as possible, which correlates with maximizing the dispersion of the binary representation of $h(x)$. We address the non-convex nature by leveraging linear programming (LP) duality and gradient-based optimization augmented with population-based perturbations.

The method: represent $h(x)$ as a binary sequence (for step-function realization) and formulate the problem as minimizing the maximum EMD between $h$ and a complementary shift-invariant distribution. The optimization uses projected subgradient methods with random restarts from several starting points (binary sequences derived from the parent) to escape local optima.

The key innovation is the dual formulation and spatial separation of the shift-dependent terms, achieved through EMD. The bottleneck is the binary restriction; however, the convex relaxation in the LP helps mitigate this by allowing continuous variables initially and projecting back to binary.

2. **Mathematical Specification**

We represent $h(x)$ as a binary sequence $h_i \in \{0,1\}$ with $\sum h_i = n_{\text{points}}/2$ (approximately for odd $n_{\text{points}}$). The overlap at lag $k$ is given by:

$$
O_k = \sum_{i} h_i (1-h_{i+k})
$$

The goal is to minimize $\max_k O_k$. This is equivalent to minimizing the maximum EMD between the histogram of $h$ and a periodically shifted version of itself:

$$
\text{EMD}_k(h) = \frac{1}{n_{\text{points}}} \|h - \sigma_k(h)\|_{TV}
$$

where $\sigma_k$ shifts $h$ by $k$ positions (clamping outside [0, $n_{\text{points}}$)].

The optimization formulation:

Minimize $\max_k \text{EMD}_k(h)$  
Subject to:
- $0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \forall i$
- $\sum_{i=1}^{n_{\text{points}}} h_i = S$ (target sum, $\approx n_{\text{points}}/2$)

The LP relaxation: allow $0 \leq h_i \leq 1$ and round the solution to $\{0,1\}$ with minimum perturbation. The primal-dual gap is controlled by solving a penalized LP.
