1. Approach and Rationale:
   The Erdős minimum overlap problem seeks to minimize the maximum overlap integral of a step function with integral 1 over [0,2]. The nonlinear and non-convex optimization problem is challenging to solve with standard methods due to its high dimensionality and discrete nature. Our approach leverages both global and local search strategies while using a repair mechanism to enforce constraints. We start with a given parent solution and iteratively try to improve it by:
   - Increasing the grid resolution to capture potentially better solutions
   - Using a multistart local search based on the DIRECT algorithm for global exploration
   - Repairing solutions to maintain feasibility
   The key innovations are:
   - Adaptive grid resolution to find solutions at different scales
   - Repair function to maintain [0,1] bounds and sum constraint
   - Dynamic phase switching between exploration and exploitation based on time remaining

   This addresses the need for:
   - Efficient global exploration in high-dimensional space
   - Handling nonlinear constraints effectively
   - Adapting to different grid resolutions to escape local optima
   Limitations include the complexity of the repair function and the potential for correlation artifacts in function evaluation.
