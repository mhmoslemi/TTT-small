**Key new idea: Use a "temperature" analogy**

Think of C5 as an energy. We want to minimize it. The log-sum-exp with alpha is like a "soft max" that approximates the max. As alpha increases, the approximation gets sharper.

- Slowly increase alpha (sharpening the landscape)
