Approach and Rationale
The core idea is to reframe the optimization problem in the frequency domain, where the essential characteristics for minimizing overlap can be captured using sparse Fourier coefficients. This approach leverages the shift-invariance property of the problem, which implies that the optimal solution will be periodic or have periodic components. By focusing on a sparse set of Fourier coefficients, we can reduce the effective dimension of the optimization problem while still capturing the key features needed for a lower Erds constant.

The main rationale is that:

3. This sparse representation reduces computational complexity.
4. The optimization is more numerically stable and less prone to local optima due to the smoothness of Fourier features.
