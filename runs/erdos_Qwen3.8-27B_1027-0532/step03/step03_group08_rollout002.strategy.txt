1. **Approach and Rationale**:  
   The Erdős minimum overlap problem seeks to minimize the maximum overlap integral \( C_5 \) of a function \( h \) integrated over all shifts. The previous approach used multi-stage hybrid optimization, but it may not be sufficiently adaptive. Here, we employ a population-based evolutionary strategy (Particle Swarm Optimization, PSO) with adaptive mutation to refine candidate solutions distributed in a **Fourier series representation**.  

   Fourier basis ensures smooth, periodic solutions which inherently lower overlaps. The PSO framework allows global exploration followed by local exploitation. The objective minimizes the maximum correlation term.

   The key bottleneck is escaping local optima in sequential optimization steps, which Fourier PSO circumvents by maintaining population diversity and adaptive mutation.
