So C₅ = 0.5 for constant h. That's the trivial bound.

To get below 0.5, we need to make h non-constant such that the "overlap" (the integral of h(x)(1-h(x+k))) is reduced for all k.

This means we want h(x) and h(x+k) to be "unrelated" or "anti-correlated" for all k.

This is related to the concept of "uncorrelated" sequences or "low autocorrelation" sequences in signal processing!

In fact, the problem is equivalent to finding a signal h with unit integral (area = 1) and values in [0,1] that has minimal maximum cross-correlation with (1-h) at any lag.

This is related to radar waveform design! The goal is to minimize the peak sidelobe level of the autocorrelation.

For binary sequences (h ∈ {0,1}), this is the well-known problem of minimizing the aperiodic autocorrelation. The m-sequences and Zadoff-Chu sequences have good properties.

For our problem with h ∈ [0,1] (continuous), we have more freedom.
