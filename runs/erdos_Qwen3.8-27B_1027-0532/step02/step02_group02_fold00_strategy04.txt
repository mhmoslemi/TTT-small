To push further, we introduce a **multi-resolution, frequency-domain optimization** leveraging the observation that optimal solutions often exhibit periodic structure.  
   The key innovation is to:  
   - Precompute a set of **periodic basis functions** covering common low-overlap patterns (e.g., sine waves, square pulses).  
   - Use a **greedy frequency selection** mechanism: iteratively select the best combination of frequencies that minimizes the overlap integral.  
   - Optimize over **low-dimensional embeddings** of the function space, ensuring feasibility via projection to the integral constraint.  
   **Limitations addressed**:  
   - Escape local optima by exploring frequency combinations (not just random or fixed patterns).  
   - Reduce computational cost by optimizing in a lower-dimensional space (number of frequencies vs. full grid points).  
   - Avoid premature convergence by balancing exploration (frequency innovation) with exploitation (optimized weights).

   **Objective**:  
   Minimize \(C = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1-h(x+k))  dx\), discretized as:  
   \[
   C \approx \frac{\text{np.correlate}(h, 1-h, \text{mode}=\text{'full'})_{\text{max}} \times (2.0 / n)
   \]
