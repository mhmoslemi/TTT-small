1. Approach and rationale:
We introduce a multi-resolution optimization framework that combines dimensionality transfer and spectral projection. The core idea is to optimize the function representation across different sample points (n) while preserving the essential integral property. Unlike traditional gradient-based methods, this approach leverages dimension expansion (from n to 2n) to capture more function variation, combined with coordinate-wise optimization and smooth approximation. The method escapes local optima by:

- Applying adaptive dimension transfer (n, 2n, 4n) to leverage the tensor structure of Walsh functions
- Maintaining integral constraints with a bounded-projection method rather than clipping-based repair
- Incorporating frequency-domain regularization to suppress high-frequency artifacts
- Using line search with smooth approximation for efficient step sizing

The limitation is the computational complexity grows with dimension, but early termination ensures we focus on promising scales first.
