- Use **gradient-based refinement** (SLSQP) with adaptive barrier penalties after DE finds a candidate on a coarse grid.  
- Automatically **refine grid resolutions** (400 → 600 → 800) by interpolation, maintaining feasibility through projection.  
- Avoid running DE on high-resolution grids by using **gradient descent** for finer grids, reducing overall time.  
- Tighten the alpha schedule for rapid convergence while enforcing integral constraints via projection.  
This improves by: (1) avoiding DE on expensive grids, (2) adapting barrier strength dynamically, and (3) ensuring smooth convergence.
