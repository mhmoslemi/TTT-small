The central idea is to use a multi-constrained optimization framework that simultaneously minimizes the maximum correlation between h and 1-h while enforcing the integral constraint. Unlike previous approaches that rely on sequential local optimization steps, this method employs a gradient-based optimization with a constraint-handling mechanism that respects both the [0,1] bounds and the integral constraint during gradient computation. This ensures feasible solutions at every iteration, avoiding the need for expensive post-processing projections.

1. **Gradient Projection with Momentum**: Faster convergence using Adam's adaptive learning rate with momentum.
