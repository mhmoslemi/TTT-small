The current solution achieves a bound of 0.380889, and our goal is to find a lower value, specifically aiming for 0.3808.  
   We adopt a **multi-resolution optimization strategy** with the following key ideas:  
   - **Basis Function Expansion**: Represent \( h(x) \) as a linear combination of basis functions (e.g., scaled and shifted tent functions) to control the function's shape and penalize oscillations.  
   - **Progressive Refinement**: Start at a coarse grid and iteratively refine it using interpolation and optimization, ensuring exact arithmetic at the coarsest grid and re-laplacian for fine grids.  
   - **Duality and Convex Relaxation**: Formulate the problem as a convex optimization by introducing auxiliary variables, separating correlation terms, and using penalty methods.  
   - **Gradient Projection**: Use projected gradient descent with a damping parameter to escape local optima while leveraging the gradient structure of the correlation objective.  
   - **Stochastic Refinement**: After resolving the coarse grid, switch to a stochastic optimization approach to explore the fine grid space systematically.  
   This strategy addresses the non-convexity and high dimensionality of the problem by leveraging inter-grid communication.

  **2. Mathematical specification**  
   We represent \( h(x) \) on an \( n \)-point grid \( \{x_i\}_{i=0}^{n-1} \) with steps \( \Delta x = 2/n \). The problem is:  
   - **Objective**: \( C_5 = \max_k \left| \sum_{i} h_i (1 - h_{i+k}) \Delta x \right| \), where indices are modulo-2n handling as in `mode='full'`.  
   - **Constraints**: \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \) and \( \sum_i h_i \Delta x = 1 \) (here \( \Delta x = 2/n \), so \( \sum h_i = n/2 \)).  

   For Optimization:  
   Introduce auxiliary variables \( g_k(h) = h + (1-h) \) for lag \( k \), but instead, we use a projected gradient on \( \mathbb{R}^n_{[0,1]} \) with:  
   - **Projected Gradient Step**: \( h^{new} = \Pi_{[0,1], \, \sum h_i = n/2}^{\mathbb{R}^n} (h^{old} - \mu \nabla C(h)) \).  
   - **Damping**: \( \mu \) is adjusted based on the relative decrease in \( C_5 \).  

   For the basis representation:  
   Let \( h_i = \sum_{p=1}^m \alpha_p \phi_{p,i} \), where \( \phi_{p,i} \) are tent functions centered at a fixed set of points. This reduces dimensionality from \( n \) to \( m \).  

   **Algorithm** follows three main phases:  
   - **Phase 1: Coarse-to-Fine Expansion** (gradient-based).  
   - **Phase 2: Stochastic Refinement** (random sampling for exploration).  
   - **Phase 3: Local Optimization** (polishing the solution).  

   **Feasibility Check**: Verify \( \sum h_i = n/2 \) and \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \) via projection using `project_feasible`.

  **3. Implementation procedure**  
   Steps for the `run` function:  

   **Step 1: Initialization**  
   - Set `n_min = 100` (trial grid to relearn the problem), `n_max = 400` (parent grid).  
   - Projection: `best_h = project_feasible(initial_h_values, n_max/2)` if available, else initialize randomly.

   **Step 2: Phase 1 - Gradient Projection for Coarse Grid**  
   - Start with `n0 = 40 (good initial size)`, then create grid sequence: `40, 80, 160, 320, 400`.  
   - For each \( n \):  
     1. Interpolate parent solution to \( n \) points (`interpolate_to`) and project.  
     2. Initialize params: \( \mu_0 = 0.5, \max\_iter=500, tol=1e-6 \).  
     3. Compute gradient of \( C(h) \):  
        \[
        \nabla_i C(h) \approx \sum_k (h_i g_k) \Delta x \quad (use FP)
        \]
     4. Use projected gradient:  
        \[
        h_{i+1} = \Pi_{[0,1]} (h_i - \mu \nabla_i C(h))
        \]
        With dynamic damping: \( \mu = \mu * (0.8) \) if decrease <0.5, else \( \mu * 1.1 \).  
     5. After each iter: check feasibility and record best \( C_5 \).

   **Step 3: Phase 2 - Stochastic Refinement**  
   - Use a Rademacher basis (initialized from best_h) and a sequential sampling budget: 3000 evaluations.  
   - Support function: \( h \in \mathcal{A}_{[0,1]} \) but restrict \( \alpha_p \) range via penalty term in the objective.  
   - Objective: \( C_5(\alpha) + \lambda \|\alpha\|_1 \), \( \lambda=0.1 \).  
   - Sample new points by flipping \( \alpha_p \) with \( \Delta\alpha_p \) drawn from uniform [-0.2, 0.2].

   **Step 4: Phase 3 - Local Optimization**  
   - Use a low-damping SLSQP phase with weighted objective: minimize \( C_5 \) while keeping integral fixed.  
   - After `s2`, do a final LP projection to tighten bounds.
