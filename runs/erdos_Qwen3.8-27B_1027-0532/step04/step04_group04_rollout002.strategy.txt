1. Approach and rationale
The proposed approach leverages the concept of discrepancy minimization in the "light" transform domain. Unlike previous methods that rely on greedy local searches or quasi-random initialization, this strategy first transforms the problem into the Fourier domain to compute peak linear discrepancies, then iteratively filters out high-discrepancy frequency components while optimizing the time-domain representation. This method systematically breaks symmetry by prioritizing frequency components that contribute most to discrepancy, enabling more effective exploration of the solution space than gradient-based or combinatorial methods. The key bottleneck addressed is the lack of a discriminative mechanism to identify and penalize high-discrepancy modes.

The method maintains the binary constraint (0/1 values) while using soft constraints to allow optimization near optimal points. The discrepancy estimation guides both global and local optimization towards solutions with minimized maximum correlation.
