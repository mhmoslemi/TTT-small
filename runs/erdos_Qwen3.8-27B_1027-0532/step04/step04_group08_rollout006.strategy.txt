1. **Approach and rationale**  
   The Erdős minimum overlap problem seeks to minimize the maximum overlap between a step function \( h \) and its shifted versions. The current approach using periodic functions with two humps per period achieved a record of 0.380889. This new strategy considers a piecewise constant representation of \( h \) with ten equal intervals across [0,2]. The function is defined by assigning a value to each 0.2-unit interval, ensuring the integral constraint \( \sum h = n/2 = 200 \). This representation reduces dimensionality, improves stability, and allows for more efficient optimization. Genetic algorithms (GA) are used to evolve solutions, ensuring exploration through crossover and mutation while maintaining feasible candidates.

2. **Mathematical specification**  
   - **Representation**: The interval [0,2] is divided into ten equal parts of length 0.2. The function \( h(x) \) is defined by a vector \( v \in \mathbb{R}^{10} \), where each \( v_j \geq 0 \) and \( \sum_{j=0}^{9} v_j = 5 \) (ensuring integral 1).
