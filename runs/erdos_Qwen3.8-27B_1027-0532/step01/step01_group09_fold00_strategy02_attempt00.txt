 Approach and rationale:
 The original method relies heavily on gradient-based optimization and local search. However, the problem is non-convex and multi-modal, and thus may get stuck in local optima. Bayesian optimization (BO) is a global optimization technique that balances exploration and exploitation using an acquisition function. We use BO to optimize the step function over a range of resolutions, which is a common technique in hyperparameter tuning (Treating resolution as a hyperparameter). We build a surrogate model (Gaussian process) to approximate the objective function over the dimensions of the step function.
