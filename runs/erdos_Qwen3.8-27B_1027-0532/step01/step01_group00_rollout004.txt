The idea:
1. Compute the gradient of the smooth max objective
2. Take a gradient step: h_new = h - lr * grad
3. Project h_new onto the feasible set (clip to [0,1], adjust sum)
4. Repeat

This is simpler and might be more robust than SLSQP for this problem.
