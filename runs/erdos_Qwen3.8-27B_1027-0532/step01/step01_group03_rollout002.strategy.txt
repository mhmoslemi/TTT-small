**Objective function**:  
Minimize `C5(h) = np.max_k [∫h(x)(1−h(x+k))dx]`  
Smoothed version:  
`S_α(h) = (1/α) * log(∑_{k} exp(α * C_k))` for large α, where `C_k` is the raw overlap at lag k.  

**Constraints**:  
- `0 ≤ h(x) ≤ 1`  
- `∫h(x)dx = 1` (enforced via `W^T W`*)  
- Smoothness: Gradient of `S` is bounded to avoid foundation instability during Newton-type steps.  

**Solver interface**:  
Use `scipy.optimize.minimize` with bound-constrained 'trust-constr' method, tracking gradient (via central differences) for Cauchy-Schwarz constraints during wavelet transforms.
