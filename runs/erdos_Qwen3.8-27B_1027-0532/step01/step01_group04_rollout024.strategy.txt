Approach and rationale
The goal is to minimize Erdos's minimum overlap integral for a step function h on [0,2] with integral constrained to 1. Given the non-convex nature of the problem, we need a robust global optimization method to escape local minima. Traditional local methods often get stuck around the parent solution (331 grid points). We combine multi-resolution optimization, Bayesian optimization (GP) for regression and acquisition, and hybridization with local optimizers.

Key improvements over previous local-only strategies:
1. **Multi-resolution approach**: Explores solutions at dimensions 139, 331, and 983 to balance grid size and optimization complexity.
2. **Bayesian optimization**: Uses Gaussian Process regression to model the objective surface, enabling efficient global exploration.
3. **Adaptive hybridization**: Switches between Bayesian exploration and local refinement based on error reduction.
4. **Expectile-based objective**: Employs a rho-convex surrogate to approximate the mode='full' correlation.

This strategy should overcome the shallow global exploration and local minima issues of previous SLSQP-based approaches while respecting the parent solution.
