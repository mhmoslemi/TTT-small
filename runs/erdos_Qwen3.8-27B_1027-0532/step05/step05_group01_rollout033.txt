(where we interpret h[out_of_bounds] = 0)

If the max moves to another lag, the change is more complex.

1. Precompute the current correlation
2. For each candidate swap, compute the change at the active lag(s)
3. Only if the change seems promising, recompute the full correlation to verify

This gives us an O(n²) per step (for all candidate swaps) or O(n) per step (if we only check a subset).
