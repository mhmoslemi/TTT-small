1. Approach and Rationale
The current state-of-the-art solution for the Erdős minimum overlap problem uses a multi-phase optimization strategy combining differential evolution (DE), sequential least squares programming, subgradient methods, surgery transformations, and block descent. While this approach has been effective in finding good local optima, it may be prone to getting stuck in suboptimal regions due to its reliance on gradient-based methods and complex repair mechanisms. 

The proposed approach uses a hypergraph-based optimization framework combined with a novel low-overlap encoding scheme. The core idea is to represent the step function as a piecewise linear approximation with special discontinuity points that naturally minimize correlation across lags.
