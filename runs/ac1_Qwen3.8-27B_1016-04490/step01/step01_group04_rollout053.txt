So my FFT computation gives $\sum_k w[k] a[i+k]$ but I want $\sum_k w[k] a[k-i]$.

To fix: I need to compute $\sum_k w[k] a[k-i]$. Let $j = k-i$, so $k = j+i$:
$\sum_j w[j+i] a[j]$

This is the cross-correlation of $a$ and $w$ at lag $i$, where $a$ is the "signal":
$\text{corr}[i] = \sum_j a[j] w[j+i]$
