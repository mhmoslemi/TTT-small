The objective `2*n*max_b/S^2` is minimized when `b` is as flat as possible. The "flattest" nonnegative sequence convolution would be one where b[k] is constant for all k. This is like a "perfect" sequence (in the radar/communications sense).

For the specific problem of finding a nonnegative sequence of length n with flat autoconvolution, this is related to "flat-top" window design.
