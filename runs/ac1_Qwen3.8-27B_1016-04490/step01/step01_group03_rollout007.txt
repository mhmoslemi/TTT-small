R[1] = a[0]*a[1] + a[1]*a[2] + a[2]*a[3].

These are very different! conv[1] is just 2*a0*a1, while R[1] sums adjacent products.

So the structure of the full np.convolve(a,a) is:
- conv[k] for k = 0 to n-1: "inner" products of a with a reversed, with decreasing overlap
- conv[k] for k = n to 2n-2: mirror of the first part

Specifically, conv[k] = conv[2n-2-k] (symmetric).

And conv[n-1] = sum_i a[i]*a[n-1-i] (full overlap, like dot product of a with rev(a)).
