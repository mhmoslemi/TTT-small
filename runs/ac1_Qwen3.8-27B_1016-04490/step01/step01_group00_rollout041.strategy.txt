### 1. Approach and rationale

Our strategy involves iteratively improving a sequence through **gradient-based optimization** with careful **temperature annealing** to escape local minima and **momentum-based updates** to stabilize convergence. The core idea is to optimize the sequence by:

- Representing the sequence as equal energy components clustered in distinct time-frequency bands to ensure non-negativity and boundedness.
- Computing the gradient of the AC1 objective via **scaled cross-correlation** using Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to avoid O(n²) convolution costs.
- Adjusting the sequence through **velocity vectors** rather than direct gradient steps, preserving energy and enabling smooth optimization.
- **Annealing the temperature parameter** from high initial values to low final values, controlling exploration vs exploitation tradeoff.
- **Restarting** the optimizer periodically with new sample seeds to break symmetry, matching the evaluator's normalization and constraints.

The method directly minimizes the AC1 value by leveraging:
- The objective function's structure (ratio of max autocorrelation to R²)
- Non-negativity constraints handled through initialization and projection
- Upper bounds enforced through clipping
- Numerical stability ensured through careful temperature control

The primary bottleneck is the large sequence length, but we use FFT-based convolution and gradient computation to achieve O(n log n) complexity, making the approach feasible for thousands-long sequences.

### 2. Mathematical specification

- **Representation**: Sequence of real coefficients $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $0 \leq x_i \leq 1000$
- **Objective**: Minimize $f(x) = \frac{2n \cdot \max(| \operatorname{full\_convolve}(x, x) |)}{(\sum x)^2}$
- **Variables**:
  - Sequence length $n = 4498$
  - Normalization factor $N = \sqrt{2n}$
  - Temperature parameter $\tau \in [0.01, \infty)$
- **Constraints**:
  - Non-negativity: $x_i \geq 0 \forall i$
  - Upper bound: $x_i \leq 1000 \forall i$
  - Sum constraint: $\sum x \geq 0.01$
  
#### Objective derivation:
The objective is derived from the evaluator function:
$f(x) = \frac{2 \cdot n \cdot \max(\operatorname{full\_convolve}(x,x))}{(\sum_{i=1}^n x_i)^2}$

Note: Full convolution produces $2n-1$ elements, so we take the maximum of the entire convolution output.

#### Temperature schedule:
$\tau(t) = \tau_{\text{min}} \exp(-t / \tau_{\text{ref}})$

where $\tau_{\text{min}} = 0.05$, $\tau_{\text{ref}} = 0.8 \cdot \frac{\text{budget steps}}{\text{max steps}}$

#### Gradient computation:
The gradient is approximated via soft-scaled cross-correlation:

Let $p_k = \exp\left(\frac{(\operatorname{conv}_k - M)}{\tau}\right)$ for each convolution point $k$, where $M = \max(\operatorname{conv})$

Then $g = 2 \cdot \operatorname{cross\_correlate}(p, x)$

Normalized gradient: $\hat{g} = g - \frac{\sum g}{n}$ (mean-centering)

#### Optimizer update rule:
$x_{t+1} = x + \nu \odot \hat{g} + \epsilon$

where $\nu = \exp(-v^2)$ (damped vector) and $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 0.1^2 I)$
