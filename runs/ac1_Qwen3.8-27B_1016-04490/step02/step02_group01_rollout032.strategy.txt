Approach and rationale

The previous program used a projected gradient method with FFT-based convolution and gradient approximation. The new program focuses on computational efficiency and reliability by using a logarithmic scale representation and more aggressive optimization steps.
The previous strategy had bottlenecks in the expensive convolution and gradient computations. This new approach addresses these by:
1. Using logarithmic scales to reduce the scale gap between large and small coefficients
2. Implementing a more aggressive initial update based on the ratio of the convolution to the sum
3. Leveraging the provided parent sequence to guide the initial setup
