The score is 2n * max(P^2) / (sum P)^2 where P is the polynomial. By scale invariance, set sum(P) = 1. Then score = 2n * max(P^2).

We want to minimize the L∞ norm of P^2. This is equivalent to minimizing the L∞ norm of P (since P >= 0, P^2 has max at the same place as P, and max(P^2) = (max P)^2).

Wait, that's not right. P^2 here means the polynomial square (coefficient-wise product via convolution), not the pointwise square. So max of P^2 (as a polynomial) is NOT (max of P)^2.

OK so we definitely need to minimize the max coefficient of the polynomial P(x)^2.
