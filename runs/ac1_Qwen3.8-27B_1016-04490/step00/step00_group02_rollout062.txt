= 4/(n²(n-1)²) * [(n-1)*sum j - sum j²]
= 4/(n²(n-1)²) * [(n-1)*n(n-1)/2 - n(n-1)(2n-1)/6]

= 2(n-2)/(3n(n-1))
≈ 2/(3n) for large n.
