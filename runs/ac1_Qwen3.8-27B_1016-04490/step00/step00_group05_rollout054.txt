If f(j)*f(n-1-j) has negative sum (i.e., f is "anti-symmetric" about the center: f(n-1-j) ≈ -f(j)), then the center convolution DECREASES to second order!

So x_j = 1/n + eps * cos(pi*j/(n-1)) (clipped to be nonnegative) would decrease the center convolution.
