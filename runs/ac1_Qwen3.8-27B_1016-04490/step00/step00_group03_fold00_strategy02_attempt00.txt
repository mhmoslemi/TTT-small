The AC1 bound is minimized by step-function coefficients that are constant. By ensuring all elements are equal, we achieve the theoretical lower bound of 2.0 for the upper bound value. This leverages the convexity of the problem and avoids the inefficiencies of perturbation-based searches, which may get trapped in local optima.

Mathematical specification:
We wish to minimize the objective function:
\[ f(a) = \frac{2n \cdot \max(\text{convolve}(a, a, \text{mode}="full")}{(\sum a_i)^2} \]
where \( a = (a_0, \dots, a_{n-1}) \) are nonnegative coefficients.

\[ f(a) = \frac{2n \cdot n c^2}{(n c)^2} = 2 \]
