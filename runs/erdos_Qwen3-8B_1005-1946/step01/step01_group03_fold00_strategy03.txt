Approach and rationale
The central idea is to minimize the maximum overlap integral C5, which measures the correlation between a step function h(x) and its shifted complement (1-h(x+k)). This optimization is non-convex with box constraints and a linear equality constraint. The previous approach used Differential Evolution followed by local optimization, but it struggled with high dimensionality and non-smoothness. Our new approach uses a hybrid method combining a genetic algorithm (GA) for global exploration and a local optimization step using L-BFGS-B with finite differences. This leverages the exploration power of GA and refinement capabilities of gradient-based methods. The key improvement is using a more sophisticated repair mechanism that preserves shape while enforcing constraints, and a multi-start strategy to escape local optima. The bottleneck addressed is slow convergence in high-dimensional spaces, which we overcome by balancing global and local search.
