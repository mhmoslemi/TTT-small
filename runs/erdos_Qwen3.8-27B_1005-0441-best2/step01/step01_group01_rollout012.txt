Let me try a different approach: **Projected subgradient/gradient descent with adaptive step sizes**.

The objective f(h) = max_k corr[k] * dx is piecewise bilinear (max of bilinear forms). Its subgradient at h is:
- If unique max at k*: subgrad = gradient of corr[k*] * dx
- If multiple max at k* in S: subgrad is in convex hull of {gradient of corr[k] * dx : k in S}

For the smooth approximation g(h) = (1/alpha) * log(sum_{k in S} exp(alpha * corr[k])) * dx, the gradient is:
g'(h) = dx * sum_k w_k * grad(corr[k]) where w_k = exp(alpha*corr[k]) / sum_j exp(alpha*corr[j])

This is a weighted average of the bilinear gradients, which is linear in h for fixed weights. So we can iterate:
1. Compute current corr
2. Compute weights (softmax over top lags)
3. Compute weighted gradient
4. Take projected gradient step
5. Repeat within a few inner iterations before updating weights

This is essentially a generalized FISTA / accelerated proximal gradient method.
