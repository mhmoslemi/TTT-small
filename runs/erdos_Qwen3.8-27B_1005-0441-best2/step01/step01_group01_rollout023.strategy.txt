$$C_5 = \max_{k} \left( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i](1 - h[(i + k) \% n]) \cdot dx \right)$$

We approximate this with a quadratic proxy:
$$C_{smooth} = \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} h[i] (1 - h[j]) W_{i,j}$$
where $W_{i,j}$ encodes overlap weights based on shift distance $|i-j|$

#### Constraints
1. $h[i] ∈ [0, 1] \quad ∀i$
2. $\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i] = n / 2$

#### Discretization
- Grid spacing: $dx = 2.0 / n$
- Overlap indices computed modulo n for circular boundary handling
