∇h C5_smooth = (Σ_k exp(τ * c_k * dx) * ∇h c_k) / (Σ_k exp(τ * c_k * dx)) * dx

This gives a weighted average of the subgradients, weighted by how close each c_k is to the max. This is smoother and might converge better.
