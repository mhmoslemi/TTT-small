In "full" mode, k ranges from -(n-1) to n-1.

So:
c[k] for k = -(n-1), ..., -1, 0, 1, ..., n-1

For k = 0: c[0] = sum_{n=0}^{n-1} (1-h[n]) * h[n] = sum h[n](1-h[n])
For k > 0: c[k] = sum_{n=0}^{n-1-k} (1-h[n]) * h[n+k]
For k < 0 (let k = -m, m > 0): c[k] = sum_{n=m}^{n-1} (1-h[n]) * h[n-m] = sum_{m=0}^{n-1-m} (1-h[m+k]) * h[m] ...
