1. Compute f(0) and f(1)
2. At the minimum, find which lines are active (at the max)
3. If all active lines have A > 0, minimum is at t=0
4. If all active lines have A < 0, minimum is at t=1
5. Otherwise, the minimum is at the intersection of the active lines with A ≤ 0 and A ≥ 0
