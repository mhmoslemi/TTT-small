### Objective

### Constraints
1. $h[i] \in [0, 1]$ for all $i$.

   \text{overlap}_k = \sum_i h[i] (1 - h[i+k \mod n_{\text{points}}]).

2. Use the current $\text{overlap}_k$ values to approximate the maximum constraint.
