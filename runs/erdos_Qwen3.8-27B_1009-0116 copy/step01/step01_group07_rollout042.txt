= max_{λ≥0, sum λ=1} min_h sum_k λ_k f_k(h)

The inner problem is: min_h sum_k λ_k * sum_i h[i](1-h[i+k])*dx
= min_h dx * sum_k λ_k * [sum_i h[i] (over valid i for lag k) - sum_i h[i]h[i+k]]

This is a quadratic program in h! (The h[i]*h[i+k] terms make it non-convex in general, but if we fix λ, it's a specific QP.)

Wait, is it convex? The -h[i]*h[i+k] term... the Hessian would have -1 at positions (i, i+k) and (i+k, i). This is NOT positive semidefinite in general. So it's not a convex QP.

But we could still use it as a subproblem in a dual approach: iteratively update λ (based on which lags are active) and solve the inner problem.
