For a binary sequence of length n with n/2 ones, the full correlation has 2n-1 values. The value at shift s (where s goes from -(n-1) to (n-1), corresponding to index s+n-1 in the full output) is:
c[s] = sum_{i: 0≤i<n, 0≤i+s<n} h[i] * (1-h[i+s])

For s=0: c[0] = sum h[i](1-h[i]) = 0 (binary)
For s>0: c[s] = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-s} h[i]*(1-h[i+s])
For s<0: let s' = -s > 0, c[s] = sum_{i=s'}^{n-1} h[i]*(1-h[i-s']) = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-s'} h[i+s']*(1-h[i])

By symmetry of the problem (reversing the sequence), c[s] for s>0 and c[-s] are related but not equal in general.

The maximum will typically be at small |s|.
