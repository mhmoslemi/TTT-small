np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full') for h of length n gives an array of length 2n-1.
The maximum of this array, times dx, is our C₅.

For binary h with n/2 ones:
- At the center (index n-1), the value is sum_i h[i]*(1-h[i]) = 0 (since binary)
- At index n-1+k (k>0), the value is sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k} h[i+k]*(1-h[i])
- At index n-1-k (k>0), by symmetry, it's sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k} h[i]*(1-h[i+k])
