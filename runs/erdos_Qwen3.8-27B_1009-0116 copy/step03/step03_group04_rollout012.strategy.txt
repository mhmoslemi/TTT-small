Approach and rationale
The current algorithm suffers from several limitations: SLSQP optimization is slow and prone to local optima, the analytical gradient only considers top-3 correlations, the pair-wise local search is shallow, and hyperparameter tuning is fixed. Our approach addresses these by:

- Using a two-phase optimization strategy: first optimizing a smooth surrogate of the overall objective, then targeting correlations of the worst shifts
- Implementing a smarter gradient method that handles both strong and weak correlations
- Using a confidence-weighted optimization for multiple shifts with decreasing focus
- Adapting the step size dynamically based on improvement magnitude
- Adding a novel corner-burst search mechanism to prevent local minima

This multi-scale optimization prioritizes critical correlations while maintaining global feasibility, enabling deeper exploration of the solution space.
