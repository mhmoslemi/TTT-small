This is `2 * (cross-correlation of w and a at lag m)` for `m = 0, ..., n-1`.

In numpy: `grad = 2 * np.correlate(w, a, mode='full')[:n]`

Wait, let me check the numpy correlate indexing again.

`np.correlate(a, v, mode='full')`: the output `c` has length `len(a) + len(v) - 1`.
`c[k] = sum_n a[n+k] * v[n]` (for valid indices).
