def project_simplex(v):
    """Project v onto the probability simplex {x >= 0, sum(x) = 1}."""
    n = len(v)

The subgradient of `max_k c[k]` at a point is a convex combination of the gradients at the active (max achieving) positions. If I use only the single argmax, that's valid (it's one of the subgradients). But using multiple positions (averaging) gives a different (also valid) subgradient.

The issue is: for a non-symmetric sequence, the argmax of the convolution is typically at one specific position. The gradient at that position is `2*a[k*-i]` for valid indices, which is a reversed and shifted copy of `a`. The gradient step `a - lr * grad` would change `a` in a way that reduces the convolution peak at that position.
