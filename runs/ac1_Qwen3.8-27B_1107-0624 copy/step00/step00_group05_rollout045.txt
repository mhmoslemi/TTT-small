If f is constant (all 1/n), then f*f has a triangular shape (peaked at the center). The peak is at k=n-1 with value `sum_j (1/n)(1/n) = n * 1/n^2 = 1/n`. The score is `2n * (1/n) / (1)^2 = 2`.

To flatten the spectrum, we need to reduce the central peak. This means making f less "concentrated" in a way that the convolution is flatter.

One idea: use a sequence that is high at the edges and low in the middle (or vice versa). This would reduce the central convolution value.
