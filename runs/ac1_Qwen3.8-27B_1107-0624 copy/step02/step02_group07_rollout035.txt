Let me also try a different optimization strategy: **Frank-Wolfe (conditional gradient)** method.

The problem: min max_k conv_k(s) subject to sum(s) = 1, s >= 0.

This is equivalent to: min t subject to conv_k(s) <= t for all k, sum(s) = 1, s >= 0, t >= 0.

The Frank-Wolfe method would:
1. Compute the gradient of the linearized objective at the current point.
2. Solve the linear minimization problem over the feasible set (simplex).
3. Take a step towards the solution.

The gradient of the (smoothed) objective is grad[i] = 2 * (p ⊛ s)[i] (as computed above).
The linear minimization over the simplex {s : sum(s) = 1, s >= 0} is just: pick the coordinate with the smallest gradient, set s = e_i (the i-th basis vector).
