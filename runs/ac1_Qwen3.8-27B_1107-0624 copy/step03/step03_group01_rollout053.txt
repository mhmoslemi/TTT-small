**Iterative Peak Smoothing (IPS):**
1. Start with a feasible a (sum=1, a>0).
2. Repeat:

   b. Compute the "flat direction": a direction d such that C[m*] decreases while keeping sum(a)=1 and a>=0.
   c. Take a step in direction d.
   d. If C[m*] is now below the second-maximum, go to the next peak.

A simple choice: d[j] = -a[m*-j] (the gradient of C[m*] w.r.t. a[j], negated). Then project onto the tangent space of the simplex: d_proj = d - mean(d) * 1. And ensure a + t*d_proj >= 0.

This is a projected gradient step on the specific peak C[m*].
