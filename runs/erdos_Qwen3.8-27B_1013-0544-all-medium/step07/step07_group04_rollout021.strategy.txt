### Approach and Rationale
We propose a novel iterative optimization strategy that combines supervised learning with constrained optimization to generate improved step function candidates. The core idea is to use a penalized regression formulation for generating initial candidates with appropriate properties, followed by iterative refinement with gradient-based optimization. This approach combines global exploration with local exploitation phases to escape local optima.

Key advantages:
1. Penalized regression leverages linear algebra solvers for efficient initialization
2. Summer and autumn phases balance exploration and exploitation
3. Unlike previous methods, our formulation directly incorporates the overlap constraints into the objective
4. We use invariant penalty weights derived from Hermite polynomials for better conditioning

The method systematically balances between:
- Maintaining integral constraint
- Enforcing domain bounds
- Maximizing overlap
- Using adaptive smoothing

Potential limitations:
- Requires careful tuning of penalty parameters
- May still miss optimal solutions trapped in deep local minima
- Balanced between interpretability and tight bounds

**Variables:**
- `n` samples on grid [0,2] with spacing `dx=2/n`
- Vector `h = [h₀, h₁, ..., h_{n-₁}]` with entries in [0,1]
- Constraint: `sum(h) = n/2`

**Objective:**

**Constraints:**
