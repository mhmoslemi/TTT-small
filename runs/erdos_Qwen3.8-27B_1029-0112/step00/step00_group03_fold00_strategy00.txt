1. Approach and rationale

1.1. Principal idea  
Optimize the supplied 45-sample construction directly with the exact evaluator at its native resolution, while also maintaining copies at doubled/tripled resolutions generated by exact duplication and projection. Use two exact constrained search mechanisms:

- Feasible two-coordinate transfer moves. Since the sum constraint is equality, a safe move is to decrease one entry and increase another. These moves preserve zero-to-one and the sum exactly (up to a tiny projection repair), and they directly change only the correlation entries involving the two coordinates.

- Full-space linearized minimax descent. At the current iterate, compute the true correlation values, identify the active/large lags, and form a linearized minimization of the highest normalized correlation subject to box and box-sum constraints. Solve this small linear program to get a high-dimensional descent direction. Accept the direction only if the true discrete evaluator improves it.

1.2. Why this can improve the parent  
The supplied parent is already a high-quality construction that must be close to a local optimum with respect to one-dimensional coordinate moves and simultaneous-equation KKT blocks. A tiny target point at 0.3808 versus 0.38085 requires:

- removing active-lag surface slack in many coordinates simultaneously, because tiny improvements usually require simultaneous repair of multiple close lags;

- exploiting finite-resolution effects by moving over finer grids reconstructed from the same piecewise structure, because re-quantization and optimization at N=90 or N=135 occasionally lower the raw Riemann correlation;

- several stochastically independent but nearby starts, because this is a bilinear nonconvex problem and reaching a neighboring basin often requires escaping local optima with short perturbations.

1.3. Bottleneck addressed  
The bottleneck is not low-dimensional coordinate miss-search but nonconvex local plateaus: simple coordinate descent updates a single transfer pair and may leave all active constraints essentially unchanged because the product form C = max_k g_k couples many samples. The full-space linear program accounts for active lags at once and chooses aggregate transfers. Multi-resolution then gives a cheap continuation path from the parent and also exploits cases where the optimized continuous function is represented more accurately at a higher N.

1.4. Guarantees vs limitations  
This is heuristic continuous nonconvex optimization, not a certified global optimization. At all times:

- every candidate is validated using the exact evaluator objective;
- feasibility is enforced exactly by one of two mechanisms: exact pairwise transfer or projection to the box-sum feasible set;
- the program always maintains a valid fallback solution. If nothing improves, it returns the best feasible parent/refined vector found.

Do not claim global optimality. The output is an improved upper bound for the promised discrete construction, not a proof.
