## Approach and Rationale

The goal is to optimize the outgoing Triangle Multiplicative Update (TriMul) operation from the AlphaFold3 paper. This operation involves three matrix multiplications that form the core bottleneck: it computes for each batch element the sum of products of two projected tensors over their sequence dimension. 

The key optimization is to replace the naive triple nested loop with a single `@triton.jit` kernel that performs a blocked fused matrix multiplication. Unlike the previous attempts that were computationally heavy, this new approach carefully reshapes tensors to maximize hardware-level parallelism. 

We choose to tile the spatial dimensions and process them in multiple nested loops. The critical difference is that this version handles the entire contraction chain without intermediate PyTorch operations. This requires:

1. Performing all operations within the kernel to minimize device/host crossings
2. Using a template that computes `(flash_attention_output = input @ attend @ output)`
   for B batches and N sequence lengths

The operation counts match: Ω(N^3) are transformed into a schedule that only makes sense for this specific spatial-only multiplication.

The output projection relies entirely on fixed-shape tensor reshaping and masking, limited only by the tiled operations.
