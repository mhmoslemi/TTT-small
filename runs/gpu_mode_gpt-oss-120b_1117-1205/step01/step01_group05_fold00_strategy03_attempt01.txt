 The operator acts on a 4D tensor [B, N, N, C] and involves several linear projections and element-wise operations.

  2. Apply linear projections (without bias) to x_norm to get left and right, both of shape [B, N, N, hidden_dim].
  3. Apply gating functions (sigmoid) to the projections for left, right, and output gate. However, note that the output gate is applied after the contraction.
