**1. Approach and Rationale**
The TriMul operator, as defined in the AlphaFold3 paper, requires a specific computation that is inherently expensive due to its dependence on computing pairwise products over a third dimension. This triple nested loop structure leads to high computational complexity, particularly when the sequence length (N) is large. To optimize this, we will implement the operation using a batched matrix multiplication (batched GEMM) with explicit management of the input distribution (Normal or Cauchy) and optional masking. This approach leverages PyTorch's `torch.bmm` for efficient handling of the large O(N^3) complexity while retaining controls over floating point precision and additional transformations such as gating.

Our strategy centers on these steps:
- We avoid using trivial operations like average pool and reduce multiple recomputations by batching operations.
- We apply LayerNorm, gating, masking, and final projections in a controlled sequence. Matrix multiplications are batched to improve performance.
- We perform the core contraction (batched GEMM) only once, as specified, to simplify the computation structure and avoid duplicated work, especially when dealing with large tensors.

**2. Mathematical Specification**
The core of the TriMul operator is defined over a 4D tensor `x` of shape \([B, N, N, C]\), following sequential and channel labels. The operation involves:

1. LayerNorm Application:
   \[
   x_{\text{norm}} = \text{LayerNorm}(x, \text{norm\_weight}, \text{norm\_bias})
   \]

2. Projection to Hidden Dimensions:
   \[
   \text{left}_{\text{proj}} = \text{Linear}_\text{proj}(x_{\text{norm}}, \text{left\_proj\_weight})
   \]
   \[
   \text{right}_{\text{proj}} = \text{Linear}_\text{proj}(x_{\text{norm}}, \text{right\_proj\_weight})
   \]

3. Gate Application and Masking:
   \[
   \text{left\_gate} = \text{Linear}_\text{gate}(x_{\text{norm}}, \text{left\_gate\_weight}).\text{sigmoid()}
   \]
   \[
   \text{right\_gate} = \text{Linear}_\text{gate}(x_{\text{norm}}, \text{right\_gate\_weight}).\text{sigmoid()}
   \]
   The masking operation (if applicable) scales the left and right projections by their respective gates and the mask tensor.

4. TriMul Contraction:
   \[
   \text{out} = \text{torch.einsum}('...ikd,...jkd->...ijd', \text{left\_proj}, \text{right\_proj})
   \]
   This step computes the summation over k from 0 to N-1.

5. Output LayerNorm and Scaling:
   \[
   \text{out\_norm} = \text{LayerNorm}(\text{out}, \text{to\_out\_norm\_weight}, \text{to\_out\_norm\_bias})
   \]
   \[
   \text{out\_final} = \text{Linear}(\text{out\_norm}, \text{to\_out\_weight}) \times \text{out\_gate}
   \]
