## Approach and rationale
The TriMul operator involves two main computational bottlenecks:
1. Applying gating operations to left/right projections (sigmoid activation)
2. Performing the O(N³) contraction

Our approach focuses on optimizing the contraction using a blocked matrix multiplication over the sequence dimension. The key insight is that the contraction can be viewed as a 2D tensor product in the sequence space, but with the hidden dimension separating parallel computations.

We choose to implement:
- LayerNorm in PyTorch to leverage its optimizations
- Linear projections using PyTorch
- The contraction using a custom Triton kernel with shared memory and blocking strategy

This reduces the host-side overhead while maintaining numerical stability.
