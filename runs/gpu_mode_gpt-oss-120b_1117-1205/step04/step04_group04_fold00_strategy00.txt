### Approach and rationale
#### Central idea
The TriMul operator performs a batched operation combining element-wise multiplications and gating mechanisms from protein sequence data. The core bottleneck is the O(N³) operation that computes element-wise products between projected sequences:

```
out[b, i, j, d] = sum_{k} (left[b, i, k, d] * right[b, j, k, d] * left_gate[b, i, k, d] * right_gate[b, j, k, d])
```
