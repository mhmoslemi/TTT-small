**Approach and rationale:**  
We address the core TriMul operation without flattening the 4D partitioned tensor. Instead, we rely on the broadcasted nature of the masking and gating operations to separate the components that are computed in PyTorch from those that are accelerated by Triton. The key is to split the contraction over `k` such that each thread in the kernel only needs to write (but not read) from a contiguous block in the output tensor. This avoids the costly load-scatter mechanisms required for out-of-place computations. Intermediate results are kept in a dedicated output buffer and updated in-place.

**Mathematical specification:**

$$ \text{out}_{b,i,j,d} = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \text{left}_{b,i,k,d} \cdot \text{right}_{b,j,k,d} $$
