But wait, we want to MINIMIZE the overlap at k*, which is ∑ h[i]*(1-h[i+k*]) = ∑ h[i] - ∑ h[i]*h[i+k*]. Since ∑h is fixed, minimizing the overlap = maximizing ∑ h[i]*h[i+k*]. Yes, that's correct.

1. Find k* = argmax_k overlap_k
2. Update h to maximize ∑ h[i]*h[i+k*] (via gradient ascent on this quadratic form)
3. Repeat

This is a form of coordinate ascent / block coordinate descent.

But there's a catch: by increasing the correlation at k*, we might decrease it at other lags, potentially making THOSE worse. So we need to track all lags and pick the worst one each time.

This is essentially a "bottleneck" optimization approach.
