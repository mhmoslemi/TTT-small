The integral is over what? If h is on [0,2] and we compute h(x+k), for x+k to be in [0,2], we need x ∈ [-k, 2-k]. Combined with x ∈ [0,2], the integral is over [max(0,-k), min(2, 2-k)].

This means the "effective" lags are only k from -(n-1) to (n-1) in discrete terms, i.e., from about -2 to 2 in continuous terms.

For the linear correlation, the maximum is likely achieved at small lags (near k=0), because at k=0, corr = sum h_n (1-h_n) which is maximized when h is binary (all 0s and 1s): corr = n/2.
