- Output[0] = sum a[i] * v[i] (lag 0 in circular sense)
- Output[k] for k > 0 = sum a[i] * v[(i-k) mod N]
- Output[k] for k < 0 (or equivalently N-k) = sum a[i] * v[(i+k) mod N]

fft_corr = ifft(fft(a) * conj(fft(v)))
