This smoothing transforms the discontinuous step function into a continuous and differentiable approximation that allows for gradient-based optimization. The tent functions naturally arise from epigraph or hypograph descriptions of intervals and can be interpreted as fused lasso regularized surrogates for indicators. This approach addresses the nonconvexity by introducing smoothness (parameterized by tau) that enables iterative refinement.

Unlike combinatorial interval optimization or simple grid-based methods, tent functions 1) decompose the total variation, 2) preserve the essential discontinuous nature while making gradients accessible, and 3) can be differentially tunable.
