1. **Approach and Rationale**
This solution employs a hybrid optimization strategy combining a genetic algorithm (GA) for global exploration and a derivative-free local optimizer for refinement. The approach leverages the GA's ability to escape local optima by maintaining a diverse population, while the local optimizer applies gradient information to attain higher precision in promising regions. The key innovation is adaptive dimensionality control— GA operates on a coarser grid (800 points) to efficiently explore the complex topology, then refines to higher resolution. This balances computational efficiency with the accuracy needed to find the global minimum.

Unlike previous gradient-based optimizers that tend to converge prematurely, the GA will systematically sample the entire search space through crossover and mutation. The local optimizer acts as a phase transition: after identifying promising candidates, we "zoom in" with higher resolution and adaptive gradient steps. This design addresses the non-convexity of the objective, which simple gradient descent struggles with.
