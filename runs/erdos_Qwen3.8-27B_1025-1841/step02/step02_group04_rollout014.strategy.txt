### Approach and Rationale

**Central Idea**: 
We represent the step function h(x) using a multi-scale basis of functions that are designed to model correlations at different lags. The basis functions will be chosen to have controlled auto-correlations, meaning that the correlation decreases as the lag increases. By optimizing the coefficients of these basis functions, we can minimize the maximum overlap integral.

**Why this approach**:
- Continuum optimization methods can get stuck in local optima. A multi-scale representation allows us to escape these by providing multiple paths for gradient ascent.
- The use of basis functions that decay in correlation allows us to model a wide range of h(x) shapes, from purely periodic to aperiodic, while maintaining feasibility (0 <= h(x) <= 1 and integral 1).

**Bottleneck Addressed**:
- The parent solution uses local optimization and periodic patterns, which are limited in the structures they can capture. Our approach will use a more flexible representation that can adapt to different correlation profiles.
