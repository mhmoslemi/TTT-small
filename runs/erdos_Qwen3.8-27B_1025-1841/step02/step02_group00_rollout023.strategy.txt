**Central idea**: Use a **Fourier-based** approach to represent the function `h` as a sparse combination of waves, reducing the problem to optimizing a few coefficients. This leverages the shift-invariance of the correlation integral to capture global structure efficiently, avoiding the combinatorial explosion of grid-based searches.

**Rationale**:

- The optimization is separable in Fourier space, allowing efficient gradient-based updates.

**Limitations**:
- May underfit highly irregular structures, but the max-Correlation Erdős problem typically relies on smoothness.
