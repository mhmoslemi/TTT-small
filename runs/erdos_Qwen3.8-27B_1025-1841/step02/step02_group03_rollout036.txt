This counts pairs (i-j, i) where the first is 0 and the second is 1, at distance j.

Similarly c[n-1-j] = Σ_{i=0}^{n-1-j} h[i] * (1-h[i+j]) = |{i : 0 ≤ i ≤ n-1-j, h[i]=1, h[i+j]=0}|

This counts pairs (i, i+j) where the first is 1 and the second is 0, at distance j.

So c[n-1+j] counts "0-to-1" transitions at distance j, and c[n-1-j] counts "1-to-0" transitions at distance j.

The max of all c[k] is what we want to minimize.
