numpy.correlate(a, v, mode='full'):
c[k] = sum_n a[n+k] * conj(v[n])  (for real: a[n+k] * v[n])

where the sum is over all n where both a[n+k] and v[n] are defined.

So for a=h (length n), v=1-h (length n):
c[k] = sum_n h[n+k] * (1-h[n]) for n where 0≤n<n and 0≤n+k<n

So:
- k ranges from -(n-1) to (n-1), and c is indexed 0 to 2n-2
- c[k_index] corresponds to k = k_index - (n-1)
