Actually, let me think about this more carefully. The overlap at shift k is:
f_k = sum_i h[i] * (1-h[i+k]) for valid i

To reduce f_k, we want to either:
- Change h[i] from 1 to 0 where h[i+k]=0 (reduces f_k by 1, but might increase other f_j)
- Change h[i+k] from 0 to 1 where h[i]=1 (reduces f_k by 1, but might increase other f_j)
- Swap: change h[i] from 1 to 0 and h[i+k] from 0 to 1 (net effect on f_k is -1, but affects other shifts)

The swap approach maintains the sum constraint automatically.
