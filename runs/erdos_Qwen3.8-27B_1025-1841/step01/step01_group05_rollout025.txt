$\sum_k dx \cdot \sum_i h[i](1-h[i+k]) = dx \cdot \sum_i h[i] \sum_{k: \text{valid}} (1-h[i+k])$

The inner sum $\sum_{k: \text{valid}} (1-h[i+k])$ counts the number of positions $j$ such that $j$ is in range and $h[j]$ appears, minus the sum of $h$ over those positions. For a fixed $i$, as $k$ ranges over all valid lags, $i+k$ ranges over all valid $j$. So the inner sum is $(n-1) - \sum_j h[j] + h[i] = (n-1) - n/2 + h[i]$.

So the total sum is $dx \cdot \sum_i h[i] \cdot [(n-1) - n/2 + h[i]] = dx \cdot [(n-1-n/2) \sum_i h[i] + \sum_i h[i]^2]$
$= dx \cdot [(n/2 - 1) \cdot n/2 + \sum_i h[i]^2]$
$= dx \cdot [n^2/4 - n/2 + \sum_i h[i]^2]$

The average correlation is this divided by $(2n-1)$:
$\text{avg} = \frac{dx}{2n-1} [n^2/4 - n/2 + \sum_i h[i]^2]$
$= \frac{2/n}{2n-1} [n^2/4 - n/2 + \sum_i h[i]^2]$
$\approx \frac{1}{n^2} [n^2/4 - n/2 + \sum_i h[i]^2]$ (for large n)
$\approx \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2n} + \frac{\sum_i h[i]^2}{n^2}$

Since $\sum_i h[i]^2 \geq (\sum_i h[i])^2 / n = (n/2)^2 / n = n/4$ (by Cauchy-Schwarz), we get:
$\text{avg} \geq \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2n} + \frac{n/4}{n^2} = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2n} + \frac{1}{4n} = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{4n}$

So the average correlation is at least $\approx 1/4 = 0.25$, and the max must be at least the average. This gives a lower bound of $C_5 \geq 0.25$ (approximately).

        else:
            expr = cp.sum(h_var[-k:] * g[:n + k])

        except Exception:

    return None
