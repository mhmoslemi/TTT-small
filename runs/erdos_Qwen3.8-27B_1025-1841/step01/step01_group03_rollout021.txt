So the net change in overlap(L) from swapping a[p]=1→0 and a[q]=0→1:
Δoverlap(L) = [a[p+L] - (1-a[p-L])] (from changing a[p]) + [-(a[q+L]) + (1-a[q-L])] (from changing a[q])
= a[p+L] - 1 + a[p-L] - a[q+L] + 1 - a[q-L]
= a[p+L] + a[p-L] - a[q+L] - a[q-L]

where each a[·] is 0 if the index is out of range [0, n-1].

This is O(1) per lag, so O(n) per swap evaluation. With O((n/2)²) possible swaps (choosing which 1 to move to which 0), the total per full pass is O((n/2)² * n) = O(n³/4). For n=80, that's about 128,000 operations per full pass. Very fast.
