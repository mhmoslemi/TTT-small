If h is a step function (0 or 1), then h(x)(1-h(x+k)) = 1 iff h(x)=1 and h(x+k)=0.

    """Project h onto {h : 0<=h[i]<=1, sum(h)=n/2}"""
    h = np.clip(h, 0.0, 1.0)
