Actually, let me be more careful. The cross-correlation of a and b is:
(a ⋆ b)[k] = Σ_m a[m] b[(m-k) % n]

Its DFT is: DFT(a ⋆ b)[ω] = conj(DFT(a)[ω]) * DFT(b)[ω]

So (a ⋆ b) = IFFT(conj(DFT(a)) * DFT(b))

For autocorrelation (a = b = h):
(h ⋆ h)[k] = IFFT(conj(DFT(h)) * DFT(h))[k] = IFFT(|DFT(h)|^2)[k]

So auto[k] = IFFT(|FFT(h)|^2)[k] = Σ_m h[m] h[(m-k)%n]

And c[k] = Σ_m h[m](1-h[(m-k)%n]) = Σ_m h[m] - Σ_m h[m]h[(m-k)%n] = h.sum() - auto[k]
