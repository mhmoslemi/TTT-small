### Approach and Rationale

The problem requires finding a step function \( h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1] \) with integral 1 that minimizes the maximum overlap integral \( C_5 = \max_k \int_{0}^{2} h(x)(1-h(x+k)) \, dx \), which represents the worst-case misalignment between the set where \( h(x) = 1 \) and its complement.
