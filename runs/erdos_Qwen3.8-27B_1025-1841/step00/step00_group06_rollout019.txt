    """Repair h to satisfy sum = n/2, 0 <= h <= 1."""
    h = np.clip(h, 0.0, 1.0)

Final consideration: the `np.correlate` function. For mode='full', the output has length 2n-1. The middle element (index n-1) corresponds to shift 0. Elements before n-1 are negative shifts, and after are positive shifts.

The correlate computes:
C[n-1+k] = ∑_{i=0}^{n-1-|k|} a[i+|k|] * v[i] (for k ≥ 0)
or equivalently depends on the convention.

Actually, np.correlate(a, v, 'full') computes:
c[k] = ∑_i a[i] * conj(v[i-k]) (for valid i)

So c[n-1] = ∑_i a[i] * v[i] (shift 0, autocorrelation)
c[n-1+k] for k > 0: ∑_{i=k}^{n-1} a[i] * v[i-k]

With a = h and v = 1-h:
c[n-1] = ∑ h[i](1-h[i]) (shift 0)
c[n-1+k] = ∑_{i=k}^{n-1} h[i] * (1-h[i-k]) (shift k, k > 0)

This is the sum over positions where h[i] is multiplied by (1-h[i-k]). For binary h, this counts positions where h[i]=1 and h[i-k]=0.

This is exactly what we want: the cross-correlation between h and its complement.

And C₅ = max(c) * dx.
