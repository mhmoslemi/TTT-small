One more thought: the "greedy polish" at the end is crucial. After annealing finds a good region, a pure local search (only accept if it improves) can squeeze out the last few thousandths. The previous code's "binary_search_v2" was essentially this.

For the greedy polish, the key optimization is:
- For each 1-position p, find the 0-position q that minimizes the new max correlation
- Apply the best (p,q) swap
- Repeat until no improvement
