### 1. Approach and Rationale  
The core challenge is obtaining a tight upper bound for the Erdős minimum overlap problem constant by finding a step function \( h: [0,2] \to [0,1] \) that minimizes the overlap integral \( C_5 \). The provided solution achieved \( C_5 \approx 0.380893 \) in 1100 seconds. To improve it, we transition to numerical projection-based optimization over continuous variables, leveraging the constraint \( \sum h_i = 1 \) and the bounds \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \). This replaces the binary search with a more sophisticated gradient projection method that finds higher-quality local optima.  

We use the minimization of the local maximum correlation as the quadratic penalty formulation. The key innovation is hyperparameter tuning (projection degrees and discrete grid resolution) to balance solution quality and computational feasibility.

### 2. Mathematical Specification  
#### Objective function:  
Minimize \( P = \max_k ||C_k||^2 \) where:  
\[ C_k = \int h(x) \cdot (1 - h(x+k)) dx \]  
but formulated as:  
\[ \min h \left( \max_k ||\text{correlation}(h, 1-h)_k || \right) \]  

#### Constraints:  
1. \( \sum_{i=1}^N h_i = 1 \)  
2. \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \)  

#### Variables:  
- \( h \in \mathbb{R}^N \) for \( N \) points  

#### Setup:  
- Penalty formulation:  
  \[ \min \max_k \left( -|| \text{correlation}_k ||^2 + \lambda (1 - \sum_i h_i) \right) \]  
- Projection to valid bounds (\( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \)) and global sum via projection operator:  
  \[ h_{\text{proj}} = \text{clip}\left(h, 0, 1\right) \downarrow \frac{1}{2} \]  

#### Algorithm:  
Projected Gradient Descent with momentum (\( \mu = 0.9 \)) and adaptive step sizes:  
\[ h^{t+1} = \text{proj}\left( h^t - \alpha_t \cdot g_t \right) \]  
where:  
- \( g_t \) = approximated gradient of \( ||\text{correlation}||^2 \) via central finite-differences  
- \( \alpha_t \) = step size, \( \alpha_t = \alpha_{\text{min}} + \alpha_{\text{max}} \cdot e^{-t/T} \)
