For the gradient computation, the derivative of the (linear) cross-correlation is:
- For lag L > 0: c_L = ∑_{i=0}^{N-1-L} h[i] * (1-h[i+L])
  ∂c_L/∂h[i] = (1-h[i+L]) for i < N-L
  ∂c_L/∂h[i] = -h[i-L] for i ≥ L

- For lag L < 0 (i.e., -M where M > 0): c_{-M} = ∑_{i=M}^{N-1} h[i] * (1-h[i-M])
  ∂c_{-M}/∂h[i] = (1-h[i-M]) for i ≥ M
