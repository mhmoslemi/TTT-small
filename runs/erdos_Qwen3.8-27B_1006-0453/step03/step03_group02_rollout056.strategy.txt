The current optimization strategy relies heavily on local search methods with stochastic perturbations. However, these may not adequately explore the solution space, especially around flat regions or multi-modal optima. The core bottleneck is the limited exploration of the function space, particularly neglecting significant geometric shifts in the solution.

The GDM injects geometrically structured changes to escape local optima, while SPR applies progressive level-specific adjustments for fine-grained optimization, balancing exploration at genetic scales and exploitation at local scales.

This approach addresses three key weaknesses in the current strategy:
