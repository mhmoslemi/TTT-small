<h1>Ridgelet Pursuit with Convexification</h1>
<p>This approach uses ridgelet transforms to project the sequence into the frequency domain, then applies convex optimization to minimize the <code>evaluate_sequence</code> function. Unlike previous methods that treat the sequence as a bag of coefficients, this representation considers the coefficients as a function transforming it into a ridgelet basis. The optimization problem is projected into a lower-dimensional space and solved using entropy-regularized gradient descent, which balances pursuit accuracy with computational expenditures.</p>
<blockquote>
  Objective: <strong>2*n*max(convolve(a,a))/sum(a)^2</strong> (lower-is-better)
  <strong>Benchmarks: 1.5027 tuning target</strong>
</blockquote>
<p><strong>Key innovation:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Uses <code>np.convolve</code> to compute the full-length convolution</li>
  <li><em>Ridgelet projection</em> transforms coefficients into meaningful gradients</li>
  <li>Adaptive learning rate maintenance prevents divergence during optimization</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Mathematical specification:</strong></p>
<p>We define a ridgelet transform as:
  <code>f(tau, theta) = np.fft.irfft(np.fft.rfft(w) * np.exp(1j*tau*theta))</code>
  and seeks to solve:
  <code>min_theta ||evaluate_sequence(projection(theta))||</code></p>
<p><strong>Implementation procedure:</strong></p>
<ol>
  <li>Define parameter space including ridgelet angles (tau) and convsecondation coordinates (theta)</li>
  <li>Precompute gradients using finite differences with automatic simplex projection</li>
  <li><em>Projected gradient descent</em> in the Ridgelet space</li>
  <li>Convexification via mirror descent prevents local optimum trapping</li>
</ol>
