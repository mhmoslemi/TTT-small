**Mathematical Instantiation**  
The optimization occurs over non-negative coefficients \(a_0, \ldots, a_{n-1} \in [0, 1000]\) where \(n = L\) (oscillator length parameter). The objective is \(F(a) = 2n \max_k [a * a][k] / (\text{sum}(a))^2\), which equals:
\[
F(a) = \frac{2n}{(\sum a_i)^2} \cdot \max_{k} \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a_j a_{k-j}
\]
A hierarchy of objectives is defined by recursive convolution stages \(s_d = d \cdot a * (\ldots * a))\) for \(d = 1, 2, \ldots\) and embedding depths \(d_e \in [1, \log n]\). The optimization minimizes \(\max_d \max_{k} s_d[k]\).
