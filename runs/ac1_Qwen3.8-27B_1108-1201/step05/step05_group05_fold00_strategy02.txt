## Approach and Rationale

The central idea is to leverage the known optimal structure for sequences with minimal auto-correlation magnitude. Previous works have shown that sequences composed of a small number of distinct values clustered in sub-intervals can effectively minimize \(\max(f*f)\). This approach systematically explores multiple structural templates through cyclic refinement, each with tailored optimization hyperparameters. We focus on candidates generated via:

1. **Repetitive Cluster Optimization** - Where the sequence is divided into \(k\) repeated blocks, each with identical values but different positions
2. **Sub-Interval Structured Optimization** - Where the sequence is divided into \(k\) non-overlapping sub-intervals with constant values
3. **Self-Similar Optimization** - Where peaks are maintained while troughs are strategically reduced

The key bottleneck in earlier approaches was either local convergence or premature termination due to computational complexity. Here, we delay strict convergence checks by first scanning for promising templates before committing to heavy-large optimization cycles.
