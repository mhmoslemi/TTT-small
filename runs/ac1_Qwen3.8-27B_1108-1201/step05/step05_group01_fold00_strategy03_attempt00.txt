- Nesterov-accelerated projected gradient descent with adaptive temperature and momentum
- Frank-Wolfe algorithm with view transformation
- Golden-section coordinate descent
- Fourier-domain interpolation between different sequence lengths

Each method is tested on sequences of increasing length, but we also use dimensionality reduction to avoid intractable large- esize problems. The idea is to find a sequence that avoids too many peaks while maintaining the quality needed to attain the target score.

2. Mathematical specification
The objective to minimize is:
score = (2 * n * max_conv) / (sum(a))**2

Where:
- n: sequence length
- max_conv: maximum value from the full convolution of a with itself
- sum(a): sum of sequence coefficients (>0.01)

The sequence must satisfy:
0 ≤ a_i ≤ 1000 for all i
sum(a) ≥ 0.01

Through the optimization process, we seek to find coefficients that do not unduly peak in the convolution.
