Another idea: **Use the structure of the gradient**. The gradient at the peak lag k* is `2 * s[k*-j]` (shifted/reversed s). This means the direction of steepest descent is to subtract a shifted/reversed copy of s from s itself. This is very structured!

For example, if the peak is at the center (k* = n-1), the gradient is `2 * s[n-1-j]` = `2 * s_reversed[j]`. So we'd be doing:
`s_new[j] = s[j] - eta * 2 * s[n-1-j]`
