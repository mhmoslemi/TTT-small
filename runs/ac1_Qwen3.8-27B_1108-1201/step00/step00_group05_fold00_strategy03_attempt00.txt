This method is chosen because it strikes a balance between global exploration and local exploitation. ## Mathematical specification

Given a sequence \( a \) of length \( n \), we wish to minimize:
\[ J(a) = \frac{2n \cdot \max(\text{convolve}(a, a))}{(\text{sum}(a))^2} \]

Our optimization process will:

- Use block updates rather than single coefficient updates
- Formulate the search as a block-coordinate descent

The optimization variable is the sequence itself, \( a \), but we will update it in blocks \( B \) of consecutive coefficients.

### Objective function:

\[ \text{objective}(a) = \frac{2n \cdot \max(\text{convolve}(a, a))}{(\text{sum}(a))^2} \]
