# Approach and rationale
We are minimizing a quantity related to the coefficients of a step function: 2*n*max(convolution)/sum^2, where the convolution is the full linear convolution of the sequence with itself. The Belief Propagation Genetic Algorithm (GP) balances exploration and exploitation by maintaining a population of sequences of variable lengths, evolving them through crossover and mutation, and using monitoring-like operations to track changes. The algorithm prioritizes brevity and computational efficiency by limiting the population size and phasing out less promising solutions over generations.

# Mathematical specification
We work with sequences of coefficients that satisfy:
- Non-negative elements (0 ≤ coefficients ≤ 1000)
- Sum ≥ 0.01 for normalization

The evaluation function is given by:
`obj = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / sum(a)^2`

Where `conv(a, a)` is the full linear convolution of length 2n-1.
