  Considering the complexity of directly minimizing the objective, we adopt a strategy of first generating candidate sequences from two key families that have shown promise in previous works: *SineProfile* and *RaisedCosineProfile*. These profiles mimic temporal patterns often used in optimization of convolution and autocorrelation properties.
  - *SineProfile*: This profile generates sequences that alternate between two positive values, scaled exponentially to ensure non-negativity and long-term feasibility. The key parameters are the frequency `f`, and the ratio `r` between min and max values.
  - *RaisedCosineProfile*: This profile creates a sequence with a single peak at the beginning and tapers off exponentially to the end. Two hyperparameters define the shape: `r1` (ratio of first two values) and `alpha` (taper rate).
  These families are chosen for their ability to:
  - Distribute values in a way that limits simultaneous high values
  - Ethan to explicit synchronization points
  - Provide a smooth optimization landscape suitable for gradient-based methods
