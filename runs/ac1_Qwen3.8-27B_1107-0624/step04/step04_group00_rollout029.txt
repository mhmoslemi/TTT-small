**Better idea: Use the subgradient of the max, projected onto the tangent space of the simplex**

2. Compute c = conv(a, a)
3. Let k* = argmax(c) (or use softmax weights for smoothness)

5. Project g onto tangent space: g_proj = g - mean(g)
6. Update: a = a - eta * g_proj
7. Project onto simplex: a = max(a - lambda, 0) for appropriate lambda
8. Normalize: a = a / sum(a)
9. Repeat with decreasing eta

This is a projected subgradient method. It should converge to a local minimum.
