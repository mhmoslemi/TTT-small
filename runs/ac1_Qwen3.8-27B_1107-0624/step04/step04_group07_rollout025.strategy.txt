The key observation is that the maximum of the self-convolution corresponds to the maximum of the modulus squared of the Fourier transform. By optimizing in the Fourier domain, we can separate the optimization of frequency components and the time domain representation, potentially leading to faster convergence and lower objective values.

The objective function is: 2 * n * max(|FFT(a)|²) / (sum(a))², where a is the sequence. The strategy involves:
1. Generating initial candidates in the Fourier domain.
2. Projecting the Fourier domain candidates back to the time domain.
3. Iteratively refining the candidates with a combination of gradient-based optimization and constraint handling.
