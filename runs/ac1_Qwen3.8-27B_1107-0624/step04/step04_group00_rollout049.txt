The LP approach:
- Given current f with M = max(f*f)
- Solve: maximize sum(g) subject to (f*g)(k) <= M for all k, g >= 0
- Rescale g to have the same sum as f
- Update: f_new = (1-t)*f + t*g

The key advantage is that this directly ensures the max doesn't increase (since (f*g)(k) <= M and g is being "filled in" to the valleys).
