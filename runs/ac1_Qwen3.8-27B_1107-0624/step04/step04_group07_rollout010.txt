**Algorithm: Entropy-regularized alternating optimization**
1. Start with p (sum=1, p>=0).
2. Compute M = max(conv(p,p)).
3. Compute v = conv(lambda, p) for some lambda (initially uniform or from the max lag).

5. Update p = (1-t)*p + t*b.
6. Update lambda based on which constraints are violated.
7. Repeat.

This is similar to the LP approach but with a smoother, differentiable update. The SOPLEX/LP gives the "extreme point" solution, while the entropy-regularized version gives a "soft" solution that might escape local optima.
