The circular autocorrelation is IFFT(FFT(a_padded) * conj(FFT(a_padded))) = IFFT(|FFT(a_padded)|^2).

Since a is real and non-negative, FFT(a_padded) is complex. Let's write it as F = |F| * e^{iφ}.

The constraint is: max_k IFFT(|F|^2)[k] is minimized.

One approach: fix the magnitude |F| and optimize the phase φ. But the phase and magnitude are not independent (they must correspond to a real, non-negative, zero-padded sequence).

Another approach: use the "projected gradient" method in the frequency domain:
1. Compute F = FFT(a_padded).
2. Compute the "peak power" in the time domain: P = IFFT(|F|^2).
3. Compute gradient of max(P) with respect to F.
4. Update F in the direction that reduces the peak.
5. Project F back to the feasible set: IFFT(F) must be real, non-negative, and zero (positions n to N-1).

The projection step (5) is the tricky part. It requires:
- Taking IFFT(F) to get x.
- Setting x[i] = max(0, x[i]) for i=0..n-1.
- Setting x[i] = 0 for i=n..N-1.
- Taking FFT again to get the projected F.
