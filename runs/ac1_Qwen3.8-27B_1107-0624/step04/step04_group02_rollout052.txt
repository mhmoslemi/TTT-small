Actually, let me reconsider the problem. The key formula is:
Objective = 2*n*max_k c[k] / (sum a)^2

Ah ha! So the theoretical lower bound is 1 (as n→∞), not 2! The uniform distribution gives 2, but we can do better by making the convolution more "spread out" (i.e., closer to uniform across all lags).

The ideal case would be if all c[k] are equal, in which case c[k] = (sum a)^2/(2n-1) for all k, and the objective = 2n/(2n-1) ≈ 1.
