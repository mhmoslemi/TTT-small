The Lagrangian dual: max_{λ_k >= 0, sum λ_k = 1} min_{a>=0, sum(a)=1} sum_k λ_k b_k

= min_a a^T (sum_k λ_k B_k) a  s.t. a >= 0, sum(a) = 1

The dual problem is: max_{λ in simplex} (min value of the QP)
