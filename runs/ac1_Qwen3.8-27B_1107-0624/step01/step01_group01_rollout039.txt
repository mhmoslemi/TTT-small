   - Objective: 2 * max_k (sum_i f_i * f_{i+k}) / (sum f_i)^2 (for the discrete version at length m).
   - Constraints: f_i >= 0, sum f_i = 1 (normalization).

   For m = 512, this is a 512-dimensional optimization problem. Too many variables for a direct approach.

   **Approach 1: Spline parameterization.**

   **Approach 2: Basis expansion.**
   Use a fixed basis (e.g., Chebyshev polynomials, or the first K Fourier modes) and optimize the coefficients.

   **Approach 3: Alternating optimization.**
   Fix most of the profile and optimize a few points at a time.
