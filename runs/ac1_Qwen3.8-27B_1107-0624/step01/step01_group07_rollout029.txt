        S = a.sum()

        active = np.where(c >= max_c - 1e-10)[0]

        grad = np.zeros(n)

                if 0 <= j < n:

            grad[i] = 2.0 * n * (S * contribution / len(active) - 2.0 * max_c) / (S**3)

For a non-negative sequence a of length n with sum S:
The convolution c[k] = sum_j a[j]*a[k-j]
The mean of c is S^2/(2n-1)
The max of c is at least S^2/(2n-1) (trivially)
But also, c[n-1] = sum_j a[j]*a[n-1-j]

By the rearrangement inequality, for non-negative sequences, c[n-1] is maximized when a is "aligned" with its reverse. The minimum of c[n-1] (over all non-negative a with sum S) is achieved when a is as "anti-aligned" with its reverse as possible.
