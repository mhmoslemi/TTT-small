Let me try another angle. What about using the fact that we're on the simplex and using **Riemannian optimization**?

The manifold is {a >= 0, sum a = 1} (the simplex). The Riemannian gradient is the projection of the Euclidean gradient onto the tangent space (which is {v: sum v = 0}).

Riemannian gradient descent:
- Compute Euclidean gradient g
- Project to tangent space: g_t = g - (sum g / n) * 1
- Extrinsic step: a_new = exp_meta(g_t * lr) (Riemannian exponential map)
- For the simplex, the exponential map is conjugate exponentiation: a_new[i] = a[i] * exp(g_t[i] * lr) / sum_j a[j] * exp(g_t[j] * lr)

This is equivalent to **mirror descent** with the entropy barrier! It naturally stays in the interior of the simplex.
