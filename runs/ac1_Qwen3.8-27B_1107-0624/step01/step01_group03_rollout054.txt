For a symmetric sequence s[i] = s[n-1-i], the center convolution is sum s[i]^2. By Cauchy-Schwarz, this is >= (sum s)^2 / n. So for symmetric sequences, the center is at least (sum s)^2/n, giving score >= 2.

For asymmetric sequences, the center convolution is sum s[i]*s[n-1-i], which can be less than (sum s)^2/n if the sequence is "oppositely sorted" (one side high, other side low).

So the strategy is: make the sequence asymmetric, with one side high and the other side low. This reduces the central convolution.
