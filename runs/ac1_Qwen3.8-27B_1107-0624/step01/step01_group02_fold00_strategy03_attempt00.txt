**Approach and Rationale**

This approach combines genetic algorithms (GAs) with local optimization using linear programming (LP) to find a sequence that minimizes the evaluation function. While GAs typically explore a broad search space effectively, our challenge lies in efficiently optimizing complex step-function properties. By using a hybrid of GA-driven global optimization and LP-based local refinement, we aim to overcome the limitations of both methods individually:

1. Use a GA population starting from the provided sequence and diverse random shapes to explore the search space globally
2. For promising candidates from the GA, apply a local LP optimization on their correlated peaks
3. The LP step acts as a local search, refining solutions that require multi-dimensional optimizations
4. This combination leverages the simplicity and data-flow control of heuristic search while enhancing optimization quality through mathematical guarantees
