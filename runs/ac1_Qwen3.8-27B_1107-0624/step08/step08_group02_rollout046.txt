def _next_pow2(n):

For large n, what's the asymptotic behavior? If the optimal max is approximately c/n (like uniform), the score would be 2n * c/n = 2c. For uniform, c = 1, so score = 2.

But we're achieving score ≈ 1.5, which means max(conv(a,a)) ≈ 1.5/(2n) = 0.75/n. This is less than the uniform case (1/n), so the optimal solution is NOT uniform.

For a non-symmetric sequence, the center of the convolution can be much less than sum(a_i^2). The key is to arrange the sequence so that the "overlap" of a with its reverse is minimized at all shifts.

This is related to the "cross-correlation" problem in compressed sensing and signal processing. The goal is to find a non-negative sequence whose cross-correlation with its reverse is as flat as possible.

In the continuous case, this is related to the "sidelobe" minimization for radar/sonar waveforms. The optimal real non-negative waveform for minimizing peak sidelobe level is known to be a truncated Gaussian or a "Williams-Olson" type waveform.

For discrete sequences, the problem is NP-hard in general (I believe), so we need heuristics.
