- Be asymmetric (to reduce the central peak)

**Key insight**: The problem is fundamentally about making the aperiodic autocorrelation as flat as possible. This is a well-studied problem in signal processing (related to Barker sequences, Zadoff-Chu sequences, etc.).

For real, non-negative sequences, the problem is more constrained. But the idea is the same: we want a sequence whose autocorrelation has minimal peak sidelobe.

One approach used in radar/communications is to start with a good sequence (e.g., a comma-free sequence or a sequence with known low peak autocorrelation) and then optimize it.

For our non-negative constraint, a good starting point might be:
- A smoothed version of a Barker-like sequence (but made non-negative)
- A sequence with a specific spectral shape (e.g., flat spectrum, which corresponds to low autocorrelation peaks)

Actually, by Wiener-Khinchin theorem, the autocorrelation is the inverse Fourier transform of the power spectrum. For a sequence a with DFT A(f):
autocorr[k] = sum_i a[i] a[i+k] = (1/N) sum_f |A(f)|^2 e^{2πifk/N}

So the peak of the autocorrelation is related to the "flatness" of |A(f)|^2. A flat power spectrum gives a delta-function autocorrelation (peak at k=0, zero elsewhere).

But our constraint is a >= 0, which means the DFT has a specific structure (no negative values in time domain).
