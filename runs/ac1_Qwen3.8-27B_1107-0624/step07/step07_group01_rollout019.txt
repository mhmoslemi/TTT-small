If the fluctuations are large relative to $\bar{a}$, the autocorrelation is more uniform (good), but we're limited by $a_i \geq 0$ which means the fluctuations can't be too negative.

The optimal trade-off is: make the fluctuations as large as possible (to flatten the autocorrelation) while keeping $a_i \geq 0$. This means $a_i$ should be close to 0 at some positions (to maximize the relative size of fluctuations) and large at others.

So the optimal sequence should be "biphasic" in some sense - close to 0 at some positions and large at others. This is similar to a "binary" sequence!
