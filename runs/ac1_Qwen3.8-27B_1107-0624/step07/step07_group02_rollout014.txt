To make the sum distribution uniform on [0, 2n-2], we'd need w*sqrt(2) ≈ 2n, so w ≈ 2n/sqrt(2) ≈ 1.4n. But the maximum width of a distribution on [0, n-1] is about n (for the uniform distribution, w = n/sqrt(12) ≈ 0.29n).

So the sum distribution will always be "peaked" relative to uniform. The question is how to minimize the peak.

I think the key insight is: **the optimal distribution is NOT symmetric**. It should be "lopsided" in a specific way to reduce the peak of the convolution.

For n=2, the optimal is [1/3, 2/3] (ratio 2:1). For larger n, the optimal shape might be a specific non-uniform profile.
