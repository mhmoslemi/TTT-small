Let me think about the BB (Barzilai-Borwein) step size:
- At iteration t, compute `s_t = x_t - x_{t-1}` and `y_t = g_t - g_{t-1}`

- BB- step: `alpha_t = <s_t, y_t> / <y_t, y_t>`
- Use BB+ when `<s_t, y_t> > 0`, BB- otherwise
