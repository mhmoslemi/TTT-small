2. Compute c = a*a
3. In the frequency domain: A = FFT(a), |A|² is the power spectrum

5. We want c to be as flat as possible. In the frequency domain, this means we want |A|² to be as "spread out" as possible (low peak in time domain of |A|²).
6. Adjust |A|² to reduce peaks in c: for each frequency bin w, reduce |A[w]|² where it contributes to peaks in c
7. Convert back: a_new = IDFT(A_new), clip negatives, renormalize
8. Repeat
