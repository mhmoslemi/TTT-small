For $a \geq 0$, $\sum a = 1$:
- $(a*a)_0 = a_0^2$ (just the first element squared)
- $(a*a)_{2n-2} = a_{n-1}^2$ (just the last element squared)
- $(a*a)_{n-1} = \sum_i a_i a_{n-1-i}$ (the "center" term)
- In general, $(a*a)_k = \sum_i a_i a_{k-i}$ (with valid indices)

The max of the convolution is at some lag $k^*$. For the uniform sequence, $k^* = n-1$ (center) and the max is $1/n$.

To get a better score, we want to reduce the peak at the center while keeping the edges small. This means we want a sequence that is "spread out" in a way that the self-convolution is flatter.

One approach: make the sequence approximately periodic with a small period. If $a$ is periodic with period $p$ (where $p$ divides $n$), then the convolution will have a different shape.
