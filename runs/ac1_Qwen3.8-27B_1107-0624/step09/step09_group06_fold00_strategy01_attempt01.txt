**Adaptive Contraction**:
For any remaining time, the algorithm contracts the best solution found by oversampling and applying 
gradient clipping. This helps escape local minima and improves peak convolution values.

The key bottleneck is that any single optimization might not yield the global minimum. By using iterative 
scales and adaptive optimization, we better approximate the true minimum compared to fixed-n optimization.
