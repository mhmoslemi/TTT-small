- a[0]*a[n-1] small (center contribution from the pair (0, n-1))
- But we need sum a = 1, so some values must be large.

The optimal solution balances these. The large values should be in the "middle-left" region, where they contribute to the center (paired with small values on the right) but don't create large edges.
