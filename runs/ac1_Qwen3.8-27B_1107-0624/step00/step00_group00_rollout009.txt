The issue is that the constraint a >= 0 is binding for many components. So the KKT conditions are:

This means that at the optimum, a[k*-j] is constant for all j where a[j] > 0. In other words, a is constant on the "reflection" of its support through position k*/2.

So the optimal solution (for the problem of minimizing (a*a)[n-1] subject to sum(a) = S, a >= 0) is a symmetric sequence that's constant on its support.

If the support is the entire [0, n-1], then a is constant (uniform), giving F = 2.
