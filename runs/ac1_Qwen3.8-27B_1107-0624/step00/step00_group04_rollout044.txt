The LP approach from the reference:
1. Start with f, normalize so sum(f) = sqrt(2n).
2. Find M = ||f*f||_inf.
3. Solve LP: maximize sum(g) subject to g >= 0 and ||f*g||_inf <= M.
4. Rescale g to have sum = sqrt(2n).
5. Update f = (1-t)*f + t*g.
6. Repeat.

The key insight is that step 3 finds the "best" direction to move that doesn't increase the convolution peak (when crossed with the current f). This is a form of projected subgradient method.
