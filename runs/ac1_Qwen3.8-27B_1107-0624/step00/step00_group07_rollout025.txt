The key is: when we zero out position $j$, we reduce $(a*a)_k$ for all $k$ (since $a_j$ appears in $(a*a)_k$ for $k$ from $j$ to $j+n-1$). The reduction at position $k$ is $a_j \cdot a_{k-j} = 1 \cdot a_{k-j}$.

- $(a*a)_j$ by $a_j \cdot a_0 = 1$
- $(a*a)_{j+1}$ by $a_j \cdot a_1 = 1$

- $(a*a)_{j+n-1}$ by $a_j \cdot a_{n-1} = 1$
