Actually, each constraint $(a*a)_k \leq t$ is a quadratic constraint. The standard form for conic optimization would be:
- Linear objective: minimize $t$
- Convex quadratic constraints: $a^T B_k a \leq t$ for all $k$
- Linear constraints: $a \geq 0$, $\sum a = 1$

This can be solved with a general convex optimizer. However, for $n = 6121$, the number of constraints is about 12000, which might be too many for most solvers.

Alternative: use a first-order method like projected subgradient, or a cutting-plane method.
