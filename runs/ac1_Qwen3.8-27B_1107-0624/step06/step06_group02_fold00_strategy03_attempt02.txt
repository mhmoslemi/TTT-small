### Approach and Rationale
We are tasked with finding a sequence of non-negative coefficients that minimizes the spectral metric defined by the evaluator. The metric forms a ratio of the maximum normalized convolution value to the square of the sum of the sequence's elements. 

Our key insight is that sequences with larger attacksurface (i.e., longer length) tend to have flatter convolutions, but direct optimization over large lengthnsequences is computationally expensive per evaluation. Therefore, we will:

### Mathematical Specification
We aim to minimize the spectral metric:
$$ score(a) = \frac{2n \cdot \|a * a\|_\infty}{\left(\sum ja_j\right)^2} $$
subject to:
- Each a_j ≥ 0
- ∑ ja_j ≥ 0.01
