OK so the issue is that for non-symmetric sequences, the peak of the convolution can be much larger than at the center. The linear ramp works because the peak stays at the center (or close to it).

Let me reconsider. For the linear ramp a[i] = i+1:
- The sequence is increasing
- The convolution at index k (for k near n-1) involves products of large and small values
- The peak is at the center because the "balance" of large*small products is maximized there

For the quadratic ramp, the values grow too fast, so the products of two large values (near the right end) dominate.

So the key is to find a shape where:
1. The mass is spread out (to reduce the peak)
2. The growth is not too fast (to prevent the peak from shifting to a position where large*large products dominate)
