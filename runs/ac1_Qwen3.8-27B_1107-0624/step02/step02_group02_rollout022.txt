But this bound is loose. A better bound comes from the fact that for non-negative sequences, the convolution is unimodal (or at least, it increases then decreases). Actually, that's not true for general non-negative sequences, but for symmetric ones it is.

For a symmetric non-negative sequence, the convolution is also symmetric and unimodal, with the peak at the center. The peak value is `sum_j a[j]^2 = ||a||^2`. By Cauchy-Schwarz, `||a||^2 ≥ S^2/n`, giving score ≥ `2n * S^2/n / S^2 = 2`. So for symmetric sequences, the score is at least 2!
