    candidates = np.where(c >= threshold)[0]

So concentrating at the edges makes the center peak (position n-1) dominant. The center peak is sum_i a[i]*a[n-1-i], which for a symmetric sequence is sum_i a[i]^2. By Cauchy-Schwarz, this is >= (sum(a))^2/n = 1/n for sum = 1.

So for a symmetric sequence, the center of the convolution is at least 1/n, giving objective >= 2n*(1/n)/1 = 2.

This confirms: for symmetric sequences, the minimum is 2 (achieved by uniform).

To get below 2, we NEED an asymmetric sequence! The center peak sum_i a[i]*a[n-1-i] can be less than sum_i a[i]^2 for asymmetric sequences.
