Actually, the key insight is: for a non-negative sequence, the max of the convolution is at least as large as max(x[i]^2) (the corners), and also at least as large as the center value sum x[i]*x[n-1-i].

To minimize the max, we want:
1. All x[i] to be small (to minimize corners): max x[i]^2 should be small.
2. The center to be small: sum x[i]*x[n-1-i] should be small.
3. The sum to be large.

These are somewhat in tension. If all x[i] are equal (uniform), the corners and center are both n*c^2, and the score is exactly 2.

To beat 2, we need to "shift" mass so that the center is reduced while the corners don't increase too much. The [1,...,1,a] structure does this: the corner a^2 might be an issue, but for a up to ~50 (for n=2535), a^2 < center value.
