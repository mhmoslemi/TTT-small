I think 1.5 might be close to optimal for two-level sequences, but a more complex structure might do better.

One idea: use a sequence that's high at the very edges and low in the middle, like a "U-shape". This would minimize the center convolution (since the center involves products of edge-mid and mid-edge values, which would be small) while keeping the sum large.
