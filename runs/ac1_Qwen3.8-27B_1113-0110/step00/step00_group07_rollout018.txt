For the convolution to be flat, we need all lags to have approximately the same value. The center lag has value `||a||_2^2` (for symmetric a). The edge lags have values `a[0]^2` and `a[n-1]^2`.

For flatness, we'd want `a[0]^2 ≈ ||a||_2^2`, which means `a[0] ≈ ||a||_2`, which means all the mass is at position 0. That's the opposite of what we want!
