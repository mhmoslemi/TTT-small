### 1. Mathematical reformulation

For a discrete vector `h∈[0,1]^N` with step size `dx = 2/N` the overlap integral for a shift `k` (integer index) is  

```
I(k) = ∫ h(x)(1–h(x+k))dx = 1 – C(k) ,   C(k) = ∑_{i=0}^{N-1} h[i]·h[i+k]·dx .
```

With zero‑padding outside `[0,N‑1]`, the full correlation `C(k)` is the linear convolution of `h` with itself.  
The objective is  

```
minimise   T   subject to    T ≥ I(k)  for all k = –(N‑1)…(N‑1) .
```

Equivalently we minimise `T = 1 – min_k C(k)`.

#### 2.2 Linearisation of the quadratic term  
Introduce binary auxiliary variables  

```
y[i,k]  ∈ {0,1}    for each i∈[0,N‑1] and shift index s = k-(N‑1) ∈ [-(N‑1), …, N‑1] .

`y[i,k]` is intended to represent the product `h[i]·h[i+s]`.  Enforce the McCormick‐type constraints

```

If `i+s` lies outside `[0,N‑1]` set `y[i,k]=0` directly (no variables needed).

The objective is `minimise T`.
