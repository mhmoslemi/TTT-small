For the gradient, the key operation is:
for each lag k (with weight alpha[k]):

  - Subtract alpha[k] * h[j+d] from grad[j] for j in range(0, N-d)

grad[d:N] += alpha[k] * (1 - h[0:N-d])
grad[0:N-d] -= alpha[k] * h[d:N]

For negative lags (d < 0, dp = -d):
grad[0:N-dp] += alpha[k] * (1 - h[dp:N])
grad[dp:N] -= alpha[k] * h[0:N-dp]

This is already vectorized (numpy array slicing and addition). The loop over k is the only sequential part.
