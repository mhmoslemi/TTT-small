    For each lag ell, the gradient of C_ell w.r.t. h is:
    - ell=0: (1-2*h) * dx at all positions

    """Project v onto {0 <= x <= 1, sum(x) = T}."""
    n = len(v)

        if total_room > 0:
            scale = min(1.0, diff / total_room)

        if total_avail > 0:
