The minimum-overlap objective for sampled step functions is nonconvex because the evaluator is a maximum of shifted products h_i(1-h_j). Starting from the supplied construction (if present), minimize a stable log-sum-exp surrogate of the exact discrete overlap vector, then accept candidate h vectors only if they reduce the exact evaluator.
