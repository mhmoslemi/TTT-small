1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea.  
The returned construction is judged by the original finite-support maximum correlation, not by any surrogate. The search is therefore built as an exact-feasibility, exact-acceptance hybrid. The primary novelty is not a generic smooth-gradient or single-trust-region refinement, but a **structured low-discrepancy exchange search**:

- feasible seeds are generated by balanced “rank-fill” constructions from many deterministic low-discrepancy/rotation orderings, followed by seeded random scolled and parent-derived perturbations;
- states are then refined by an exchange/annealing metasolver that only uses nonzero-sum-preserving pair or rotation moves;
- every promising move is accepted for the record only after an exact recomputation of the evaluator objective and after a joint box-and-sum feasibility repair;
- a parent-valued feasible candidate is retained as an exact fallback at all times.
