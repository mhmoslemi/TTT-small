1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central algorithmic idea

Use an exact-evaluator guarded block-coordinate descent along mass-preserving coordinate-pair and small coordinate-block feasible directions. At every accepted move the vector is decided solely by the original correlation checker, not by a smoothed objective or a tangent model. The main search is a deterministic and randomized coordinated-pass descent on the discrete feasible polytope.

At a vector h, choose coordinate pairs (i, i_j). Keep the sum h_i + h_j constant while moving along the entire feasible transfer segment. Sample and refine this one-dimensional segment using the original checker, correcting all candidates to box-plus-sum feasibility before acceptance. The accepted state is then selected by exact objective improvement. A priority field from a temporary active-lag gradient selects which pairs are most likely to reduce the maximum correlation. For escape from pure pair-transfer local minima, identical exact-evaluator guarded searches are run along selected triple-transfer blocks and along randomized transfer perturbations. A portfolio is run over the supplied parent and several compact, reproducible, in-basin multip-% of variants.

1.2 Why this can improve or reach the parent

The parent is extremely close to the target, so one probably needs a coordinated local adjustment while preserving the sum. A feasbi % deterministic descent over pairs is especially appropriate because the feasible polytope box-sum constraints admit only mass-preserving coordinate movements as its normal primitive movements. Minimization on all coordinate-pair lines transfers is a stronger version of keeping h feasible: every line evaluation remains nearly exact and selected improvements move along feasible edges or paired coordinates, avoiding rescaling-and-clipping feasibility distortions. The local descent accepts no linearized surrogate solution and only uses the true objective, so adversarial gradient approximation can cause prioritization, never accepted false solutions.

The plan is heuristic. It does not prove global optimality. It remains local and multi-start, but it is materially different from strategies centered wholly on smooth-maximum gradient steepest descent, epigraphic non-NUXXIL SLSQP, active linear programming cuts, or simple pair sample sweeps. It is best viewed as a precision transfer block coordinate minimizer with exact line/refinement and systematically prioritized feasible inputs, plus controlled perturbation and resolution steps only when the native block coordinate minimization stalls.
