    for k in active:
        d = k - (n - 1)
        g += grad_lag(h, d)

    # At the optimum, g should be in span{1} ∪ {e_i : h_i at boundary}
    free = (h > 1e-8) & (h < 1 - 1e-8)

Let me also think about the **direction projection**. The subgradient g should be projected onto the tangent space of the feasible set:

- For g_i > 0 (decreasing h_i): need h_i - α*g_i ≥ 0 → α ≤ h_i/g_i
- For g_i < 0 (increasing h_i): need h_i - α*g_i ≤ 1 → α ≤ (1-h_i)/(-g_i)
