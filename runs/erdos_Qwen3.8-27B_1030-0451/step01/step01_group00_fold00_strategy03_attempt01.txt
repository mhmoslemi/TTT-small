1.1 Central idea

The proposed solution is a stochastic multi-start, exact two-coordinate descent method. The new search operator is not a scalar soft-max gradient. At each feasible iterate, the algorithm repeatedly chooses an ordered transfer pair (source  s, destination  t) and considers the exact one-dimensional perturbation

h_s(ε) = h_s − ε,
h_t(ε) = h_t + ε,
ε ≥ 0.

This perturbation exactly preserves the integral constraint and it maintains bounds by restricting ε to an interval. For each chosen pair, the algorithm evaluates the exact evaluator on many ε samples and refines around the best sampled points, accepting the move only if the exact evaluator decreases
