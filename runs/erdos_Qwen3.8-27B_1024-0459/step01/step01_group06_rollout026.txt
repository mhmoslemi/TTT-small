Let me also think about the CVXPY approach. Even though the problem is non-convex, we can solve a sequence of convex relaxations:

Iteration:
1. Linearize c_k at current h^0: c_k(h) ≈ c_k(h^0) + ∇c_k(h^0)·(h - h^0)
2. Solve LP: min t s.t. c_k(h^0) + ∇c_k(h^0)·(h-h^0) ≤ t for all k, 0≤h≤1, sum h = n/2
3. Update h
4. Check convergence

This is the "outer approximation" or " cutting plane" method for non-convex problems. It's not guaranteed to find the global optimum, but it can be very effective.
