Each S(m; h) is linear in h[j] (since S(m) = sum_i h[i](1-h[i+m]), and h[j] appears linearly).

So C₅ as a function of h[j] is the max of several linear functions, which is convex and piecewise linear. The minimum of a convex piecewise linear function on [0,1] occurs either at a breakpoint or at a boundary.

To find the optimal h[j]:
1. For each lag m, compute the coefficient of h[j] in S(m) and the constant term
2. The function is max_m (a_m * h[j] + b_m)
3. Find the minimizer over h[j] ∈ [0,1]

This can be done by:
- Evaluating at h[j] = 0 and h[j] = 1
- Finding all breakpoints (where two linear functions cross)
- Evaluating at each breakpoint
- Taking the minimum

There are at most (2n-1) linear functions, so at most (2n-1)² breakpoints, but in practice much fewer.
