But actually, I can do better. The gradient of the smooth max is:
grad = alpha * dx * sum_k softmax_weight[k] * grad_c[k]

where grad_c[k] is the gradient of c[k] w.r.t. h. And c = correlate(h, 1-h, 'full').

The key: c[k] = sum_i h[i] * (1-h[i+m_k]) where m_k = k-(n-1)
grad_c[k][i] = (1-h[i+m_k]) - h[i-m_k] (with boundary conditions)

grad[i] = alpha * dx * sum_k sw[k] * ((1-h[i+m_k])*1{valid} - h[i-m_k]*1{valid})
= alpha * dx * (sum_k sw[k]*1{i+m_k valid} - sum_k sw[k]*h[i+m_k]*1{valid} - sum_k sw[k]*h[i-m_k]*1{valid})

The second term: sum_k sw[k]*h[i+k-(n-1)] for valid = cross-correlation of h with sw
The third term: sum_k sw[k]*h[i-k+(n-1)] for valid = cross-correlation of h with sw (or its reverse)

I can compute these using np.correlate or np.convolve in O(n log n) with FFT.
