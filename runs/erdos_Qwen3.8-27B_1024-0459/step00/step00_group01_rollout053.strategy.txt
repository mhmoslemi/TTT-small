### 1. Approach and Rationale

The approach adopted is centered around a **dual-evolution** optimization framework that combines global exploration and local refinement within a multiresolution environment. The solution leverages **score-driven evolution** with adaptive resampling and **pruning** based on estimated improvement margins.

**Core Idea:**  
We optimize the discrete step function representation across varying resolution levels (n_points) through a hierarchy of evolutionary strategies:
1. A **population-mode** adaptive CMA-ES that explores broad topographies
2. A **gradient-aware local optimizer** to exploit promising regions
3. **Cross-resolution search** where parent solutions from one resolution inform lower resolutions
4. **Overlap score decomposition** to prioritize updates with potential benefit

**Bottleneck Addressed:**  
The current method escapes local optima by:
- Maintaining a **sojourn memory** tracking recent objectives to avoid plateaus
- Using **approximate gradient directions** from shift-wise correlation decomposition to guide large jumps
- Dynamic resampling that favors low-overlap configurations

**Limitations/Hypotheses:**
- The multiresolution approach may miss globally optimal configurations but provides tighter bounds
- Score-driven evolution assumes overfitting to local shifts but has shown promise in similar combinatorial optimization problems

### 2. Mathematical Specification

**Representation:**  
Discrete step function h approximated by 2-D vector: [h_0, h_1, ..., h_{n-1}] ∈ ℝⁿ  
Integral constraint: ∑_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i dx = 1 with dx=2/n  
Bounds: 0 ≤ h_i ≤ 1 for all i

**Objective Function:**  
C₅ = (1/dx) × max_{k∈{-n+1,...,n-1}} ∑_{i=0}^{n-k-1} [h_i × (1 - h_{i+k})]  

**Notation:**  
dx = 2/n  
The full correlation array S has length 2n-1:  
S[k] = ∑_{i=0}^{n-1-k} [h_i × (1 - h_{i+k})] for k=0,1,...,n-1  
S[-k] is handled by symmetry: S[-k] = S[k] (overlap preserved by absolute shift)

**Operator Definitions:**  
1. **Score Correlation (ψ_k):** ψ_k = S[k] (unnormalized overlap magnitude)  
2. **Directional Mutation (∇ψ):**  
   ψ_k(h_i) = h_i(1-h_{i+k})  
   ∇ψ_k(h_i) = (1-h_{i+k}) - h_i  
   This is computed for top-scoring k to guide mutations

**Algorithm Output:**  
(h_values, c5_bound, n_points) where:
- h_values (1D array): Configured to satisfy integral and bounds
- c5_bound (float): Computed as max(k) [ (1/dx) S[k] ] for final config
- n_points (int): Chosen resolution
