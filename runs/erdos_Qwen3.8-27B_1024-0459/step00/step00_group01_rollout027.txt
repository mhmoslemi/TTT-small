Let s = m - (n+1) + 1 = m - n + 1. So:

And b_m = corr[m] (current value)

We want to minimize f(delta) over delta such that:
- h[i] + delta ∈ [0,1] → delta ∈ [-h[i], 1-h[i]]
- h[j] - delta ∈ [0,1] → delta ∈ [h[j]-1, h[j]]
- Combined: delta ∈ [max(-h[i], h[j]-1), min(1-h[i], h[j])]

Since f is convex (max of linear functions), we can find the minimum:
- Compute f at the endpoints
- Find where the subgradient contains 0

The subgradient of f at delta is the convex hull of {a_m : corr[m] + delta*a_m = f(delta)}.
