# Erdős Minimum Overlap Problem: Improved Upper Bound  

## 1. Approach and Rationale  
The Erdős minimum overlap problem requires finding a step function \( h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1] \) with integral 1 that minimizes the maximum correlation between \( h(x) \) and \( 1-h(x+k) \) for all shifts k. This is equivalent to minimizing the maximum area of overlap between shifted versions of h and its complement.  

The current record is \( C = 0.38085 \), and the parent solution at 47 points provides a starting point. The key bottleneck is escaping local optima in high-dimensional spaces (h represented as n points) by combining dimensionality reduction, population-based exploration, and convex relaxation techniques.  

We use a hierarchical approach:  
- **Resolution selection**: Experiment with n=47 and nearby points (50, 65) for flexibility.  
- **Population-based search**: Use covariance matrix adaptation evolution strategy (CMA-ES) to escape local optima.  
- **Convex relaxation**: After finding promising candidates, solve a convex relaxation (saddle-point problem) to verify improvements.  

This approach leverages:  
- Step function parametrization to reduce dimensionality.  
- Cost-effective black-box optimization.  
- Interpretability through analytic gradients.  
- The known record solution's structure (piecewise constant) to initialize searches.  

The main limitation is that convex relaxation may not always bind globally, but if it does, we get strict upper bounds. Population-based methods provide local improvements, and manual shifts/dimensionality changes ensure escape from narrow valleys.  

## 2. Mathematical Specification  
### Variables and Representation  
- \( h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1] \) represented as a step function with \( m \) segments (m=50 by default).  
- Internally, parametrize h's segments by boundaries \( x_i \) and heights \( h_i \), but store in vector form for numerical optimization.  
- \( n \) points for discretization (vary m/n ratio).  

### Constraints  
- duration: \( \int_0^2 h(x) dx = 1 \)  
- box: \( 0 \leq h(x) \leq 1 \)  
- Step function: \( h \) piecewise constant between \( x_i \).  

### Objective Function  
- Overlap integral maximized over all shifts:  
  \( C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x) \cdot (1 - h(x+k)) dx \)  

### Convex Relaxation  
After finding a non-convex candidate, solve:  
\[  
\min_{\rho(t), \mu(t)} \max_k \int_0^2 \int_0^2 \rho(t) e^{-t t'} K_{idx}(t, t', k) dt dt'  
\]  
subject to entropy constraints, where \( K \) encodes overlap for each shifted segment.  

### Notation Details  
- \( \rho(t) \): Bipartite measure  
- \( K_{idx}(t, t', k) \): Kernel matrix corresponding to shift k (indexed by segment endpoints)  
- The relaxation is a doubly non-negative program (DNN, can be approximated by semidefinite relaxation).
