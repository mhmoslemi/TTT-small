The following strategy presents a fundamentally different approach to the Erdős minimum overlap problem compared to the previously proposed methods. The core idea is to use a randomized rounding technique for solving a discrete resource allocation problem and a dimensionality reduction technique for optimization. This approach balances exploration and exploitation to find a better upper bound for the Erdős constant.

---


### 1. Approach and Rationale
**Central Idea**  
We model the problem as aperiodic resource allocation on a circle of circumference 2. The goal is to distribute mass (from the integral constraint) in a way that minimizes the maximum overlap with any shifted version. The key insight is that the optimal solution likely exhibits some periodic structure, but aperiodic distributions can capture more complex trade-offs between peak separation and height.

**Why Different from Previous Methods**  
Previous approaches mainly:
- Used simulating annealing/score-driven optimization on the continuous representation.
- Emphasized period with fixed frequency patterns.

Our approach breaks the problem by:
1. Representing the distribution as a high-dimensional binary choice (1 if allocated, 0 otherwise)
2. Using randomized rounding to project the continuous solution into discrete choices
3. Applying dimensionality reduction for efficient optimization
4. Balancing between exploration and exploitation with adaptive constraints

**Bottlenecks Addressed**  
- **Complex Constraint:** Ensured constraint enforcement via repairable basis projection  
- **Non-convex Objective:** Handled with probability-based constraints and stochastic search  
- **Feasibility:** Maintained exact integral and bounds through tailored repair  
- **Computational Cost:** Reduced problem dimensions with basis function selection

**Guidance Note:**  
"This plan is not a binding specification. You may correct, adapt, simplify, extend, or replace its components when justified by the task. Normalization doesn't carry correct rounding into existence. Use mathematical structure and evaluation before optimization."

### 2. Mathematical Specification
**Representation**  
Sample length: $n$ points  
Variables: Binary vector $\mathbf{b} \in \{0,1\}^{n}$, where $\sum_i b_i = N$ ($N = \lfloor n/2 \rfloor$ for even $n$)

**Objective**  
Minimize: $C_5 = \max_k \sum_{i} b_i (1 - b_{i+k})$

**Constraints**  
- $b_i \in \{0,1\}$ for all $i$  
- $\sum_i b_i = N$

**Dimensionality Reduction**  
Represent $\mathbf{b}$ via basis functions $W_j$:  
$\mathbf{b} = \sum_j \theta_j W_j$  
$\theta_j \in [0,1]$ undergo constrained optimization

**Repair Algorithm**  
- Gradient projection + coordinate-wise satisfaction:  
  $\theta_j = \min(\max(0,1),\theta_j)$  
  Normalize sum if $\epsilon$-far from constraint
