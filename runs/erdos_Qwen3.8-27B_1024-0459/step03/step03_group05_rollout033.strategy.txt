  Approach and rationale

The amplitude of this wave is optimized to minimize the maximum overlap. This approach is inspired by the fact that optimizing at is greater detail requires handling constraints and initializations properly.

Important limitations: The optimization is performed on a grid (sample points) to ensure computational feasibility. The solution relies on interpolation between different grid sizes and dynamic adjustment to maintain the integral constraint.
