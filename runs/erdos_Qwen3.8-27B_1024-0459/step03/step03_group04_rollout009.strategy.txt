   h_i = score(x_i) for x_i = (i+0.5)dx, dx=2/n

   Subject to:
   - 0 ≤ h_i ≤ 1 ∀i
   - Σ h_i dx = 1 ⇒ Σ h_i = n/2

   The aliasing error for shifts is controlled by:
   E(k) = ||h_|| / ||1-h_|| is bounded by |k|/n < 1/2 to prevent domain wrapping effects

   We parametrize the optimization vector in unconstrained form:
   z_i in ℝ with pmap h(z) = σ(z) / (σ(z) + τ(z)) for logistic projection

   The error penalty used is:
