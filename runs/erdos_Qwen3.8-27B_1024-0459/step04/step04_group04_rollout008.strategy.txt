   - Phase 1: Use a **population-based optimization** (particle swarm or genetic algorithm) for binary solutions at multiple grid resolutions (n=100, 300, 500, ..., 700) to find globally competitive candidates.  
   - Phase 2: **Double the grid resolution** (n = 2, 4, 8 times original) for the best candidate, interpolating and re-constraining to maintain integral and bounds.  
   - Phases 3-5: Refine solutions through constrained nonlinear programming (NLP) with specialized solvers, leveraging smooth approximations to balance exploration and exploitation.  

   This approach reduces dependence on initial points and systematically explores higher-resolution grids, increasing the chance of finding exact or near-exact minima.
