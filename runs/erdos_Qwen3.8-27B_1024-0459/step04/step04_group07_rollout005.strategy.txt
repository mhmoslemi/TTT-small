### 2. Mathematical Specification

**Representation:**
- Time domain: $h \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $0 \le h[i] \le 1$ and $\sum h[i] / n = 1$ (integral constraint)
- Frequency domain: $H = \mathcal{F}(h)$, where $\mathcal{F}(\cdot)$ denotes the DFT with toroidal boundary conditions
- Enhanced representation: Banded spectral decomposition with parameterized truncation

**Objective Function:**
$$
\mathcal{J}(h) = \min_{ω \in \{-1,0,1\}^n} \max_{k} \left\{ \mathrm{Re}\left(\mathrm{II}_\LARGEω(h(x),1-h(x+k))) \right\}
$$
where:
- $ω$ is the lag vector focusing function
- $\mathrm{II}_\LARGEω(\cdot,\cdot)$ is the filtered inner product

**Constraints:**
- $0 \le h[i] \le 1$ for all $i$
- $\frac{1}{n}\sum h[i] = 1$ (integral constraint)
- Spectral smoothness through $L_1$ regularization on $|H^*_k|$ for $k \notin [-β,β]$

**Spectral Project:**
$$
h ← \mathcal{F}^{-1}\left( \lambda \cdot |\mathcal{F}\{h\}|_+ \right)
$$
followed by integral constraint projection.
