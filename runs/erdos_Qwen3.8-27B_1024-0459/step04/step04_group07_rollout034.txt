    Let me also add a "Nesterov-accelerated" variant of the log-sum-exp minimization. The idea is:
    - Maintain two iterates: x (current) and y (extrapolated).
    - y = x + (t_{k-1}-1)/(t_k+1) * (x - x_prev)
    - x = project(y - α * ∇f(y))
    - t_k = (1+√(1+4t_{k-1}²))/2
