   C₅ = max_{k} ℱ h(x)(1-h(x+k))

   ∫₀² h(x) dx = (2/n) * ∑_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i] = 1, implying ∑_{i} h[i] = n/2.

   The objective function is computed via:
   L = np.max(np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full')) * (2/n)

   The optimization proceeds by viewing grid resolution n and iteration g, maintaining state s_g ∈ ℝ^{d*(n/max_n)} where d is intrinsic dimension.
