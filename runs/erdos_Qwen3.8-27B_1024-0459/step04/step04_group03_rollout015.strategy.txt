**1. Approach and rationale**

Instead of using multi-start methods from scratch or scaling the parent solution, we perform a coordinate-wise optimization that adjusts one variable at a time while keeping others fixed. This method:

- Maintains feasibility through direct clipping
- Does not require expensive global optimizers
- Can be implemented at any resolution
- Uses gradient information from the entire system

The key innovation is the diagonalization of the optimization problem: rather than optimizing all variables simultaneously (which requires second-order information), we optimize one coordinate at a time while maintaining global feasible points. This avoids local optima more effectively than standard gradient methods while being more efficient than multi-start methods.

  - The objective is minimized
