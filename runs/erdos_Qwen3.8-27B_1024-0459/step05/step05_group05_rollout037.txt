For the ±1 representation: x[i] ∈ {+1, -1}, sum x = 0 (balanced).
The autocorrelation R(k) = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-|k|} x[i] * x[i+|k|].
The "broad-sense" autocorrelation (dividing by n-|k|) should be close to 0 for good sequences.

The relationship to our corr:
corr(k) = sum h[i+k](1-h[i]) for k > 0
= sum ((x[i+k]+1)/2) * (1-(x[i]+1)/2)
= sum ((x[i+k]+1)/2) * ((1-x[i])/2)
= 1/4 * sum (x[i+k]+1)(1-x[i])
= 1/4 * sum (x[i+k] - x[i+k]x[i] + 1 - x[i])
= 1/4 * (sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k} x[i+k] - R(k) + (n-k) - sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k} x[i])

For a balanced sequence (sum x = 0), the partial sums sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k} x[i] and sum_{i=k}^{n-1} x[i+k] are not zero in general. So the relationship is complicated.
