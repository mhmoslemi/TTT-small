Now, what's the correlation of the Thue-Morse sequence? The Thue-Morse sequence has the property that its autocorrelation is bounded by O(√n) (it's a "Sturmian-like" sequence with sublinear autocorrelation). Specifically, |Σ_{i=0}^{n-1-k} s[i]s[i+k]| ≤ C√n for all k, where s = ±1.

corr[d] ≈ (n-|d|)/4 - autocorr[d]/4

def build_V(h, n):

    d = np.arange(-(n-1), n, dtype=int)

        idx_neg = p - d
        mask_neg = (idx_neg >= 0) & (idx_neg < n)
        V[p, mask_neg] += h[idx_neg[mask_neg]].astype(np.int8)
