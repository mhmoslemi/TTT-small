- For each k, the term where p + offset = j, i.e., p = j - offset: was h[j-offset]*(1-0) = h[j-offset]
  Now (1-h[j]) changes from 1 to 0. So corr[k] decreases by h[j-offset].

So the total change in corr[k] when swapping i (1→0) and j (0→1):
Δcorr[k] = -(1-h[i+offset_k]) + h[i-offset_k] + (1-h[j+offset_k]) - h[j-offset_k]

where offset_k = k - (N-1), and we only include terms where the indices are valid.

        for idx, k in enumerate(lags):
