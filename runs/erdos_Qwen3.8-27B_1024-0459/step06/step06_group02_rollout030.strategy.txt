1. Approach and Rationale

The Central Idea:
Our approach combines several key innovations to escape local optima:

1. Multi-resolution Progressive Optimization (MRPO): The algorithm starts with low resolution (n=100) and successively refines to higher resolutions (n=2048), leveraging dimensionality reduction early while building structure.

2. Sample-Optimized Sparsity: We explicitly incorporate a sparsity parameter γ (default 0.7) that controls the "feature-richness" of candidate solutions. This allows interleaving between sparse representations (few 0/1-like features) and dense solutions.

3. Trust Region Adaptation with Target Enhancement (TRATE): A state-of-the-art gradient-based technique with two novel components:
   - Asynchronous adaptive trust region radius calculation based on last 100 function improvements
   - Dynamic objective weighting that de-emphasizes shallow extrema
   - Harmonic cold Restarting (Synthesized from Bayesian annealing but with continuous restart risk assessment)

This differs from traditional SLSQP by addressing the core optimization bottlenecks: (i) gradient noise is filtered in two steps (auto-differentiation plus gradient clipping), (ii) the max_k constraint is replaced with a smooth exponential weighting for differentiability, (iii) memory usage is controlled by caching only last 10 gradients for curvature estimation.

Bottleneck Addressed:
- Local convergence: TRATE with adaptive restarts escapes plateaus
- Computational viability: Multi-resolution starts from faster-to-converge low-res problems
- Feature learning: Progressive resolution hinting at structure emergence
