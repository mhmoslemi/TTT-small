For β = 500-5000 and 2n-1 shifts, the softmax will be concentrated on maybe 5-20 shifts (depending on β and the spread of corr values). So using K=50 should be a good approximation.

But there's a subtlety: if β is small, the weights are more spread out, and K=50 might not be enough. For β=50, the effective number of active shifts could be 50-200. Let me use K=100 for safety, or adapt K based on β.

Actually, for the gradient to be correct, I need ALL weights. But computing 2n-1 gradients is O(n²) which is too slow for n=4096.

These are cross-correlations of h with W_pos!
