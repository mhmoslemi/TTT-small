Now let me think about the pair swap move. For a pair swap (p1, p2: 1→0, q1, q2: 0→1):

So C[k] = (number of 1s in valid range) - Σ_i h[i]*h[i+d]

The change in C[k] is just the negative change in Σ_i h[i]*h[i+d].

For a swap of p(1→0) and q(0→1):
Δ(Σ_i h[i]*h[i+d]) = change due to p + change due to q
- Due to p (1→0): the terms involving h[p] in the product h[i]*h[i+d] are:
