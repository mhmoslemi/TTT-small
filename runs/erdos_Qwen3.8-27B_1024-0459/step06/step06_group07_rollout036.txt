numpy.correlate(a, v, mode='full'):
The full correlation is defined as:
c[k] = Σ_n a[n+k] * conj(v[n])

where the sum is over n such that both a[n+k] and v[n] are defined.

For a of length na and v of length nv, the output has length na+nv-1.
The index k in the output ranges from 0 to na+nv-2.
The "lag" is k - (nv-1), ranging from -(nv-1) to na-1.

So for a=h (length n) and v=1-h (length n):
output[j] = Σ_i a[i + (j-(n-1))] * v[i]  where both indices are valid.
