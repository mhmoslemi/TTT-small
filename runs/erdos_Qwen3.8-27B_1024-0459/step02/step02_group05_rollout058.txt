This is equivalent to: minimize the maximum "disagreement" between h and its shifted version, weighted by h itself.

For the discrete version with n points:
- We have n values in [0,1] summing to n/2
- C5 = (2/n) * max_k corr_full[k]
- where corr_full is np.correlate(h, 1-h, 'full')

At k=0 (shift 0, which corresponds to the center of the full correlation):

For sum(h) = n/2:
corr_full[n-1] = n/2 - Σ h[i]²

By Cauchy-Schwarz: Σ h[i]² ≥ (Σ h[i])²/n = (n/2)²/n = n/4
