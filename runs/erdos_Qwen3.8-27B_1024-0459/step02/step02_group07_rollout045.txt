We want to maximize min_k R(k) where R(k) = ∫ h(x)h(x+k) dx.

This is a non-convex optimization (R(k) is quadratic in h). But if we fix the "shape" of h (e.g., as a linear combination of basis functions), it becomes a finite-dimensional non-convex problem.

R(k) = sum_{m,n} a_m a_n ∫ φ_m(x) φ_n(x+k) dx

This is a quadratic form in a, and we want to maximize the minimum eigenvalue of this quadratic form (subject to constraints). Still non-convex, but the dimension M might be small.
