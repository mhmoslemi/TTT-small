<strategy>1. Approach and Rationale:
The central idea is to use **trigonometric optimization with constrained Fourier coefficients** to build a piecewise-constant function that minimizes the maximum overlap. The approach leverages the observation that optimal solutions often exhibit periodic structure, which can be captured efficiently with trigonometric expansions.
