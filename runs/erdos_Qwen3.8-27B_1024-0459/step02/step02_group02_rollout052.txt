For a binary sequence with sum = N/2:
R(h, 1)[k] = number of 1s in positions i where i and i+k are valid = N - |k| (approximately)
R(h, h)[k] = number of i where h[i]=1 and h[i+k]=1

So R(h, 1-h)[k] = (N - |k|) - R(h, h)[k]

We want to minimize max_k R(h, 1-h)[k] = max_k [(N-|k|) - R(h,h)[k]]
