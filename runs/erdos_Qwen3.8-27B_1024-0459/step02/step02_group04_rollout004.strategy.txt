### 2. Mathematical Specification

**Representation**:
- Let \( h: [0, 2] \to \mathbb{R} \) such that \( 0 \leq h(x) \leq 1 \) and \( \int_0^2 h(x)  dx = 1 \).
- Discretization: \( h_i \) values at \( n \) points, with \( x_i = 2i/n \) for \( i=0,\ldots,n-1 \).
- Gradient projection to enforce integral constraints.

**Objective**:
- \( C_5^* = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x) (1 - h(x+k))  dx \)
- Discrete computation: 
  \[
  C_5 = \max_{k \in \{-m,\ldots,m\}} \| h \circ (1-\tilde{h}) \|_1 \cdot \frac{2}{n}
  \]
- Where \( \tilde{h} \) is \( h \) shifted by \( k \) (zero-padded outside domain).

**Equality Constraint**: 
- \( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i = n/2 \) (enforced via projection).
- Inequality constraints: \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \).

**Optimization Formulation**:
- **Variables**: \( h \in \mathbb{R}^n \).
- **Objective**: Minimize \( C_5 \).
- **Constraints**: \( \sum h_i = n/2 \), \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \).

**Gradient Calculation**:
- For lag \( L \), 
  \[
  g_L(i) = 
  \begin{cases} 
  1 - h_{i-L} & \text{if } i + L = i \\
  h_i & \text{if } i - L = i 
  \end{cases}
  \]
- Directional derivative: \( \partial_k C = \|\nabla_k h\|_2 \) over active lags.

**Note**: The gradient must account for integral constraints \( \sum_i h_i = n/2 \), projected back via a penalty term \( \lambda (\sum h_i - n/2)^2 \).
