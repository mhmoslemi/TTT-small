Approach and rationale\n\nUse a derivative-free, feasible population search: projected differential evolution (DE) ranks every proposal by the exact finite-support C₅ evaluator, followed by pairwise exchange pattern search for local refinement. This is materially different from an epigraph nonlinear-program solver or projected descent on a smoothed maximum: the program uses neither a lag-gradient matrix nor a surrogate objective, and handles the nonsmooth maximum directly as a black-box score. DE supplies diverse basin exploration; pairwise exchanges make small, sum-preserving changes near promising candidates.\n\nThe correlation identities, capped-simplex projection, and feasibility of the pairwise moves are mathematical facts. The expectation that population diversity and local exchanges may improve the supplied parent is a heuristic, not a guarantee. The objective is generally nonconvex, so this method does not certify a global optimum or guarantee the target score.
