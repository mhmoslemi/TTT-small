Approach and rationale\\nUse feasible pairwise mass transfers to optimize the original, nonsmooth maximum directly. Only the zero lag and the two lags at the selected pair’s separation have nonzero quadratic coefficients; all other lag values are affine. Build the upper envelope of those affine functions, then minimize the maximum of that envelope and the three remaining quadratic functions by checking its finite set of endpoints, intersections, and stationary points. Accept a move only after scoring it with the exact finite-support evaluator.\\n\\nThis is materially different from an epigraph SLSQP search or a log-sum-exp projected-gradient search: it uses neither a general constrained solver nor a smoothed maximum, and it performs exact one-dimensional minimization of the original objective along each selected pair direction.
