Let me also double-check: `np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full')` - what does this compute?

For arrays a and b of lengths n and n, `np.correlate(a, b, mode='full')` computes:
c[k] = sum_i a[i] * b[i + k - (n-1)] for k = 0, ..., 2n-2

So c[k] = sum_{i=max(0, k-(n-1))}^{min(n-1, k)} a[i] * b[i + k - (n-1)]

With a = h and b = 1-h:
c[k] = sum_i h[i] * (1-h[i+k-(n-1)])

For k = n-1: c[n-1] = sum_i h[i]*(1-h[i]) (this is the "lag 0" term)
For k = n-1+s (s=1,...,n-1): c[n-1+s] = sum_{i=s}^{n-1} h[i]*(1-h[i-s]) = sum_{j=0}^{n-1-s} h[j+s]*(1-h[j])
For k = n-1-s (s=1,...,n-1): c[n-1-s] = sum_{i=0}^{n-1-s} h[i]*(1-h[i+s])

So the 2n-1 values cover all the terms we need. The max of these times dx gives C5. This matches the problem statement exactly.
