1. Approach and Rationale

The core objective is to minimize the maximum overlap integral $C_5$ for step functions on [0,2] with integral 1 and values in [0,1]. We build upon the initial 45-point solution by:

- Exploiting the connection between periodicity and symmetry through spectral decomposition
- Employing a multi-resolution optimization strategy that transfers solutions between grids
- Combining global structure search with local optimization to escape local minima

The key innovations are:
- Using a mixed-integer spectral representation controlled by carefully parameterized harmonic components
- Progressive grid refinement up to 360 points while preserving fundamental properties

The approach directly addresses the challenge of finding balanced solutions across all time shifts using constrained spectral optimization and periodic boundary conditions.

2. Mathematical Specification

We represent the candidate function $h(x)$ on $n$ grid points with spacing $dx=2/n$. The optimization variables are coefficients $c_k$ in Fourier series:

$$h(x) = \sum_{k=-m}^m c_k e^{2\pi i k x}$$

with $n$-fold periodicity. The integral constraint becomes:

$$ \int_0^2 h(x) dx = \sum_{k=-m}^m c_k \frac{\sin(2\pi k)}{\pi k} = 1 $$

However, for practical implementation, we use:

- Bounded trigonometric polynomials with $c_k$ constrained by $0 \leq h(x) \leq 1$
- Frequencies up to $m=\lfloor \frac{n}{4} \rfloor$ to ensure $C^2$ smoothness at roots
- Optimization of low-frequency components to preserve boundary conditions

The objective function is:
$$ \min_{h} \max_{k} \int_0^2 h(x) [1-h(x+k)] dx $$

Implemented via:
1. Full grid convolution to compute all shifts ($O(n^2)$)
2. Normalization by $dx$: $\max_k [\text{corr}_k] \cdot dx$

The constraints are enforced through:
- Sum constraint: $\sum_{i} h[i] = n/2$ (exact)
- Value bounds: $0\le h[i] \le 1$ enforced via projection

Gradient information is available through automatic differentiation in the implementation.
