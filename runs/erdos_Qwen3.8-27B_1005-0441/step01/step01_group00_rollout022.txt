The Lagrangian is: L = t + Σ_k λ_k (c_k - t) = (1-Σλ_k)t + Σ_k λ_k c_k

At the optimum, λ_k > 0 only for active constraints (c_k = t), and Σ λ_k = 1.

For fixed λ, the inner problem is:
min_h Σ_k λ_k c_k subject to 0≤h≤1, Σh=n/2

And Σ_k λ_k c_k = Σ_k λ_k Σ_i h[i+m](1-h[i]) for appropriate m

This is a quadratic in h but with a specific structure. Let me compute it:

Σ_k λ_k c_k = Σ_{m} λ_{n-1+m} Σ_{i: valid} h[i+m](1-h[i])

Let me define: for each pair (i, j) with j = i+m, the term is λ_{n-1+m} * h[j](1-h[i])

This is a quadratic form in h. Specifically:
Q(h) = Σ_{m} λ_m' Σ_i h[i+m](1-h[i]) = Σ_m λ_m' (Σ_i h[i+m] - Σ_i h[i+m]h[i])

The first part is linear in h, the second part is a bilinear form.
