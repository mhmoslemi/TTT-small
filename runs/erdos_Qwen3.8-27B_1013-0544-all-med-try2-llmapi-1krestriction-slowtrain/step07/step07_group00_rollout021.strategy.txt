We parameterize the step function `h` using a Fourier series:
$$ h(x) = \sum_{k=1}^{M} a_k \cos(k \pi x) + b_k \sin(k \pi x) + c $$
where:
- `c` is the mean
- `(a_k, b_k)` are coefficients to train
- `M` is the number of harmonics

The constraints are:
- **Bounds**: $0 \le h(x) \le 1$ for all $x$
- **Integral**: $\int_0^^2 h(x) dx = 1$

The objective is:
$$ \min \max_{\tau} \left| \int_0^2 h(x) \cdot (1 - h(x+\tau)) dx \right| $$

$$ f(\theta) = \frac{1}{\alpha} \left( \log \left( \sum_{\tau} \exp(\alpha (\overline{I(\tau)})) \right) \right) $$
