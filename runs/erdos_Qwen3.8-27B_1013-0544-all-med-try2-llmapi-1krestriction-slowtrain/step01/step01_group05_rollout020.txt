1. Initialize p = uniform over k

f_k(h) = Σ_i h[i+k](1-h[i]) = Σ_i h[i+k] - Σ_i h[i+k]h[i]

Σ_k p_k * f_k(h) = Σ_k p_k * (Σ_i h[i+k] - Σ_i h[i+k]h[i])
= Σ_k p_k * Σ_i h[i+k] - Σ_k p_k * Σ_i h[i+k]h[i]
= Σ_j (Σ_{k: j-i=k for some i} p_k) * h[j] - Σ_k p_k * Σ_i h[i+k]h[i]
