3. **Extensive pair search** with decreasing step sizes (remaining time)

The pair search is the key. Let me make it as efficient as possible:
- Precompute the correlation once
- For each candidate move, only recompute the affected entries of the correlation
- This reduces each c5 evaluation from O(n²) to O(n) (only the shifts affected by changing h[i] and h[j] need updating)

Wait, that's a great optimization! If we change h[i] by δ and h[j] by -δ, then:
- Only shifts k where i or j is in the valid range are affected
- The number of such shifts is at most 2n (all shifts where i or j appears)
- Updating those shifts takes O(1) per shift (just add/subtract the contribution)

So instead of recomputing the full correlation (O(n²)), we can update it in O(n) time per candidate move!
