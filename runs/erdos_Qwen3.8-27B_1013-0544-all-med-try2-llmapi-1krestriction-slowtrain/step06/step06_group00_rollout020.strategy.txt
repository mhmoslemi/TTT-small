### Mathematical Specification  
The problem is to minimize  
\[
C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x) (1 - h(x + k))  dx
\]  
subject to  
\[
\int_0^2 h(x)  dx = 1, \quad 0 \leq t \leq 1 \text{ for } t \in h.
\]  
Discretized as  
\[
h \in \mathbb{R}^n, \quad \sum h_i \cdot \frac{2}{n} = 1, \quad 0 \leq h_i \leq 1.
\]  
The objective is approximated by  
\[
C_5^{\text{est}} = \beta \cdot (\max_k |\text{corr}_k| + 1),
\]  
where \(\text{corr}_k\) is the cross-correlation between \(h\) and \(1 - h\) at lag \(k\).  

Constraints are central:  
- **Feasibility**: Use projection projection(h) = clip/clamp values and adjust integral via weighted shifts.  
- **Objective smoothness**: Add quadratic penalty \( \frac{\alpha}{2} \sum_i (h_i - 0.5)^2 \) to gradient calculations.  

**Solver specifications**:  
- **Continuous phase**: Use SLSQP with analytic gradients.
