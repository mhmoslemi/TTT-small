The central idea is to use a transformation approach inspired by recent advances in discrepancy theory and harmonic analysis to directly construct candidates with improved overlap properties. Instead of optimizing an empirical approximation of the overlap integral, we seek to enforce key structural properties of optimal solutions through an analytical construction. This approach addresses the limitations of heuristic optimization by:

- Leveraging the convolutional nature of the overlap integral
- Transforming the problem into a multiple constrained optimization
- Using harmonic analysis to enforce symmetry and frequency constraints
- Constructing candidates directly in Fourier space

The method differs substantially from gradient-based optimization by employing a principled analytical transformation rather than iterative refinement. The construction maintains mathematical consistency without relying on runtime optimizations, making it both faster and more reliable. The key limitation is that this transformation may not capture all pathologies of the discrete problem.
