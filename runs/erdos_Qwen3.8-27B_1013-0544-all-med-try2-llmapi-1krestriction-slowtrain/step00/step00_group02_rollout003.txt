The "full" mode output is indexed from 0 to 2n-2, where index i corresponds to k = i - (n-1).

- Index n-1+k for k>0: overlap of h shifted right by k with (1-h)
- Index n-1-k for k>0: overlap of (1-h) shifted right by k with h
