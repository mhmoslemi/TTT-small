      I_k ≈ Δx/2 · sum_{i=1}^{m} h(x_i) · (1 - h(x_i+k·Δx))

The objective measures the peak correlation between h and its shifted versions.

Feasibility constraints:
  0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 for all i
  sum(h) = m/2

  - GP regression (Scikit-Optimize) for global optimization
  - Nelder-Mead (SciPy) for local refinement
