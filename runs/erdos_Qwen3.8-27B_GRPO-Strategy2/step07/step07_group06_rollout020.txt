Actually, the key insight is that for a (i,j) move, the effect on the correlation vector is:
- Linear term: can be computed as the difference of two "position gradients"
- Quadratic term: at most 2 non-zero entries

The previous code's `_grad_at_pos` computes the gradient for a single position. The difference gives the linear term. The quadratic terms are at k1 = n-1+(j-i) and k2 = n-1-(j-i).
