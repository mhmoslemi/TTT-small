Our approach treats the overlap minimization as a two-point correlation problem on the torus. We introduce a low-dimensional coordinate system based on spherical harmonics, which captures the translation invariance property central to the problem. This harmonic representation provides both convexity guarantees for certain formulations and natural regularization through smoothness constraints.

The central innovation is a transformation from the time/space domain to harmonic frequencies, decomposing the optimization into independent mode coefficients.
