This new approach introduces a different paradigm by generating all possible configurations of a fixed number of identical unit-width intervals positioned at grid points. The rationale is that the minimum overlap occurs for configurations with consistent spacing, and this uniform sampling guarantees a thorough exploration of interval configurations without overly detailed or time-consuming neighborhood searches.
