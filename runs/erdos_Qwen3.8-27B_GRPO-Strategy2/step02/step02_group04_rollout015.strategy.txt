## 1. Approach and Rationale

The Erdős minimum overlap problem seeks to find a step function h(x) on [0,2] with integral 1 that minimizes:
C5(max_k ∫h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx

Despite the high-dimensional (70-dimensional) discrete nature of the problem, we will use increasingly refined convex relaxations and adaptive sampling to escape local optima. The core innovations are:

This hierarchical structure ensures both exploration breadth and exploitation precision.
