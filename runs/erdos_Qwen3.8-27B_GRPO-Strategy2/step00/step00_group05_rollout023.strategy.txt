## 1. Approach and Rationale

The central idea is to use a **gradient-based optimization** combined with a **stochastic search** to explore the space of step functions. We aim to minimize the overlap integral \( C_5 \) by **locally refining candidate solutions** starting from the parent structure and supplementing with **random patterns**. This approach balances global exploration through randomization with local exploitation via gradient descent. Unlike the previous genetic algorithm strategy, we focus on deterministic optimization to avoid local optima.

The key bottleneck addressed is the highly non-convex nature of the objective function. We mitigate this by:
- Initializing from the parent solution's structure
- Injecting randomness through Poisson sampling during global search
- Using L-BFGS-B for local optimization with careful gradient handling
- Implementing a budget-aware scheduling algorithm

The method ensures we make consistent progress toward the \( C \leq 0.3808 \) bound while respecting the time constraint.

## 2. Mathematical Specification

### Variables:
- \( h \in \mathbb{R}^{n_{\text{points}}} \) - Discretized step function, 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 for all i
- Integral constraint: \( \sum_{i=0}^{n_{\text{points}}-1} h[i] \cdot dx = 1 \), where \( dx = 2.0 / n_{\text{points}} \)

### Objective Function:
Minimize \( C_5 = \max_k dx \cdot \sum_{i} h[i] \cdot (1 - h[i+k]) \)  
during overlap, evaluated via discrete correlation.

### Optimization Formulation:
- Box constraints: 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1
- Equality constraint: sum(h) * dx = 1
- Objective: -max_k (correlation at shift k) to be minimized

### Use of Relaxation:
None directly, but use projected gradients to enforce constraints.

### Constraint Handling:
- Integral constraint enforced via post-projecting gradients
- Bound constraints handled via clipping
- Robust projection to constraint plane to ensure feasibility
