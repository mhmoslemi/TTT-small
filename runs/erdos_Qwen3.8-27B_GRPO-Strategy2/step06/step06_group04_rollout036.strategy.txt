2. Mathematical specification
   The Erdos problem aims to find a step function h: [0,2] -> {0,1} such that:
      Integral h(x) dx = 1
      E[h(x)(1-h(x+k))] is minimized for all k

   The optimization formulation uses a Fourier-coefficient-based surrogate for the maximum correlation, defined as:

      C(o) = max_k ∫|h(x+k) - h(x)| dx

   Our algorithm leverages:

      Fourier transform for shift invariance (accessing correlation via cyclic convolution)
      Sobol sampling to ensure dimension-specific diversity

   The objective can be expressed in the frequency domain as:

      C(ω) = ∑ |X(0)(k) - X(k)(k)|^2

   where X is the Fourier transform of h.

   This is justified because convolution becomes pointwise multiplication. However, the step function Fourier transform is not defined in the classical sense - we must use windowed transforms or periodized versions.

   The solver ensures frequency continuity by projecting onto L2 and then thresholding.
