The provided solution uses periodic patterns and gradient-based optimization with high regularization parameters to minimize the Erdős minimum overlap constant. While effective, the log-sum-exp barrier function may be overly restrictive and slow convergence. We propose a hybrid approach combining these techniques with:

1. Improved constraint handling using exact Lagrange multipliers
2. A density transformation method to reduce the optimization space
3. Progressive resolution refinement while preserving integral constraints
4. Adaptive gradient clipping for better numerical stability

This approach aims to find a smoother, more globally optimal solution by:

- Maintaining tighter control over constraints
- Reducing the effective search space through transformation
- Exploiting problem structure more effectively
- Handling numerical issues that may cause local minima

The central idea is to transform the problem into one where the function resembles a normalized probability density, allowing stricter constraint enforcement and faster convergence.
