1. Approach and rationale

The problem is to find a feasible discrete vector h of length n, with 0 <= h[i] <= 1 and sum_i h[i] = n/2, that minimizes the exact finite-support cross-correlation objective
C(h) = max_z dx * (h correlated with 1-h, full, lag z), where dx = 2/n and z ranges over all 2n-1 full-correlation lags. The supplied `initial_h_values` is a known construction with a current bound around 0.38085. The goal is to attempt a numerical improvement to about 0.38080 while returning a rigorously feasible and exactly evaluated vector.

The central idea is a multi-start, multi-level local optimization over the exact discretized objective, not over a circular or unmeasured surrogate. I will combine:
- exact evaluation via `np.correlate(h, 1.0 - h, mode="full") * dx`;
- a smooth maximum of the full lag values to obtain differentiable descent directions;
- an epigraph nonlinear program solve for a direct min-t subproblem;
- deterministic and stochastic feasible initializations, including balanced binary sequences and feasible affine-neighborhood polish;
- repair of equality and box constraints jointly by a continuous scaling-dilation with exact residual correction;
- strict acceptance only on feasible candidate recomputed by the exact evaluator.

Why this should improve the parent:
- The supplied construction is close to the desired threshold, so any improvement likely lies in a boundary of one or more active blend vectors. Epigraph SLSQP makes max constraints visible and can move along the boundary rather than using only a smoothed scalar objective collapsed onto a subset of lags.
- Smoothmax warm starts avoid getting stuck at the nonsmooth max and give good nearby basins.
- Using multiple resolutions is beneficial because discretization of supports can create or destroy equality constraints and boundary terms. A parent n=63 can be lifted to larger even resolutions, but the returned length can be selected when there is a genuine feasibility-valid improvement.
- Small-scale randomized feasible perturbations can reveal nearby changes that deterministic starts miss.

Limitations and justification:
- C(h) is nonconvex because terms include products -h_p h_q. Therefore no CVX/GLOP/CBC/QCQP direct solve isMathematical justifiable for a global optimum. Every returned bound is an achieved upper bound on C5 based on the exact evaluator, not a certified global lower or optimized across all h.

- Epigraph solve is a local constrained NLP; fairness relies on exact revalidation.
