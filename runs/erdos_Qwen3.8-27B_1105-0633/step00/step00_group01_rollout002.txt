- If we change h[i] by +δ and h[j] by -δ:

The coefficient of h[i] in term with lag τ:
- If τ = 0: coef = 1 - 2*h[i]
- If τ > 0:

This is exactly the gradient of term τ at point i! So the change in term τ from a pair exchange (i: +δ, j: -δ) is:
