1. Approach and rationale
=========================

1.1 Problem as a finite-dimensional minimax problem
- The decision variable is a vector `h` of length `n`, interpreted as samples of a step function on `[0, 2]`, with cell width `dx = 2.0 / n`.
- Feasible set: `0 <= h_i <= 1` and `sum_i h_i = n/2`.
- The exact objective used by the evaluation harness is
  `C(h) = max(np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full") * dx)`.
  This is a finite-support linear correlation of `h` and `1-h`, not circular correlation. The full array contains all shifted overlaps. Need terminations at both ends are included event by their only `np.correlate(...) full` convention.
- The maximum over shifts is retained exactly in scoring; do not replace by circular formulas or autocorrel-only objectives without preserving all lag terms because boundary terms differ.

1.2 Primary optimization idea
- Use a smooth approximation to the finite minimax objective and optimize it with local derivative-based solvers under box and sum constraints. Then score candidates with the exact objective, keep only feasible validated vectors, and run exact score descent / randomized pair moves.
