1. Approach and rationale

Goal and central difficulty

The task is to find a discrete length-n sequence h[0..n-1] with 0 <= h[i] <= 1 and sum(h)=n/2 that minimizes the maximum finite-support overlap integral

C(h)=dx * max_{r=0..2n-2} correlate(h,1-h)_r, dx=2/n.

The objective is a maximum of many bilinear functions of h. By retaining the evaluator's exact formula for the objective and validating every returned candidate with that formula, all heuristic surrogate steps may be used for search while the final answer remains a rigorous discrete construction with a verified upper bound.

The supplied parent initial_h_values, and perhaps better alternatives at nearby or different n, likely contains much of the structure of the current record. The central algorithm is therefore:

- preserve and validate the supplied parent;
- exploit exact invariance symmetries (reversal, complement, cyclic rotations) that can reduce edge lag values without changing feasibility;
- perform smooth-max constrained local optimization from many points near the parent and from generated continuations;
- transfer and compare candidates across nearby resolutions, so that smaller resolutions and larger smooth continuations are both tested;
- use population/recombination and multimodal random generation to escape local optima, while always accepting a point only after exact evaluation;
- keep a robust 0.38085-level parent fallback if no better construction is found.
