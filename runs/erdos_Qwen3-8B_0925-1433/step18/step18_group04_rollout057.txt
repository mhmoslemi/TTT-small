- Compute the current C₅.
- While time remains:
   - Generate a neighbor solution by perturbing h slightly.
   - Compute the C₅ for the neighbor.
   - Accept the neighbor with probability based on the temperature and the change in C₅.
   - Decrease the temperature.

The perturbation can be done by randomly selecting a subset of points and adjusting their values, ensuring the constraints are met.
