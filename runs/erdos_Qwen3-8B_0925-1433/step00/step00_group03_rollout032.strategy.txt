The objective is transformed into maximizing the minimum auto-correlation over all lags. The gradient of the maximum overlap is approximated numerically to guide updates. The constraint is maintained through clipping and adjustment of the last element to preserve the sum.

3. Implementation procedure
Inputs: initial_h_values (float array), n_points
Steps:
a. Initialize h = initial_h_values
b. Compute dx = 2.0 / n_points
c. Normalize h to ensure ∑h = n_points/2
d. For each iteration (limited by time budget):
   i. Compute correlation = np.correlate(h, 1 - h, mode="full")
   ii. O = correlation * dx
   iii. Find k_max = argmax(O)
   iv. Compute gradient via finite differences around h[i]
   v. Update h[i] = h[i] - learning_rate * gradient[i]
   vi. Clip h to [0, 1]
   vii. Adjust sum: delta = ∑h - n_points/2
        if delta ≠ 0: h[-1] += delta, then clip
e. Track best h found and its C5 value
f. Return h, C5, n_points
