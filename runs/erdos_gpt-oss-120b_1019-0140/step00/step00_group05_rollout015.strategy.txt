This approach reduces the optimization dimensionality by two orders of magnitude and leverages properties of unitarity and orthogonal signaling.

The key insights are:
1. Functional correlation corresponds to phase differences
2. Continuous optimization can occur in the frequency domain
3. Translation invariance maps to shifted phase vectors

- Primary layer performs phase optimization using numerical continuation
- Secondary layer verifies and refines solutions with genetic operators

<h3>Phase Representation</h3>
We represent the function $h(x)$ as:
\begin{equation}

\end{equation}
