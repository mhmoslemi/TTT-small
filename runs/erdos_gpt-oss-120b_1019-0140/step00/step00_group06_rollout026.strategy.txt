# Discord and Re-Aware Approach for Erdős Minimum Overlap Problem

## 1. Approach and Rationale

The Erdős minimum overlap problem requires finding a step function h(x) on [0,2] with integral 1 that minimizes the maximum shift overlap:

C_5 = max_k ∫ h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx

This non-convex optimization problem lacks standard convex structure, making gradient-based methods challenging. Our novel approach combines:
1. A graph-theoretic representation of potential shifts
2. Sparse representation of the overlap function
3. Coordinate-wise optimization with adaptive block initialization

The central idea is to represent the problem using a metric graph where vertices correspond to potential shift points and edges encode forbidden overlaps. This allows us to:
- Identify critical overlapping regions more efficiently
- Apply piecewise optimization to sparse high-points
- Exploit symmetry properties of optimal solutions

This mixed-graph method avoids the computational bottlenecks of direct correlation computation while providing a structured way to escape local optima common in convex relaxations.

## 2. Mathematical Specification

### Variables
- h ∈ [0,1]ⁿ with ∑h_i = n/2 (exactly for integer n)
- dx = 2/n
- n-number of samples (95, 190, 380 initially)

### Objective
Minimize C_5 = ∥correlate(h,1-h,mode='full')∥_∞ * (2/n)

### Constraints
1. 0 ≤ h_i ≤ 1 for all i
2. ∑h_i = n/2

### Representation
We use a specialized sparse representation focusing on:
- High-magnitude "islands" in the solution
- Boundary-aware interpolation from parent solution
- Hierarchical multi-resolution optimization
- Shift-adaptive overlap penalty function

The objective is evaluated using the original finite-difference formula with mode='full' to ensure exact correspondence to the continuous problem.
