### 1. Approach and Rationale
The Erdős minimum overlap problem seeks a step function \( h: [0, 2] \to [0, 1] \) with integral 1 that minimizes the maximum overlap \( C = \max_k \int h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx \). The current record is 0.38085. Our approach involves:

- **Parent Transfer**: Start from a given parent solution (n=45 samples) as a feasible initial point.
- **Neighbor Generation**: Explore minimal local modifications to discover better candidates.
- **Constraint Handling**: Repair infeasible solutions by blending values and scaling.
- **Adaptive Search**: Gradually increase the problem size to break local optima, while maintaining parent feasibility.

This heuristic search balances simplicity with ambition to prove a better bound than 0.38085.

- \( h \in [0, 1]^{n_{points}} \)
