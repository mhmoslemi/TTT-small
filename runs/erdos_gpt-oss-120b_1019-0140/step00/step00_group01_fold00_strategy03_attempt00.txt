### 1. Approach and rationale
The current upper bound for the Erdős minimum overlap constant C is 0.3808. This approach seeks to improve this bound by optimizing a step function representing a non-constant solution. Unlike previous methods (population-based evolutionary algorithms), we use continuous optimization constrained to a compact set of piecewise constant functions. This allows better numerical control and avoids computational bottlenecks. The optimization minimizes a penalized overlap metric subject to integral constraints and domain bounds, with a focus on structured yet flexible representations. The key advantages are higher numerical efficiency, better constraint enforcement, and directionality in gradient descent through projected steps.

### 2. Mathematical specification
- **Representation**: Discretize [0,2] into \( n \) samples, \( \{x_i\} \), and represent \( h(x) \) as a vector \( \mathbf{h} \in [0,1]^n \) such that \( \sum \mathbf{h}_i = n/2 \).
- **Overlap objective**: Minimize  
  \[
  \mathcal{C} = \max_{|k| \leq n-1} \left| \sum_{i} h_i (1 - h_{i+k}) \Delta x \right|
  \]
  where \( \Delta x = 2/n \) is the grid spacing. The score \( C_5 \) is this maximum normalized by \( \Delta x^{-1} \).
- **Variables**: \( n \)-dimensional vector \( \mathbf{h} \in [0,1]^n \).
- **Constraints**:
  - \( \sum_{i} h_i = n/2 \)
  - \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \) for all \( i \) 
- **Penalty term**: To handle constraints, add a penalty \( P(\mathbf{h}) = 10^6 |\sum_{i} h_i - n/2| + 10^4 \sum_i \max(h_i - 1, 0) \max(0, -(h_i)) \).
- **Objective**: Minimize \( \mathcal{C} + P(\mathbf{h}) \), where \( \mathcal{C} \) is scale-invariant (normalized by \( \Delta x^{-1} \)) alignment with the Evaluator.
