## 1. Approach and Rationale
The core idea is to model the step function `h(x)` over `[0,2]` using a continuous periodic representation that is then discretized into `n_points` samples. This representation is parameterized as a mixture of sinusoidal components (Fourier-like basis) rescaled to fit the interval `[0,2]`. By optimizing the parameters of this continuous function, we reduce the optimization problem from 80 discrete variables to a smaller, continuous-dimensional space, enabling more efficient local search.
