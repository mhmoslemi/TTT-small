The key insight is to leverage the initial solution with n=42 and progressively move to higher resolutions (n up to 84) to explore better configurations. We use a repair mechanism to enforce constraints and local search to iteratively improve solutions, ensuring the sum constraint is maintained.

**Why this improves**: The initial solution handles only n=42, but a higher resolution (e.g., n=84) allows for more precise optimization. Higher grid resolution generally leads to better (lower) overlap constants by capturing more detailed step function behaviors.
