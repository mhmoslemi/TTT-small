The objective is to minimize:
$$C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)\left(1 - h(x+k)\right) dx$$

over step functions $h: [0,2] \to [0,1]$ with $\int_0^2 h(x)dx = 1$.

Constraints:
- $0 \leq h[i] \leq 1$ for all $i$

Successive Improvement Strategy:
