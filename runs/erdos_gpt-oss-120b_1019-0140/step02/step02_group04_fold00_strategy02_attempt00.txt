### Mathematical Specification

We represent the step function h through binary decisions at \( n \) points, with the integral constraint \( \sum h[i] \cdot dx = 1 \) (equivalent to \( \sum h[i] = n/2 \) for \( dx=2/n \)). Candidates are evaluated via:
\[
\mathsf{score} = \max_k \left( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k_{\text{overlap}}} h[i] (1 - h[i+k]) \cdot dx \right)
\]

Mathematical variables:
- Input: \( h[i] \in [0, 1]^n \)
- Output: Scalar \( C_5 \equiv \mathsf{score} \)

Variables to optimize:
- \( h[i] \in [0, 1] \) for all \( i \)
- Subject to: \( \sum h[i] = n/2 \)

### Implementation Procedure

**Initialization & Setup**:
1. Determine \( n \): default 80, or parent-provided if available.
2. Start with candidate from `initial_h_values` or a random feasible vector.

**Core Update Loop**:
For each iteration (up to budget):
1. Identify top 3 binding constraints (lags with highest overlap) via ranking by score weight.
2. For each most-constraining lag \( k \):
   a. Select two indices \( i, j \) from its support.
   b. Apply inversion or translation mutation:
        - 50%: Binary flip: mutate \( h[i] \) from 0/1 to 1/0.
        - 50%: Translation: shift \( h[i] \) by \( \delta/\|\text{grad}\| \).
   c. Preserve integral by compensatory change in another point.
3. If no binding constraints: mutation loses priority over previously best solution.

**Feasibility Checks**:
- After mutation: clip values to [0,1] and project sum to \( n/2 \).

**Final Cleanup**:
- Repair any gradient-based artifacts (bound breaches) via clipping and sum-projection.
- Maintain best solution found, including parents if they yield better results.
