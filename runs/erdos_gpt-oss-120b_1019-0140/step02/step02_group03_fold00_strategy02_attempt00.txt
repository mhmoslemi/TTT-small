Approach and rationale
The central idea is to leverage a multi-resolution optimization strategy that combines evolutionary algorithms with local search to find a near-optimal step function solution to the Erds minimum overlap problem. This approach differs from previous methods by:
1. Using a dual-resolution representation (coarse grid analysis and fine grid refinement)
2. Combining global search (evolutionary strategies) with local optimization to escape local minima
3. Dynamically adjusting the search space based on solution quality

The key bottlenecks this approach addresses are:
- Scalability limitations of grid-based methods on high-resolution domains
- Local optima trapping in gradient-based optimization
- The challenge of finding global optima in non-convex, nonlinear optimization problems

The algorithm works by first performing coarse-grid optimization to identify promising solution patterns, then refining those patterns through linear interpolation to the fine grid, and finally applying local optimization techniques. This hierarchical approach balances exploration vs. exploitation while Dynamic Adaptation adjusts parameters based on convergence.
