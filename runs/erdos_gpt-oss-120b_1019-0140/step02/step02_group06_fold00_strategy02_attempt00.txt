Approach and rationale
The central idea is to use a constructive search over piecewise-constant functions with a small number of active intervals. The gradient-based optimization approach may get trapped in local optima due to the high dimensionality of the problem. Our method instead explicitly searches for functions that are non-zero only in a limited number of intervals, leveraging the observation that a high-performing function likely has a sparse structure. We draw inspiration from mathematical morphology and comb filter theory, but with the added constraint that the integral must be 1. The optimization is performed by evolving interval configurations with simplex crossover and 1-bit flips for fine-tuning. This probabilistic machine is expected to escape shallow local optima in regions where the gradient-based method often stalls.
