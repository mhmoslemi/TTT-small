### Approach and Rationale

The primary approach is to use a hybrid stochastic local search methodology based on Coordinate Ascent and Stochastic Tunneling Mutation to explore the solution space more effectively than the previous SLSQP method. The key observation is that the Optimization landscape is sparse and has multiple local optima. The previous method was too conservative and prone to local optima trapping. This new approach employs:

2. **Adaptive coordinate ascent**: Sequentially optimizes each coordinate while maintaining feasibility
3. **Dimension-wise interpolation**: Smoothly records optimization trajectories to prevent stagnation
