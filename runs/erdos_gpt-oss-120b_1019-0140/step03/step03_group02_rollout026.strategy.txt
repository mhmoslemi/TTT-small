### 1. Approach and Rationale
The core idea is to use a **multi-resolution optimization** strategy with two key innovations:
- **Fractional Binary Initialization**: Start from continuous solutions using the parent's grid and scale them non-integerally to explore structures not possible on integer grids while preserving feasibility.
- **Resolution Transfer without Binary Repair**: Allow continuous representation between grids to maintain optimization quality, with minimal binary repair at the final step to stay close to the binary constraint.

This addresses the challenge of the non-convex optimization by leveraging continuous relaxation at intermediate steps for better search, then enforcing binary feasibility at the finest resolution.

Potential limitations: The interpolation might produce low-quality binary representations, and the repair step could miss the global optimum. However, these are outweighed by the improved exploration.
