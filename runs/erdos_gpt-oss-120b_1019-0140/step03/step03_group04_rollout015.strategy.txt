## Approach and Rationale

The central idea is to synthesize periodic step functions with low autocorrelation properties to minimize the maximum overlap when shifted. Unlike previous approaches that relied solely on gradient-based optimization with local searches, we use a hybrid strategy combining global random search over predefined reference sequences with local projection methods. We hypothesize that sequences with minimal autocorrelation (like sequences derived from Costas arrays or Golay sequences) naturally satisfy the maximin minimization requirement. This avoids getting trapped in local optima by using complementary starting points drawn from families with proven low-discrepancy properties.

Key theoretical oversight addressed: The problem requires a step function whose autocorrelation is minimal at nonzero shifts. Conventional gradient methods struggle with combinatorial constraints, while standard mutation operators in evolutionary methods often degrade signal structure. Our solution leverages exact constructions from coding theory (which guarantee minimal maximal correlations) and then refines them within a constrained optimization envelope.

Limitations: The approach depends on constructing reference sequences of specific length and cannot be directly extended beyond modest grid sizes. However, the refinements are designed to scale efficiently.

## Mathematical Specification

The optimization seeks to minimize:
\[
\max_{k \in \mathbb{Z}} \int_0^2 h(x)(1-h(x+k))\\mathrm{d}x
\]
subject to:
\[
\int_0^2 h(x)\\mathrm{d}x = 1
\]
and:
\[
0 \le h(x) \le 1
\]

Discretization on uniform grid $x_i = i \cdot \frac{2}{n}\\ \forall i=0,...,n-1$:
\[
h^{\\text{grid}} = [h(0), h(1), \\dots., h(n-1)]^T \in \{0,1\}^n
\]
with:
\[
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h^{\\text{grid}}(i) = \frac{n}{2}
\]

Let $c(h^{\\text{grid}}, k)$ denote the integrated overlap at shift $k$, then the objective is:
\[
C_5 = \frac{2}{n} \cdot \max_{k=-\ (n-1)}^{n-1} c(h^{\\text{grid}}, k)
\]

The solution strategy combines:
1. A finite set of reference sequences with low autocorrelation properties (derived using linear algebra)
2. Local optimization gradient projection updates
3. Combinatorial mutation operators that preserve integral constraints

The low-autocorrelation reference sequences $s \in \{0,1\}^n$ are constructed that minimize: 
\[
\frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=-(n-1)}^{n-1} |\text{sum}(h \\otimes (1-h)[i:i+k]|_2
\]

Implementation preserves the binary domain through integral constraint projection:
\[
\text{Project}(v, t) = \begin{cases} \text{Round}(v, t) & \text{if } \| \text{Round}(v,t) - v \| < \delta \epsilon \\ v & \text{if constraint already satisfied} \end{cases}
\]
