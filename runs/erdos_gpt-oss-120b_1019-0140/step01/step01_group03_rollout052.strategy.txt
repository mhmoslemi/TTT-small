The core idea is to first find promising patterns in a coarse grid (64 points) using Bayesian optimization, then refine these patterns at higher resolutions (128 and 256 points) using local optimization. The algorithm adapts its grid resolutions based on a time budget, prioritizing coarser grids to escape local optima while maintaining focus on promising candidates throughout the optimization process.
