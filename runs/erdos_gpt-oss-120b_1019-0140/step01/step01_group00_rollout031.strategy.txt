Approach and Rationale
The central idea is to use binary representations for the step function h, converting intermediate values to either 0 or 1. This means every point is either fully selected or not selected, which reduces the search space and better mimics a true step function. The original approach used fixed-point interpolation and continuous optimization, which might get stuck in local optima. By direct binary optimization, we avoid the complexity of continuous tuning and focus entirely on valid step functions. We address the overlap problem as a genetic search over binary vectors with controlled variance, using uniform crossover and swap mutation. This approach directly targets the discretized problem, where the correlation between the pulse and its complement determines the overlap maximum. Binary solutions are both valid and potentially optimal for minimizing overlap.
