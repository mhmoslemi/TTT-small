## Approach and rationale
The outgoing version of the Triangle Multiplicative Update (TriMul) operator in AlphaFold3 requires processing a 4D tensor [B, N, N, C] for each batch B, sequence length N, and channel dimension C. The operation involves:

1. Normalizing each element using LayerNorm (along dim)  
2. Projecting the normalized tensor using left and right linear layers and gate projections  
3. Masking zero values where necessary  
4. Computing the einsum operation (equivalent to a matrix multiplication)  
5. Optional masking (nomask or mask) can be applied to the input  

The einsum operation's complexity (O(N^3)) poses a significant challenge, especially with large N (up to 1024). However, since LayerNorm and other operations are separable by the channel dimension C, we can break the computation into:
- Host-side handling for normalization, projection, and masking
- Device-side kernel for the O(N^4) operation, with optimizations for data layout and parallelism  

The host-side operations remain in PyTorch for clarity and ease of integration, while the kernel handles the computational bottleneck.

## Mathematical specification
The input tensor `x` has shape [B, N, N, C]. The operator performs:

$$\begin{align*}
x_{\text{norm}} &= \text{layer\_norm}(x, \text{norm\_weight}, \text{norm\_bias}) \\
\text{left\_proj} &= \text{linear}(x_{\text{norm}}, \text{left\_proj\_weight}, \text{no\_bias}) \\
\text{right\_proj} &= \text{linear}(x_{\text{norm}}, \text{right\_proj\_weight}, \text{no\_bias}) \\
\text{mask} &= \text{sampling\_mask}(\text{mask\_rate}, \text{random\_mask}) \text{ (optional) } \\
x_{\text{masked}} &= x_{\text{norm}} \cdot \text{mask} \\
\text{left\_gate} &= \text{sigmoid}(\text{linear}(x_{\text{masked}}, \text{left\_gate\_weight}, \text{no\_bias})) \\
\text{right\_gate} &= \text{sigmoid}(\text{linear}(x_{\text{masked}}, \text{right\_gate\_weight}, \text{no\_bias})) \\
\text{out\_gate} &= \text{sigmoid}(\text{linear}(x_{\text{masked}}, \text{out\_gate\_weight}, \text{no\_bias})) \\
\text{output} &= \text{linear}(\text{einsum}(\text{left\_proj} \cdot \text{left\_gate}, \text{right\_proj} \cdot \text{right\_gate}), \text{to\_out\_weight}) \cdot \text{out\_gate} 
\end{align*}$$

The core operation (einsum) results in a new tensor `out` of shape [B, N, N, C] where:

$$ \text{out}[b, i, j, k] = \sum_{f=0}^{\text{hidden\_dim}-1} (\text{left\_proj}[b, i, f, k] \cdot \text{left\_gate}[b, i, f, k] \cdot \text{right\_proj}[b, j, f, k] \cdot \text{right\_gate}[b, j, f, k] ) $$

The final output is also normalized in hidden_dim space and gated.
