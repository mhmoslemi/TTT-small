<strategy>1. Approach and Rationale:
The core of the TriMul operator is the einsum operation that involves pair-wise summation over sequence indices. To optimize this, we'll use a blocked reduction over k (the summation index) with tiling scheduled along sequence dimensions. The mask is handled by masking the projected values before the reduction. For computational efficiency, we'll use mixed precision: all intermediate calculations except normalization are done in float16, and the mask and gating are applied before reduction to limit the reduction range. LayerNorm is performed on host-by-host basis due to its need for element-wise statistics.

Input: x (tensor [B, N, N, C])

- left_gate_weight: (H, C)

- to_out_weight: (C, H)   [Note cis hidden_dim=C, so output C]
