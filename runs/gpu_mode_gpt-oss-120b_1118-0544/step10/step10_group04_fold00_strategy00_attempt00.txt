We are going to implement the TriMul operator as described in the AlphaFold3 paper using a Triton kernel.
 The operator takes a tensor `x` of shape [B, N, N, C] and a mask tensor (optional) of shape [B, N, N].
 Steps in the PyTorch implementation:
 1. Apply LayerNorm to the input tensor along the channel dimension.
 2. Perform linear projections (left_proj, right_proj) and gating projections (left_gate, right_gate, out_gate) for the hidden_dim.
 3. Apply the mask (if present) to the gated projections (element-wise multiplication) and then use a reduction (out[b,i,j] += left[b,i,k] * right[b,j,k] for k from 0 to N-1).
 4. Apply a second LayerNorm to the result, then multiply by the out_gate, and finally apply a linear projection to get back to the original channel dimension.

 The challenge is the reduction step, which is O(N^3) and memory-bound due to the large N.

- Transient dimensions to maximize parallelization
