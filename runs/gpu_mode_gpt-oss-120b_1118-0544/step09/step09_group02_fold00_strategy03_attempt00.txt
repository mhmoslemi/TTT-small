 The operator is defined on a 4D tensor of shape [B, N, N, C] (where C is the channel dimension and hidden_dim is 128, 384, or 768).

 The operation in the forward pass is:
  1. Apply LayerNorm on the input tensor over the channel dimension C. (But note: the hidden_dim projection and gating will require normalization over the input channel dimension)
  2. Then, apply linear projections (no bias) for left_proj and right_proj, and gating projections for left_gate and right_gate (with sigmoid), and then multiply the projected and gated tensors with the mask if provided.

 However, note that the previous implementation used LayerNorm on the input channel dimension and then a second LayerNorm on the output hidden dimension.
