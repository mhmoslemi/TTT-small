### Mathematical Specification

The TriMul operation updates \( \text{out}[b,i,j,d] \) as:
\[
\text{out}[b,i,j,d] = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \text{left}[b,i,k,d] \times \text{right}[b,j,k,d]

- \( x \) is first L2-normalized by its last dimension.
- \( \text{left} = \text{gate\_scale(left\_gate)} \times \text{proj\_left}(\text{project\_left}(x)) \)
- \( \text{right} = \text{gate\_scale(right\_gate)} \times \text{proj\_right}(\text{project\_right}(x)) \)

Numerically, this involves scaling and projections using matrix multiplications. Gating logics (sigmoid) are approximated with transformed linear operations to avoid explicit computations. The decomposition ensures stability by avoiding precision drops until final output.
