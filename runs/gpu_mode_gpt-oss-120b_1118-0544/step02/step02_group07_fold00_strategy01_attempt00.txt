We are to implement the outgoing version of the TriangleMultiplicativeUpdate (TriMul) operator from AlphaFold3.
 The operator takes a 4D tensor of shape [B, N, N, C] and applies a series of operations including LayerNorm, linear projections, optional masking, gating with sigmoid, and a reduction over the sequence dimension.

 The reduction operation is defined as:
   out[b, i, j, d] = sum_{k} left[b, i, k, d] * right[b, j, k, d]

 However, note that the reduction is only over k (the inner index) and the output is of the same shape [B, N, N, C] (but note that the original PyTorch code used a hidden dimension and then projected back).

 In our case, we have:
   left_proj and right_proj output tensors of shape [B, N, N, hidden_dim], which we then multiply element-wise by the corresponding gating projections (sigmoid) and by the mask (if present).

 Then, we perform the reduction. The reduction can be seen as a batched matrix product with the two (B, N, N, hidden_dim) tensors.

 Steps in the original PyTorch code:

 1. Apply LayerNorm to the input tensor [B, N, N, dim].
 2. Apply linear projections (without bias) for left and right, resulting in [B, N, N, hidden_dim].
 3. If a mask is present, multiply left and right by the mask (broadcasted on the last dimension).
 4. Apply gating: sigmoid to left_gate, right_gate, out_gate. Then multiply left and right by their respective gates.
 5. Compute the reduction: out = einsum with left and right, which is equivalent to: for each b, d, and for i, j in [0, N-1], 
        out[b, i, j, d] = sum_{k=0}^{N-1} left[b, i, k, d] * right[b, j, k, d]
 6. Apply LayerNorm on the hidden dimension (to_out_norm) and then multiply by the out_gate (element-wise).
 7. Project back to the original channels (dim).

 We are allowed to use mixed precision, but the output must be float32.

   Input distributions: standard Normal or heavy-tailed Cauchy (gamma=2).
   Mask: either there is no mask, or a randomly sampled mask (boolean or float).
