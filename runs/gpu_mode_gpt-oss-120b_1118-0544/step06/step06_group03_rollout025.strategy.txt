**Approach and Rationale:**  
The TriMul operator performs a core <ins>fusion of normalization, projections, gating, and an optional mask</ins> to update pairwise representations efficiently. Given the high computational intensity (N³ operations) during the reduction phase, we must <ins>optimize the sequence dimensions using block-sparsity</ins> with Triton’s tensorcore capabilities. The implementation splits the operation into stages: (1) applying LayerNorm, (2) projecting/filtering hidden dimensions, (3) masking, (4) kernel-driven reduction, and (5) post-processing LayerNorm and gating. This approach avoids Python-induced bottlenecks during the N³ reduction.
