**Matrix Multiplication Kernel Design**

**Objective**: Compute `out[j,i,k] = dot(a[i,k], b[j,k])`, where:
- `a` is of shape `[B*H, N]`
- `b` is of shape `[B*N, H]`

**Optimization Stage 1: Layout and Caching**

- Utilize **shared memory** caching for intermediate results to minimize memory access latency.
- The kernel's grid program will handle matrix multiplication for all $(batch, hidden)$ pairs.

**Optimization Stage 2: Tiling Strategy**

Employ a tiling strategy where you:
1. Load a block of matrix `a` into $\\text{TL}${B}*HxN block.
2. Compute its product with the corresponding block of matrix `b` (transposed) store the result in shared memory.
3. Ensure efficient reuse of cached `a` and `b` blocks across different sections.

The tiling strategy ensures that the cache is bound by architectural limits rather than arbitrarily increases memory footprint.
