 The goal is to translate it into a highly optimized Triton kernel.

 Steps in the PyTorch implementation:
 1. LayerNorm (normalization) on the input tensor.
 2. Two linear projections (left_proj and right_proj) without bias.
 3. Three sigmoid gates (left_gate, right_gate, out_gate) computed from the input and multiplied by their respective projected tensors.
 4. Fusion of the mask (if present) with the gates for the left and right tensors. Note: in the PyTorch, the mask is multiplied with the left and right projected tensors, and then the gates are applied.

    out[b, i, j, d] = sum_{k} [left[b, i, k, d] * right[b, j, k, d]]
