 The goal is to compute: 
   out[b, i, j, d] = sum_{k} left_proj[b, i, k, d] * right_proj[b, j, k, d]

 However, note the paper constraints: the input might come from a standard Normal distribution or Cauchy distribution, and there might be an optional mask.

   2. Linear projections (left_proj, right_proj) to a hidden dimension (hidden_dim)
