  5. Reduction over `k`: For each `(i, j, d)`, compute `out[b, i, j, d] = sum_{k} left[b, i, k, d] * right[b, j, k, d]`.
  6. Apply output gate and final projection.
- **Output:** `out` tensor of shape `[B, N, hidden_dim]` (final projection).
