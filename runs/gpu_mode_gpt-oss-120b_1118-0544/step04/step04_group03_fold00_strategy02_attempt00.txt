### Approach and Rationale
The Triangle Multiplicative Update (TriMul) operator from AlphaFold3 involves a computationally expensive O(N³) reduction over the sequence dimension. To optimize this, we will leverage Triton's capabilities with batched matrix multiplications and masking, avoiding explicit Python loops. 

The core operation in TriMul is to compute attention scores by multiplying projected representations of input pairs and summing over intermediate indices. We will use lower-precision arithmetic (fp16) for performance, combined with mixed-precision for numerical stability. The approach involves:

1. Precomputing LayerNorm and linear transformations outside the kernel.
2. Reshaping the input to align dimensions optimally.
3. Performing a batched GEMM kernel with masked operations to compute partial products.
4. Accumulating results with minimal FLOPs per kernel thread.

### Mathematical Specification
Given input tensor `x` of shape `[B, N, N, C]`:
- `x` undergoes LayerNorm to fix channel means/vars.
- Two projections with gates are computed: `left_proj` (shape `[B, N, N, H]`) and `right_proj` (shape `[B, N, N, H]`), where gates apply sigmoid.
