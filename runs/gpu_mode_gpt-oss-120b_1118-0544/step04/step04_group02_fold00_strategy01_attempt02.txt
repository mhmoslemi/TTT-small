Key aspects of the TriMul operator:
1. LayerNorm on the input tensor of shape [B, N, N, C].
2. Two linear projections (left and right) to hidden_dim, resulting in tensors of shape [B, N, N, hidden_dim].
3. Apply sigmoid activation and gates (left_gate, right_gate) as element-wise operations.
4. If mask is provided, mask the projected tensors and the gates.
5. The core operation: an einsum that effectively computes a batched matrix multiplication over the sequence dimension N, i.e., for each (b, i, j, d):
        out[b, i, j, d] = sum_{k=0}^{N-1} left[b, i, k, :] * right[b, j, k, :]   [without mask] or with mask applied appropriately.
