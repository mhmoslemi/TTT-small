 The input is a 4D tensor [B, N, N, C] (batch, sequence length, sequence length, channels) and an optional mask [B, N, N] (boolean or float).

 Steps of the operation (from the PyTorch code):

  2. Linear projections: left_proj and right_proj, which transform the tensor from [B, N, N, C] to [B, N, N, hidden_dim].
