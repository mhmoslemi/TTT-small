The key steps in the original PyTorch implementation are:
1. LayerNorm on the input tensor (last dimension C).
2. Two linear projections (left_proj and right_proj) that reduce the input to hidden_dim and then apply sigmoid to the gating outputs (left_gate, right_gate, out_gate).
