2. Linear projections for left and right: transform the input from [B, N, N, C] to [B, N, N, hidden_dim] for both left and right.
3. Apply optional mask: if provided, the mask is broadcasted to the hidden_dim dimension and multiplied element-wise to both left and right tensors.
4. Compute gating: apply three linear layers to get gates for left, right, and output, and pass through sigmoid activation. Then multiply the projections (left and right) by their respective gates.
