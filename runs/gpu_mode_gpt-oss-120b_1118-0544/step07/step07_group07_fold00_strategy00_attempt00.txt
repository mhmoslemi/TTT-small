          - gates (left_gate, right_gate, out_gate) are applied element-wise
          - mask is conditionally applied

        Mathematically, it's a series of operations along a large 4D tensor, effectively reducing one dimension while creating a new one.

     c. Output processing:
        Apply LayerNorm on h-dimension, then element-wise multiply with out_gate, then project back.

   The operation sequence:

   ```
   y = layer_norm(x)  #Normalization and affine map
   left = y @ left_linear_weight + left_bias
   right = y @ right_linear_weight + right_bias
   left_gate = activation.sigmoid(left_old_weights)  #sigmoid of another affine
   right_gate = activation.sigmoid(right_old_weights) #same
   out_gate = activation.sigmoid(out_old_weights)    #sigmoid's input for out gate

   left_masked = left * left_gate * mask     #if mask provided
   right_masked = right * right_gate * mask  #if mask provided

   #If no mask: just left*left_gate and right*right_gate

   Then compute out = contracted of left_masked and right_masked

   Then apply layer_norm (h normalization), and out_gate multiplication

   Then project to (x @ to_out_linear_weight + to_out_bias) [but note: to_out is another affine]

   Finally, restore the original dimension (dim) with a final projection.
   ```
