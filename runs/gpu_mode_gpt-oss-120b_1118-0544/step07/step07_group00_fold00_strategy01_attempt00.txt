**6.1 Approach and Rationale**

The TriMul operator performs a depth operation over a 4D tensor `[B, N, N, C]`, where `N` is the sequence length. The outgoing variant involves projecting the input tensor through linear layers, applying gating functions and optional masking, and then computing a reduction that updates each element `(i, j, h)` as the sum over `k` of the gated outputs from two projections. The challenge is pairing `(i, j)` with their corresponding `k` indices efficiently, especially since large `N` (dim `N=768`) and small `c=128`/`c_z=768` force explicit handling of dimensions. We must ensure correctness while optimizing performance through triton kernels.
</text>

<text>
**6.2 Mathematical Specification**

**Inputs**:
1. `x` of shape `[B, N, N, C]`
2. `mask` of shape `[B, N, N]` (optional)
**Parameters**:
- `dim`: input channel dimension C
- `hidden_dim`: hidden dimension H from layers
**Outputs**:
1. `out` of shape `[B, N, N, C]`

**Operations**:
1. **LayerNorm**: Normalize `x` over `[B, N, N, C]` dimensions.
2. **Projection** and **Gating**:
   - Define `left_proj`, `right_proj`, `left_gate`, `right_gate` linear projections (matrices).
   - Compute `gated_left` and ``gate field right` by multiplying respective projections and gates (cell withスポ).
   - Apply `mask` conditionally: if `mask[b,i,k]==1`, update `gated_left[b,i,k]` and `gated_right[b,j,k]`.
3. **Reduction** (core tensor plan):
   - Compute `out[b, i, j, h] = Σ_k gated_left[b, i, k, h] * gated_right[b, j, k, h]` using blocked computation and caching.
 - The reduction inherently assumes indexing bounds `[0, N)` for all dimensions.

**Derivations**:
- Let `gated_left[b, i, k, h] = left_proj[b, i, k, h] * left_gate[b, i, k, h] * mask[b,i,k]` (if mask exists, else just the projection).
- Similarly for `gated_right`.
- Standard matrix multiplication is assumed `out[b,i,j] = gated_left[b,i,:] @ gated_right[b,j,:]` for each hidden_dim `h`.
</text>
