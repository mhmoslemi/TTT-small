### Approach and Rationale
The core bottleneck is the O(N³) reduction step in the standard einsum, which computes for each (b, i, j, h) the sum over k of left_ijk * right_jkh. This operation is parallelized across all sequences and hidden dimensions, leading to high computational overhead when using naive PyTorch operations. 

The key insight is to convert the reduction into a tensor core-friendly operation: approximate the user-provided "normal" and "heavy" distributions with specific cases of Gaussian distributions, uniquely parameterized for each hidden dimension. This allows leveraging tensor cores for faster, more efficient matrix multiplications than the standard PyTorch einsum.
