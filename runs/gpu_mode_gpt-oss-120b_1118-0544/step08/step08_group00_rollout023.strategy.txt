**Approach and rationale**

We are going to implement an optimized Triton kernel for the Triangle Multiplicative Update (TriMul) operation, specifically the outgoing version from AlphaFold3. The incoming program used `torch.bmm` for the heavy reduction operation, which had high latency (~3200μs).
