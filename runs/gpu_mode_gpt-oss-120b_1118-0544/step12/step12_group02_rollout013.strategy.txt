We are implementing the TriangleMultiplicativeUpdate (TriMul) operator from AlphaFold3.
 The input tensor is of shape [B, N, N, C] and there may be a mask tensor [B, N, N] (or not, if nomask=True).

 Steps:
 1. Input normalization: Apply LayerNorm over the channel dimension (C).
 2. Linear projections: Two linear projections (left_proj and right_proj) without bias, each from channel dim to hidden_dim.
    We also compute gating projections (sigmoid activation): left_gate, right_gate, out_gate (each from channel dim to hidden_dim).
 3. Mask application: If a mask is provided and not ignored (nomask=False), then apply the mask (broadcasted to hidden_dim) to each of the projected tensors.
 4. Core operation: For each hidden dimension, compute a multiplicative update over the sequence dimensions (N) by summing over the intermediate dimension (k).
    This is a triply indexed operation: output[b, i, j, h] = sum_{k} (left[b, i, k, h] * right[b, j, k, h])
 5. Output processing: Apply LayerNorm over the hidden dimension, multiply by out_gate (broadcasted from hidden_dim to [B, seqlen, seqlen, hidden_dim]) and then final linear projection back to C.

 The challenge is that the core operation is O(N^3) because we do a reduction over k (which is same as the sequence length N) for each hidden dimension.

## Approach and rationale

The key bottle-neck is the O(N^3) reduction over the hidden dimension (k), achieved via pairwise multiplications between sequence positions i and j with intermediate indices k across all batches and hidden channels.
