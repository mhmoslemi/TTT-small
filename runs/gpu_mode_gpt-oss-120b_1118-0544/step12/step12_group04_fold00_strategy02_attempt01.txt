### Approach and Rationale
We are to implement the "outgoing" version of the Triangle Multiplicative Update (TriMul) operator from AlphaFold3. This operator processes a 4D tensor `[B, N, N, C]` to `[B, N, N, C]` through a series of linear projections, gating mechanisms, and a triangular reduction. The triangular reduction involves summing contributions from intermediate indices `k` for each pair `(i, j)`.

The reduction operation is:
`out[b, i, j, h] = sum_{k} factor_term left[b, i, k, h] * right[b, j, k, h]`

This is a highly compute-intensive operation (O(N^3)) and requires a small tiling strategy to maximize occupancy and data reuse. Instead of using nested loops, we will reshape tensors to align with cache-friendly dimensions and use block-wise processing to compute each `(b, h, i, j)` independently.

- `mask` of shape `[B, N, N]` (optional)
