**1. Approach and Rationale**  
The outgoing Triangle Multiplicative Update (TriMul) operator in AlphaFold3 requires triangu三形 multiplication for protein structure prediction. The primary bottleneck is the $O(N^3)$ reduction step due to the large sequence length $N$ (up to 1024). To optimize, we implement a tile-based descending loop using Triton, splitting hidden_dim and sequence indexing via grouped reduction. The kernel leverages shared memory for efficient access and avoids expensive operations like maskedmath by handling gates separately.  
**Key Innovation**: Direct tiling of the reduction without masking the reduction kernel itself, accepting precomputed masked and gated tensors from the PyTorch initialization pass.
