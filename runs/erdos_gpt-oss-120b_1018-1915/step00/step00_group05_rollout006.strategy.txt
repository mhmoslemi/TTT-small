1. Approach and Rationale:
The central idea is to use a hybrid genetic algorithm (GA) approach combined with a repair mechanism to minimize the overlap integral. The GA explores the solution space broadly while a repair procedure ensures candidate functions meet the integral constraint. This approach maintains diversity in the population while enforcing feasibility, which is crucial for handling a non-convex optimization problem like this one. The algorithm specifics differ from previous strategies in their explicit use of repair mechanisms during crossover and mutation phases, rather than relying solely on constraints or separate repair during evaluation.

2. Mathematical Specification:
- Representation: A step function on the grid [0,2] represented as a normalized n_points-length vector.
- Objective: Minimize the maximum absolute value of the autocorrelation of the function and (1-function), scaled by dx.
- Constraints:
  - Integral constraint: sum(h) = n_points/2 (since dx=2.0/n_points)
  - Box constraints: 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 for all i

The repair mechanism adjusts the function to meet the integral constraint while maintaining binary values. The GA uses tournament selection, a crossover operator, and elitism strategy.
