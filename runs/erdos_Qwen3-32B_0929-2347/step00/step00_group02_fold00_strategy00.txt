Starting from the initial feasible solution `initial_h_values`, we iteratively update the function h to reduce the maximum cross-correlation value. # Mathematical specification

The goal is to minimize:

$$
C_5 = \max_k \left( \text{cross\_corr}[k] \cdot \frac{2}{n\_points} \right)
$$

where:

$$
\text{cross\_corr}[k] = \sum_{i} h[i] \cdot (1 - h[i + k])
$$

Constraints:

1. $0 \leq h[i] \leq 1$ for all $i$
2. $\sum h[i] = \frac{n\_points}{2}$

At each step, we compute the gradient of cross\_corr for the lag $k$ yielding the maximum overlap and update h accordingly.
