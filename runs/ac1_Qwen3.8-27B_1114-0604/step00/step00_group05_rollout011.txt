where subgrad_i is the subgradient of the max constraint.

This is essentially a mirror descent method with the negative entropy as the Bregman divergence. It naturally maintains non-negativity.

    """Use multiple hot spots for a richer subgradient."""
    conv = fast_convolve(f, f)
    # Find the n_hot largest values
    hot_indices = np.argsort(conv)[-n_hot:]
    
    # Weighted average of subgradients at hot spots
    grad = np.zeros(len(f))
    weights = np.zeros(len(hot_indices))
    for i, k in enumerate(hot_indices):
        idx = k - np.arange(len(f))
        valid = (idx >= 0) & (idx < len(f))
