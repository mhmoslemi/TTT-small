def project_simplex(v):
    """Project vector v onto the probability simplex {x: sum(x)=1, x>=0}."""
    n = len(v)
    u = np.sort(v)[::-1]
    cssv = np.cumsum(u)
    rho = np.where(u * np.arange(1, n+1) > (cssv - 1))[0]

The key relationship: $b_k = \mathcal{F}^{-1}|A(\omega)|^2(k)$ (circular, with appropriate padding for linear).

If we parameterize by the power spectrum $P(\omega) = |A(\omega)|^2$, then:
- $b_k = \mathcal{F}^{-1}P(k)$
- We want to minimize $\max_k b_k$
- Subject to $b_k = \mathcal{F}^{-1}P(k)$ being consistent with a non-negative time-domain signal

This is a convex problem in $P$ (minimize the max of linear functions of $P$, subject to $P \geq 0$ and the consistency constraint). But the consistency constraint (that $P$ must be the power spectrum of a non-negative sequence) is non-convex.

However, we can use an iterative approach:
1. Start with an initial $P$ (e.g., uniform)
2. Compute $a = \text{IFFT}(\sqrt{P} \cdot e^{i\phi})$ for some phase $\phi$
3. Clip $a$ to non-negative
4. Update $P = |\text{FFT}(a)|^2$
5. Adjust $P$ to reduce the peak of $\text{IFFT}(P)$
6. Repeat
