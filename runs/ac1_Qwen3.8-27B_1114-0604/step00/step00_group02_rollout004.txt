The constraints (a*a)[k] ≤ t are quadratic (second-order cone representable? No, they're general quadratic constraints). This is a quadratic programming problem with quadratic constraints, which is non-convex in general.

However, we can use the **outer approximation** method: iterate between
1. Given current peak lags K = {k: (a*a)[k] ≈ max}, solve: minimize t subject to (a*a)[k] ≤ t for k ∈ K, sum(a) = 1, a ≥ 0.
   This is still non-convex due to the quadratic constraints.

Alternatively, we can use **successive approximation**:
1. At current point a, the active constraint is (a*a)[k*] = max.
2. Linearize: (a*a)[k*] ≈ (a*a)[k*] + g[k*]^T (a - a_current) where g[k*] is the gradient.
3. Solve: minimize g[k*]^T (a - a_current) subject to sum(a) = 1, a ≥ 0.
   This gives a direction to move in.
4. Take a step in that direction.

This is essentially what I described before (subgradient descent).
