If the sequence exhibits a loss of precision due to peaks (primarily for step function height regulation), this fallback ensures recovery within given boundaries.

Final comparison: After optimization or fallback, evaluate the best candidate using the official `evaluate_sequence` function.

We set a primary strategy:

   - **Fallback phase**: This ensures that the sequence doesn't get stuck if local maxima are found.

The fall happens if:
