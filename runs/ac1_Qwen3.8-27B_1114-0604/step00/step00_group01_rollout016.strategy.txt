 Previous programs worked well but have a high local minimum rate (especially in dimensions > 256). We address this by:
 1. Using stochastic gradient descent (SGD) with adaptive clip bounds and projection onto the simplex.
 2. Implementing a dual constraint: enforce non-negative coefficients AND satisfy the sum constraint of 0.01 minimum.
 3. Using smooth descent by envelope projections and caching intermediate computations.

 Mathematical specification
 Let n = 6121. The objective is O(n) dimensional with constraints:
   ⊙ Each coefficient x_i ≥ 0 (simplex)
   ⊙ sum(x_i) ≥ 0.01 (additional bound)

 The gradient of the objective is not continuous due to the max in the convolution. Thus, we use:
   f(x) = 2*n*max_{k=0..2n-2} [ (full_convolve(x, x))[k+ n-1 ] ] / (sum(x))^2
