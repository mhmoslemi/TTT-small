**Key optimization for coordinate descent:**

Define g(δ) = min{subgradient of h at δ} and g̅(δ) = max{subgradient of h at δ}.
At the optimum, g(δ) ≤ 0 ≤ g̅(δ).

g(δ) = 2·min_{k*∈argmax} c[k*] + 2δ·I(2j ∈ argmax)
g̅(δ) = 2·max_{k*∈argmax} c[k*] + 2δ·I(2j ∈ argmax)
