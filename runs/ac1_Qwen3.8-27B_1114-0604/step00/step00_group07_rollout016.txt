The number of non-zeros in column j is min(n, 2n-1-j).
For j < n-1: min(n, 2n-1-j) = n (since 2n-1-j > n when j < n-1)
For j >= n-1: min(n, 2n-1-j) = 2n-1-j

Total non-zeros = sum_{j=0}^{n-2} n + sum_{j=n-1}^{n-1} (2n-1-(n-1))

= n^2 - n + n

By C-S, integral a^2 >= (integral a)^2 = 1, with equality when a is constant. So the continuous minimum is 2, achieved by the constant function.

The resolution is that in the discrete case, the boundary effects matter. The convolution at the center involves n terms, but at other positions, it involves fewer terms. For a non-palindromic sequence, the center might not be the max, and the actual max might be at a position with fewer terms.

In the continuous limit (n -> infinity), the boundary effects vanish (the fraction of "boundary" terms goes to 0), and the min is 2.
