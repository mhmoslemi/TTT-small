**Approach and Rationale**  
The central idea is to combine multiple optimization techniques within a single optimization framework to overcome the limitations of dependency on fixed parent and sequence length. We use a multi-expert approach where each expert optimizes a specific aspect of the sequence—either the sequence length and initial structure via Bayesian Optimization, the subgradient via Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), or the final details via L-BFGS polish. This allows us to explore different regions of the solution space effectively.  

**Bottleneck Addressed:**  
The previous script was heavily reliant on the initial parent sequence and a fixed sequence length, which often led to local minima. By introducing structural variations—sinusoidal components, peaks, and varying sequence lengths—we increase exploration of the solution space. Additionally, we employ research-based gradient descent with stability measures to ensure convergence to the global minimum.

**Mathematical Specification**  
We define a sequence of non-negative heights \( h = \{h_0, h_1, \ldots, h_{n-1}\} \). The goal is to minimize the evaluation metric:  
\[ \text{score} = \frac{2n \cdot \max(\text{convolve}(h, h))}{(\sum h)^2} \]  
subject to:  
1. \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1000 \)  
2. \( \sum h_i \geq 0.01 \)  

**Representation:**  
- **Structure Optimization:**  
  - Variation 1: **Sinusoidal Perturbations** \( h_i = \exp(-\beta \cdot (i - i_0)^2) + \gamma \cdot \max(\cos(f \cdot i + \phi), 0) \)  
  - Variation 2: **Steps plus Peaks** \( h_i = \text{step}_i + \sum_{j=1}^{k} \exp(-\lambda \cdot (i - p_j)^2) \)  
  - Variation 3: **Random Sampling** \( h_i = \text{uniform}_i + \delta_{ij} \cdot \text{noise}(i) \)  

**Bayesian Optimization (BO):**  
The BO expert optimizes the sequence length \( L \) (from 1 to 600) and a set of influence parameters \( \alpha \) (peak amplitudes) or \( \beta \) (frequencies). The state space is:  
\[ \mathbf{p} = [\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \ldots, \alpha_k, \beta_1, \beta_2, \ldots, \beta_m] \]  
where \( \beta \) defines the frequency and phase.  

**Stochastic Gradient (SGD):**

  - The sequence starts with a set of influence parameters optimized by the previous expert.
