- sum(λ_j) = 1

2. Given the active set and t, solve for λ (this is a linear system: maximize sum(a*a)[k] - t subject to... actually it's the dual)
3. Given λ and the active set, solve for a (this is the constraint that the weighted autocorrelation is constant)

def _normalize(a):
    a = np.asarray(a, dtype=np.float64).copy()
    a = np.where(np.isfinite(a), a, 0.0)

def _fft_conv_self(a, L, m):
    F = np.fft.rfft(a, L)
