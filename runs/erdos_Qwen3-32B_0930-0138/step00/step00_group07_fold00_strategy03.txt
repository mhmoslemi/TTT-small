# Mathematical specification

1. 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 for all i

The objective is to minimize:
C₅ = max_{|k| < n} [ (h ⊛ (1 - h))[k] * (2/n) ]

where ⊛ denotes cross-correlation. This can be expressed as:
C₅ = (2/n) * max_k (h ⋆ (1 - h))[k]

with h ⋆ (1 - h) computed via np.correlate.

The DE algorithm maintains a population P of size NP, where each individual p ∈ P is a feasible h vector. The mutation, crossover, and selection steps are designed to explore the space while respecting the constraints.
