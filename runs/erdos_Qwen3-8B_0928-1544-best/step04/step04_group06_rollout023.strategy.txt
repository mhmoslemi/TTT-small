CMA-ES is particularly effective for non-convex, non-smooth optimization problems with unknown search space properties. It maintains a population of candidate solutions and evolves them by learning the covariance matrix of successful solutions, allowing it to adaptively explore promising regions of the search space.

Key advantages:
1. Global search capability: Better handles local optima than SLSQP or gradient methods.

4. Robustness: Operates effectively even with a small population size relative to problem dimensionality.

This approach differs fundamentally from previous methods by:
- Using evolutionary adaptation rather than gradient descent.

$$C_5 = \max_{k} \left(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i (1 - h_{(i + k) \mod n}) \cdot \Delta x \right)$$
where $\Delta x = 2.0 / n$.

### Constraints
1. Element-wise bounds: $0 \leq h_i \leq 1$.
2. Sum constraint: $\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i = n / 2$.

1. Clip $h$ to $[0, 1]$.
2. Scale to meet the sum constraint:
   $$h_{\text{final}} = \frac{h}{\sum h} \cdot \frac{n}{2}$$
