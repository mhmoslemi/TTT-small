1. Approach and Rationale

The optimization is over a high-dimensional space (length 2535) with box constraints (each coefficient between 0 and 1000) and a single lower-level constraint (sum >=0.01). The objective function is defined as the max of the self-convolution of the clipped candidate, scaled by the sum of the clipped candidate. Since the function is highly nonconvex and expensive to evaluate (due to the convolution), we need a robust and efficient algorithm.

We use a gradient-based method mixed with random resampling to ensure diversity in our search. The rationale is to balance between fast gradient-based updates in the interior and occasionally jumping to completely different regions (random resampling or mass transfer) to avoid local minimization.
