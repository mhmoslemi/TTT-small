Our goal is to minimize the AC1 upper bound for a sequence of non-negative coefficients. The AC1 bound is defined as 2*n*max(convolve(a,a)) / (sum(a))^2. The key insight is that scaling the sequence does not change the metric, allowing us to optimize under the constraint that the sum of coefficients is 1. We use a projected gradient method with adaptive step sizes, combining additive updates with multiplicative updates to balance exploration and exploitation. To handle the large dimensionality efficiently, we approximate the full convolution gradient using only the top K largest values, reducing computational cost while maintaining sufficient accuracy.

Mathematical specification:

Objective(a) = 2*n * max_{k=0}^{2*n-2} convolve(a,a)[k]
