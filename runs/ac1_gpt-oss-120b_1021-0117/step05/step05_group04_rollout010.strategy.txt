## 2. Mathematical Specification  

### Variables  
- **Coefficient Vector**: $x ∈ ℝⁿ⁺$  
- **Autocorrelation**: $\|x ∗ x\|_∞ = \sup_k |(x ∗ x)_k|$  
- **AC1 Score**: $score(x) = \frac{2n}{(\sum x)^2} \cdot \|x ∗ x\|_∞$  

### Objective  
Minimize $score(x) \to \min$, subject to $x_i ≥ 0$, $\sum x_i ≥ 0.01$  

### Optimization Algorithm  
#### Step 1: Autocorrelation Analysis  
Compute ∞-norm using convolution:  
$$ \|x ∗ x\|_∞ = \max_k \sum_{i+j=k} x_i x_j $$  

#### Step 2: Polynomial-Based Gradient  
Approximate $f(x) = \|x ∗ x\|_∞$ with Chebyshev basis $T_m(z)$ truncated at degree d:  
$$ f(x) ≈ \sum_{m=0}^d c_m T_m\left(\frac{x \odot x}{\|x\|_2}\right) $$  

Gradient estimate:  
$$ \nabla f(x) ≈ \sum_{m=0}^d c_m \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j} T_m\left(\frac{x_k x_k}{\|x\|_2}\right) $$  

Verification via finite differencing:  
$$ \|\nabla f(x) - [\Delta x \mapsto (f(x+\Delta x) - f(x))/\|\Delta x\|]\|_∞ < \epsilon $$  

#### Step 3: Structured Update  
Apply conjugate gradient descent with projected updates:  
$$ y = x - \alpha \nabla f(x) $$  
$$ x_{new} = \min(\max(0, y), 1000) + \beta \cdot (x - x_{old}) $$  
where $\alpha$ from L-BFGS line search, $\beta$ momentum factor  

#### Step 4: Structural Modifications  
With probability $p = 0.2$:  
- **Spectral Inversion**: $x ← 1/x$ for indices with $x_i > 0.1$  
- **Component Pruning**: Remove bottom 10% smallest coefficients  
- **Magnitude Boost**: Increase extreme values (>90th percentile) by 10%
