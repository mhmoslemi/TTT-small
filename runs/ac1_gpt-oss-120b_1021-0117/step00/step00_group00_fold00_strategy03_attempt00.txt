[#1 Approach and Rationale](#)
The core idea is to optimize the sequence coefficients in the spectral domain using a gradient-based method combined with a local search to refine the solution. This approach leverages the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to compute critical quantities cheaply during optimization, then validates potential candidates via direct convolution. It improves upon the initial perturbation method by handling higher dimensions efficiently and incorporating constraint enforcement.

🐡**Why effective**: It reduces O(n²) convolution costs to O(n log n) per update, enabling adaptive learning and convergence near the theoretical bound while ensuring non-negativity. Blocks are updated collectively within sparse constraints.

[#2 Mathematical Specification](#)
- **Objective**: Minimize AC1($\mathbf{a}$) = 2n $\cdot$ $\Vert \mathrm{full-conv}(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{a}) \Vert_\infty$ / (∑$a_j$)²
- **Constraints**: $[0, 1000]^{4806}$, $\sum a_j \ge 0.01$
- **Optimization Variables**: $\mathbf{a} \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$ ($n=4806$)
- **Gradient Estimation**:  
  For update direction $\mathbf{d}$:  
  $ \nabla \mathrm{AC1} \approx [\partial(\max_\tau \mathrm{AC1})/\partial a_i] $ via  
  $ \partial \mathrm{AC1} = \frac{2n \cdot (\partial \max_\tau \mathrm{AC1})}{(\sum a_j)^2}$  
  uses $\partial \max_\tau (\mathrm{full-conv}_\tau) = \sum_{\tau \text{ with } \mathrm{full-conv}_\tau=\max} \frac{\partial \mathrm{full-conv}_\tau}{\partial a_i}$  
  and $\partial \mathrm{full-conv}_\tau / \partial a_i = a_{\tau - i \text{ if valid}}$
- **Gradient Projection**: $\mathbf{d} \in \left\{ \mathbf{a} + \alpha (\mathbf{W}^\top [\nabla \mathrm{AC1}]) \mid \alpha \in \mathbb{R}, \mathbf{W} \text{ ensures non-negativity} \right\}$
