## 1. Approach and Rationale

The core approach involves minimizing a non-linear, non-convex objective function over a very high-dimensional (3809-variable) space with box constraints. The objective depends on the full convolution of the sequence, making derivative-based methods challenging due to the high computational cost of evaluating the gradient. Direct optimization is ruled out by the time budget and convolution cost.

The strategy balances exploration (seeking new regions) and exploitation (improving in promising areas) through three key components:
- * Implicit dimensionality reduction* using a harmonics decomposition to create sophisticated neighborhood candidates without explicit grids
- * Physics-inspired self-organization* through Fourier synthesis, leveraging autocorrelation properties implicit in the optimization problem
- * Multiscale trust region adaptation* dynamically adjusting search scales based on objective behavior

This differs fundamentally from previous approaches by:
1. Avoiding expensive dense matrix operations while maintaining rigorous mathematical grounding
2. Using harmonic analysis to capture autocorrelation structures without explicit enumeration
3. Incorporating physics-informed dynamics rather than stochastic perturbations
