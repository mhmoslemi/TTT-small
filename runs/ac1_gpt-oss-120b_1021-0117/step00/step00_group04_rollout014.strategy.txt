We start with an initial candidate sequence (either the provided parent if available or a uniform sequence) and iteratively explore the space using exponential perturbations and simulated annealing while maintaining a block-based structure. This method balances exploration and exploitation by allowing occasional acceptance of worse solutions to escape local minima, which is crucial for optimizing a non-convex objective like the AC1 bound. The key limitation is the computational cost of full convolutions, but the block-based updating reduces the effective search space.
