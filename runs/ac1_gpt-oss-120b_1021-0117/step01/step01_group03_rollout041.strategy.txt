I approach this using a two-phase method: initialization from a known good heuristic sequence (the classic δ-function sequence) followed by a search for improvements using three distinct operations that preserve the normalization constraint while encouraging the sequence to decay smoothly to zero at the edges.

The goal is to find a sequence where the autocorrelation does not produce excessively large peaks. This is achieved by promoting sequences that are concentrated in the middle and decay to zero at both ends. The decay property helps reduce the maximum autocorrelation value, which directly reduces the AC1 bound.
