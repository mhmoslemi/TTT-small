This is NOT circular. It's linear correlation. So the "overlap" interpretation is:
∫₀² h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx

For k > 0: this integrates over x from 0 to 2-k
For k < 0: this integrates over x from -k to 2

So it's the overlap of h with (1-h) shifted, but only over the intersection of domains.

This means for a {0,1} function, the correlation at lag k is:
- For k ≥ 0: number of i in [0, n-1-k] where h[i]=1 and h[i+k]=0, times dx
- For k < 0: number of i in [|k|, n-1] where h[i]=1 and h[i-|k|]=0, times dx

The maximum over all k gives C₅.

For the "no wrap" case, the edges are treated differently. This means the optimal pattern might not be perfectly periodic.
