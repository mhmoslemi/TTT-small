    # Use water-filling

result[m] = sum_{i} h[i] * (1-h[i+m]) (where the sum is over valid indices)

For m=0: sum h[i]*(1-h[i]) = sum h[i] - sum h[i]² = n/2 - sum h[i]²
For 0-1 h: this is n/2 - n/2 = 0. Good.

For m≠0: sum_{i: i and i+m valid} h[i]*(1-h[i+m])

For 0-1 h, this counts the number of i where h[i]=1 and h[i+m]=0.
