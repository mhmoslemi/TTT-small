For Sturmian words: A Sturmian word of slope alpha and intercept rho is defined by:
w_i = floor((i+1)*alpha + rho) - floor(i*alpha + rho)

For alpha = 1/2, this gives the periodic pattern 101010... or 010101...
For alpha near 1/2 but not exactly, we get aperiodic patterns.

The golden ratio conjugate 1/phi = (sqrt(5)-1)/2 ≈ 0.618... gives the Fibonacci word.
