1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea  
The supplied evaluator is a finite-dimensional minimum-overlap problem: we must choose a feasible vector h in [0,1]^n with sum(h) = n/2 and minimize
    C(h) = max_m dx * (np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode="full"))[m],  dx = 2/n.
This is a nonsmooth, nonconvex max-of-quadratic objective. The previous approach mostly worked on a log-sum-exp surrogate of the maximum and then repaired the sum. Those methods can become weak near nonsmooth active sets: they smooth the maximum away from the true objective and then make decisions on a surrogate.

The proposed search keeps all decision priority on the true evaluator but replaces each local increase-move by a convex linearized minimax master problem. At the current feasible point h0, compute the exact value and gradient for every correlation lag, then solve the linear program
    minimize t
    subject to, for every lag,  t >= linearization of the corresponding lag function at h0,
