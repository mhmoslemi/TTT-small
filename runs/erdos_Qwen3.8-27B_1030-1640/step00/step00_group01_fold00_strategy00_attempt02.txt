The central idea is to replace the hard max-over-lags objective, which contains products h_i(1-h_{i+d}), by a smooth "smoothober" maximum (a softened maximum of all finite-support correlations), optimize that smooth objective with a nonlinear solver subject to the exact box and equality constraints, then gradually sharpen the smoothing and finally verify the candidate with the raw evaluator formula.
