1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea
The requested objective

  C₅(h) = max_k [np.correlate(h, 1-h, "full")] * dx

is finite-dimensional but nonsmooth and nonconvex. The plan optimizes the same rebuilt discrete representation of the parent problem wherever feasible. The main local engine optimizes a log-sum-exp smoothed approximation to the maximum, but no candidate is accepted on smooth value alone: every candidate is repaired to strict feasibility and scored with the original exact maximum using the prescribed np.correlate formula. A fallback is the best feasible current construction (the supplied parent, if valid), so the returned lower C5 is never worse than the validated parent.

The proposed search uses a sequence of resolutions anchored around the supplied 45-point parent. At each resolution it evolves a small population of feasible vectors using local smooth maximum optimization, perturbations around the best members, structured seed generators, and multi-lambda smoothing. A small amount of extra refinement is applied to the best population member before returning. The method is intentionally local/nonconvex and has no global-optimality guarantee; improvements are claimed only through exact recomputation.
