1.1 Core idea and why this differs from the historical method
The previous solver centered on deterministic active-set LP directions, small line searches, and resampling. It is efficient but gets trapped in narrow basins of the nonsmooth exact objective and cannot make the tiny improvement from about 0.38117 toward the target 0.3808.

This program is built around a coherent different search architecture:

1. **Global population search over the flexible feasible set** using `differential_evolution` (DE), coupled with a fast deterministic feasible-repair map. This immediately supplies broad exploration of the nonconvex bilinear landscape, rather than relying only on deterministic descent from the supplied parent.
2. **Smooth surrogate drivers inside DE**. Exact max objectives are highly cropped and nongradient. DE is therefore run with several objectives:
   - an exact max objective,
   - flattening p-norm/log-sum-exp surrogates matched to exact evaluation,
   - mixed objectives combining the two.
3. **Analytical smooth gradient polishing** after DE using `scipy.optimize.minimize` with `SLSQP`, with equality `sum(h)=n/2` and bounds `0≤h≤1`.
4. **Small-region exact LP/line-search polishing** for active constraints, to move between the smoothing objective and the exact objective.
5. **Structured binary/balanced-vector exploration and smoothing** over valid even-length balanced binary states, then smooth boolean transforms and exact objective reevaluation.
6. **Conservative best-solution retention**. Every candidate is repaired/verified, exact objective is recomputed with the authoritative task metric, and parent fallback is never destroyed.

The bottleneck addressed is not inadequate enough cycles of local descent; it is a search landscape containing nonigrations of active overlapping lags. Stochastic global population methods plus smooth-goodness of the stochastic surface navigate lags that are not active at the single parent.
