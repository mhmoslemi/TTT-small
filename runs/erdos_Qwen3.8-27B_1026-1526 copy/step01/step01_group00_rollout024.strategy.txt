1. Approach and rationale

This dual-level strategy addresses the high-dimensional optimization challenge (n=180-360) and non-convex objective function. The CMA-ES provides broader exploration capability to avoid local optima, while local optimization ensures fine-grained convergence. 

Key limitations addressed:
- Limited effectiveness of gradient-based methods in escaping local optima
- Insufficient exploration by traditional local search alone
- Inefficient evaluation of high-dimensional functions
- Difficulty in handling both continuous and sum constraints simultaneously

- n_points ∈ {180, 360} (fixed interval [0,2] with dx=2/n_points)
- h ∈ [0,1]^{n_points}
- target_sum = n_points/2 (fixed integral constraint)
- Based on covariance matrix adaptation evolution strategy (CMA-ES) for global optimization
- Local optimization uses gradient-based projected methods
- Objective: min-max integral overlap C5 defined by finite convolution

- Constraints: sum(h) = n_points/2 and 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 for all i

Implementation notes:
- Global optimization uses CMA-ES with adaptive covariance matrix
- Local optimization uses projected gradient descent with precise derivatives
