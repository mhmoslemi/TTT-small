**Approach 1: Gradient-based with better smoothing**

The key idea is to use the smooth approximation:
f(h) = (1/α) * log(sum_k exp(α * g_k(h)))

The gradient of f with respect to h[j] is:
∂f/∂h[j] = (1/sum_k exp(α*g_k)) * sum_k exp(α*g_k) * ∂g_k/∂h[j]

g_k = sum_i h[i] * (1-h[i+k]) * dx
