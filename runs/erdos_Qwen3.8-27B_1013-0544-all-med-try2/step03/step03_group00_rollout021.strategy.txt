   - \(\int_0^2 h(x)dx = 1\)  

   The discretization:  
   - \(dx = 2/n\)  
   - \(\sum h_i \cdot dx = 1 \implies \sum h_i = n/2\)  

   Gradient derivation (for refinement):  
   The gradient of the correlation for lag k is:  
   \[
   \nabla h_i = \begin{cases} 
   (1-h_{i-k}-h_i) & \text{if } i-k \geq 0 \\
   -h_i(1-h_{i+k}) & \text{if } i-k < 0
   \end{cases}
   \]
