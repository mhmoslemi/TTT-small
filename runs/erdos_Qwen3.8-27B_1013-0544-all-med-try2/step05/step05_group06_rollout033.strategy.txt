# Approach and Rationale

Our proposed solution uses a **gradient-based optimization with line search** that leverages the structure of the overlap integral. The key insight is to use **constrained optimization** and **truncated-Newton methods** (also known as ￼￼￼trust region methods) for faster convergence. Unlike the previous approach that relied heavily on gradient-free local search, our method:

1. Calculates the full gradient of the objective function
2. Uses entropy-based projection to maintain feasibility
3. Implements bounds-constrained optimization
4. Uses sparse gradient information to reduce computational cost

This method is expected to find better optima faster by escaping local minima while respecting all constraints. The algorithm balances exploration and exploitation by combining:

- Global linear search (using backtracking)
- Local gradient descent
- Entropy-based projection

The choice of truncated-Newton method is motivated by its efficiency in handling large-scale optimization problems with ￼￼￼nonlinear constraints, which our problem is. This differs from the previous approach that relied on SLSQP and pair descent which are gradient-free but typically converge slower in deep optimization.

            alpha *= rho
        return alpha
