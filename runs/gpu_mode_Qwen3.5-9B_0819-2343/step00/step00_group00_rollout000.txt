Below is the `custom_kernel` implementation. It moves the bottleneck—the $N^2 \times N$ accumulation (the $N^3$ operation)—into a Triton kernel. This kernel iterates over the `seq_len` dimension (`K`) to accumulate the product of `left` and `right` matrices.

To optimize for the L40S (Ada Lovelace):
1.  **Mixed Precision**: The main accumulation happens in `bfloat16` using Triton's `tl.dot` (which handles FP16/BF16 and uses tensor cores) for speed and memory bandwidth efficiency. The non-bottleneck Linear layers and normalizations remain in `float32` on the CPU/PyTorch side to preserve precision and avoid the overhead of casting small tensors.
2.  **Blocked Access**: We use a block size of 128 for the hidden dimension (`BLOCK_D`) to benefit from shared memory reuse and tensor core fusion.
