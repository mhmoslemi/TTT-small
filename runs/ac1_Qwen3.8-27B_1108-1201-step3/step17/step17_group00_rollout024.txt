where `w_k = exp(beta * c_k) / sum_m exp(beta * c_m)`

We can compute this efficiently:
- First compute all `c_k` via FFT (O(n log n))
- Compute `w_k` (O(n))
- `grad_j = (1/beta) * (sum_k w_k * a_{k-j} * [0<=k-j<n] + a_j * sum_k w_k * [0<=k-j<n])`
- The first sum is a convolution: `(w * a)[j]` (with appropriate range)
- The second sum is `a_j * (sum of w_k for k in [j, j+n-1])`

So the gradient can be computed in O(n log n) total! This is very efficient.
