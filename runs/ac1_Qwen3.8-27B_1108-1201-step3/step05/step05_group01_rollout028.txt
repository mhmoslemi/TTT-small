The auto-correlation of a Hann window is known to be a "triangular" shape (in the continuous limit). The peak is at lag 0.

In the continuous limit, if f(x) = 0.5*(1+cos(2πx)) on [0,1] (normalized to have integral 1), then:
integral_0^1 f(x) dx = 0.5*(1 + 0) = 0.5 (since integral of cos(2πx) over [0,1] is 0).

The auto-correlation at lag 0: integral_0^1 (1-cos(2πx))^2 dx = integral (1 - 2cos(2πx) + cos^2(2πx)) dx = 1 - 0 + 1/2 = 3/2.

The auto-correlation at lag t: integral f(x)f(x+t) dx.

For f(x) = 1-cos(2πx):
R(t) = integral (1-cos(2πx))(1-cos(2π(x+t))) dx
= integral [1 - cos(2πx) - cos(2π(x+t)) + cos(2πx)cos(2π(x+t))] dx
= 1 - 0 - 0 + integral cos(2πx)cos(2π(x+t)) dx
= 1 + 1/2*cos(2πt)
