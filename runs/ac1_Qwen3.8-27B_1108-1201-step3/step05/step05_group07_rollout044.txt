Actually, I realize there's another important approach: **iterative peak shaving** in the autocorrelation domain.

Algorithm:
1. Start with a sequence (e.g., ramp).
2. Compute the autocorrelation b = conv(a, a).
3. Find the lag k* where b is maximum.
4. Find the pair (i, k*-i) that contributes most to b[k*].
5. Reduce a[i] and/or a[k*-i] and redistribute.
6. Repeat.
