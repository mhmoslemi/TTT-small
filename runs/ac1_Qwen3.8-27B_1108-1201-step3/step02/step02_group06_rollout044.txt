- The gradient of `conv[k]` for active lags must be "balanced" in some sense

This is essentially what the LP approach exploits. The LP finds `g` that maximizes `sum(g)` subject to `conv(f,g)[k] <= M` for all k. At the optimum, the active constraints define the feasible set.
