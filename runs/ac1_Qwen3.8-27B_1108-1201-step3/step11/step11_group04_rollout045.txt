    9. **Key mathematical insight for the subgradient approach**:
       
       The subgradient of $f(a) = \max_k (a*a)_k$ at $a$ is $2 \cdot a_{\text{rev}}$ where $a_{\text{rev}}$ is $a$ shifted to align with the argmax lag. Specifically, if $k^* = \arg\max_k (a*a)_k$, then the subgradient component at position $i$ is $2a_{k^*-i}$ (with appropriate zero-padding).

       So the update is: $a_i \leftarrow a_i \cdot \exp(-\eta \cdot 2a_{k^*-i})$ (multiplicative weights)
       Then normalize to sum to 1.

       - Maintain $y$ (the "lookahead" point) and $x$ (the current point)

       - Compute subgradient at $y$
       - $x_{\text{new}} \leftarrow x \cdot \exp(-\eta \cdot g(y))$
       - Normalize
