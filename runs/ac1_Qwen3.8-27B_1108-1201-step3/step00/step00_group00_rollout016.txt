**Approach: Iterative Peak Reduction (IPR)**
1. Normalize $a$ so $\sum a = 1$.
2. Compute $b = \text{convolve}(a, a)$.
3. Find the set of "hot" lags $H = \{k : b_k > \text{threshold}\}$.
4. For each hot lag $k$, the "pressure" on element $a_j$ is proportional to $a_{k-j}$.
5. Update: Reduce $a_j$ proportionally to the total pressure from hot lags, and redistribute the mass to elements with low pressure.

This is like a "reaction-diffusion" process: mass flows from high-pressure to low-pressure regions.

Let me formalize:
- Pressure on $a_j$: $P_j = \sum_{k \in H} a_{k-j}$ (sum over hot lags where $k-j$ is valid)
- Update: $a_j \leftarrow a_j \cdot (1 - \eta P_j)$, then renormalize.

Or in a multiplicative form: $a_j \leftarrow a_j \cdot \exp(-\eta P_j)$ (mirror descent on the simplex).
