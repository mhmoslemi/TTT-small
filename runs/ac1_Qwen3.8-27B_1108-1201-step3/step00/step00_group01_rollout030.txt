$\frac{\partial \tilde{c}}{\partial a_i} = \sum_k w_k \frac{\partial c_k}{\partial a_i} = \sum_k w_k a_{k-i}$

where $w_k = \frac{\exp(\beta c_k)}{\sum_j \exp(\beta c_j)}$.
