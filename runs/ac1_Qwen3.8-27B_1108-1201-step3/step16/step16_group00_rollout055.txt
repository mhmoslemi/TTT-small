For k ≥ n: Let's compute S(k) = sum_{i=k-n+1}^{n-1} (i+1)(k-i+1)
Let j = i+1, so j ranges from k-n+2 to n:
S(k) = sum_{j=k-n+2}^{n} j(k+2-j)

Let m = k+2, L = k-n+2, U = n:

= m*(L+U)(U-L+1)/2 - [U(U+1)(2U+1) - (L-1)L(2L-1)]/6

With L = k-n+2, U = n, m = k+2:

L+U = k-n+2+n = k+2 = m
