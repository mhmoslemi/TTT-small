- Use a stochastic gradient-free method (Randomized Coordinate Descent) for initial exploration.  
- Switch to a deterministic gradient-based method (Projected Gradient with Momentum) for fine-tuning, leveraging the sparsity of optimal solutions.

- Constraints: \( a_i \geq 0 \) for all \( i \), and non-differentiability of the max.  

Optimization uses projected gradient steps with a momentum term to accelerate convergence, restricted to coordinates that temporarily dominate the convolution.
