**Another creative approach: Iterative water-filling**

The idea: start with a uniform sequence. Identify the lag with the maximum convolution value. Then "push down" the convolution at that lag by adjusting `a`. This is similar to the "balancing" approach.

4. One way: take mass from positions `j` and `k*-j` and move it to positions that don't contribute to `c[k*]`.

This is essentially what the coordinate descent approach does, but we can be more systematic.
