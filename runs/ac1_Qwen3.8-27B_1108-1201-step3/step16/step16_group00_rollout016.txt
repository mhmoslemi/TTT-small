OK so now I understand the problem much better. The key insight is:
- For non-symmetric sequences, the convolution peak can be lower than sum_a^2/n
- The optimal sequence tends to be "front-loaded" (larger values at the beginning)
- This creates an asymmetric convolution profile

For large n, I expect the optimal sequence to be a decreasing sequence (front-loaded). Let me verify with n=4.
