Additionally, we use geometric temperature reductions during the optimization process to transition from neighborhood evaluations to refined minimization steps.

We maintain two sequence representations:
- `a0`: The temperature-smoothed version of the candidate in log-spacer space for gradient descent.
- `a1`: The foregone evaluation sequence on the full convolution.

At each iteration, we:
1. Warm-start `a0` to satisfy normalization and non-negativity constraints.
2. Employ self-adaptive learning rates and projection schemes.
3. Combine the best of coordinate-descent steps and gradient-based iterations.

This dual representation ensures that even if a formulating part of our update causes a temporary degradation, we are always prepared with a feasible solution. We represent the auto-convolution problem using FFT-based convolution for efficient evaluation, and use detailed timing to distribute heavy computations across chosen optimization methods.

### 2. Mathematical Specifications

- **Sequence Structure**:
  - Input sequence `x` returned as a nonnegative list of length `n`.
  - Must satisfy `sum(x) >= 0.01`.
  - Auto-convolution computed as full convolution (length 2n-1), denoted `y` = `convolve(x, x)`
  
- **Integral Objective**:
  ```python
  score = (2 * n * max(y)) / (sum(x) ** 2)
  ```

- **Softmax Auto-convolution**:
  We introduce a softmax component to convert the sequence into logarithmically separable space. This allows continuous gradient operations and efficient updates. The `SoftMax_formulation` is defined via:
  ```python
  z = np.log(x + np.heaviside(x==0,1)*1e-300)
  ```

- **Surrogate Objective and Gradient**:
  The objective function `f(z)` is defined as `LogSumExp` of the auto-convolution contributions. However, we are minimizing `score` and using a Lipschitz surrogate for the gradient. The formulation includes:
  - **Duality-based adjustment** for the L2 penalty with adjustable scaling.
  - **Robustification step** that handles peaked auto-correlations specially.

### 3. Implementation Procedure

#### Initialization and Input Handling
1. We take the `parent_sequence_height` from `height_sequence_1` if available, otherwise default to `np.ones(768)/768`.
2. We enforce consistency with boundary conditions: `sequence_length = parent_length`, say `768` if parent exists, `512` otherwise. We adjust the temperature and damping parameters.

####Core Optimization Loop
3. We initialize `a0` as a tetrahedral sequence (not at the peak) by first building a tetrahedral simplex in log-space and then taking softmax.
4. Then, we begin an iterative process where we sample several variations around `a0` to ensure we don't stay stuck in local minima:

   - **GradientUpdates**: We take a gradient descent step in log-space, but only for coordinates that are active in the auto-convolution maxima. The active coordinates are determined by the top-20 contributions in the auto-convolution convolution.

   - **Convexification**: We project `a0` onto the probability simplex (simple conditions requiring that the sum is above 0.01) and then use a convex programming relaxation (primarily L-BFGS with geometric damping) to re-softmaxize.

   - **Importance Sampling**: We select subsets and move mass from non-contributory regions to contributory ones. This includes:

        - **The gold standard**: After initial convergent steps, use `NesterovAcceleration` to push the center value above the prior (as the previous strategy did).

   - **AdaptiveDamping**: Introduce a damping factor `γ` in the auto-convolution that smoothly softens the maximum, allowing even convergence within `evaluations`. The damping factor starts at `0.7` and adapts every `100` steps, with `γ` decreasing between iterations.

####TerminationHandlers
5. At every `lap` (every `10` seconds), we:
   - Re-evaluate the auto-convolution to update `score1`.
   - Sample three variations: wild tetrahedral adjustment, damped interpolation, and direct peak shaving.
   - Use `mirror_descent` in small steps (`20` iterations) to move away from local convergence.

6. If the best score shows no improvement for `200` consecutive steps, we trigger a `true_restarting` mechanism:

   - We double the sequence length (from `768` to `1536`) and then optimize with geometrically down-scaled damping from `μ=10.0` to `μ=0.1`.
