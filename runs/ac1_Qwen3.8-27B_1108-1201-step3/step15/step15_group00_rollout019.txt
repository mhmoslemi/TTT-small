Then sum |A(ω)|² = N, so sum a_i² = 1 (by Parseval: sum |A(ω)|² = N * sum a_i²). This means sum a_i² = 1 and sum a_i = 1, which by Cauchy-Schwarz means all a_i are equal (a_i = 1/n). The key insight is: we want the auto-convolution peak to be small, which means we want |A(ω)|² to have its "energy" spread out in the time domain (i.e., its inverse DFT should be flat). But |A(ω)|² is non-negative, so its inverse DFT at k=0 is always positive and equals (1/N) sum |A(ω)|² = ||a||²₂.

The peak of the auto-convolution is at some lag k*, and its value is:
c[k*] = (1/N) sum_ω |A(ω)|² e^{iωk*}

For this to be small (smaller than ||a||²₂ which is c-average), we need the |A(ω)|² to have "destructive interference" at lag k*. This means |A(ω)|² should be modulated (not flat) in a way that cancels at specific lags.
