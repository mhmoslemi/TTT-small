1. Approach and rationale
The central idea is to represent the step function as a Fourier series with fixed integration constraint and optimize the coefficients to minimize the maximum cross-correlation integral. Unlike the parent approach that used coordinate descent and simplex method, this method:
1. Uses low-frequency Fourier series to reduce oscillation and maintain feasibility
2. Imposes periodic behavior inherent to the domain [0,2]
3. Automatically satisfies integral constraint
4. More direct control over correlation structure through frequency selection

The main bottleneck is handling the [0,1] and integral constraints directly. J=2 and J=3 are chosen to balance between complexity and performance, as higher J values require more optimization time without proportional gains. This approach generates smoother functions than comb-like constructions, which helps maintain lower correlation values.

2. Mathematical specification
- Representation: h(x) = 0.5 + ∑_{j=1}^{J} (a_j cos(jπx) + b_j sin(jπx))
