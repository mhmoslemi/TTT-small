**1. Approach and Rationale:**  
We enhance a local search using **Simulated Annealing (SA)** for its ability to escape local optima while maintaining feasibility. The key steps are:  
- **Binary step functions:** Represent solutions with step functions (binary choices, 0–1 values) on a discrete grid.  
- **Feasibility enforcement:** Use projection to ensure exactly 125 ones (integral 1) via mass adjustment.  
- **Correlation-based evaluation:** Compute the overlap integral via cross-correlation, returning the normalized maximum.  
- **Temperature schedule:** Anneal by exponentiation to balance exploration early and convergence later.  
- **Greedy repair:** Transition between binary swaps to fix feasibility dynamically.  

**2. Mathematical Specification:**  
**Variables:**  
- \( h \in \mathbb{R}^n \), \( x_i = i \cdot \frac{2}{n} \) (grid points).  
- Constraint: \( \sum h_i \cdot dx = 1 \rightarrow \sum h_i = \frac{n}{2} \) (125 ones).  

**Objective:**  
\[ C_5 = \max_k \int_0^2 h(x) (1 - h(x+k))  dx \]  
  Discretized: Compute `np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full') * dx` then take the maximum.
