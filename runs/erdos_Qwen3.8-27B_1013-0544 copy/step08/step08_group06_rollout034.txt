1. Compute A[m] for all m (the linear coefficient)
2. For m ≠ n-1: new_c[m] = c[m] + δ * A[m]
3. For m = n-1: new_c[n-1] = c[n-1] + δ * A[n-1] - 2δ²
4. Find δ that minimizes max_m new_c[m]
