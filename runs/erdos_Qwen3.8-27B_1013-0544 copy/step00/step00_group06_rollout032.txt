To minimize c_{k*}, we want h[n+k*] and h[n] to be "anti-correlated" at lag k*: when h[n+k*] is large, h[n] should also be large (so 1-h[n] is small).

This suggests that the optimal h should have a "self-repulsive" structure at the critical lag: h(x) and h(x+k*) should be as correlated as possible (both high or both low).

But this constraint must be balanced against other lags. The optimal solution is a compromise.
