For each such i, the change is:
h_new[i+k]*(1-h_new[i]) - h_old[i+k]*(1-h_old[i])

Let me just compute this directly:
h_new[t] = h_old[t] + delta[t] where delta[i0] = +1, delta[j0] = -1.

h_new[i+k]*(1-h_new[i]) - h_old[i+k]*(1-h_old[i])
= (h_old[i+k] + delta[i+k])*(1-h_old[i]-delta[i]) - h_old[i+k]*(1-h_old[i])
= h_old[i+k]*(1-h_old[i]) - h_old[i+k]*delta[i] + delta[i+k]*(1-h_old[i]) - delta[i+k]*delta[i] - h_old[i+k]*(1-h_old[i])
= -h_old[i+k]*delta[i] + delta[i+k]*(1-h_old[i]) - delta[i+k]*delta[i]

So the change for each affected i is:
-h_old[i+k]*delta[i] + delta[i+k]*(1-h_old[i]) - delta[i+k]*delta[i]

The total change in c[m] is the sum over affected i's.
