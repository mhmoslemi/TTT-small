The central idea is to:

- Represent the piecewise constant function using coefficients of orthogonal polynomials on [0,2]
- Smooth the non-differentiable max operation using a tailored exponential transformation
- Employ a two-phase optimization: (1) an initial geometric search, and (2) a coordinate-wise refinement

The main bottleneck we address is the high computational cost of the original SLSQP method by replacing it with a more efficient coordinate-wise optimization that maintains the descent direction globally while ensuring constraint satisfaction.
