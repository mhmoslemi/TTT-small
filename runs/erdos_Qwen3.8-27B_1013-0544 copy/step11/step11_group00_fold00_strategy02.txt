Approach and Rationale:
Our approach is to find a function structure that minimizes overlap by avoiding alignment at different shifts. We use a multi-modal strategy that combines: (1) sinusoidal patterns that spread mass, (2) candidate families from sparse representations, (3) asynchronous dimension adjustment, and (4) target-adaptive optimization. The key insight is that overlap reduction occurs when peaks in one region are offset from their shifted counterparts in another, achieved by extremal contrast patterns.
