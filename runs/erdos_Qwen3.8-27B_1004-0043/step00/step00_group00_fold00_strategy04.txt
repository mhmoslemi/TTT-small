## Approach and Rationale

We propose a particle swarm optimization (PSO) approach to tackle the Erdős minimum overlap problem. PSO is particularly suited for non-convex optimization problems where traditional gradient-based methods may struggle with local optima. The strategy uses a swarm of particles to explore the solution space, each representing a potential step function h. Particles update their positions based on their own best solution and the swarm's best solution, guided by the overlap integral objective.

Key advantages:
1. **Global Search**: PSO explores multiple regions of the solution space simultaneously.
2. **Robustness**: Less likely to get stuck in local minima compared to gradient descent.
3. **Parallelizability**: Evaluations can be vectorized for efficiency.

Limitations:
1. **Computational Cost**: Each particle requires a full correlation evaluation.
2. **Parameter Sensitivity**: Swarm performance depends on inertia, cognitive, and social weights.
