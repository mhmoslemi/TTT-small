1. Approach and rationale

This strategy uses sparse grid representation and entropy-based optimization to minimize the maximum correlation. Sparse grids allow us to represent the function with fewer degrees of freedom while maintaining accuracy, focusing on regions of high overlap. The entropy-based objective encourages sparse activity in the function, which can reduce peak correlations. This differs from previous approaches by explicitly balancing sparsity and smoothness through entropy, and by using a novel grid generation method based on scattered data interpolation. The primary limitation is that the optimization may struggle with sharp discontinuities, but we incorporate constraints to prevent extreme values.

2. Mathematical specification

Variables:
- $h(x) \in [0,1]$ for $x \in [0,2]$, with $\int_0^2 h(x)dx = 1$
- Discretized as $\mathbf{h} \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$ with $N$ points, $0 \leq h_i \leq 1$, $\sum h_i = N/2$
- Objective: $C_5 = \max_k \int h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx$
- Constraints: $0 \leq h_i \leq 1$ and $\sum h_i = N/2$

Representation:
- Use sparse grid points $X = \{\tau_j\}_{j=1}^M$ with weights $w_j$
- Reconstruct function via $h(x) = \sum w_j \phi(\|x-\tau_j\|)$

Objective:
- $f(\mathbf{h}) = \max_k |\langle h, g_k \rangle| + \alpha \langle h, -\log h \rangle$
