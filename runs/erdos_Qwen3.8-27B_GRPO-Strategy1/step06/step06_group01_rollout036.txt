- From h[i-L] with i-L=j (i=j+L, L+j < N, i < N): h[j+L] * (-1)

And for m = N-1 (L=0):
corr[m] = Σ_i h[i]*(1-h[i]) = Σ_i h[i] - Σ_i h[i]²
∂/∂h[j] = 1 - 2h[j]
