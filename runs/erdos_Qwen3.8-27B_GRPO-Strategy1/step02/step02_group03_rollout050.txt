2. As h[i-s] when i-s=j, i.e., i=j+s: contributes -h[j+s] if 0<=j+s<=n-1

So d(cr[k])/d(h[j]) = (1-h[j-s]) * [0<=j-s<=n-1] - h[j+s] * [0<=j+s<=n-1]

where s = k-(n-1).

For the gradient of the softmax objective:
d/dh[j] [log(sum_k exp(beta*cr[k]))] = sum_k [exp(beta*cr[k])/sum_m exp(beta*cr[m])] * d(cr[k])/d(h[j])
= sum_k w[k] * d(cr[k])/d(h[j])

where w[k] = exp(beta*(cr[k]-cr.max()))/sum_m exp(beta*(cr[m]-cr.max()))

This matches the previous code's formula. Good.

        g = grad_c5(h_look, dx, b)

                # Perturb and restart

                stuck = 0
