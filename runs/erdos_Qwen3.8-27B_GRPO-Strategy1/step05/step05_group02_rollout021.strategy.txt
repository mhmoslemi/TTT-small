\max_k \ dx \cdot \rho(k) = \max_k \ dx \cdot \sum_i h[i] \cdot (1 - h[(i+k) \mod (n_{\text{points}}-1), \text{wrapping}]=1
\]
but approximated via DFT to $(1/n_{\text{points}}) \max_k |\text{IDFT}(\text{DFT}(h) \odot \text{DFT}(g))|$ where $g=1-h$

**Formulation**:
\begin{align*}
\min_{h} & \left(\max_k |\langle h(x), (1-h(x+k))\rangle| + \epsilon\|\nabla h\|_1\right)\\
\text{s.t.} & \sum_i h[i] \cdot dx = 1 \\
& 0 \le h[i] \le 1 \forall i
\end{align*}
Approximated as quadratic program with sparse initializer $l_0$-like constraints.

**Constraints**: Both the integral and box dispre are enforced by dual projections after each optimization step.

**Variables**: Real-valued sample coefficients in $[0, 1]$

**Solver**: Use CVXOPT with interior-point solver for quadratic programs subject to bounds, unless initial guess is good. Add explicit $L^p$ sparsification penalties via equality constraints.
