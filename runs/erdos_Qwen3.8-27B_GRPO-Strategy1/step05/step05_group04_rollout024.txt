```python
            grad[:n-a] += w * (1.0 - h[a:])
            grad[a:] -= w * h[:n-a]
        else:
            grad += w * (1.0 - 2.0 * h)
    return grad
```

This is O(n²) per gradient evaluation (the loop over m_idx is O(n), and each iteration is O(n)). For n=160, that's 25,600 operations, which is fast.
