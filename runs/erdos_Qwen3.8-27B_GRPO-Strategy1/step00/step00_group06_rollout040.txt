This is related to the concept of "minimum autocorrelation" sequences or "Barker sequences" type problems.

For a perfectly spread binary sequence, at each lag k, roughly (n/2)*(n/2)/n = n/4 of the 1s would have a 0 at offset k. So the expected max would be around n/4 + some fluctuation.
