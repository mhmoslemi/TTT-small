For binary h, h[i]*(1-h[i+k]) = 1 when h[i]=1 and h[i+k]=0.

The sum over i of h[i]*(1-h[i+k]) = (number of 1s) - (number of 1s at i where h[i+k] is also 1)

= n/2 - autocorrelation at lag k

So minimizing max_k (n/2 - AC[k]) * dx is equivalent to maximizing min_k AC[k].

Where AC[k] = sum h[i]*h[i+k] (with appropriate boundary handling).

    corr = np.correlate(h, 1.0 - h, mode="full")

    corr = np.correlate(x, 1.0 - x, mode="full") * dx
