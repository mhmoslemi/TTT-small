So C₅ = max(all elements of the correlation array) * dx. This includes the lag-0 correlation.

For binary h, the lag-0 correlation is sum_i h[i]*(1-h[i]) = 0. For general h, it's sum_i h[i]*(1-h[i]) = sum_i h[i] - sum_i h[i]².

Since sum_i h[i] = n/2, the lag-0 value is n/2 - sum_i h[i]². To minimize this, we want sum_i h[i]² to be as large as possible, which means h should be as close to binary as possible (0s and 1s).
