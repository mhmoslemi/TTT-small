Specifically, if worst shift is k (max O_k), then O_k = S_k - A_k. To reduce O_k, we can either reduce S_k (decrease number of ones in left part) or increase A_k (increase overlapping pairs). Since total number of ones fixed, reducing S_k for shift k means moving ones out of left part (i < n - k) into right part (i >= n - k). This reduces number of ones in overlapping region. Also we can increase A_k by moving a 1 at position i where i < n - k, to position i + k which is also 1 (or moving a 0 to a location that becomes a new overlapping pair). So targeted moves: pick a 1 in the left side (i < n - k) and a 0 in the right side i + k (maybe). Then swap them, moving the 1 to right side.

Simplify: To reduce O_k, we prefer to move ones toward the right end to reduce S_k, or cluster them so they appear in both sides.
