### 1.  Mathematical formulation (discretised)

1. **Variables** – Choose a modest number of grid points `N` (e.g. `N = 160` or the given 140).  
   The step function is represented by the vector `h ∈ ℝⁿ` with entries `h[i] ∈ [0,1]`.

2. **Mass constraint** – The integral condition becomes  
   \[
   \frac{2}{N}\sum_{i=0}^{N-1} h_i = 1 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \sum_i h_i = \frac{N}{2}.
   \]

3. **Objective** – For a shift of `s` grid steps (corresponding to a continuous shift `k = s·dx`) the overlap integral is  
   \[
   C(s) = \frac{2}{N}\sum_{i=0}^{N-1-s} h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+s}\bigr)
   = \frac{2}{N}\Bigl(\sum_{i=0}^{N-1-s} h_i - \sum_{i=0}^{N-1-s} h_i h_{i+s}\Bigr).
   \]
   The quantity to minimise is  
   \[
   C_5(h)=\max_{s\in\{-N+1,\dots,N-1\}} C(s).
   \]

4. **Gradient of a single shift** – For a fixed shift `s` the derivative of `C(s)` w.r.t. each component is
   \[
   \frac{\partial C(s)}{\partial h_i}= 
   \begin{cases}
      \frac{2}{N}\bigl(1-h_{i+s}\bigr)   & 0\le i\le N-1-s,\\[4pt]
      0                                   & \text{otherwise},
   \end{cases}
   \qquad
   \frac{\partial C(s)}{\partial h_{i+s}}=
   \begin{cases}
      -\frac{2}{N}h_i & 0\le i\le N-1-s,\\[4pt]
      0                & \text{otherwise}.
   \end{cases}
   \]

   For a negative shift the same formulas apply with the indices swapped.
