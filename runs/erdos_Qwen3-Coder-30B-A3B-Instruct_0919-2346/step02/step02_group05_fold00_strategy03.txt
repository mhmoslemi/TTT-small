**4. Stage B – Smooth‑max surrogate and projected gradient ascent**

The exact max is replaced by a smooth upper bound  

\[
\Phi_\beta(h)=\frac{1}{\beta}\log\!\Bigl(\sum_k\exp\bigl(\beta\,c_k(h)\bigr)\Bigr),\qquad

\]

For large β, Φ_β ≈ max c_k and its gradient is analytic:

\[
\frac{\partial\Phi_\beta}{\partial h_j}
=
\frac{\displaystyle\sum_k w_k\bigl(1-2h_{j+k}\bigr)}
     {\displaystyle\sum_k w_k},
\qquad 
w_k=\exp\bigl(\beta\,c_k(h)\bigr).
\]

**Algorithm** (projected gradient ascent, optionally L‑BFGS‑B):  

1. Initialise with the best binary vector from Stage A, cast to float.  
2. Loop while the allocated time remains:  

   a. Compute `c = np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full')*Δx`.
