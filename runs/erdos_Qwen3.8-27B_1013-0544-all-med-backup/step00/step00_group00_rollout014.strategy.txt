We address the Erdos minimum overlap problem by reformulating it as a constrained optimization problem and using a multi-phase search strategy. The key innovations are:
1. Using a smooth relaxation of the box constraints to make the optimization landscape more navigable
2. Implementing gradient-based optimization with trust regions to balance local search and robustness
3. Adding a restart mechanism based on Chebyshev polynomials to explore different regions
4. Maintaining feasible solutions throughout the optimization by projecting steps

Our approach directly minimizes the overlap integral while ensuring the integral and box constraints. The original function's non-convexity is addressed through:
- A tailored penalty function for integral constraint
- Polynomial projection for step enforcement
- Trust-region control to prevent numerical instability
