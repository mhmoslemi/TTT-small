1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea

The selected construction will be produced by a multistart, feasibility-directed, exact one-dimensional search over the feasible sample manifold. The search is organized around a target bound tau: for a proposed vector direction v satisfying sum_i v_i = 0, every correlation value along h(s) = h + s v is exactly a quadratic polynomial in s. Therefore, the condition that every correlation be at most tau along that line is an exact one-dimensional problem: intersect all bounded intervals/subsets defined by quadratic inequalities with the interval imposed by 0 <= h_i + s v_i <= 1. A feasible interval on the line gives an exact candidate satisfying every discrete correlation constraint at tau, except for floating-point tolerance. The candidate is then jointly repaired and scored by the authoritative evaluator before it is accepted.

This differs from a black-box nonlinear-programming formulation and from a mixed-integer relaxation. It directly minimizes the actual returned quantity by checking exact feasibility at lowered objective levels. It also preserves both constraints constructively:

- the equality sum constraint is between the two exchange regions.
  A direction v has zero mean, so h + s v has exactly the same discrete sum up to controlled roundoff.
- the box constraints are handled by the allowable segment s interval before repair.
  Repair is only a final numerical equalization and is defined jointly, not by a naive clip-plus-rescale cascade.

The primary method is therefore best described as exact threshold feasibility for correlation polynomials on carefully chosen zero-mean lines. It addresses the main finite-dimensional bottleneck: the finite-support evaluator’s maximum over all signed correlations contains boundary terms and cannot safely be replaced by a circular autocorrelation or by an unconstrained relaxation.

1.2 Why this can improve parent and what is guaranteed

The supplied parent construction is retained, validated, and becomes the incumbent fallback. Starting from the parent or from valid variants, the search attempts to walk to any strictly lower exact score. It is not a global certificate and no claim of optimality is made. However, each accepted candidate satisfies the exact official evaluator bound and both feasibility constraints.

Fact: for any line h(s)=h+s v with sum_i v_i=0, the object's constraints can be expressed exactly by 2n-1 quadratic polynomials in s. Testing feasibility at a fixed tau along that line is therefore exact up to finite precision.
Hypothesis: the proposed panel of directions, including pairwise mass exchanges, active-edge exchange directions, and stochastic zero-mean directions, contains enough feasible descent lines to improve the supplied parent.
Workflow: The method gives a conservative local descent operator. If no descent line is found from a given start, the search changes start, resolution, threshold, or direction family. Works before the time limit welds valid fallback.
