    sum_i h_i = n / 2.

The problem is specifically square and nonconvex: every nonzero-lag correlation contains terms such as h_i(1 - h_j), hence product pairs. A direct convex formulation is unavailable, and previous smooth or nonconvex قولد approaches may stagnate at active local constraints.
