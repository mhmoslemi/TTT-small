The problem's non-convexity and combinatorial nature make conventional optimization methods prone to local optima. By decomposing the problem into manageable subproblems, DP allows us to handle the constraints directly and track multiple objectives simultaneously.

The approach is particularly suited for finding optimal step functions under integral constraints. It builds solutions incrementally, making decisions at each point while preserving feasibility, and integrates overlap calculations at each step to guide the optimality.
