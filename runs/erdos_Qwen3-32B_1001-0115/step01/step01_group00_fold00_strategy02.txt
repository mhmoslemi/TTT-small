### 2. Mathematical specification

#### Decision Variables
Define:
- $h \in [0,1]^{n_{points}}$ where $n_{points}=80$
- Binary variables $b_i \in \{0,1\}$ representing the idealized function
- Matrix $X \in \mathbb{S}^{n_{points}}$ such that $X = hh^T$

#### Objective
Minimize the maximum cross-correlation:
$$\min_{h} \max_{k} \left( \sum_{i=0}^{n_{points}-1} h_i (1 - h_{(i+k) \bmod n_{points}}) \right)$$

#### Relaxation
Reformulate using matrix variables:
$$\max_k \sum_{i,j} X_{i,j} A^{(k)}_{i,j}$$
where $A^{(k)}$ is a circulant matrix capturing the shift and overlap structure.

The problem becomes an SDP:
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \min_{X, t} \quad t \\
& \text{subject to} \\
& t \geq \sum_{i,j} X_{i,j} A^{(k)}_{i,j} \quad \forall k \\
& \text{Tr}(X) = \frac{n_{points}}{2} \quad (\text{sum constraint}) \\
& X \succeq 0 \quad (\text{positive semidefinite}) \\
& 0 \preceq X \preceq I \quad (\text{entrywise bounds})
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Rounding
Recover a feasible $h$ using:
$$
h_i = \begin{cases}
1 & \text{with probability } z_i \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
where $z$ is derived from the SDP solution.
