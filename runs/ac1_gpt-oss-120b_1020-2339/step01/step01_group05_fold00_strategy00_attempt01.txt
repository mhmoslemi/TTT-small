**Mathematical Specification**
Let $f = (f_0, \dots, f_{n-1}) \in \mathbb{R}^n$ be a vector of coefficients. The objective to minimize is:

$$J(f) = \frac{2n \cdot M(\mathbf{f})}{\left(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} f_i \right)^2}$$

where $M(\mathbf{f}) = \max_{k=0}^{2n-2} c_k$ and $(c_k) = \text{full convolution of } f$.

Gradient at the ith coordinate:

$$\frac{\partial J}{\partial f_i} = \frac{2n}{S^2} \cdot \frac{\partial M}{\partial f_i} - \frac{4n M}{S^3} \cdot f_i$$

where $S = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} f_j$.

Derivative of $M$ is $d_{i,K} = \sum_{p+q=K} f_p [p=i] + \sum_{p-q=K} f_q [q=i] = 2 \sum_{p=0}^{K} f_{K-p} [i \text{ appears in the first half}]$ unless $M(\mathbf{f})$ is not attained at the maximum.

Hessian Elements:

$$H_{ij} = \frac{\partial^2 J}{\partial f_i \partial f_j} = \frac{2n}{S^3} \left( \frac{\partial M}{\partial f_i} \frac{\partial d_j}{\partial f_j}|_{K} \right) + \text{similar terms} - \text{higher terms}$$

which are partially computed using the gradient sum.
