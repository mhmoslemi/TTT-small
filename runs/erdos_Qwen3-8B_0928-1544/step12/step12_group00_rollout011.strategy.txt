### Mathematical specification

Define the optimization problem as:
$$C_5 = \max_{k \in [-2,2]} \int_{0}^{2} h(x)(1 - h(x + k)) dx$$
Subject to:
1. $$h(x) \in [0,1]$$
2. $$\int_{0}^{2} h(x) dx = 1$$

We convert this to a discrete problem with:
- $$h \in [0,1]^{n}$$
- $$\sum_{i=1}^{n} h_i = n/2$$
- Objective: $$C_5 = \max_{k} \text{corr}(h, 1 - h, k)$$

The correlation calculation is as per `np.correlate`, which computes cross-correlation:
$$\text{corr}(h, 1-h, k) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_i (1 - h_{i+k}) \text{ when } i + k \leq n$$
