# Particle Swarm Optimization for Erdős Minimum Overlap Problem

## 1. Approach and Rationale

**Central Idea**:  
We propose a particle swarm optimization (PSO) approach to find a step function \( h \) that minimizes the overlap integral \( C_5 \). PSO is a population-based metaheuristic that balances exploration and exploitation through swarm intelligence, making it suitable for the non-convex nature of the problem.

**Why It Could Improve**:  
- **Diverse Exploration**: PSO maintains a swarm of candidate solutions, reducing the risk of converging to local optima.
- **Efficient Constraint Handling**: Directly enforces the box constraints \( h_i \in [0, 1] \) and the integral constraint \( \sum h_i = n/2 \) through projection.
- **Adaptive Search**: Particles adapt their search behavior based on historical performance, potentially escaping poor regions of the search space.

**Bottlenecks Addressed**:  
- Non-convexity of the objective function due to cross-correlation.
- Efficient handling of constraints and boundary conditions.

**Limitations**:  
- PSO does not guarantee global optimality but has shown empirical success on similar problems.
- Parameter tuning required for swarm dynamics to balance exploration and exploitation.

## 2. Mathematical Specification

### Variables:
- \( h \in \mathbb{R}^{n} \), where \( n = n_{\text{points}} \).
- \( dx = 2.0 / n \).
- \( k \in \{ -n+1, ..., n-1 \} \) are the lags.

### Objective Function:
Minimize \( C_5 \):
\[
C_5 = \max_{k} \left( \text{np.correlate}(h, 1 - h, \text{"full"})[n + k - 1] \times dx \right)
\]

### Constraints:
1. \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \) for all \( i \).
2. \( \sum_{i=1}^n h_i = \frac{n}{2} \).

### PSO Dynamics:
Each particle \( j \) has:
- Position: \( \mathbf{p}_j \in \mathbb{R}^n \).
- Velocity: \( \mathbf{v}_j \in \mathbb{R}^n \).
- Best position: \( \mathbf{p}_j^* \).
- Global best position: \( \mathbf{p}_g^* \).

Update rules:
\[
\mathbf{v}_j(t+1) = \omega \mathbf{v}_j(t) + c_1 r_1 (\mathbf{p}_j^* - \mathbf{p}_j(t)) + c_2 r_2 (\mathbf{p}_g^* - \mathbf{p}_j(t))
\]
\[
\mathbf{p}_j(t+1) = \mathbf{p}_j(t) + \mathbf{v}_j(t+1)
\]
Where:
- \( \omega \) is the inertia weight (0.7 initially).
- \( c_1 = 2 \), \( c_2 = 2 \) are cognitive and social coefficients.
- \( r_1, r_2 \) are random numbers in [0, 1].

### Projection to Feasible Region:
After updating \( \mathbf{p}_j \), project onto [0, 1]^n and normalize to satisfy \( \sum h = n/2 \):
\[
\mathbf{p}_j' = \text{clip}(\mathbf{p}_j, 0, 1)
\]
\[
\mathbf{p}_j' = \mathbf{p}_j' / \left( \sum \mathbf{p}_j' \right) \times \frac{n}{2}
\]
