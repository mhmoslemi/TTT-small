2. Mathematical specification
We minimize the AC1 objective function:

    AC1(a) = (2 * n * max_bc(convolve(a, a))) / (sum(a))^2

- a ∈ [0, 1000]^n is the sequence.
- convolve(a)(k) is the linear convolution at index k.

The optimization is performed subject to:
- sum(a) ≥ 0.01
- 0 ≤ a_i ≤ 1000 for all i
