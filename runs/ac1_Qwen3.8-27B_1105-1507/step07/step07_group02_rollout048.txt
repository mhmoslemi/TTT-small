The problem is that the cross terms at the center always add up.

Let me think about this more carefully. The center value (a*a)[n-1] = sum_i a[i]*a[n-1-i].

By the rearrangement inequality, for non-negative a, the minimum of sum_i a[i]*b[π(i)] over all permutations π is achieved when a and b are sorted in opposite orders.

Here, b[i] = a[n-1-i] (the reverse of a). So we're computing a · reverse(a).

If a is sorted in decreasing order (a[0] >= a[1] >= ... >= a[n-1]), then reverse(a) is in increasing order, and by the rearrangement inequality, a · reverse(a) is minimized.

So to minimize (a*a)[n-1], we should sort a in decreasing (or increasing) order!

But (a*a)[n-1] is NOT the max of the convolution in general. The max could be at a different position.

However, if we sort a in decreasing order, then (a*a)[0] = a[0]^2 is likely the largest (since a[0] is the largest element). So the max might be at k=0.
