### Approach and rationale

The current solution employs a multi-stage optimization process starting from a parent sequence, which involves initializing with a large length sequence (1500), performing smoothed gradient descent, then polishing with high-radius SOCP and LP steps. While this approach generates sequences that pass safety checks but uses inefficient numerical optimization methods that struggle to achieve meaningful progress within the time budget.

Our enhanced strategy is centered on a hierarchical approach that combines deterministic and probabilistic optimization elements to create sequences with lower objectives. The **Reflection Component** will generate initial sequences by reflecting the parent across multiple geometric and algebraic operations to create diverse starting points, ensuring greater exploration early in the search.

- `n`: Sequence length, ranging from 400 to 2000
