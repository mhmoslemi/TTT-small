The key properties are:
- The sequence is non-negative.
- It has a fixed length (2535 from the parent).
- The full convolution (mode="full") produces 2n-1 coefficients.

We start with the provided parent sequence and perform gradient-based optimization while ensuring non-negativity and value limits (0 to 1000). The method is inspired by the initial LP-based approach but adapts it to handle the scale-invariant property and to avoid local minima through structured perturbations.
