Let me think more carefully about the iterative flattening algorithm.

The key update is:
- Compute $g = a * a$ (full convolution).
- Find the "active" positions where $g(k)$ is above a threshold.
- For each active position $k$, the "pressure" on $a_i$ is $a_{k-i}$ (with zero-padding).
- The total pressure is $p_i = \sum_{k \in \text{active}} w_k a_{k-i}$.
- Update: $a_i \leftarrow a_i \cdot \exp(-\eta \cdot (p_i - \bar{p}))$ (multiplicative update to maintain non-negativity).
- Renormalize.

The multiplicative update is nice because it automatically maintains non-negativity. The centered update ($p_i - \bar{p}$) approximately maintains the sum.

For efficiency, I can compute the pressure vector using convolution:
$p = \sum_{k \in \text{active}} w_k \cdot \text{reverse\_and\_pad}(a, k)$

This can be computed as a convolution of $a$ with a "sparse" sequence that has weights at the active positions.
