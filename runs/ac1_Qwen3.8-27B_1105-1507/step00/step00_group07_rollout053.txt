    # This is the "sliding dot product" of w (shifted) with a.
    # We can compute this as: for each i, take w[i:i+n] and dot with a.
    # This is equivalent to the convolution of w with reversed a, evaluated at specific points.
    # Or: it's the cross-correlation.

    # Let a_rev = a[::-1] (reversed a, length n).

    # = sum_{m=0}^{n-1} w[m+i] * a[m] (with w[m+i] = 0 for m+i >= 2n-1)
