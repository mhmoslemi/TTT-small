  conv_new[k] = sum_i x'[i] * x'[k-i]

  - i≠j, k-i=j (i.e., i=k-j): x[k-j]*x'[j] = x[k-j]*(x[j]+delta) = x[k-j]*x[j] + delta*x[k-j]

  But when k=2j, the original had x[j]*x[j] = x[j]^2, and the new has (x[j]+delta)^2 = x[j]^2 + 2delta*x[j] + delta^2. So the change is 2delta*x[j] + delta^2. And 2*delta*x[k-j] = 2*delta*x[j]. So it's consistent: change = 2*delta*x[k-j] + delta^2 (when k=2j) or 2*delta*x[k-j] (when k≠2j).
