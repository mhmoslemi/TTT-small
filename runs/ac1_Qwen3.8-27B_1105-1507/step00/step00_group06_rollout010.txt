For k = N-1 (center), Q has 1s at positions (0,N-1), (1,N-2), ..., which is a permutation matrix (specifically, the reverse-identity). The reverse-identity matrix has eigenvalues +1 (with multiplicity ceil(N/2)) and -1 (with multiplicity floor(N/2)). So it's NOT PSD!

This means the problem is NOT convex. The constraint f^T Q_k f <= t is not a convex constraint.
