Let me think about this more carefully. The issue is that for the "middle" index k = n-1, we have:
conv[n-1] = sum_{i=0}^{n-1} a[i]*a[n-1-i]

This is the "reversed" dot product. For the sequence to have a small value here, we need a[i] and a[n-1-i] to be "anti-correlated" (one large, one small, for each pair).

But for other indices k, the convolution involves different pairings. The challenge is to make ALL of them small simultaneously.

One approach that might work: make the sequence have a specific structure that "spreads out" the contributions.
