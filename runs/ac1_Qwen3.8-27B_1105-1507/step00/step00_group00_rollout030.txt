Dual:
$\min M \cdot \mathbf{1}^T y$ subject to $A^T y \ge \mathbf{1}$, $y \ge 0$.

The dual has $2n-1$ variables and $n$ constraints. This is similar in size to the primal.

The dual interpretation: find non-negative weights $y_k$ on the constraints such that the "coverage" $A^T y$ is at least 1 everywhere, minimizing the total weight. The optimal dual objective is $M \cdot \sum y_k$, and by strong duality, this equals $\sum g^*$ (the optimal primal objective).

So $S^* = M \cdot \sum y_k^*$. We need $S^* > 1$, i.e., $M \cdot \sum y_k^* > 1$.

Since $M = \max(f*f)$ and $\sum f = 1$, we have $\sum(f*f) = 1$, so $M \ge 1/(2n-1)$. For large $n$, $M \ge 1/(2n)$ approximately.
