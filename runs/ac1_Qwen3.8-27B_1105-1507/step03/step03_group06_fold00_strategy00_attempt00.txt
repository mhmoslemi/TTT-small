### 1. Approach and Rationale

We wish to minimize the value J(a) = (2 * n * max_b) / (sum_a^2) for a fixed-length nonnegative sequence `a` (n=512 in this task). The objective depends nontrivially on the normalized autocorrelation of `a` — specifically, it scales with the maximum linear convolution of `a` (conv_mode='full').

The core idea is to use a spectral optimization approach directly inspired by compressed sensing and the properties of the discrete Fourier transform. We observe that while the autocorrelation operation is non-convex with respect to the nonnegative constraint, its Fourier transform representation provides valuable structure.

*   **Rationale**: Minimizing the maximum of `a * a_full_convolve` is challenging and dissipates fast due to the previous constructive LP methods. We aim for a fresh approach by considering the autocorrelation in the Fourier domain. Formant sequences (normal sequences optimized for acoustic-like design) often exhibit properties that reduce high-order temporal features such as sharp autocorrelation peaks. We hypothesize that sequences optimized for flat time-domain formants will possess lower J(a) values due to diffusion of energy across the convolution.
