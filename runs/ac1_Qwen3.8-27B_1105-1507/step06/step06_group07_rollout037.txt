This is simply 2 times the cross-correlation of a with a (shifted). Specifically:
dS_p/d(a[i]) = 2 * S_p^(1-p) * sum_k (conv[k])^(p-1) * a[k-i] (if 0 <= k-i < n)
= 2 * S_p^(1-p) * (w * a)[i]

where w[k] = (conv[k])^(p-1) and (w*a)[i] = sum_k w[k] * a[k-i] (with a[m]=0 for m out of range).

This is a cross-correlation, computable via FFT in O(n log n).
