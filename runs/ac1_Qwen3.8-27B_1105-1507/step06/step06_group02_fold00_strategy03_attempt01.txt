# Approach and Rationale

We approach minimizing the step function's autocorrelation maximum versus its L1 norm ratio using:
1. **Fourier-domain constraint optimization** - Enforces minimal autocorrelation magnitude
2. **Nonlinear gradient projection** - Balances energy across all autocorrelation channels
3. **Sparse representation swaps** - Conserves support points while reshaping patterns
4. **Iterative resampling with quality checks** - Diversifies candidates without straying from solutions

This optimization improves upon previous methods by:
- Using an eigenflow transformation to maintain spectral properties
- Implementing adaptive gradient clipping to ensure numerical stability
- Providing explicit fallbacks in the event of evaluation failures

Our primary goal is achieving the theoretical minimum of **1.5027**, while addressing the autocorrelation objective's sensitivity to energy distribution and convergence stability.

# Mathematical Specification

## Objective
Minimize $J(a) = \frac{2n \cdot \max(\text{conv}(a)))}{(\sum a)^2}$
where $\text{conv}(a)$ is the full autocorrelation (length $2n-1$)

## Inputs & Transformations
1. Initial base sequence $b$ (either provided parent or optimized uniform)
2. Eigenflow transformation: $f = T(b) = \max(0, \text{FFT}(b) \cdot |\text{FFT}(b)|^p \cdot \text{sign}(\text{FFT}(b)))$
3. Gradient flow projection: $g = \text{Proj}_g(f) = \frac{f}{\|f\|_{\text{flow}}}$
