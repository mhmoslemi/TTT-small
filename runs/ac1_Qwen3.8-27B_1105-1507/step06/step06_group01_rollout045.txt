- If we concentrate the mass, individual products are large but there are fewer of them contributing to each lag.

The optimal is somewhere in between.

= (n+1)*[n(n-1)/2 + n-1] - (n-1)n(2n-1)/6 - n(n-1)/2

= (n-1)(n+1)(n+6) / 6

c[n] = 4/(n^2(n+1)^2) * (n-1)(n+1)(n+6)/6 = 4(n-1)(n+6)/(6n^2(n+1))
