## 1. Approach and Rationale
We introduce a **constrained optimization framework** with **adaptive spectral preconditioning** and **frequency-domain regularization**. The core idea is to integrate the smooth Nesterov optimization with **explicit control over the bandlimit** and **training schedule**. This approach enhances coverage by:
- Using a **multi-modal initialization** with both parent-derived and analytical waterfalls
- Implementing a **two-phase optimization**: aggressive gradient descent followed by refined spectral shaping
- Tuning learning rates through **dimension-aware scheduling** based on problem and solution scales

This differs fundamentally from previous methods that primarily relied on spectral compression. We maintain their gradient-descent foundation but introduce constraints that prevent overspecialization and enable systematic improvement.

## 2. Mathematical Specification
We minimize the objective:  
\[J(a) = \frac{2 \cdot n \cdot \|f \star f\|_\infty}{(\sum a)^2}\]  
where \(f\) is the normalized sequence (\(\sum a = \sqrt{2n}\)).

### Variables
- \(a \in [0, \infty) \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n\)
- Normalized energy (\(\sum a = \sqrt{2n}\))

### Constraints
We enforce three implicit binding constraints:
1. **Non-negativity**: All coefficients must be ≥0
2. **Bandlimited Structure**: Ensures finite linear and quadratic phase decomposition
3. **Scale Invariance**: Normalization maintained throughout iterations

### Objective Transformation
For computational efficiency, we minimize:  
\[J_{\text{scaled}}(a) = \|f \star f\|_\infty / (\sum a)^2\]  
which is equivalent to minimizing the original \(J(a)\).
