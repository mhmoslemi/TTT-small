\[ J(a) = 2n \times \|a\ast a\|_\infty / (\sum a)^2 \]
where \( \ast \) is the full linear convolution.

1. **Adam Optimization**: 
   - We optimize in log-space: \( x_i = \log a_i \).
   - The constraint is \( \|a\ast a\|_\infty \le \|a\ast a_{\text{original}}\|_\infty \).
   - Gradient: \( \nabla_x = 2(a \ast g) \ast a \) (derivative of the convolution).

2. **Linear Programming (LP)**:
   - We solve: \( \max \sum g \) subject to \( (a \ast g)_k \le \|a\ast a\|_\infty \) for all \( k \), with all \( g \) nonnegative.

3. **Coordinate Descent**:
