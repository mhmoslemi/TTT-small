Let me think about the Anderson acceleration more carefully:

Anderson acceleration for a fixed-point iteration x_{k+1} = G(x_k):
- Maintain a window of m recent iterates: x_1, ..., x_m
- Compute residuals: r_i = x_i - G(x_i)
- Find alpha = (alpha_1, ..., alpha_m) minimizing ||sum alpha_i * r_i||^2 subject to sum alpha_i = 1
- New iterate: x_{k+1} = sum alpha_i * G(x_i)

This accelerates linear convergence to quadratic (in the number of function evaluations).
