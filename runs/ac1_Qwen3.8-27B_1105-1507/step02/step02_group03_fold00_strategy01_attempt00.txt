1. **Approach and rationale**  
   The central idea is to minimize the "attack surface" of the step function's autocorrelation, which is achieved by flattening its spectrum. This means reducing the largest convolution value relative to the squared sum of the sequence. To optimize, we start with a simple uniform sequence and iteratively modify it using rotations, translations, and scales to avoid local minima and find globally optimal coefficients.

   **Detailed approach**:  
   - Begin with a sequence of identical coefficients (all equal to 1). This ensures neutrality—a flat shape in Fourier domain with maximum energy at DC.  
   - Apply a geometric sequence of modifications: rotation, signed subtraction, reconstruction, and resampling. Each modification is viewed as a "greedy" optimization step targeting a flatter convolution spectrum.
