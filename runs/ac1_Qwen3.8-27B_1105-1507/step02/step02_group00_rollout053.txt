Let me also think about a different approach: **direct optimization in the frequency domain**.

If we zero-pad a to length L >= 2n-1, then the circular convolution of the padded sequence with itself equals the linear convolution. The DFT of the padded sequence is H[m] = sum_{j=0}^{n-1} a[j] * exp(-2πi mj/L).

The circular convolution is IFFT(|H|^2) (up to a scaling). We want this to be as flat as possible (or at least have a low peak).

This suggests an approach: optimize the phase of H to make |H|^2 as flat as possible, subject to the constraint that the first n samples of IFFT(H) are non-negative.
