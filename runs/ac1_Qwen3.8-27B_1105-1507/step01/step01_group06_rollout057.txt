**Approach: Alternating Minimization with "Peak Splitting"**

The idea: If the peak is at lag k*, we can try to "split" the energy by adjusting a[m] and a[k*-m] (since they contribute 2*a[m]*a[k*-m] to C[k*]).

Specifically, fix all coordinates except m and k*-m. Then C[k*] as a function of a[m] (with a[k*-m] fixed) is:
C[k*] = 2*a[m]*a[k*-m] + (other terms)

This is linear in a[m]. To reduce C[k*], we should decrease a[m] (or a[k*-m]). But this increases other C[k] values (because a[m] contributes to other lags too).

The optimal tradeoff is: reduce a[m] just enough so that C[k*] drops below the second-highest C value, then move on to the next peak.

This is essentially a "water-filling" approach: reduce the peaks one by one until they're all at the same level.
