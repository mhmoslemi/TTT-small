Let me also think about the smooth approximation. The log-sum-exp approximation of the max is:
smooth_max(b) = (1/beta) * log(sum exp(beta * b_i))

The gradient of this is:
grad_smooth_max = softmax(beta * b) (element-wise)

And the gradient of (a*a) with respect to a is:
grad_a = 2 * (a * p) where p is the "soft max" distribution over lags, and (a*p) is the cross-correlation.

More precisely:
d/d(a_i) [(a*a)_k] = 2 * a_{k-i} (for linear convolution)

So grad J = 2n * sum_k p_k * 2 * (a flipped and shifted by k) / (sum a)² - 4n * max_b * a / (sum a)³

Where p_k = softmax(beta * b_k).

This can be computed efficiently using FFT.
