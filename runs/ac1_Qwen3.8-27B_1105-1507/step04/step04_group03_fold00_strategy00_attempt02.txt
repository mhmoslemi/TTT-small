### Approach and Rationale

The central idea is to use gradient-based optimization with a dual-boundary strategy. We'll maintain multiple sequences of different lengths, optimize each individually, and then combine them optimally across lengths. This hybrid approach aims to:

1. Preserve the initial good seed strategy from the parent sequence
2. Introduce more aggressive gradient-based cuts in the first phase for rapid improvement
3. Expand search to sequences of lengths that are powers of 2 for global optimization

This method was originally motivated by earlier research but has been significantly simplified and adapted to work entirely within the scalable constraint space.

### Mathematical Specification

The optimization objective is:

```python
J(sequence) = 2 * n * max(conv(sequence, sequence)) / (sum(sequence))**2
```

Where:
- `n` is the sequence length
- `conv` is full convolution (2*n-1 points)
- All coefficients must be ≥0

We solve constrained optimization problems by gradient descent, where constraints are enforced by projection onto non-negative values.

### Implementation Procedure

We use a hybrid approach combining:
- Local optimization through projected gradient descent
- Global optimization via exponential lambda-bracket distances
- Maintaining multiple candidate sequences simultaneously
