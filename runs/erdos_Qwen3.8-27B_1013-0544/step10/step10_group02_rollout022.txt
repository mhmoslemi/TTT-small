The objective is f(h) = dx * max_m c[m] where c[m] = ∑_i h[i] * (1-h[i+m-(n-1)]).

Let S = {m : c[m] = max(c)} be the set of active lags.

The subgradient is:
s[p] = dx * ∑_{m ∈ S} [(1-h[p+m-n+1]) * I(0≤p+m-n+1<n) - h[p-n+1+m] * I(0≤p-n+1+m<n)]

We want to minimize f, so we move in the direction -s:

Then project onto the feasible set.

The step size η should be small. A good choice is η = α / ||s||² (from subgradient theory) or just a diminishing sequence.

        s[pa] += (1.0 - h[qf]) - h[qb]
