## 1. Approach and rationale

## 2. Mathematical specification

Variables:

- n discrete samples on [0,2]

Objective:
$$\min_{h} \max_k \int_0^2 h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx$$

Discrete Form:
$$C_5 = \frac{1}{n} \max_k \left(\sum_{i} h_i \overline{(1-h_i + \Delta)} \right)$$
for appropriate shift indices Δ.

Constraints:
- 0 ≤ h_i ≤ 1 
- Σh_i = n/2

Algorithmic Representation:
h = φ(θ) where θ is harmonic parameters (phases, amplitudes, frequencies)
