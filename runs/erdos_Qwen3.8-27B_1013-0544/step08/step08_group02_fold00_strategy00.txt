### Mathematical specification  
**Objective function**  
Minimize:  
\[
C5 = \max_{k} \left( \int_0^2 h(x) \cdot (1 - h(x+k))  dx \right)
\]  
Integer resolution \(n_\text{reso}\) samples \(h\) over \([0,2]\) with \(\Delta x = 2 / (n_\text{reso} - 1)\). The integral is approximated by:  
\[
C5 \approx \max_k \left( \frac{\max_{\tau}(h \star (1 - h)_\text{shift}) \cdot \Delta x}{n_\text{reso}} \right)
\]  
where \(\star\) is cross-correlation (`mode='full'`) and \(\tau\) is lag index.  

**Constraints**  
1. \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) for all \(i\)  
2. \(\sum_{i} h_i \Delta x \approx 1\) (conservation point)  

**Derived quantities**  
- Projection operator \(\mathbf{P}\): enforce bounds and adjust sums to 1.  
- Energy function \(E(h) = \max_k [(h \star (1-h)_\text{shift})_{k} \cdot \Delta x]\)  
- \(n = 2^{\text{exponent}}\) control solution smoothness and complexity  

**Optimization formulation**  
This is a non-convex box-constrained optimization problem. A Bayesian optimization method is suitable due to the function's complex landscape. Formulate as:  
\[
\min_{h} E(h) = \max_k \left( \frac{E_k(h) \cdot \Delta x}{n} \right) + \lambda \cdot \left( \sum_i h_i - \frac{n}{2} \right)^2
