2. Mathematical specification

$$C_5=\max_k \int_{0}^{2} h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx$$

1. Compute correlation: $c = \text{np.correlate}(h, 1-h, \text{mode}="full")$
2. Scale by $dx$: $C5 = \max(c) \cdot dx$
