Actually, I realize I should think more carefully about what "block CD" means here. If I want to optimize h[p] and h[q] simultaneously, the problem becomes:
minimize max_m c_new[m]
where c_new[m] = c[m] + (x_p - h[p]) * a_p[m] + (x_q - h[q]) * a_q[m] + interaction terms

The interaction terms are:
- If p and q both appear in c[m] (i.e., m such that both jf and jb include p and q), there's a cross term.
- For the diagonal (m = n-1): h[p](1-h[p]) + h[q](1-h[q])

This makes it a 2D problem that's harder to solve exactly. A simpler approach:
- Alternate between optimizing h[p] (given h[q]) and h[q] (given h[p]) for a few iterations
- This is just 2 iterations of standard CD on the pair

So "block CD" of size 2 is just doing 2 steps of standard CD. Not very different.
