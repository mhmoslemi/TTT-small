1.Approach and rationale
We introduce a multi-resolution optimization framework that combines a frequency-adaptive initialization with a hybrid optimization method. The key innovations are:

- **Frequency-adaptive initialization**: Generates candidate functions with multiple harmonic components to escape local optima
- **Hybrid optimization**: Combines stochastic gradient-based methods with fixed-point iteration
- **Adaptive frequency tuning**: Gradually refines the underlying period structure during optimization

Bottlenecks addressed:
- Fixed-frequency sinusoidal initialization was limiting diversity in the search space
- The previous constrained optimization was computationally expensive
- Local optima were preventing further improvement

The approach maintains theoretical soundness by preserving:
- The integral constraint through projection
- The box constraints through clipping
- Mathematical equivalence to the optimization objective

        # Scale integral
        current_sum = h.sum()
        h *= (0.5*n/current_sum)
        h = validate_parent(h)

        def grad(approx):
            c_val = compute_c(approx, n)
            idx = np.argmax(c_val)
            # Central difference
            g = np.zeros(n)

                h_try = approx.copy()
                h_try[i] += 1e-4
                h_try = validate_parent(h_try)

            return g
