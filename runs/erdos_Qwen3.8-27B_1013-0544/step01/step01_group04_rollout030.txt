3. **Compensation step**: After changing h[j] to t, adjust another coordinate to maintain the sum.

            h_new, c5_new = coord_descent(h_try, n_final, dx_final, max_passes=5000, seed=seed + 700 + rc4, t0=t0, t_end=p4_end)
            if c5_new < best_c5 - 1e-14:
                best_h = h_new
                best_c5 = c5_new
        rc4 += 1
        if rc4 > 50:
            break
    
    return (best_h, best_c5, len(best_h))
