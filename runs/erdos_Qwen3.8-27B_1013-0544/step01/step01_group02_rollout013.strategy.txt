1. **Approach and Rationale**
We will use a two-stage approach combining a global search with local optimization. The central idea is to exploit the structure of minimum overlap functions by focusing initial search on periodic patterns and balanced distributions. Unlike previous methods that rely solely on gradient-based optimization, we incorporate geometric constraints and symmetry considerations to explore meaningful solution spaces. This method is designed to escape local optima by targeting specific function shapes thought to minimize overlap peaks.

The key innovation is projecting the minimization problem onto a space of periodic functions, which have demonstrated strong properties in related minimization problems. We will use a phase-based curriculum learning strategy: starting with coarse periodic functions, then incorporating more complex variations. The algorithm requires careful handling of discontinuities while maintaining feasibility.

Limitations: The method strongly favors structured patterns which may not represent global optima. The optimization may diverge for highly non-periodic functions. Time complexity is dominated by correlation computations, which is O(n_points^2).
