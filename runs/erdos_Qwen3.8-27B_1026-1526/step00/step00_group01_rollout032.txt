        else:

For coordinate descent: fix all elements of h except h[m], and find the value of h[m] that minimizes max_k C5(k). Since C5(k) is linear in h[m] (for fixed other coordinates), this is a 1D optimization with a piecewise-linear objective. We can solve it exactly!

C5(k) as a function of h[m] (call it x):
C5(k) = dx * [a_k * x + b_k] where a_k and b_k depend on the lag and other h values.

Specifically, h[m] appears in C5(k) as:
- In h[i+l] term: contributes dx*(1-h[i])*x when i+l=m, i.e., i=m-l
- In (1-h[i]) term: contributes dx*(-h[i+l])*x when i=m

B_k = C5(k) - A_k * h[m] (evaluated at current h[m])
