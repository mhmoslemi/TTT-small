Let me try a different approach: **Water-filling**.

The idea: We want to equalize the active correlations. If c_p is the maximum, we want to reduce it by increasing h[i] where h[i] appears with a negative coefficient in c_p (i.e., where increasing h[i] decreases c_p) and decreasing h[i] where it appears with a positive coefficient.

This is essentially what the coordinate descent does, but done in a coordinated way.

**Algorithm: Water-filling coordinate descent**
1. Compute all c_k.
2. Identify the top M active correlations (c_1, ..., c_M).
3. For each active correlation c_m, compute the gradient w.r.t. each h[i].
4. Find the direction that most reduces the maximum correlation.
5. Take a step in that direction (with projection).

This is essentially a "minimax" approach (like the one used in game theory).

def compute_c5(h, n):
