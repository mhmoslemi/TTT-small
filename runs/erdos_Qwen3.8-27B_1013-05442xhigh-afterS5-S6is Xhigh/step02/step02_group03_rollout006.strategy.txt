**Variables**

**Objective**

   - Maintain parent solution for comparison
   - Use smooth_max objective with β=5 for exploration

3. **Phase2: Local refinement (300ms budget)**
   - Focus on lag maxima identified in Phase1
   - Apply projected subgradient to specific regions:
     * Identify worst lags (top 3)
     * Compute gradient for shift by those lags
     * Reduce h where it currently overlaps too much when shifted

4. **Phase3: Resolution upgrade (100ms budget initially, extended if budget remains)**
   - Double grid points (n→2n)
   - Transfer solution by linear interpolation
   - Adjust for new integral constraint
   - Recompute baseline C_5 before optimization

5. **Phase4: SLSQP continuation (600ms budget max)**
