Actually, let me think about this differently. The cross-correlation c[k] = Σ h[i]·(1-h[i+L]) where L=k-(n-1).

For lag L, c[L] = #{i: h[i]=1, h[i+L]=0} = A - #{i: h[i]=1, h[i+L]=1} = A - (autocorrelation of h at lag L).

Wait, that's not quite right because the valid range for i depends on L. For |L| < n, the number of valid i is n - |L|. So:
