### 4. Greedy local polishing (Phase C)

When the gradient phase ends we have a high‑quality candidate `h_best`.  A cheap deterministic refinement can still lower the maximal shift.

**Swap‑based neighbourhood search**

* For a fixed budget (≈ 0.25·budget_s seconds) repeat:
  1. Identify the set `S` of lag indices where `g_s` is within `1e‑4` of the current maximum (`C₅/dx`).
  2. Build a list `I` of all positions that actually participate in any of those critical lags (i.e. any `i` for which `h_i` or `h_{i±s}` appears in a critical term).
  3. Randomly pick two indices `p,q ∈ I` with `h_p > 0` and `h_q < 1`.  Transfer a small amount `δ = min(0.02, h_p, 1‑h_q)`.

  6. Occasionally (probability 0.1) perform a random perturbation of a single coordinate by a tiny Gaussian to escape plateaus.
