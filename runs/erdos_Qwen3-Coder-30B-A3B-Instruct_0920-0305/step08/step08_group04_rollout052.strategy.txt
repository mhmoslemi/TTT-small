**Mathematical reformulation**

The objective can be written as  

\[
C_5=\max_{k}\int_0^2 h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)\,dx
      =1-\min_{k}\int_0^2 h(x)h(x+k)\,dx .
\]

Thus we must **maximise the smallest autocorrelation**
\[
S(k)=\int_0^2 h(x)h(x+k)\,dx .
\]
The constraints are  
\[
0\le h(x)\le 1,\qquad \int_0^2h(x)\,dx=1 .
\]

Working on a uniform grid of \(n\) points (\(dx=2/n\)) the discrete version is  

\[
S_k =\sum_{i} h_i\,h_{i+k},\qquad 
C_5 = 1-\min_{k} S_k\;dx .
\]

The problem is non‑convex because of the bilinear term \(h_i h_{i+k}\).  
The new approach tackles the non‑convexity by **reducing the dimension of the search space**, using a **global stochastic optimiser** on a low‑dimensional parametric family, and then **refining the result with a deterministic sub‑gradient descent** that works directly on the original high‑resolution grid.  A **mass‑transfer heuristic** and a **multi‑resolution scheme** are added to push the solution past local minima.
