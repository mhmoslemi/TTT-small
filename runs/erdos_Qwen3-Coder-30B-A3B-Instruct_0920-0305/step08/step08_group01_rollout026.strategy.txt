**Step‑wise refinement algorithm**

1. **Compute the full correlation array** `c = correlate(h_bin, 1‑h_bin, mode='full')`.

3. **Candidate moves** – for each interval i:
   * Move the **left edge** `s_i` left/right by a tiny step `δ = dx/2` (if the move respects ordering and non‑negativity of gaps).
