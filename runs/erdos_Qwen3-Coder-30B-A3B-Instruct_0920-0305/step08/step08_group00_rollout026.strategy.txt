2. **Mismatch count** –  
   For a shift \(k\ge0\) let the overlapping index set be  
   \[
   I_k=\{i\mid 0\le i\le n-1-k\}.
   \]  
   The contribution of shift \(k\) to the objective is the *mismatch count*  
   \[
   m_k=\sum_{i\in I_k} h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+k}\bigr)

   \]

   Hence minimising \(C_5\) is equivalent to **minimising the maximum mismatch count**
   \[
   M:=\max_k m_k .
