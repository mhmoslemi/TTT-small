The term `h_i·(1‑h_{i+k}) = h_i – h_i·h_{i+k}` shows that the only non‑linear part is the **pairwise product** `h_i·h_{i+k}`.  

If we **force every h_i to be binary** (`h_i ∈ {0,1}`) then `h_i·h_i = h_i` and the term `h_i·(1‑h_i)` vanishes, i.e. the worst‑case shift will never be the zero shift.
