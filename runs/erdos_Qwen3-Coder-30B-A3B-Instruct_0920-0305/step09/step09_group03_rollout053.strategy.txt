### 1.  Smooth surrogate for the max

For any vector \(F\) the log‑sum‑exp  

\[

\[
\mathcal{L}_\alpha(h)=S_\alpha\!\bigl(F(h)\bigr),\qquad

### 2.  Analytic gradient of the surrogate

Write \(b=1-h\).  Then  

\[
F_{\ell}=h^\top\!b^{(\ell)},\qquad  b^{(\ell)}_i=b_{i+\ell}.
\]

\[
\frac{\partial F_{\ell}}{\partial h_j}
=1-(h_{j+\ell}+h_{j-\ell}) .

Let  

\[
w_\ell =\frac{e^{\alpha F_{\ell}}}{\sum_{\ell'}e^{\alpha F_{\ell'}}}\quad(\text{softmax weights}),\qquad\sum_\ell w_\ell=1 .
\]

The gradient of the surrogate is the weighted average of the individual gradients:

\[
\nabla\mathcal{L}_\alpha(h)_j
= \sum_{\ell} w_\ell\Bigl[1-(h_{j+\ell}+h_{j-\ell})\Bigr]
= 1 - \Bigl( (w*h)_{j}^{\text{forward}}+(w*h)_{j}^{\text{backward}}\Bigr),
\]

where the two convolution‑like terms are

* forward: \((w*h)^{\!f}_j =\sum_{\ell} w_\ell\,h_{j+\ell}\),

Both are ordinary linear convolutions of the weight vector \(w\) with \(h\); the “backward’’ version is simply the convolution with the reversed weight vector \(w_{\text{rev}}\).  They are computed efficiently with FFT (zero‑padding to length \(L=2n-1\)) in \(O(n\log n)\).
