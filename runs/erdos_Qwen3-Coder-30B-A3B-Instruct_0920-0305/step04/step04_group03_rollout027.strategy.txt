**1. Re‑formulate the objective**

For a discretised step function `h∈[0,1]^N` (with `dx = 2/N`) the quantity evaluated in the benchmark is  

\[
F(h)=\max_{k}\; \int_0^{2} h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)\,dx
      =\max_{s}\; \sum_{i=0}^{N-1-|s|} h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+s}\bigr)\,dx .
\]

Because `dx` is constant, minimising `F(h)` is equivalent to maximising the *minimal* autocorrelation  

\[
C_{\min}(h)=\min_{s}\; \sum_{i=0}^{N-1-|s|} h_i\,h_{i+s}\,,
\]

since  
\[
\sum_i h_i = \frac{N}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_i h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+s}\bigr)=\frac{N}{2}-\sum_i h_i h_{i+s}.
\]

Hence the problem becomes  

\[
\max_{h\in[0,1]^N}\; C_{\min}(h)
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\sum_i h_i = \frac{N}{2}.
\]
