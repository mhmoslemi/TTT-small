### 1. Mathematical preprocessing

### 5. Sub‑gradient polishing (optional, cheap)

1. Compute the full correlation vector `c = np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode='full')`.

5. Take a small step `h ← h – α·grad` (project back onto `[0,1]` and renormalise the sum after each step).  
6. Reduce step size `α` geometrically (e.g., α ← 0.9 α) and stop after a few dozen iterations or when the bound stops decreasing.
