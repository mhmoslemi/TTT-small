     \[

### Phase 2 – Continuous polishing of the binary optimum  

If the binary optimum is slightly above the target (e.g. \(C_5\approx0.381\)), we can improve it by allowing fractional values on a few “boundary” positions while keeping the overall integral fixed.

2. **Local optimiser** – Run a deterministic constrained optimiser (SLSQP) on the original smooth objective  
   \[
     \max_d \bigl( \sum_{i} h_i - \sum_i h_i h_{i+d}\bigr)\Delta x

Providing gradients accelerates convergence and avoids the premature “flat‑spot” problems seen in the previous SLSQP runs.
