**1. Problem Reformulation**

The objective  
\[
C_5(h)=\max_{k}\int_0^2 h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)\,dx
\]  
with the constraints \(0\le h\le 1\) and \(\int_0^2h=1\) can be expressed on the discrete grid (step size \(\Delta x = 2/n\)) as  

\[
C_5(\mathbf h)=\Delta x\;\max_{\ell\in\{-n+1,\dots,n-1\}}
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+\ell}\bigr),
\qquad
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i = \frac{n}{2},
\qquad
0\le h_i\le1 .
\]

Since \(\sum_i h_i\) is constant, for a given shift \(\ell\)

\[
\sum_i h_i(1-h_{i+\ell}) 
= \frac{n}{2} - \sum_i h_i h_{i+\ell}.
\]

Thus **minimising** \(C_5\) is equivalent to **maximising** the *minimum* autocorrelation  
\(A_\ell(\mathbf h)=\sum_i h_i h_{i+\ell}\).  
The objective is a non‑convex min‑of‑quadratics problem.
