**1. Reformulate the objective**

For a discretisation with *n* points (`dx = 2/n`) the quantity to minimise is  

\[
C_5(h)=\max_{k\in\{-n+1,\dots ,n-1\}}
\;c_k(h),\qquad 
c_k(h)=\Bigl(\,h\star(1-h)\Bigr)_k .
\]

Because  

\[
c_k(h)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i-\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i\,h_{i+k},
\]

the first term is constant (`∑h_i = n/2` by the integral constraint).  
Thus  

\[
C_5(h)=\frac{2}{n}\Bigl( \tfrac{n}{2}-\min_{k} I_k(h) \Bigr),\qquad
I_k(h)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k}h_i\,h_{i+k}.
\]

*Goal*: maximise the **minimum** auto‑correlation \(I_k\) over all lags.

**2. Enforce the box constraints via a sigmoid re‑parameterisation**

Introduce unrestricted variables \(\mathbf{y}\in\mathbb{R}^n\) and define  

\[
h_i(\mathbf{y}) = \sigma(y_i)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-y_i}}\in[0,1].
\]

With this map the box constraints are automatically satisfied; only the linear
integral constraint remains.

--------------------------------------------------------------------

**3. Handle the integral constraint with an augmented‑Lagrangian penalty**

Let  

\[
S(\mathbf{y})=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i(\mathbf{y})\quad\text{and}\quad 
S_{\mathrm{target}}= \frac{n}{2}.
\]

Add a quadratic penalty to the objective  

\[
P(\mathbf{y}) = \lambda\;\bigl(S(\mathbf{y})-S_{\mathrm{target}}\bigr)^2,
\qquad\lambda\gg 1 .
\]

(The gradient of the penalty is trivial and will be used later.)

**4. Replace the non‑smooth max by a smooth log‑sum‑exp surrogate**

When \(\beta\) is large, \(\widetilde I_{\min}\) approaches \(\min_k I_k\), but the

The total smooth objective is

F(\mathbf{y})=\widetilde C_5(\mathbf{y})+P(\mathbf{y}).
\]

\frac{\partial \widetilde I_{\min}}{\partial h_j}
 =\sum_{\ell} w_\ell\,\frac{\partial I_\ell}{\partial h_j}.
