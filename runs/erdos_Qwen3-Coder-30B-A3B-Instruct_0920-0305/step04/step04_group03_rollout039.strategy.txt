**1. Problem Re‑formulation**

- Let \(h\) be sampled on a uniform grid of \(N\) points on \([0,2]\) with spacing \(dx=2/N\).  
- Variables: \(h_i\in[0,1]\) for \(i=0,\dots,N-1\) and the integral constraint becomes  
  \(\displaystyle \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} h_i = N/2\)   (because \(\sum h_i\,dx =1\)).  

- Define the auto‑correlation vector  
  \(\displaystyle a_s = \sum_{i=0}^{N-1-s} h_i\,h_{i+s},\qquad s=0,\dots,N-1\)  
  (the ‘full’ correlation is symmetric, negative shifts give the same values).  

- The overlap integral for a shift \(s\) (discrete version) is  
  \(\displaystyle f_s = \sum_{i=0}^{N-1-s} h_i\,(1-h_{i+s}) = S - a_s\) with  
  \(S=\sum_i h_i=N/2\) (constant because of the integral constraint).  

- Hence  
  \(\displaystyle C_5 = \max_s f_s = S - \min_s a_s\).  
  Minimising \(C_5\) is **equivalent** to maximising the **worst‑case** auto‑correlation \(\min_s a_s\).
