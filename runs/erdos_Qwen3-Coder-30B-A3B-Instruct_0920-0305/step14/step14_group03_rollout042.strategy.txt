* dx = 2 / n, target_sum = ∑ h[i] = 1 / dx = n / 2*

\[
\text{corr}[s] \;=\; \sum_{i=0}^{n-1-s} h[i]\;h[i+s]\qquad
s = -(n-1),\dots,n-1 .
\]

The overlap for shift s is  

\[
O[s] = dx\Bigl(\sum_i h[i] - \text{corr}[s]\Bigr) = dx\;(target\_sum-\text{corr}[s]).
\]

Hence  

\[
C_5 = \max_{s} O[s]

The problem is to **minimise C₅**, i.e. to **maximise the minimal auto‑correlation** under the box constraint 0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 and the linear mass constraint ∑h[i]=target_sum.

**2. A smooth surrogate for the max**
