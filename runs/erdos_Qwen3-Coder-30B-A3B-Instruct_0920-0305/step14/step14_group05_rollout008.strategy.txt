### 1. Numerical backbone (common to all phases)

* **Fine grid** – Choose a power‑of‑two size for the FFT, e.g. `n = 256`.  
  `dx = 2.0 / n` and the required sum is `target = n/2`.

* **FFT‑based correlation** –
