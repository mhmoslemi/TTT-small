### 4.  Smooth Approximation of the Max  

The exact max over all lags is nondifferentiable.  Replace it by the

\[
\Phi_\alpha(h)=\frac{1}{\alpha}\log\Bigl(\sum_{\ell}

\]

When \(\alpha\to\infty\) we recover the true max.  Starting with a modest
\(\alpha\) (e.g. 4) gives a smooth landscape; we increase \(\alpha\) during
optimisation (geometric schedule) so the algorithm gradually focuses on
the worst lag.

### 5.  Fast Gradient of the Soft‑max  

For a fixed \(\alpha\),

\[
\frac{\partial \Phi_\alpha}{\partial h_j}
= \sum_{\ell} w_\ell\,\frac{\partial c_\ell}{\partial h_j},
\qquad
w_\ell =\frac{\exp(\alpha c_\ell)}{\sum_{k}\exp(\alpha c_k)} .
\]

The derivative of a single lag is

\[
\frac{\partial c_\ell}{\partial h_j}
=1- h_{j+\ell} - h_{j-\ell},
\]

with out‑of‑range indices treated as zero.  In vector form

\[
\nabla\Phi_\alpha = \mathbf{1}

\]

where \(*\) denotes linear convolution.
