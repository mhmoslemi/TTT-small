### 2. Fast heuristic stage – targeted stochastic local search (TLS)

**2.1 Representation**

`np.correlate` for `n≤200` is a tiny C loop (≈10⁵ elementary ops) and is fast enough to be called millions of times.

**2.3 Core move – “worst‑shift swap”**  

* Compute the full correlation array `c = np.correlate(h, 1‑h, mode='full')`.  
* Identify the *worst* shift `ℓ* = argmax(c)`. (ℓ*≠0 because for binary `c₀=0`.)  
* A *bad* position is an index `i` with `h_i = 1` **and** `h_{i+ℓ*}=0` (the pair contributes 1 to `c_{ℓ*}`).

* Swapping a bad `1` with a good `0` *reduces* `c_{ℓ*}` by exactly 2 (two mismatched pairs become matched).

