### Phase 1 – Binary MILP (max‑min overlap)

3. **McCormick (product) linearisation** – for every `i,k` add three linear constraints that are exact for binary `h`:

   ```
   y[i,k] ≤ h[i]
   y[i,k] ≤ h[i+k]

   ```
   f_k(h) = Σ_{i} h_i·(1 – h_{i+k})
