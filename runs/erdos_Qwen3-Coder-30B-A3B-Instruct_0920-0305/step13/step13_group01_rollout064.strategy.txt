**1. Problem Reformulation**

The objective can be written in discrete form for a vector  
\(h\in[0,1]^n\) (grid step \(dx=2/n\)) as  

\[
C_5(h)=dx\;\max_{k\in\{-n+1,\dots ,n-1\}}
\Bigl(\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i\;-\;\sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k}h_i\,h_{i+k}\Bigr).
\]

Because \(\sum_i h_i = n/2\) is fixed, minimizing \(C_5\) is equivalent to  

\[
\min_h\ \max_k\ -\sum_{i}h_i\,h_{i+k}
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
\max_h\ \min_k\ \sum_i h_i\,h_{i+k}.
\]

Thus the problem is a *min‑max* over inner products of the vector with its shifts.
The only constraints are the box \([0,1]\) and a single linear equality
\(\sum_i h_i=n/2\).
