### 1. Reformulate the objective as a pure integer epigraph problem

For a shift (lag) ℓ (ℓ=1,…,n‑1) the overlap term is  

\[
L_\ell = \sum_{i=0}^{n-\ell-1} h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+\ell}\bigr)

Introduce auxiliary binary variables  

\[

```
p_{i,ℓ} ≤ h_i
p_{i,ℓ} ≤ h_{i+ℓ}
```

\[
\sum_{i=0}^{n-\ell-1} (h_i - p_{i,\ell}) \; \le\; T .
\]

Because the left‑hand side equals exactly \(L_\ell\), the set of constraints guarantees  

\[

\]

The optimisation problem becomes

```
minimise   T
subject to Σ h_i = M

           T integer ≥ 0
```

All constraints are linear; the only non‑convexity is the integrality of the variables.
