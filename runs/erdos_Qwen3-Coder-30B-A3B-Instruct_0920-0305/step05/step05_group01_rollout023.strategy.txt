## 3. Continuous refinement (projected‑gradient descent on a smooth max)

### 3.1 Smoothed objective
Replace the hard maximum by a **log‑sum‑exp** (soft‑max) that is smooth and converges to the true max:

\[
F_{α}(h)=\frac{dx}{α}\,\log\!\Bigl(\sum_{\ell} 
        \exp\!\bigl(α\,I_{\ell}(h)\bigr)\Bigr),\qquad 
I_{\ell}(h)=\sum_i h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+\ell}\bigr)

* We will **increase `α` gradually** (e.g. exponential schedule from 0.5 to 30) so that early iterations explore globally and later iterations focus on the worst lag.

### 3.2 Exact gradient for the smoothed objective

\[
p_{\ell}= \frac{\exp(α I_{\ell})}{\sum_{k}\exp(α I_{k})}.
\]

The derivative of `F_α` wrt a component `h_j` is a weighted sum of the lag‑wise derivatives:

\[
\frac{\partial F_{α}}{\partial h_j}=dx\sum_{\ell} p_{\ell}\,

* **Forward term** – `(1‑h_{j+ℓ})` for indices that stay inside the domain.
