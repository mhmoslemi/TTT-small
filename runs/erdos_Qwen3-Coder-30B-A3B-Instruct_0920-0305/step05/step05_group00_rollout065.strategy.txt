### 1. Mathematics needed for a gradient step
For a fixed lag \(\ell\) (integer offset, positive or negative) define  

\[
C_\ell(h)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+\ell}\bigr),\qquad
h_{j}=0\;\text{if}\;j\notin[0,n-1].
\]

Expanding,
\[
C_\ell(h)=\underbrace{\sum_i h_i}_{=n/2}
          -\sum_{i}h_i h_{i+\ell}.
\]

Hence the only \(\ell\)‑dependent part is the bilinear term
\(Q_\ell(h)=\sum_i h_i h_{i+\ell}\).

The (unscaled) gradient of \(C_\ell\) w.r.t. component \(h_j\) is

\[
\frac{\partial C_\ell}{\partial h_j}
=1-h_{j-\ell}-h_{j+\ell},

where out‑of‑range indices contribute zero.  
The **descent direction** (negative gradient) is therefore

\[
d_j^{(\ell)} = h_{j-\ell}+h_{j+\ell}-1 .
\tag{1}

\[

\tag{2}
\]

*When \(\beta\to\infty\), \(F_\beta\to\max_\ell C_\ell\); for moderate \(\beta\) the function is smooth and has a well‑defined gradient.*

\[

3. Find \(\lambda^\*\) such that \(S(\lambda^\*) = n/2\) (bisection on a safe interval, e.g. \([-2,2]\)).
