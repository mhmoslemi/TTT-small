### 1.  Continuous formulation

1. **Correlation vector**  
   For a candidate \(h\) define the (un‑scaled) correlation  
   \(\displaystyle f_\ell(h)=\sum_{i}h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+\ell}\bigr)\) for
   \(\ell\in\{-n+1,\dots ,n-1\}\).  Using the FFT,
   \(f(h)\) is obtained in \(O(n\log n)\) time.

   Replace the non‑differentiable \(\max_{\ell}f_\ell\) by a

   \[

   \exp\bigl(\gamma\,f_\ell(h)\bigr)\Bigr),
   \]

   where \(\gamma>0\) controls the tightness.
   For \(\gamma\to\infty\) the surrogate converges to the true max,
   but for moderate \(\gamma\) the objective is smooth and its gradient
   is easy to compute.

3. **Binary‑encouraging regulariser**  
   Add a convex term that pushes \(h_i\) toward \(\{0,1\}\):

   \[
   R_{\lambda}(h)=\lambda\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i(1-h_i)\;,
