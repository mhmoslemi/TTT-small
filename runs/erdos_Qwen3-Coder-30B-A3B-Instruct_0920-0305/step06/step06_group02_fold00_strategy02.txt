**1. Mathematical Reformulation (binary, min‑overlap view)**  
The Erdős constant for a step function on [0,2] with total mass 1 can be written

\[
C_5=\max_{k}\int_0^2h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx
    =\frac{1}{2}\, -\;\min_{k}\int_0^2h(x)h(x+k)dx .
\]

Because the integral is linear in each factor, the extremal solution may be taken to be an
indicator of a measurable set, i.e. a **binary** function  
\(h(x)\in\{0,1\}\).  After discretisation with \(n\) points (step size \(dx=2/n\))

* \(h_i\in\{0,1\}\) for \(i=0,\dots ,n-1\)  
* \(\sum_i h_i = n/2\)   (exactly half the points are 1)  

Define the *overlap* for a shift \(s\in\{-(n-1),\dots ,n-1\}\)

\[
O_s(h)=\sum_{i}h_i\,h_{i+s},
\qquad\text{(zero‑padded indices outside [0,n‑1])}
\]

so that  

\[
C_5 = dx \cdot \Bigl(\frac{n}{2}-\min_s O_s(h)\Bigr).
\]

Hence the optimisation problem becomes:

\[
\max_h\; \min_{s} O_s(h)
\quad\text{s.t.}\;
h_i\in\{0,1\},\;\sum_i h_i=n/2.
\]

**Goal:** raise the worst‑case overlap \(\min_s O_s\) as high as possible (equivalently lower \(C_5\)).
