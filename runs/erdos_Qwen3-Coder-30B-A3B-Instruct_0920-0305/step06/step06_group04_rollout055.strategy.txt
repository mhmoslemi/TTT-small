The Erdős constant C₅ is the worst‑case value of  

\[
C_5(h)=\max_{0\le\ell<n}\;dx\sum_{i=0}^{n-1-\ell}h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+\ell}\bigr)
      =1-dx\;\min_{\ell}f_\ell(h),
\qquad 
f_\ell(h)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1-\ell}h_i\,h_{i+\ell},
\]

with \(dx=2/n\), \(\displaystyle\sum_i h_i=n/2\) and \(0\le h_i\le1\).  
Thus **minimising C₅ ⇔ maximising the smallest autocorrelation** \(\min_\ell f_\ell\).
