### Why this is materially different

- **Dimensionality reduction**: Instead of optimising all 42 (or more) h‑values, we optimise a handful of basis coefficients, turning a high‑dimensional non‑convex problem into a tractable low‑dimensional one.

- **Analytic insight**: Leverages the Fourier/cosine basis to shape the autocorrelation directly, rather than treating the problem as a black‑box.
- **Hybrid of constructive combinatorics and continuous optimisation**: Starts from a mathematically motivated functional form, then refines with projection methods, unlike the previous purely heuristic approach.
