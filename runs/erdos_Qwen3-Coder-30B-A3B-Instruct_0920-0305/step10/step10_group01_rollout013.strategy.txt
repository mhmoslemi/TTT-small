**1. Re‑formulate the objective as a smooth max**

The quantity we must minimise is  

\[
C_5(h)=\max_{k}\;c_k,\qquad 
c_k=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}h_i\bigl(1-h_{i+k}\bigr)
\]

(the sum is over zero‑padded indices, \(N=n_{\text{points}}\)).  
Introduce a smooth approximation of the maximum:

\[
F_\alpha(h)=\frac{1}{\alpha}\log\Bigl(\sum_{k}e^{\alpha c_k}\Bigr)
\]

For \(\alpha\rightarrow\infty\) we have \(F_\alpha\to\max_k c_k\).  
We will minimise \(F_\alpha\) while slowly increasing \(\alpha\) (continuation).

**2. Analytic gradient of the smooth objective**

Let  

\[
w_k=\frac{e^{\alpha c_k}}{\sum_j e^{\alpha c_j}}\quad (\text{soft‑max weights}),\qquad
\sum_k w_k=1.
\]

From \(c_k = \sum_i h_i - \sum_i h_i h_{i+k}\) we obtain  

\[
\frac{\partial c_k}{\partial h_j}=1-\bigl(h_{j+k}+h_{j-k}\bigr)
\]

(with the convention \(h_{m}=0\) when \(m\notin\{0,\dots,N-1\}\)).  
Hence the gradient of the smooth objective is

\[
\frac{\partial F_\alpha}{\partial h_j}
   =\sum_{k} w_k\Bigl[1-\bigl(h_{j+k}+h_{j-k}\bigr)\Bigr]
   =1-\sum_{k}w_k\bigl(h_{j+k}+h_{j-k}\bigr).
\]

Define the symmetric weight kernel  

\[
\tilde w_k = w_k+w_{-k}\qquad (k\in[-N+1,N-1]).
\]

Then the two sums combine into a single correlation:

\[
g_j\;:=\;\sum_{k}\tilde w_k\,h_{j+k}
      =\bigl(\tilde w\star h\bigr)_j .
\]

Thus

\[
\nabla F_\alpha(h) = \mathbf{1} - g,
\]

where \(\mathbf{1}\) is the vector of ones and \(g\) is the **correlation of \(\tilde w\) with \(h\)**.

All correlations are computed with zero padding, which can be performed with a single FFT of length
