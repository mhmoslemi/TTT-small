**1.  Problem insight – a max‑min formulation**  
For any step function h on [0,2] with ∫h=1 we have  

\[
C_5=\max_{k\ge0}\int_0^{2-k}h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)dx

\]

Thus **minimising C₅ is equivalent to maximising the minimal self‑correlation** of h over all non‑negative shifts.  In the discrete setting ( n  samples, dx = 2/n ) the correlation at shift k is  

\[
R_k=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k}h_i\,h_{i+k},\qquad 
C_k =dx\Bigl(\frac n2-R_k\Bigr),
