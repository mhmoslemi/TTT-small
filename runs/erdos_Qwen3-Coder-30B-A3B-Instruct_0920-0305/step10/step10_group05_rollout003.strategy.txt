**1. Reformulate the objective**

For a discretised step‑function `h[0…n‑1]` on `[0,2]` with step‑size  
`dx = 2/n` and `∑ h[i]·dx = 1`, the overlap integral can be written as  

\[
C_5(h)=\max_{k}\int_0^2 h(x)\bigl(1-h(x+k)\bigr)dx
       = 1-\min_{k}\int_0^2 h(x)h(x+k)dx .
\]

Hence the problem is **maximise the minimum auto‑correlation**
\[
\min_{k} S_k ,\qquad 
S_k = \int_0^2 h(x)h(x+k)dx .
\]

In the discrete world  

\[
S_k = dx\;\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i]\;h[i+k] \qquad
\text{(indices outside }[0,n-1]\text{ are treated as }0).
\]

Thus  
\[
C_5 = 1 - dx\;\min_k\bigl(\text{auto‑corr}_k\bigr).
\]

All later algorithms will therefore **minimise C₅ by maximising the minimal auto‑correlation**.

**2. Fast evaluation of the objective**

Use the FFT to obtain the full aperiodic auto‑correlation in one call:

* Reverse the vector `h`: `h_rev = h[::-1]`.
* Compute the convolution `conv = ifft( fft(h_rev) * fft(h) )` (real part only).
* `conv` has length `2*n‑1`; the entry at index `n‑1+k` equals `∑ h[i]·h[i+k]`.
* `min_corr = dx * np.min(conv)`.
* `C5 = 1 - min_corr`.
