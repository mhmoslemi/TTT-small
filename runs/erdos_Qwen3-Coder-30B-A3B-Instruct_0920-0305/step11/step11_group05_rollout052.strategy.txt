The objective  
\[

\]  
with \(\int_0^2h=1,\;0\le h\le1\) can be rewritten in the discretised setting (step size \(dx=2/n\)) as  

\[
C_5(h)=dx\;\max_{s\in\{-n+1,\dots ,n-1\}}
\Bigl(S-\!\!\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}h_i\,h_{i+s}\Bigr),
\qquad
S=\sum_i h_i =\frac n2 .
\]

Since \(dx\,S=1\), minimizing \(C_5\) is **exactly** the same as **maximising** the minimal autocorrelation  

\[
t^\ast=\min_{s}\;\sum_{i}h_i\,h_{i+s}.
\]

Thus the problem is a *max‑min* quadratic programme:
\[
\max_{h\in[0,1]^n,\;\sum h=S}\;\;\min_{s}\;\sum_i h_i\,h_{i+s}.
\]

The product is bilinear, so the problem is non‑convex.
