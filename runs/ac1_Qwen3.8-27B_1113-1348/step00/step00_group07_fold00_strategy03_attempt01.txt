The key insight is to use sequences composed of short-duration positive impulses (spikes) separated by uniform gaps, normalized to `sqrt(2n)`, to achieve a flatter, less peaked convolution envelope. This approach aims for a distribution-like signal that minimizes the maximum value of the convolution, leading to lower scores than the constant reference.

The mathematical formulation for a candidate sequence `a` of length `n` is:

- **Number of Spikes**: `m = floor(sqrt(2*n)) + 1` (ensuring at least one spike)
- **Gap**: `g = ceil(n / (m + 1))` (uniform gap between spikes)
- **Spike Height**: `h = (sqrt(2*n)) / (number of spikes)` (normalized amplitude)

- `score = (2*n*max(np.convolve(x, x))) / (np.sum(x)**2)`
