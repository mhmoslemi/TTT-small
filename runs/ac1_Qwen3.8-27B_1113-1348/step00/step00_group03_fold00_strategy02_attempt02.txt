### Core Strategy

The objective is to minimize \(2n \cdot \|f * f\|_\infty / (\sum_j a_j)^2\) by optimizing a nonnegative sequence \(a\). The previous solution used a fixed normalisation \(\sum_j a_j = \sqrt{2n}\). We improve this by applying **auto-scaling during convolution estimation** to handle **sparse representations**, increasing the gradient's signal-to-noise ratio for **memory-bounded structures**, and eliminating a hardcoded column range patch by resampling at sensible intervals. This allows us to operate effectively on a wider range of problem scales without heavy re-parameterisation.

### Implementation Details

1. **Optimised Initialisation**:
