   - The objective is \(C_5 = \max_k \int h(x) (1 - h(x+k)) dx\). We compute this via:
        \[
        \text{corr} = \text{np.correlate}(h, 1-h, \text{mode}="full")
        \]
        and then \(C_5 = \max(\text{corr}) \times dx\).

**Smooth Surrogate**:
   - We approximate the max operation by a smooth function. Let \(a_i = \text{corr}[i]\) be the correlation value for lag \(i\), and let \(M = \max_i a_i\).
   - The surrogate function is defined as:
        \[
        S(\alpha) = -\frac{1}{\alpha} \log \left( \sum_{i} \exp(\alpha (a_i - M)) \right) + M
        \]
   - As \(\alpha \to \infty\), \(S(\alpha) \to M\), so we can vary \(\alpha\) to control the smoothness.

**Optimization Problem**:
   - We will minimize the smooth surrogate subject to \(0 \leq h[i] \leq 1\) and \(\sum h[i] \times dx = 1\).
   - The surrogate function \(S(\alpha)\) is used as the objective, and \(\alpha\) is a parameter that controls the trade-off between smoothness and accuracy.

**Representation**:
   - We represent \(h\) as a vector of length \(n_{\text{points}}\) with values in \([0, 1]\) such that \(\sum h[i] = n_{\text{points}}/2\).
