### 1. Approach and Rationale

The primary approach is to explore a broad solution space using a combination of simulated annealing for global optimization and targeted local searches (coordinate descent) for refinement. Simulated annealing prevents local optima by accepting worse solutions early in the search process, transitions to higher-quality solutions, and is guaranteed to converge globally. The steps include:

1. **Perturbation**: Randomly mutate the solution to explore nearby regions.
2. **Objective Evaluation**: Compute the overlap integral using cross-correlation.
3. **Metropolis Criterion**: Apply temperature-based acceptance to escape local optima.
4. **Progressive Refinement**: Transition to local searches on potentially improved solutions.
5. **Hybrid Loop**: Alternate between higher-temperature annealing and targeted refinement phases.

This method addresses local optima by:
1. **Gradient-independent Exploration**: Simulated annealing does not rely on derivatives, enabling exploration of flat regions.
2. **Progressive Scheduling**: Balances exploration (high temperature) and exploitation (low temperature).
3. **Memory-Based**: Retains the best solution throughout the run.

The approach prioritizes:
- **Global Consistency**: Uses sorted history for progressive refinement.
- **Efficiency Balance**: Lowers temperature exponentially, ensuring convergence within budget.
- **Barrier Handling**: Projects solutions to satisfy constraints via projection during annealing.
- **Numer-iscore Comparison**: Justified by theoretical guarantees of annealing for global optimization.

### 2. Mathematical Specification

#### Objective: 
Minimize C₅ = maxₖ ∫ h(x)(1-h(x+k))dx over shifts in [0,2] (extended via periodicity).  
Discretization: h ∈ ℜⁿ with ∑h_i * (2/ₙ) = 1 (unit integral) and 0≤h_i≤1.

#### Formulation:
- **State Space**: h ∈ ℜⁿ [0,1n], with ∑h_i = n/2 (⁰ₙ).
- **Fitness Metric**: Maximum normalized overlap over phase shifts.
- **Penalty Function**: Measures deviation from best-known solution.
- **Constraint Handling**: Leverages projection operator (project_to_sum) to force feasibility.

#### Annealing Schedule:
- **Temperature Model**: T(initial)=1.0, T(τ)=T(initial)*(cooling_factor)ᵀ.
- **Cooling Rate**: Starts rapid (0.9 change factor) then slows (after 1/2 cooling steps).
- **Penalty Reception**: Movements accepted proportional to exp(-f_new/f_old * temp), weighted by temperature.

#### Local Refinement:
- **Steepest Descent**: Iterative coordinate descent (project-to-sum constraint) seeking smallest subgradient.
- **Convergence Check**: Drift-based stagnation (50 steps without improvement).

#### Associated Functions:
- `compute_c5(h)`: Evaluates objective via cross-correlation.
- `project_to_sum(h, target)`: Gram-schmidt projection of h to sum=target.
- `simulated_annealing(h, target, **params)`: Global exploration via annealing schedule.
- `coordinate_descent(h, target, **params)`: Local refinement with gradient projection.
