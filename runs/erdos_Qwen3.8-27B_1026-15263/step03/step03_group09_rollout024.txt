C₅ = max_m corr[m] * dx

Near the optimum, the "active" lags (those at the max) are the ones that matter. If we can identify them (say top 5 lags), then locally:
C₅ ≈ max_{a in active} corr[a] * dx

And corr[a] is a multilinear (bilinear) function of h. So the local problem is:

- Alternating: fix active set, solve the min-max (which is a convex problem if we introduce t = max_a bilin_a(h))
- Actually, min max_a bilin_a(h) is NOT convex in h (bilinear is not convex)

- Gradient descent on the weighted sum (with weights from softmax)

3. Use decreasing step size (like 1/sqrt(t) or 1/t)
