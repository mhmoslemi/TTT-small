   - Central Idea: The Erdős minimum overlap problem requires optimizing a step function h on [0,2] to minimize the maximum correlation with its shifted version (1-h). Our approach combines coarse-grained optimization with function families and refinement to escape local minima while maintaining the integral constraint.
   - Innovation: Unlike the parent algorithm's reliance on penalization and local refinement, we introduce: (a) coarse-grid optimization using diverse function families with analytical gradients, (b) multi-resolution refinement preserving constraint satisfaction, and (c) adaptive neighborhood searches around promising solutions.
   - Bottlenecks Addressed: Overcomes stagnation in local optima by using gradient-based global search and diverse function shapes. Ensures integral satisfaction through integrated projection rather than post-processing clipping.
