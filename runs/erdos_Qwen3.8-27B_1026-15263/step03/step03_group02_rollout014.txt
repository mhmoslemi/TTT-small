**Better move idea: "Lag-aware" moves**

Instead of random swaps, compute which lags are currently at (or near) the maximum. Then, generate swaps that are likely to reduce the overlap at those specific lags.

For a given lag k, the overlap is O(k) = #{i : h[i]=0, h[i+k]=1}. To reduce O(k), I want to:
- Change some h[i] from 0 to 1 where h[i+k]=1 (this removes one 0→1 pair at lag k)
- But I also need to change some h[j] from 1 to 0 to maintain the sum constraint
- The change at j will affect other lags

So a "lag-aware" move would be:
1. Find the lag k* with maximum overlap
2. Find a position i where h[i]=0 and h[i+k*]=1 (a "bad" pair)
3. Find a position j where h[j]=1 and h[j+k*]=0 (a "good" pair) - flipping this doesn't hurt lag k*
4. Swap i and j

This is more targeted than random swaps and should be more efficient.

Let me also think about **multi-point moves**:
- Move a block of 1s: pick a contiguous run of 1s and move the whole thing
