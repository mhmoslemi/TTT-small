The central idea is to apply a dimensional reduction and local search around the provided initial solution while enforcing integral constraints through a feasible transformation. This method avoids brute-force exploration of the full 70-dimensional space and instead focuses on improving the initial solution through systematic searches along its gradient directions, addressing the non-convex nature of the problem.

Key novel aspects:
- Uses directional decomposition to map the high-dimensional problem into multiple 1D optimization problems
- Maintains feasibility through a custom quadratic form repair transformation
- Balances systematic search with repair constraints during optimization
- Counteracts the curse of dimensionality by focusing on relative changes rather than absolute values

Limitations:
- Limited exploration breadth compared to global optimization techniques

  def repair(h_target, n_points):
      total_sum = np.sum(h_target)
      if abs(total_sum - n_points/2) < 1e-5:
          return h_target.copy()
      h_repaired = h_target * (n_points/2) / total_sum
      h_clipped = np.clip(h_repaired, 0, 1)
      s2 = np.sum(h_clipped)
      if abs(s2 - n_points/2) > 1e-5:

      return h_clipped
