Now, here's an important observation: c[n-1] = sum h[i](1-h[i]) = sum h[i] - sum h[i]² = n/2 - sum h[i]².

To minimize this, we want sum h[i]² to be as large as possible, which means h should be as "binary" as possible (concentrated at 0 and 1).

So there's a tension:
- To minimize c[n-1] (lag 0): make h binary
- To minimize other lags: might need h to be non-binary (to smooth out boundary effects)

The optimal solution balances these.

def make_feasible(h, n):
