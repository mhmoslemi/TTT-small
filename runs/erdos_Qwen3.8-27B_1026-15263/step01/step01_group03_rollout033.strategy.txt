- $h: [0,2] \rightarrow [0,1]$ approximated by n samples
- $dx = 2/n$
- Constraint: $\int h dx = 1$

**Objective** minimize:
$$
C_5 = \max_{k} \int h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx
$$
Scaled as $\max_k \operatorname{corr}[h, 1-h](k) \cdot dx$ (from np.correlate)

**Constraints**:

    - Apply one of k perturbation functions
    - Repair to feasible set if violated
    - Track best correlation value found
4. Periodically compute best solution across all grids

**Fitness Measure**:
- Primary: $-\max_k \operatorname{corr}[h, 1-h](k) \cdot dx$ (to maximize)
- Secondary: Solution feasibility

**Representation**:
- Solutions stored as numpy arrays with length n
- Objective evaluated via np.correlate with mode="full"
