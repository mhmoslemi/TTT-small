= (total pairs at distance d) - #{pairs at distance d with same value}

Number of same-value pairs = #{i: h[i]=h[i+d]} for i = 0,...,n-1-d.

So C(d) + C(-d) = (n-d) - #{i: h[i]=h[i+d]}
