C5 = max_k ∫₀² h(x)(1-h(x+k)) dx

At lag k=0: ∫ h(x)(1-h(x)) dx = ∫h - ∫h² = 1 - ∫h²
To minimize this, maximize ∫h², which means make h as extreme as possible (binary).

At other lags: we want h(x) and h(x+k) to be "anti-correlated" - when h(x) is high, h(x+k) should be low.
