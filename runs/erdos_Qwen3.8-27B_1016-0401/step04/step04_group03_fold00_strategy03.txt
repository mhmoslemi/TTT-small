1. Approach and Rationale
The key insight is to reformulate the problem by embedding the optimization in a reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS). This approach leverages the smoothness seeking property of the optimization to automatically identify regions with minimal autocorrelation. Instead of directly searching the original n-dimensional space, we map points to a high-dimensional feature space, which reduces the correlation structure to a sparse set of quadratic forms. This leads to faster convergence and potentially better global solutions.

We let the optimization be guided by a Gaussian kernel, which penalizes solutions with large self-correlation. The dual problem is a sparse linear program over kernel coefficients, which we solve using a projected conjugate gradient method with early termination. This allows us to escape shallow local optima while ensuring the integral constraint is satisfied automatically through the mapping.

2. Mathematical Specification
Let 𝒳 be the primal variable with support on [0,2], and choose a Gaussian kernel:
    K(x, y) = exp(-γ * ((x-y)² / σ²)²)
where γ and σ are hyperparameters.

The embedding φ:ℝ→ℋ (Hilbert space) satisfies:
