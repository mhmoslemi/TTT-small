# Mathematical specification
- **Variable Definition**: \( h \) is a piecewise constant function defined on \([0, 2]\) with \( n \) discrete points. \( h_i \in [0, 1] \) for \( i = 1 \) to \( n \), and the integral constraint translates to \( \sum_i h_i \cdot dx = 1 \) with \( dx = 2/n \), or equivalently \( \sum_i h_i = n/2 \).
- **Objective Function** (\( C_5 \)): Minimize \( C_5 = \frac{dx}{n} \times \max_k \langle h, 1 - h_k \rangle \), where \( \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle \) denotes the dot product at lag shift k. In discrete terms, \( C_5 \) is computed via \( \max_k \sum_i h[i] \cdot (1 - h[(i+k) \mod n]) \) for circular cross-correlation, but per problem, it's unconstrained cross-correlation with \( 0 \leq k \leq n-1 \), and \( dx = 2/n \) is necessary for integral normalization. However, since \( \sum_i h_i dx = 1 \) implies \( \sum_i h_i = n/2 \), we use:
  \[
  C_5 = \frac{2}{n} \times \max_k \left| \sum_{i=1}^{n-k} h[i] (1 - h[i+k]) \right|
  \]
  but in code, `np.correlate` with mode="full" computes the cross-correlation and must be scaled by dx. So:
  \[
  C_5 = \max_{k: k \in [-n+1, n-1]} \int_0^2 h(x)[1-h(x+k)] dx
  \]
  is approximated discretely, but in practice, we use:
  \[
  C_5 = \max(\text{corr\_values}) \times dx
  \]
  where `corr` is `np.correlate(h, 1-h, mode="full")[0, :]` for positive lags, but note `np.correlate` returns 2n-1 values for lag -n+1 to n-1.

- **Constraints**:
  1. \( 0 \leq h_i \leq 1 \) for all i
  2. \( \sum_i h_i \cdot dx = 1 \) (equivalent to \( \sum_i h_i = n/2 \))
- **Feasibility Enforcement**: Use `project_feasible(y, n)` to enforce constraints. The function scales and shifts h to [0,1] such that near-uniform optimization points lead to proper clipping. It minimizes the L2 distance under binding constraints.
  \[
  h := \text{project-feasible}(y) \implies \arg\min_{h: sum h_i = n/2, 0\leq h_i \leq 1} \| h - y \|_2
  \]

- **Perturbation Definition**: Structured perturbations target lag-space frequencies. In discrete terms, lag indices k correspond to shifts of k*dx. "Lag k" here refers to the shift offset in impose the worst overlap in C_5.

- **Bayesian Lag Selection**: Not via explicit GP, but empirically: track historical worst lags and score their probability of contribution; select lags with high empirical impact.
- **Main Objective & Candidate Handling**:

    \[
    \text{overlap} = \max(\text{np.correlate}(h, 1-h, \text{mode="full"})) \times dx
    \]
  - Accept candidates if improvement.
