### 2. Mathematical Specification

We define the optimization of \(\mathbf{h} \in \mathbb{R}^{n}\) subject to:
- \(0 \leq h_i \leq 1\) for all \(i\)
- \(\sum_{i=1}^n h_i = \frac{n}{2}\)
- Minimize \(C_5(\mathbf{h}) = \max_{k} \left( \frac{2}{n} \cdot [\text{correlation}(\mathbf{h},1-\mathbf{h})]_k \right)\)

where \(\text{correlation}(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b},mode=\text{full})\) is the linear correlation.

We introduce a gradient surrogate for faster convergence (not the exact gradient) but with a robust penalty system. The new gradient estimate is:

\[\hat{\nabla}_i =

1.5 \cdot [\text{negative }\frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}(\text{max correlation})] & \text{if near boundary or nonlinear regime} \\
0.9 \cdot \text{original SLSQP gradient} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}\]

This hybrid approach provides more reliable updates while maintaining robustness.
