1. **Multi-lag SLSQP with smooth max**: Instead of optimizing one lag at a time, use a log-sum-exp over the top lags. This gives a smooth approximation of the max that's differentiable everywhere.

2. **Adaptive T**: Start with low T (smooth) and increase T (sharp) to converge to the true max.

3. **Subgradient with multi-lag subgradient**: The subgradient of max(f_1, ..., f_m) at a point where f_i = f_j = max is any convex combination of the gradients of f_i and f_j. We can use the average of the active lags' gradients.

4. **Averaging in subgradient method**: Keep a running average of h over the last N iterates. This gives a better final solution.

5. **Try multiple n**: Specifically n=200 and n=350 in addition to n=280.

6. **Perturbation and re-optimization**: Add small random noise and re-optimize to escape local optima.
