   a. Pick a random pair (i,j) and a small δ (e.g., 0.01, 0.02, 0.05)
   b. Compute Δcorr for all lags
   c. Find new max corr
   d. If new max < current max (and bounds are satisfied), accept
   e. Otherwise, reject
4. After convergence, try larger δ or different move types

This should be very effective for the final refinement.
