1. **Approach and rationale**
Several current approaches rely solely on SLSQP or local search, which can easily get trapped in local optima. This strategy combines two powerful optimization techniques: Broyden-Fletcher-Goldfarb-Shanno (BFGS) for local refinement and particle swarm optimization (PSO) for global exploration. The central idea is to leverage PSO's ability to explore diverse regions while BFGS fine-tunes solutions in promising areas. This hybrid balances exploration and exploitation effectively.

The key bottleneck addressed is premature convergence to suboptimal solutions. PSO's particle formulation allows maintaining multiple candidate paths simultaneously, while BFGS provides rigorous local minimization. This improves upon standard SLSQP by offering better global search capabilities and more robust constraint handling.

Limitations include sensitivity to parameter tuning and potential performance degradation if the dimensionality increases beyond 70. Heuristics here are tactical adjustments based on observed behaviors in lower-dimensional spaces, not theoretical guarantees.
