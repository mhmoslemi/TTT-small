Let me be precise. After swap: h[i]=0, h[j]=1.
- corr[l] = sum_m h'[m](1-h'[m+l])
- h'[m] = h[m] except h'[i]=0, h'[j]=1
- h'[m+l] = h[m+l] except h'[i]=0, h'[j]=1

So corr'[l] = corr[l] - [contributions involving i or j] + [new contributions]

The contribution of position m to corr[l] is h[m](1-h[m+l]).
- Position i (was 1, now 0): old contribution to corr[l] as m=i: 1*(1-h[i+l]) = 1-h[i+l]. New: 0.
- Position i as m+l (i.e., m=i-l): old: h[i-l]*(1-h[i]) = h[i-l]*0 = 0. New: h[i-l]*(1-0) = h[i-l].
- Position j (was 0, now 1): old contribution as m=j: 0*(1-h[j+l]) = 0. New: 1*(1-h[j+l]) = 1-h[j+l].
- Position j as m+l (i.e., m=j-l): old: h[j-l]*(1-h[j]) = h[j-l]*1 = h[j-l]. New: h[j-l]*(1-1) = 0.

So delta_corr[l] = -(1-h[i+l]) + h[i-l] - (1-h[j+l]) + h[j-l]
(where we treat out-of-range as 0)

This means only lags l where i+l or i-l or j+l or j-l are in [0,n-1] are affected. That's at most 4 lags for each of i and j, so at most 8 lags change per swap.
