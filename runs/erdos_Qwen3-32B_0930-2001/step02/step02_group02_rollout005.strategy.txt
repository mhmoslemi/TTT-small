### 1. Approach and rationale

The problem is fundamentally about minimizing the maximum overlap between a function and its shifted complement, subject to integral constraints. The previous SLSQP-based approach with random perturbations may struggle with the non-convex nature of the problem and can get stuck in local minima. This strategy employs a **Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)** approach, which is particularly effective for exploring non-linear and non-convex solution spaces. PSO maintains a swarm of candidate solutions that evolve by balancing exploration (searching new areas) and exploitation (refining promising areas).

### 2. Mathematical specification

- **Variables**: Swarm of candidate solutions (particles) `h ∈ ℝ^{n_points}`, each representing a step function.
- **Objective Function**: For each particle, evaluate `f(h) = max_k (correlate(h, 1-h, 'full')) * dx`.
- **Constraints**:

  - `sum(h) = 0.5 * n_points` to satisfy the integral constraint.
- **Solver**: PSO with velocity updates based on particle best and swarm best positions.

#### 3. Feasibility repair
```python
def feasibility_function(h):
    # Clip values to [0,1]
    h = np.clip(h, 0, 1)
    # Ensure sum constraint
    current_sum = np.sum(h)
    if current_sum == 0:
        return h
    # Scale to meet sum constraint
    target_sum = 0.5 * len(h)
    scaling_factor = target_sum / current_sum
    h = h * scaling_factor
    return h
```
