But this code has multiple issues:

1. The way overlaps are computed in the convex optimization is very inefficient, with for loops and many constraints. For large n, this will be too slow.

2. The use of np.interp for upsampling may not preserve the step function nature of h and may violate the sum constraint.

3. The CVXPY problem for overlaps as quadratic expressions is not convex, and thus ECOS will fail to solve it.

Given these issues, it's unlikely that this code will work within the time budget. Therefore, perhaps the correct approach is to abandon the convex strategy and refine the initial gradient-based code with better tuning.
