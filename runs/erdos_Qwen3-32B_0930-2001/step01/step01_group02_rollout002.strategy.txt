This method systematically explores the solution space by allowing occasional uphill moves, which helps escape local optima. The process involves initializing from known constructions, perturbing the solution, maintaining feasibility, and using a cooling schedule to refine the solution over time. 

### Approach and Rationale
The central idea is to apply the Simulated Annealing algorithm to explore the solution space more thoroughly and avoid getting trapped in local optima. SA is well-suited for non-convex optimization problems due to its ability to escape local minima by occasionally accepting higher-energy states. We leverage the initial solution \( initial\_h\_values \) as a starting point and introduce controlled perturbations to explore different configurations. The perturbations are designed to maintain feasibility (0 ≤ h[i] ≤ 1 and the sum constraint), ensuring all candidate solutions are valid. An exponential cooling schedule helps gradually focus the search on promising regions.

### Mathematical Specification
- **Variables**: \( h \in \mathbb{R}^{n} \) where \( h[i] \in [0,1] \) and \( \sum h[i] = n/2 \)
- **Objective**: Minimize \( C_5 = \max\limits_k \left( \sum_{i} h[i](1-h[i+k]) \cdot dx \right) \)
- **Constraints**: 
  1. \( 0 \leq h[i] \leq 1 \)
  2. \( \sum h[i] = n/2 \)
- **Perturbation**: Randomly select a fraction of points to perturb, adjust to maintain feasibility.
- **Temperature**: Start with high \( T \), decrease multiplicatively each iteration.
- **Acceptance Probability**: \( P = \exp(-\Delta E / T) \) where \( \Delta E = C_{5,new} - C_{5,old} \)

### Implementation Procedure
1. **Initialization**:
   - Load \( initial\_h\_values \) if available.
   - Ensure it meets constraints: clip values and adjust sum if necessary.
   - Compute initial \( C_5 \).

2. **Parameter Setup**:
   - Set initial temperature \( T \) (e.g., based on initial \( C_5 \)).
   - Define cooling rate (e.g., 0.995).
   - Determine fraction of points to perturb (e.g., 0.1).

3. **Main Loop**:
   - While time budget allows:
     - Perturb \( m = \text{int}(0.1 \cdot n) \) random points.
     - Clip perturbed values within [0, 1].
     - Adjust sum to maintain \( \sum h[i] = n/2 \).
     - Compute new \( C_5 \).
     - Calculate energy difference \( \Delta E \).
     - If \( \Delta E < 0 \) or \( \exp(-\Delta E / T) > \text{random}(0,1) \), accept new \( h \).
     - Update best solution if \( C_5 \) is lower.
     - Decrease temperature.

4. **Termination**:
   - Return the best \( h \) with its \( C_5 \) and \( n \).
