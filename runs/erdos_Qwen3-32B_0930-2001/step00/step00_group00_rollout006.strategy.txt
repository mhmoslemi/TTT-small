## 2. Mathematical specification

### Variables and objective
- h ∈ [0,1]^n, sum(h) = n/2
- τ ≥ 0：MM step size for majorization
- μ ≥ 0：ADMM penalty parameter
- y ∈ ℝ^n：ADMM dual variable

### Objective to minimize
$$ C_5 = \max_k \int h(x)(1 - h(x+k)) dx $$

### MM surrogate function
The overlap integral can be rewritten as:
$$ C_5 = \max_k \sum_{i,j} h_i (1 - h_j) \cdot \text{Overlap}(x_i, x_j + k) $$
where Overlap(x_i, x_j) is 1 if x_i and x_j overlap, else 0.

Using MM, we construct a convex surrogate:
$$ F(h, y, τ) = \max_k \left[ \sum_i y_i (1 - h_j) \cdot \text{Overlap}(x_i, x_j + k) - \frac{τ}{2} \|h - y\|^2 \right] $$

### ADMM update steps
1. Minimize over h with y fixed
2. Update dual variables y
3. Apply MM step to τ
