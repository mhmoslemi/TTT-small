The key insight is that the overlap integral corresponds to a cross-correlation, which can be efficiently computed using the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT). The cross-correlation between h and (1 - h) can be computed as:
$$ \text{Corr}(h, 1-h, k) = F^{-1}\{F\{h\} \cdot F\{1-h\}^*\} $$

where $F$ denotes the Fourier transform and $^*$ denotes the complex conjugate. By leveraging this property, we can formulate an optimization problem in the frequency domain, which may find lower minima compared to direct time-domain approaches.

Key advantages:

- Exploits Fourier properties for more efficient exploration
- Directly manipulates correlation structure
- Maintains the physical feasibility of h through projection steps
