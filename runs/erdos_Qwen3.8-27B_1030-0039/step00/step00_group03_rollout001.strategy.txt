1. Approach and rationale

1.1 Central idea
Use the exact discrete evaluator as the only source of truth and exploit a special one-dimensional convexity property of a feasible two-coordinate exchange. For any feasible h, replace two coordinates a and b by
h_a(t) = h_a + t, h_b(t) = h_b - t,
keeping sum h fixed. Along every such direction, every lagged correlation term is a convex quadratic (or affine) function of t, because the only mixed product whose two entries move together has a nonnegative t^2 coefficient; hence the maximum over all lags is a convex unimodal function of one variable. An exact golden-section/Brent-type minimizer on the feasible t interval gives the best move available in that two-coordinate block, evaluated by the official objective.

Use this as an exact non-smooth coordinate-descent atom. Since the global problem is nonconvex and even this block descent can be trapped in a local basin, launch many mathematically valid starts and escape basins by:
- parent transfer and validation;
- constrained simulated annealing using exact score differences;
- randomized smooth/Fourier/binary seeds;
- basin hopping of the incumbent followed by exact two-coordinate line polish.

Every candidate has to pass the original array metric; intermediate smooth surrogate scores, acceptance values from annealing, and line-search residuals are never used to report a bound.

1.2 Why this may beat the supplied parent
- The parent is a point-quality candidate. A local exact neighboring two-coordinate move decides the best achievable improvement in that direction, so it cannot leave several standard one- or two-variable moves unexploited because of bad finite stepping or surrogate error.
- The evaluator is discontinuous and has penalty terms at the zero lag from h(1-h), and finite-support boundary terms for nonzero lags. The exact probe does not replace it with an autocorrelation identity and can reduce the very edge terms whose sign changes with discretization.
- Many coordinate moves on a grid are almost binary, and exact line minimization can precisely generate small fractional values at binding constraints. These can remove low-medium first order improvements that smooth surrogate methods save outside the exact feasibility set.
- Larger arrays are included for continuity refinement: the returned administratively evaluates density dx=2/n for that grid.
