One more idea: **coordinate descent on the active lag**. 

For the active lag k, the objective is:

To minimize f_k, we want to maximize sum_{i=0}^{n-1-k} h[i]*h[i+k].

This is a max-cut-like problem on a path graph. The optimal solution would make h[i] and h[i+k] as different as possible (one near 0, other near 1) for all i.
