**1. Approach and Rationale**  
The problem minimizes the maximum overlap between a step function \( h \) and its shifts. The current method’s local search (`pair_descent`) might get stuck in suboptimal regions due to small perturbations and gradient estimation issues. We introduce **randomized descent with adaptive neighborhood exploration (RDANE)**. This approach escapes local minima by generating a random "scapegoat" solution that violates constraints, then restoring feasibility while incorporating positive improvements from the worst shift, scaled by a confidence parameter. This reduces dependence on gradient estimates and ensures diverse exploration, potentially lowering \( C \). The method is initialized from `initial_h_values` if available, else from random projections.
