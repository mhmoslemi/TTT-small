   **IFT approach**:
   1. Zero-pad a to length N >= 2n-1.
   2. Compute A[k] = fft(a_padded, N).
   3. The aperiodic auto-convolution is: c = ifft(|A|^2).real (length N, take first 2n-1).
   4. Modify |A| to reduce the peak of c.
   5. Compute new a = ifft(|A|_modified * phase(A)).real[:n].
   6. Clip to [0, ∞) and normalize.

   Step 4 is the key. Options:
   a) |A|_new = |A|^β for β < 1 (compression)
   b) |A|_new = |A| * (1-α) + α * mean(|A|) (towards flat)
   c) |A|_new = |A| - γ * ∂peak/∂|A| (gradient descent in frequency domain)

   Option (a) is the simplest and often works well. The parameter β controls the strength of compression.

   However, there's a problem: after clipping negative values in step 6, the spectrum is no longer |A|^2 of the clipped signal. So the IFT might oscillate or converge slowly.
