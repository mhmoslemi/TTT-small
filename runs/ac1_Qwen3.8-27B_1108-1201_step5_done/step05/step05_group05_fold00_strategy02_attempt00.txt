**Strategy:**

We'll use an iterative optimization approach that combines several techniques to reduce the objective function value.

**Mathematical Specification:**

We are to minimize: 
\( \frac{2 \cdot n \cdot \max\left\{ (\text{full\_convolve}(a))[0], (\text{full\_convolve}(a))[1], ..., (\text{full\_convolve}(a))[2\cdot n-2] \right\} }{(\text{sum}(a))^2} \)

Let T = the autocorrelation sequence from full convolution (length 2*n-1)
Let M = max(T)
The objective function is: \( \frac{2\cdot n \cdot M}{(\text{sum}(a))^2} \)

Constraints:
- All \( a_i \geq 0 \)
- \( \text{sum}(a) \geq 0.01 \)
- We cannot assume symmetry to reduce the problem size

**Implementation Procedure:**
We'll generate candidate sequences of different lengths and initialize them with different distribution patterns. For each candidate sequence, we'll apply an optimization process that includes:

1. Coordinate Descent (CD): 
   - Update coordinates proportionally to the decrease in T at the affected indices
   - Use a scaling factor to maintain L1-norm but keep values non-negative

2. Frank-Wolfe (FW) LP Direction:
   - Use the current best sequence as the reference (parent) for each gradient calculation
   - Adjust the sequence to reduce the correlation maximum

3. Subgradient (SG) Optimization:
   - Smooth the correlation by performing multiple gradient steps
   - This helps avoid early convergence and local minima
