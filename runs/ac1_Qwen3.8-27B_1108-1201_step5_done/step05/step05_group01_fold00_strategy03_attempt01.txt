5. **Compute Budget and Robustness**
   - **Time Allocation**: Each evaluation stretches computational time by monitoring the moiré count with optimized sampling.

6. **Validation and Return Contract**
   - **Finite Check**: Ensure each candidate sequence produces a finite score using the built-in validation.
   - **Conversion**: After optimization, convert the frequency domain coefficients back to a real-valued time sequence, verify all coefficients fall within allowed bounds, and re-evaluate with the given function.
