### 2. Mathematical Specification  

#### Decision Variables  
- \( h \in [0, 1]^n \) with \( \sum h = n/2 \).  

#### Objective Function  
Minimize:  
\[
C_5 = \max_{k} \left( \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} h[i](1 - h[(i+k) \mod n]) \cdot dx \right)
\]  
where \( dx = \frac{2}{n} \).  

#### PSO Dynamics  
- Each particle \( p \) has position \( h_p \) and velocity \( v_p \).  
- Velocity update:  
  \[
  v_p^{(t+1)} = w v_p^{(t)} + c_1 r_1 (p.pbest - h_p) + c_2 r_2 (p.swarmbest - h_p)
  \]  
  where \( w=0.7 \) (inertia), \( c_1=c_2=2.0 \) (cognition and social weights), \( r_1,r_2 \sim U(0,1) \).  
- Position update:  
  \[
  h_p^{(t+1)} = h_p^{(t)} + v_p^{(t+1)}
  \]  
- Projection to feasible region:  
  1. Clip \( h_p \) ∈ [0, 1].  
  2. Rescale \( h_p \) to ensure \( \sum h_p = n/2 \).  

#### Feasibility Enforcement  
After updating positions, each particle's \( h \) is clipped and rescaled to satisfy constraints.  

#### Fitness Assignment  
Particle fitness is \( C_5 \), scaled by feasibility.
